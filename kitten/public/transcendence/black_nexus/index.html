A Black Nexus is a high-tier Cryptotheology building found in the Religion tab that serves as a cornerstone for scaling your late-game relic production. It is designed to make the conversion of Time Crystals into Relics more efficient and to boost the passive generation of relics from space-based structures.

Core Mechanics and Costs

  • Cost: 5,000 Relics.
  • Price Ratio: 1.15.
  • Prerequisite: You must have attained Transcendence Tier 3 to unlock it.
  • Synergy: Its effectiveness is directly tied to the number of Black Pyramids you have constructed.

Primary Effects

The Black Nexus enhances two specific methods of obtaining relics:

  1. Time Crystal Refinement: Standard refinement converts 25 Time Crystals into 1 Relic. With a Black Nexus, the yield increases according to the formula: 1 + (Black Nexus × Black Pyramid) relics per 25 crystals.
  2. Relic Station Production: It significantly boosts the output of Relic Stations (upgraded Space Beacons). Each Black Nexus improves this production by 10% per Black Pyramid.

Strategic Utility: The “Type 1 Relic Engine”

In the mid-to-late game, the Black Nexus is a vital part of what players call the “Type 1 Relic Engine”.

  • Scaling Passive Income: Once you have at least 5,000 maximum Antimatter, Relic Stations produce 0.01 relics per day. By stacking Black Nexuses and Black Pyramids, you can increase this passive daily income to a point where you no longer rely solely on manual trading with Leviathans for your relic supply.
  • Synergy with Black Sky: Completing the Black Sky challenge provides an “invisible” Black Pyramid, which permanently improves the scaling of your Black Nexus buildings in all future runs.
  • Late-Game Goal: Experienced players recommend buying several levels of Black Nexus alongside Black Core (to increase the sorrow limit for more pyramids) to maximise the efficiency of your time-skipping and relic-farming operations.
