Dark Nova is a permanent endgame Cryptotheology building that increases your civilisation’s global energy production by +2% per level. Like all Cryptotheology structures, it carries over through resets, although you must re-research the “Cryptotheology” technology in each new run to view or purchase additional units.

Costs and Requirements

Building a Dark Nova represents a significant investment in late-game resources and spiritual progression.

  • Relic Cost: 75,000 Relics.
  • Void Cost: 7,500 Void.
  • Price Ratio: 1.15.
  • Prerequisite: You must have attained Transcendence Tier 20 to unlock this building. Reaching Tier 20 requires an accumulation of approximately 451.14M% Epiphany.

Strategic Utility

Dark Nova is situated in the final phase of the game (Phase 3/Endgame), where energy management becomes a primary bottleneck for scientific and industrial expansion.

  • Endgame Power Demands: As you progress toward the “positive reset” loop, you must power high-consumption buildings like AI Cores (which consume increasing energy per unit), Containment Chambers (50 Watts each), and Entanglement Stations (25 Watts each). The +2% global production bonus helps sustain these power-hungry systems.
  • Scaling for Infinity: Advanced players utilize the energy boost to support massive arrays of Sunlifters and Hydro Plants while attempting to reach the 8.14 billion Unobtainium capacity required for 100 Chronospheres.
  • Synergy with Void Rifts: The global energy production bonus stacks with other modifiers, helping to maintain a positive energy surplus during Time Skips, which is necessary for Antimatter generation and the automated “shatter engine”.

Building Tier Context

In the progression of Cryptotheology, Dark Nova is one of the final buildings unlocked, coming after other critical structures like Blazars (Tier 15) but before Mausoleums (Tier 23) and Holy Genocide (Tier 25). It is a foundational component for players seeking to “chase infinity” by overcapping resources through thousands of years of skipped time.
