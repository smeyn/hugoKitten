Black Radiance is an advanced Cryptotheology building that accelerates the rate at which Alicorns are corrupted into Necrocorns. It is a permanent structure that carries over through resets, provided you research the Cryptotheology technology in each new run.

Cost and Requirements

  • Cost: 37.5K Relics.
  • Price Ratio: 1.15.
  • Prerequisite: Transcendence Tier 12.

Core Effects

The primary function of Black Radiance is to speed up the corruption countdown initiated by Markers.

  • Corruption Bonus: The approximate bonus to corruption speed is 3.464% multiplied by the square root of (number of Black Radiances $\times$ points of Black Liquid Sorrow).
  • Synergy: Because the bonus depends on your current Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS), its effectiveness increases as you accumulate more sorrow or build Black Cores to raise your sorrow limit.

Strategic Utility

In the late-game “Relic Engine” phase, Black Radiance is used to optimize the generation of Necrocorns:

  • Feeding Leviathans: Faster corruption provides a steadier supply of Necrocorns to feed to the Leviathans, which permanently increases their energy cap.
  • Trade Efficiency: Higher Leviathan energy results in more Time Crystals and Relics per trade, making Black Radiance a vital component for accelerating the growth of your endgame resources.
