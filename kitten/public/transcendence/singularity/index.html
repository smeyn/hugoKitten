In Kittens Game, the Singularity is a high-level Metaphysics upgrade that serves as a massive force multiplier for your late-game resource production. It represents the point where your civilization’s technological and spiritual progress merge into a single, unstoppable advancement.

Key Effects

The Singularity is unique because it provides a global production bonus that scales with your total number of Metaphysics upgrades:

  • Global Production Bonus: It provides a +5% bonus to all production.
  • The “Price Ratio” Capstone: While upgrades like Golden Ratio and Vitruvian Feline reduce the price of buildings, Singularity is often the final “big purchase” players make in the Metaphysics tree before focusing entirely on Paragon hoarding or Cryptotheology.
  • Multiplier Synergy: This 5% is multiplicative. In your current stage—dealing with Enriched Uranium and Temporal Distortion—this small percentage translates to massive raw gains in Unobtainium, Antimatter, and Void.

Unlock Requirements & Cost

To see Singularity in your Metaphysics tab, you must have progressed through the “feline” hierarchy of upgrades (Malkuth, Yesod, etc.):

Requirement Type Cost / Detail
Metaphysics Tab Requires Astronomy, Philosophy, and a Ziggurat.
Zodiac Prerequisite You must have purchased the Zodiac upgrade first.
Paragon Resource 250 Paragon

Strategic Context

Given that you are managing complex late-game systems, the Singularity is essentially your “end-game” badge for a standard run.

  1. Paragon Management: 250 Paragon is a significant investment. You should only buy Singularity if you already have the core Price Ratio upgrades (Engineering, Golden Ratio, Vitruvian Feline, Renaissance). If you don’t have those, the price reduction they offer is mathematically superior to the 5% production boost from Singularity.
  2. The Dark Future: If you are pushing toward Year 40,000+ (the Dark Future), every percentage point matters. Singularity helps counteract the production penalties that can occur when Temporal Distortion gets out of hand.
  3. Reset Planning: Since Metaphysics upgrades are permanent, buying Singularity makes every subsequent run 5% faster from the very first minute. It makes the “early game slog” almost non-existent.

Expert Tip: If you are currently farming Void, the 5% bonus from Singularity also applies to the effectiveness of your Void Resonators, slightly increasing the frequency of your paradoxes and your total Void yield.

Summary: Should you buy it now?

  • YES: If you have over 750 total Paragon and already own Renaissance.
  • NO: If you are still struggling to lower building prices. Save that Paragon for Renaissance (750 Paragon) first; it is the single most powerful upgrade in the game.
