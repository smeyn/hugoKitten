The Black Library is an advanced Cryptotheology building used to scale your science storage cap by enhancing the effectiveness of your existing religious and scientific upgrades. It is a permanent structure that carries over through resets, allowing for exponential growth in science capacity across multiple runs.

Requirements and Costs

Because it is part of the endgame “Phase 3” progression, the requirements for the Black Library are steep:

  • Cost: 30,000 Relics.
  • Price Ratio: 1.15.
  • Prerequisite: You must have attained Transcendence Tier 9.
  • Technology: You must research the Cryptotheology technology each run to view or purchase these buildings.

Primary Effect: Codex Scaling

The Black Library focuses on improving the Codex Leviathanianus, a metaphysics upgrade that increases your compendia science limit based on your Transcendence tier.

  • Scaling Bonus: Each Black Library improves the Codex Leviathanianus scaling by +2%.
  • Science Storage: This allows Compendiums to continue providing +10 science storage even after you have reached the standard science cap provided by buildings.

Synergies and Advanced Scaling

The Black Library is highly integrated with other late-game religious and industrial systems:

Synergy Component Effect on Black Library
Unicorn Graveyard Directly improves the bonus provided by the Black Library.
Pact of Purity Increases the Black Library ratio by +0.05% for every Black Pyramid you own.
Transcendence As your Transcendence tier increases, the base effectiveness of the Codex it scales also rises.

Strategic Context

In the larger context of a “Relic Engine” run, the Black Library is essential for bypassing the science caps required for the most expensive endgame technologies, such as Exophysics (25M Science) or advanced space structures. By stacking Black Libraries and Unicorn Graveyards, you ensure that your stockpile of Compendiums provides a massive, stable foundation for your scientific research.
