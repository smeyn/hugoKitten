In Kittens Game, the Mausoleum is a late-game religious building that serves as a cornerstone for managing Temporal Distortion and scaling your high-level faith bonuses.

It represents a permanent monument to your fallen kittens, allowing their “legacy” to stabilize your timeline.

Key Effects

The Mausoleum provides three critical benefits for advanced players:

  • Distortion Reduction: It is the primary building used to reduce Temporal Distortion. Each Mausoleum provides a flat reduction to the distortion generated by Temporal Paradoxes, allowing you to farm Void more sustainably without crashing your production.
  • Solar Revolution Bonus: It increases the effectiveness of your Solar Revolution bonus. This allows your total production multiplier from faith to reach even higher levels, which is essential for late-game resource scaling.
  • Epimetheus Synergy: In the Metaphysics tree, the Mausoleum interacts with “The Codex” series of upgrades, further boosting your science and faith limits.

How to Unlock and Build

Since you are currently dealing with Enriched Uranium, you likely have most of these requirements met:

Strategic Use

  1. Void Farming: If you are building Chronospheres, you need Void. Use Mausoleums to keep your Distortion under control while your Void Resonators trigger paradoxes.
  2. Faith Caps: Once you have a high Transcendence Tier, Mausoleums are one of the few ways to continue growing your global production bonus when other buildings hit their diminishing returns.
  3. Stability: If you notice your resource production “flickering” or dropping unexpectedly, it’s likely due to high Distortion. Building 5–10 Mausoleums usually stabilizes the timeline for mid-range Void farming.

Summary of Benefits

  • Primary Purpose: Stabilizing time and boosting late-game production.
  • Scaling: Its costs scale moderately, making it a reliable place to dump excess Relics once your Cryptotheology is established.
