The Sky Palace is a specialized religious building that is part of the advanced Unicorn progression line.

Here is a detailed breakdown of its function, effects, and prerequisites:

Function and Effects

The Sky Palace provides multiple bonuses related to the generation of rare luxury resources and general storage:

  • Unicorn Generation: It improves unicorn generation by 50%.
  • Gold Storage: It improves gold storage by +1%.
  • Alicorn Production: It produces $0.00002$ alicorns per tick (or $0.0001$ per second, $0.08$ per year).
  • Alicorn Event: It enables a random event that grants one alicorn.
    • Each Sky Palace built increases the frequency of this event by $0.01%$ per game-day (or $1%$ per day per 100 palaces).
  • Ivory Meteors: It increases ivory gained from Ivory Meteors by $5%$.

Acquisition and Prerequisites

The Sky Palace is unlocked and constructed in the Religion tab, following a specific upgrade path:

  • Prerequisites: You must complete 1 Ivory Citadel to unlock the Sky Palace.
    • The Ivory Citadel requires completing an Ivory Tower, which in turn requires completing a Unicorn Tomb [1, 2].
  • Cost: The Sky Palace requires the following resources to build:
    • 500 Tears
    • 125,000 Ivory
    • 5 Megalith
  • Ratio: It has a price ratio of $1.15$.

Place in the Unicorn Progression

The Sky Palace is an intermediate step in maximizing unicorn-related production, leading to even more powerful buildings:

  • Once you complete 1 Sky Palace, you unlock the Unicorn Utopia, which further improves unicorn generation by $250%$ and increases the Time Crystal Refine ratio by $5%$ [3].
  • The Sky Palace, along with other advanced Unicorn buildings like the Ivory Tower and Ivory Citadel, helps generate ivory and alicorns, which are critical for late-game progression [1, 2]. Unicorns, alicorns, and tears are all considered luxury resources, contributing a bonus to happiness [4].
