The Unicorn Necropolis is an advanced endgame building found in the Cryptotheology section of the Religion tab. It is primarily used to accelerate the generation of Necrocorns by increasing the rate of alicorn corruption.

Core Statistics and Costs

Attribute Details
Cost 15 Necrocorns, 2500 Megaliths, 100 Alicorns, and 5 Void
Price Ratio 1.15
Prerequisite Unicorn Graveyard
Primary Effect Increases Necrocorn corruption speed by 10%

Mechanics and Synergy

The Unicorn Necropolis functions by speeding up the “corruption” process that transforms your Alicorns into Necrocorns.

  • Corruption Loop: While Markers initiate the ticking corruption countdown, the Necropolis serves as a multiplier for that speed.
  • The “One Necrocorn” Rule: For these buildings to function, you must maintain a balance of at least 1 Necrocorn in your inventory.

Strategic Utility and ROI

Because the building itself costs Necrocorns—which are otherwise used to feed Leviathans to increase their energy cap—there is a specific threshold for when they become a “profitable” investment.

  • The Break-Even Point: Strategic guides suggest that the Unicorn Necropolis only becomes truly profitable at 11 units.
  • Opportunity Cost: For the first 9 or 10 buildings, the rate at which Necrocorns are generated is slower than the rate at which you would have gained benefits by simply feeding those Necrocorns directly to the Leviathans.
  • Total Investment: Reaching this profitability threshold (11 Necropolises) requires a total investment of 365 Necrocorns.

Endgame Context

The building is described as “The ocean of blood at the end of the lane”. It represents the final stage of the unicorn-to-necrocorn pipeline, allowing players to eventually automate the feeding of Leviathans at high speeds to maximize trade ratios for Time Crystals and Relics.
