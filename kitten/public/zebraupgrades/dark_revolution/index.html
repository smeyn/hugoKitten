Dark Revolution is a religion-tier upgrade in Kittens Game that acts as a pivot point for your late-game faith and resource strategy. It essentially converts your religious progression into a tool for efficiency and speed, particularly regarding the “Dark Future” mechanics.

Key Benefits & Effects

  • Solar Revolution Cap: Its most significant effect is providing a way to increase the Solar Revolution production bonus cap. In later stages of the game, your faith bonus can become so massive it hits a ceiling; Dark Revolution helps push through that.
  • Kitten Sacrifice Efficiency: It increases the yield of Tears when sacrificing kittens (via the Ziggurat). This is crucial for building up your Cryptotheology structures (like Black Obelisks and Event Horizons).
  • Blackcoin Interaction: While it doesn’t directly change the market price of Blackcoin, the increased faith and relic generation it supports allow you to engage in much larger Blackcoin speculation cycles.
  • Zebra Synergy: It makes Zebra Outposts 10x more effective at increasing your Catpower production limit, which is vital for late-game hunting and trading.

How to Unlock It

To see Dark Revolution in your Religion tab, you typically need to meet several high-level requirements:

  1. Transcendence Tier: You generally need to be at least Transcendence Tier 5-10.
  2. Zebra Workshop: You must have unlocked the Zebra Workshop (which requires hunting with at least 10 Zebras and gathering Bloodstones).
  3. Cryptotheology: Having at least one Black Pyramid or Marker is often a prerequisite to unlock the “darker” side of the religion tree.

Strategic Use

You should prioritize Dark Revolution when your runs start lasting long enough to hit the Dark Future (Year 40,000+). It helps offset the production penalties that occur in the deep future and scales your resource generation to the levels needed for Chronospheres and massive paragon grinding.

Pro Tip: Combine Dark Revolution with Black Obelisks. The Obelisks increase the effectiveness of your Solar Revolution bonus, and Dark Revolution ensures you have the raw power to reach those higher caps.
