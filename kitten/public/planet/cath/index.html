Cath (${⌾}$) refers to two distinct concepts within Kittens Game: the name of the solar system where the game takes place, and a specific five-year astrological cycle.

1. The Cath Solar System

Cath is the name of the solar system in which the star Helios resides, which is a G2V spectral type star at the system’s centre. The core resources of the game, such as unobtainium, are produced on planets or satellites within the Cath solar system.

2. The Cath Astrological Cycle

Cath is one of the ten planetary cycles in the time system, with each cycle lasting five years. It is identified by the glyph ${⌾}$. The effects of this cycle are only triggered once the Numerology upgrade has been researched.

Category Detail
Duration 5 years
Glyph ${⌾}$

Effects with Numerology:

When the Cath cycle is active, it provides several bonuses and a specific penalty related to space structures:

  • Space Elevator transfer production bonus (for oil only): Increased by $+100%$. The Space Elevator structure also has its conversion production (resources produced by Smelters, Calciners, and Accelerators) on Cath boosted by a bonus of $0.4%$ per leader rank per uncapped population buildings.
  • Satellite starchart production: Increased by $+100%$.
  • Satellite observatory’s science bonus: Increased by $+100%$.
  • Space Station science bonus: Increased by $+50%$.
  • Space Beacon starchart production: Decreased by $-50%$.

Festival Effect with Numeromancy:

If the advanced Numeromancy upgrade is researched, holding a festival during the Cath cycle grants a significant $\times 2$ multiplier to Catpower income.
