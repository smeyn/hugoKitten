Umbra refers to both a deep space destination reachable via a mission and a recurring astrological cycle that impacts your production in Kittens Game.

1. Umbra Cycle (Time System)

Umbra is one of the ten cycles featured in the game’s time system, identified by the glyph ${⍦}$. Each cycle lasts for five years.

When the appropriate technologies are researched, the Umbra cycle provides specific bonuses and penalties:

  • With Numerology: The cycle affects production from specific space structures:
    • The uranium output of the Planet Cracker is decreased by $-10%$.
    • The oil output of the Hydraulic Fracturer is decreased by $-25%$.
    • The HR Harvester Energy Production is increased by $+50%$.
  • With Numeromancy (Festival Bonus): Holding a festival during the Umbra cycle grants a $1.5\times$ income multiplier for Coal, Iron, Titanium, and Gold.

2. Umbra Mission and HR Harvester

Umbra is a destination in deep space, described as a supermassive black hole at the heart of the Helios system.

  • Mission Cost and Prerequisite: The Umbra Mission is unlocked after the Yarn Mission. It is a high-cost mission, requiring:
    • 25,000 Starchart
    • 500k Science
    • 25k Kerosene
    • 15k Thorium
  • Travel Time: The journey to Umbra takes 7,600 game-days, equivalent to 4 hours, 13 minutes, and 20 seconds in real-time.
  • Building Unlocked: The successful mission unlocks the Umbra Planet and the HR Harvester building. The HR Harvester operates on the energy of the black hole evaporation to generate energy. This energy production starts at $1{W}$ and slowly increases over time.
  • Progression: The Umbra Mission subsequently unlocks the Charon Mission.
