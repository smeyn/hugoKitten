T-minus (⍝) refers to both a significant deep space mission destination and a recurring astronomical cycle in Kittens Game.

1. T-Minus Space Mission and Structures

The T-minus Mission targets Terminus, a supermassive ice giant at the far end of the Helios solar system.

Detail Specification
Prerequisite The T-minus Mission is unlocked after the Piscine Mission.
Cost 2,500 Starchart, 12,000 Titanium, 225,000 Science, and 750 Kerosene.
Travel Time 2,500 game-days (1 hour, 23 minutes, 20 seconds in real-time).
Unlocks Successful completion unlocks the T-Minus Planet, the Helios Mission, the Kairo Mission, and the Cryostation building.

Cryostation Building: The Cryostation, unlocked by this mission, is a vast storage facility complex that provides significant capacity increases for wood, minerals, iron, coal, uranium, titanium, oil, and unobtainium. Critically, each Cryostation provides $+750$ maximum Unobtainium (UO) storage. UO capacity, specifically from Cryostations and Moon Bases, defines the deep endgame progression toward positive chrono-resets.

2. T-Minus Astrological Cycle

T-minus (${⍝}$) is one of the ten planetary cycles in the game’s time system, lasting five years.

  • With Numerology: When the Numerology upgrade is researched, the T-minus cycle affects space structures:
    • Sunlifter energy output is decreased by $-50%$.
    • Cryostation Storage is increased by $+20%$.
  • Festival Effect with Numeromancy: Holding a festival during the T-minus cycle grants a $\times 2$ multiplier to Oil income.

The temporary $+20%$ boost to Cryostation storage during the T-minus cycle is significant because Cryostations are one of only two buildings that provide the maximum Unobtainium storage necessary for the late game.
