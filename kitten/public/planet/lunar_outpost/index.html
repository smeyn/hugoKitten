A Lunar Outpost is a space structure essential for late-game progression, specifically for refining uranium into unobtainium.

Here is a comprehensive overview of the Lunar Outpost based on the sources:

Primary Function and Resources

The Lunar Outpost serves a single, critical purpose:

  • It refines uranium into unobtainium. Unobtainium is a special resource required to unlock Metaphysics and to purchase high-tier buildings and upgrades, including Unobtainium Huts.
  • Consumption Rate: The Lunar Outpost consumes uranium at a rate of 1.75 per second.
  • Production Rate: It produces unobtainium at a rate of 0.035 per second. This means, at full speed, it can produce 5 unobtainium in 142.8 seconds (about 2.3 minutes).

Acquisition and Prerequisites

The Lunar Outpost is unlocked and built through the Space tab:

  • Prerequisite: You must first research the Rocketry science to enable the Space tab and space missions [5, 6].
  • Mission Requirement: The Lunar Outpost itself is unlocked by completing the Moon Mission.
  • Starchart Cost: To complete the Lunar Outpost mission, you need 650 Starcharts [7]. The total starcharts needed to reach the Lunar Outpost, starting from the Orbital Launch (250) and Moon Mission (500), is 1,400 Starcharts [7].
  • Other Costs: The Moon Mission itself also requires 45K oil [8].

Production Efficiency and Optimization

The Lunar Outpost’s production of unobtainium is noted for its inefficiency relative to its resource consumption, which heavily bottlenecks early unobtainium production:

  • Production Slowdown: If your main source of uranium is a single Accelerator (which produces $0.0125$ uranium per second), the Lunar Outpost will run 140 times slower than its maximum speed [9]. This means it could take 5.3 hours to produce 5 unobtainium, rather than 2.3 minutes [4, 9].
  • Trade Solution: Production can be sped up by trading with Dragons for uranium [9].
  • Redshift Advantage (Offline): The source notes that the “Redshift” mechanic, which prevents resources from being deliberately reduced during offline progression, effectively allows Lunar Outposts to run as if you have infinite uranium while offline. This significantly speeds up unobtainium gain during offline progress because the outpost runs at full speed [10].
  • Upgrades: The Microwarp Reactors Workshop upgrade, unlocked by Exometallurgy, makes unobtainium production 75% more effective [11].

Related Space Structures

The Lunar Outpost is key to later resource storage and production:

  • Moon Base: Once you produce 50 unobtainium using the Lunar Outpost, you can build a Moon Base [12]. The Moon Base then increases your overall Unobtainium storage capacity [12, 13].
  • Uranium Consumption: Lunar Outposts initially consume 10 Wt of Energy, although this consumption is reduced by Antimatter Bases [14].

In short, the Lunar Outpost is the specialized factory on the moon that converts titanium-derived uranium into the scarce, late-game resource unobtainium, paving the way for further progression into space and metaphysics [1, 4].
