Particle Physics is a pivotal mid-to-late-game science technology that serves as the gateway to uranium refining and advanced temporal mechanics.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Science Cost: 185,000 Science.
  • Blueprint Cost: 135 Blueprints.
  • Prerequisite: You must first research Nuclear Fission.
  • Strategic Tip: Assigning a Scientist leader can reduce the science cost of this technology by 5%, bringing it down to approximately 175,750 Science.

Unlocked Buildings: The Accelerator

Researching Particle Physics is the mandatory prerequisite for constructing Accelerators.

  • Function: Accelerators provide a steady source of Uranium by refining Titanium (at a rate of -0.015 Titanium and +0.0025 Uranium per tick).
  • Energy Impact: Each active Accelerator consumes 2 Watts of energy.
  • Storage Potential: Once the Energy Rifts upgrade is researched (via Dimensional Physics), Accelerators provide massive storage capacity for various resources, including catnip, wood, minerals, coal, iron, titanium, and gold.

Unlocked Technologies

Particle Physics is required to unlock two high-tier science branches:

  • Chronophysics: The branch dealing with time manipulation, Chronospheres, and reset preservation.
  • Dimensional Physics: The branch that unlocks the LHC (Large Hadron Collider) and Energy Rifts for expanded storage.

Unlocked Workshop Upgrades

  • Enriched Uranium: Reduces the Uranium consumption of Reactors by 25% and provides a +25% Uranium production bonus to Quarries.
  • Railgun: A permanent military upgrade that increases the Catpower production of Hunter kittens by 25%.

Strategic Importance

Particle Physics marks the transition into the “Metaphysics” era. It is essential for generating the initial Unobtainium needed for space exploration; because Lunar Outposts consume uranium to produce unobtainium, players often rely on Accelerators (and trading with Dragons) to maintain their uranium supply until more efficient space-based uranium sources like Planet Crackers are built.
