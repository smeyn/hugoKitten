Robotics is a pivotal late-mid-game science in Kittens Game, marking a critical transition in the “Prestige and Energy Transition Epoch”. It focuses on industrial automation, significant energy infrastructure shifts, and reducing the biological burden of your kitten workforce.

Costs and Prerequisites

  • Science Cost: 140,000.
  • Resource Cost: 80 Blueprints.
  • Prerequisite: You must have researched Electronics.

The Hydro Plant Pivot

Researching Robotics allows you to upgrade your Aqueducts into Hydro Plants.

  • Effect: Each Hydro Plant provides +5 Energy (which can be increased to 5.75Wt with the Hydro Plant Turbines upgrade).
  • Strategic Risk: This is described as a “high-risk” infrastructure pivot. While Hydro Plants are vital for powering your growing industrial base, upgrading them causes you to lose the +3% catnip production bonus provided by Aqueducts.
  • Recommendation: Do not upgrade until your titanium production is robust and you are certain your catnip production (boosted by Paragon and Karma) can sustain the population without the Aqueduct bonus.

Unlocked Workshop Upgrades

Robotics provides access to several upgrades that drastically improve industrial output and kitten efficiency:

  • Robotic Assistance: Reduces the catnip consumption of working kittens by 25%. This is essential for managing the “happiness liability,” where high happiness levels normally cause massive spikes in food demand.
  • Steel Plants: This causes Calciners to automatically convert 10% of their iron output into Steel (at a 100:1 ratio).
  • Rotary Kiln: Dramatically enhances Calciner efficiency, making them 75% more effective at producing Iron and 3 times as effective at producing Titanium.
  • Factory Robotics: Improves Engineer effectiveness by increasing the “know-how” of Class 1, 2, and 3 Engineers.

Resource and Progression Unlocks

  • Tankers: Robotics enables the crafting of Tankers in the Workshop. Each Tanker increases your maximum Oil capacity by 500. While useful for reaching the high oil caps required for space missions, some strategies suggest prioritizing steel-grinding for Oil Wells instead, as Tankers can be expensive in the early-space era.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Robotics is the mandatory prerequisite for the Artificial Intelligence science, which leads to AI Cores and the Gigaflop era.

Strategic Significance

Robotics represents the point where your civilization begins to “replace” marginal agricultural bonuses with high-density energy generation and automated manufacturing. By reducing food demand and automating steel and titanium production, Robotics allows you to focus on the massive resource requirements of the Space era and the transition toward Antimatter.
