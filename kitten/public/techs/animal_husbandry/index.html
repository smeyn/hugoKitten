Animal Husbandry is an early-game scientific technology that is essential for transitioning from basic survival to infrastructure development and more advanced resource management. It is unlocked after researching Archery and costs 500 Science.

Core Buildings and Effects

Researching Animal Husbandry unlocks two primary structures focused on managing the colony’s food economy and rare resources:

  • Pastures: These buildings cost 100 Catnip and 10 Wood. Each Pasture provides a -0.5% reduction in catnip demand per kitten.
  • Unicorn Pastures: These structures cost 2 Unicorns and provide a smaller -0.15% reduction in catnip demand. Additionally, they automatically generate 0.001 unicorns per tick.

The demand reduction from both building types is vital for surviving Winter, as it decreases the constant food burden imposed by the kitten population. However, this reduction is subject to strict diminishing returns and is hard-capped at 50%.

Scientific and Industrial Gateway

Animal Husbandry serves as a critical technological pivot point, acting as the mandatory prerequisite for three major scientific branches:

  • Construction: This allows for the building of Log Houses, Warehouses, Lumber Mills, and Ziggurats, as well as the enabling of crafting.
  • Mathematics: This unlocks Academies and the Celestial Mechanics upgrade to boost science gains from meteors.
  • Civil Service: This enables the Census system and the ability to assign a Leader to the colony.

Because it leads directly to Construction, Animal Husbandry is an indirect prerequisite for Engineering, which unlocks the game’s powerful policy system.

Late-Game Transitions

As the colony advances into the industrial and space eras, the role of Animal Husbandry shifts:

  • Energy Pivot: Once Ecology is researched, players can replace Pastures with Solar Farms to generate energy. This is considered a safe transition in the late game because the catnip demand reduction from Pastures is marginal by that point.
  • Unicorn Tree: Animal Husbandry remains relevant to religious progression through the Unicorn Tree. Workshop upgrades like Unicorn Selection (unlocked via Genetics) further improve the effectiveness of Unicorn Pastures by 25%.
