Machinery is a crucial mid-game technology research in Kittens Game, enabling automated production and unlocking the specialized Steamworks building.

Unlocking and Cost

  • Prerequisite Technology: Machinery is unlocked by researching Writing.
  • Cost: Researching Machinery requires 15,000 Science.
  • Strategic Context: Machinery is one of three major sciences unlocked by Writing, alongside Philosophy and Steel. Compared to Philosophy (which leads directly to Theology and further advancement), Machinery is initially viewed as a “specialist Tech that won’t help you much right now”.

Key Unlocks and Effects

Researching Machinery is essential for advancing industrial automation and resource production:

  1. Buildings: Machinery unlocks the Steamworks building. Steamworks require Steel (65 units), Gear (20 units), and Blueprint (1 unit) to construct.

  2. Upgrades: Machinery unlocks several key Workshop upgrades:

    • Printing Press: Allows Steamworks to automatically produce Manuscripts. This provides an alternative pathway for resource acquisition, as manually getting Manuscripts requires hunting Furs for Parchment and accumulating Culture.
    • Workshop Automation: Causes Steamworks to convert specific basic resources (Wood to Beams, Minerals to Slabs) when those resources are near their storage cap.
    • Crossbow: A permanent upgrade that boosts Catpower production from Hunters.

Strategic Significance (Steamworks)

The primary strategic importance of Machinery lies in the capabilities of the Steamworks:

  • Manuscript Automation: Steamworks, when paired with the Printing Press upgrade, consume Coal to automatically print manuscripts. This is helpful for overcoming the “fair bump” or hurdle presented by the high Manuscript requirements for advancing science (like Theology).
  • Energy and Boost: Steamworks provide +1 Energy and a significant +15% Magneto Boost.
  • Cost Management: Steamworks present a secondary hurdle because they require Blueprints to create, which often requires trading until their production cost can be managed.
