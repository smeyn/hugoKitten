Mechanization is a key technology research in the mid-to-late game of Kittens Game, leading directly to the advanced “Concrete era” of infrastructure development.

Here is a detailed breakdown of its prerequisites, costs, and effects:

Prerequisites and Cost

  • Prerequisite Technology: Mechanization is unlocked after researching Industrialization. It is the final step in a major scientific progression path: Physics $\to$ Electricity $\to$ Industrialization $\to$ Mechanization.
  • Cost: Researching Mechanization is expensive, requiring 115,000 Science and 45 Blueprints.

Key Unlocks and Effects

Researching Mechanization provides crucial industrial capabilities and new construction materials:

  1. Resource Unlocked (Concrete): Mechanization enables the production of concrete. Concrete is a highly important crafted material necessary for advancing housing and storage infrastructure.
  2. Building Unlocked (Factory): This technology unlocks the Factory building. Factories are essential for late-game industrial scaling and boosting crafting efficiency.
  3. Kitten Job Unlocked (Engineer): Mechanization enables the Engineer job, allowing kittens to automate crafting processes in the Workshop.
  4. Workshop Upgrades: Mechanization unlocks critical efficiency upgrades in the Workshop:
    • Concrete Pillars: A storage upgrade that increases the maximum capacity of several resources (wood, minerals, iron, coal, gold, and titanium) from Barns, Warehouses, Harbours, and Mints by $+5%$.
    • Pumpjack: This upgrade improves the effectiveness of Oil Wells by $45%$ and enables their automation, although Oil Wells will then consume 1 Watt of energy each.

Strategic Significance

Mechanization is a major checkpoint on the path to high-tier buildings and population management:

  • Concrete Era: This research is necessary for achieving Concrete Huts. Concrete Huts require a tremendous amount of titanium and grant a major reduction in the price ratio of Huts, making population expansion cheaper.
  • Technological Progression: Mechanization directly unlocks the next tier of science, Electronics.
  • Endgame Focus: While the costs for Mechanization are high, players are advised to pre-emptively grind for the huge amount of titanium required for the subsequent Concrete upgrades, as this process will often provide most of the blueprints needed to research Mechanization itself through trade bonuses.
