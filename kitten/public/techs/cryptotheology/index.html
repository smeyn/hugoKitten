The query is about Cryptotheology, which is both a specific high-tier technology and the name of the system it unlocks in Kittens Game, defining the Phase 3 or Late Game progression.

Here is a comprehensive overview of Cryptotheology, its purpose, and its related elements based on the sources:

1. Cryptotheology as a Technology

  • Unlock Requirement: The technology Cryptotheology is unlocked after researching Theology and costs 650K Science and 5 Relics .
  • Effect: Researching this technology grants access to the Cryptotheology system and unlocks the Relic Station upgrade.
  • Persistence: The technology itself does not carry over when you reset the game, even though the buildings purchased within the Cryptotheology system do persist across resets.

2. The Cryptotheology System (Phase 3)

Cryptotheology is described as the system that provides “Insights into the mysteries of time and space” and focuses on the Time, Relics, and Void mechanics of the game. It is the defining feature of the Phase 3 (End Game) of Kittens Game .

The core purpose of the system is to purchase permanent, powerful structures using Relics and Void, which often scale with the player’s Transcendence Tiers (TT).

3. Buildings and Upgrades

Cryptotheology buildings purchased persist across resets and are unlocked sequentially based on your Transcendence Tier (TT), which is bought with Epiphany.

Building/Upgrade Primary Cost Transcendence Prerequisite Primary Effect
Black Obelisk 100 Relics TT 1 Improves your Solar Revolution bonus cap by +5% per Transcendence Tier.
Black Nexus 5,000 Relics TT 3 Improves relic production from two sources: Refining Time Crystals (1 + (Black Nexus * Black Pyramid) relics) and Relic Station upgraded Space Beacons.
Black Core 10,000 Relics TT 5 Increases the Sorrow (Black Liquid Sorrow or BLS) limit by +1% .
Event Horizon 25,000 Relics TT 7 Increases global resource storage ratio by +10%. This stacks additively with Void Rifts and multiplicatively with other storage bonuses [6, 8].
Black Library 30,000 Relics TT 9 Improves Codex Leviathanianus scaling by +2% per library, further boosted by Unicorn Graveyards .
Black Radiance 37,500 Relics TT 12 Increases the alicorn corruption rate based on the amount of Sorrow (BLS) and the number of Black Radiances.
Blazar 50,000 Relics TT 15 Improves Chronomancy and Unicornmancy by 2.5%, Time Impedance by 10%, and Resource Retrieval effectiveness by 2% [9]. Blazars are vital to a Time Crystal engine .
Dark Nova 75,000 Relics, 7,500 Void TT 20 Provides a +2% global energy production bonus.
Mausoleum 50,000 Relics, 12,500 Void, 10 Necrocorn TT 23 Unlocks the ability to purchase Pacts.
Holy Genocide 100,000 Relics, 25,000 Void TT 25 Unlocks the Post Apocalypse challenge upon reset .

4. Strategic Role and Goals

  • Relic Conversion: Cryptotheology provides direct use for Relics, which are primarily created by refining Time Crystals (25 TCs = 1 Relic).
  • Late-Game Gate: Once a player can build Eludium Huts easily, they are ready for the unicorn/time crystal phase. The next major goal is to maximize the Solar Revolution bonus (capped at 1000% before Cryptotheology upgrades) and reach Transcendence Tier15 . Reaching TT 15 is necessary to purchase the Blazar, which is considered vital to a Time Crystal engine [10, 15].
  • Boosting Persistence: Cryptotheology buildings are key to late-game scaling. For example, Black Obelisks boost the cap of the Solar Revolution bonus.
  • Challenges: The ultimate building, the Holy Genocide, is required to unlock the Post Apocalypse challenge.
