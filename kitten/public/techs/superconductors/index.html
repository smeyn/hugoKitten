Superconductors is an advanced late-mid-game technology that acts as a vital transition point between industrial expansion and deep-space exploration. It focuses on enhancing energy efficiency and bridging the gap to high-tier temporal and antimatter mechanics.

Costs and Prerequisites

  • Science Cost: 225,000.
  • Resource Cost: 175 Blueprints.
  • Prerequisite: Researching Nanotechnology is required before this science becomes available.

Unlocked Technology

  • Antimatter: Superconductors is the mandatory prerequisite for the Antimatter technology, which begins Phase 4 of the game (Chrono-Optimization and Geometric Growth).

Unlocked Workshop Upgrades

Researching this technology provides access to three highly impactful upgrades that drastically improve your energy balance and production:

  • Space Manufacturing:
    • Effect: This allows your village’s Factories to provide a production bonus to space-based structures based on 75% of your factory craft ratio.
    • Impact: It specifically increases the Space Bonus from Space Elevators and Orbital Arrays by 3.75% per factory (this increases to 4.5% if you also have the Factory Logistics upgrade). This is essential for scaling Unobtainium production to the levels required for the deep endgame.
  • Cold Fusion:
    • Effect: Increases the energy output of Reactors by 25%.
    • Impact: This provides a flat boost of +2.5 Watts per active Reactor, significantly easing energy bottlenecks.
  • Cryocomputing:
    • Effect: Reduces the energy consumption of Data Centers by 50%.
    • Impact: Each Data Center’s power drain is reduced from 2 Watts to 1 Watt, allowing you to maintain massive science storage without overwhelming your power grid.

Strategic Importance

Superconductors is often considered the threshold for the “Positive Reset” loop. By unlocking Space Manufacturing, players can finally farm enough Unobtainium to build the high number of Chronospheres needed to carry over more resources than they spent during a run. Additionally, the combined effects of Cold Fusion and Cryocomputing allow for a much larger industrial and scientific infrastructure to be powered simultaneously, which is critical for supporting energy-intensive late-game buildings like AI Cores and Entanglement Stations.
