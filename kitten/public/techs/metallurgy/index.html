Metallurgy is a mid-game technology research that is unlocked by Industrialization and provides valuable workshop upgrades focused on boosting the efficiency and output of metal production and refinement processes.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the Metallurgy technology:

Unlocking and Cost

  • Prerequisite: Metallurgy is unlocked after researching Industrialization.
  • Cost: Researching Metallurgy requires 125,000 Science and 60 Blueprints.

Key Workshop Upgrades

Metallurgy is primarily important for the upgrades it provides to metal processing buildings like Smelters and Calciners. These upgrades help manage the mid-game requirement for processed metals and alloys:

  1. Electrolytic Smelting: This upgrade effectively doubles the production rate of Smelters, making them produce +95% more iron and coal.
  2. Oxidation: This significantly boosts the output of Calciners. Calciners produce +95% more iron and a substantial +285% more titanium.
  3. Mining Drill: This upgrade improves the effectiveness of Geologists. Geologists, if the prerequisite Geodesy upgrade has been researched, gain an additional +0.01 coal/tick and +0.0005 gold/tick. This upgrade costs 100,000 Science, 1750 Titanium, and 750 Steel.

Strategic Context

  • Industrial Path: Metallurgy is part of the industrial expansion pathway, which follows Electricity and Industrialization. This pathway eventually leads to the Mechanization and Electronics branches.
  • Titanium Production: The Oxidation upgrade is crucial in the mid-game for significantly accelerating titanium production from Calciners, helping the player acquire the necessary volume of titanium for advanced structures.
  • Kitten Trait: In addition to the research, kittens can have the Metallurgist trait. When assigned as a leader, this trait increases metal crafting efficiency by adding a +0.1 craft ratio for plate, steel, gear, and alloy.
