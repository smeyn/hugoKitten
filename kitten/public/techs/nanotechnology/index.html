Nanotechnology is a high-level science in the late-mid game that serves as a vital gateway to endgame industrial efficiency and space exploration. It is unlocked after researching Nuclear Fission and requires 200,000 Science and 150 Blueprints to research.

Unlocked Workshop Upgrades

Researching Nanotechnology provides access to several powerful upgrades that drastically scale your production and efficiency:

  • Augmentations: This is one of the most significant upgrades for population efficiency, as it increases the production bonus from kitten skills by 100%. This stacks with other multipliers to make your specialized workforce far more productive.
  • Nanosuits: This upgrade provides a flat +0.5 hunting bonus, increasing the yield of rare resources like Furs and Ivory from hunts. Notably, the Manager kitten trait provides a bonus equivalent to this upgrade.
  • Photovoltaic Cells: This significantly boosts your renewable energy infrastructure by making Solar Farms 50% more effective at producing energy.
  • Fluidized Reactors: This dramatically improves the output of your Calciners, causing them to produce +100% more Iron and +300% more Titanium.

Role in Scientific Progression

Nanotechnology is a mandatory prerequisite for the following advanced technologies:

  • Superconductors: The next step in the physics tree, which eventually leads to Antimatter.
  • Particle Physics: This unlocks Accelerators, which are essential for generating Uranium and expanding resource storage.

Space and Industrial Utility

In addition to workshop upgrades, Nanotechnology is a required component for the Space Elevator (when combined with Orbital Engineering). The Space Elevator is crucial for the space race as it reduces the oil requirements for space missions and structures by up to 75% through diminishing returns.
