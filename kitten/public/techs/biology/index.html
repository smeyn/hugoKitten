Biology is a mid-to-late-game technology research that unlocks the Bio Lab building, primarily serving to significantly boost scientific capacity and facilitate the advanced production of oil.

Technology Details

  • Prerequisite: Biology is unlocked after researching Geology.
  • Cost: Researching Biology requires 85,000 Science and 100 Compendium.
  • Unlocks: Biology immediately unlocks the Bio Lab building and the subsequent research, Biochemistry.

The Bio Lab Building

The building unlocked by this technology provides major science and resource bonuses, but requires moderately advanced materials to construct:

Category Detail
Cost 100 Slab, 25 Alloy, and 1500 Science.
Science Effect Provides a +35% Science bonus and increases Max Science by +1500 per Bio Lab.
Refinement Increases Catnip refinement by +10%.

Strategic Significance

Biology and the Bio Lab are important for players pushing toward the endgame, particularly for managing science bottlenecks and expanding energy production:

  1. Science Capacity: Bio Labs are used to reach very high science capacity limits (e.g., 125K maximum Science) for late-game research, reducing the need to rely on continuously constructing expensive Observatories or Temples.
  2. Industrial Transition: Biology is vital for entering the advanced energy era. It leads to Biochemistry, which unlocks the Biofuel Processing upgrade. This upgrade allows Bio Labs to convert catnip into oil at a rate of 0.10 oil per second, consuming 5 catnip per second and 1 Watt of energy. This automation is useful for overcoming oil production bottlenecks.
  3. Cross-Building Synergy: Bio Labs are connected to Data Centers (upgraded Libraries) via the Uplink and Starlink workshop upgrades, which provide reciprocal increases to maximum science, culture, and compendium caps between the two building types.
  4. Early Game Recommendation: Due to the relatively high science, Compendium, Slab, and Alloy costs of this research and its resulting building, players are generally advised to skip Biology on their first couple of runs. It becomes worthwhile once players can easily craft Alloy.

For complex industrial advancement, researching Biology helps convert your massive catnip surplus into critical industrial fuel (oil), which is essential for powering advanced space and factory structures.
