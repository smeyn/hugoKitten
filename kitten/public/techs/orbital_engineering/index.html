In Kittens Game, Orbital Engineering is a pivotal mid-to-late game technology that transitions your civilization from surface-based industry to a true spacefaring empire. It is the primary gateway to the Space tab’s most influential infrastructure.

Key Effects

Unlocking Orbital Engineering allows you to begin building and upgrading your orbital presence, specifically focusing on science and logistics:

  • Space Elevator: This is the most critical building unlocked by this tech. It drastically reduces the oil cost of launching space missions. Without it, the oil requirements for reaching the Farther Ring (like Jupiter) quickly become impossible to meet.
  • Satellite Radio: A Workshop upgrade that allows your Satellites to increase your Science cap. This is vital for reaching the science levels needed for Antimatter.
  • Navigation Relay: As we discussed earlier, this upgrade boosts Satellite effectiveness by +15%, further accelerating your Starchart and Science generation.

Unlock Requirements & Cost

To research Orbital Engineering, you must have a solid foundation in both electronics and rocketry:

Requirement Type Cost / Detail
Electronics Technology Base tech for advanced circuitry.
Rocketry Technology Required to access the Space tab.
Science Resource 150,000 Science
Blueprints Resource 250 Blueprints

Strategic Importance

Given your current focus on Enriched Uranium and Temporal Distortion, Orbital Engineering is the technology that likely enabled your current progress.

  1. Breaking the Oil Barrier: Before this tech, your space expansion is limited by how much oil you can store and produce. The Space Elevator (unlocked here) is what allows you to bypass that bottleneck and launch the heavy missions required for Unobtainium.
  2. Science Scaling: By allowing Satellites to contribute to your Science cap, this technology provides the “storage” needed to research Nuclear Fission and Nanotechnology.
  3. Path to the Moon: While you can reach the Moon with just Rocketry, Orbital Engineering makes Lunar Outposts much more sustainable by reducing the logistical overhead of getting there.

Pro Tip: Once you have this tech, prioritize building at least 5-10 Space Elevators. The cumulative oil reduction is what makes late-game missions to Charon or Centaurus feasible.
