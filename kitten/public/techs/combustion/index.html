Combustion is a key technology research in the late mid-game of Kittens Game, primarily focused on boosting the efficiency of your industrial facilities, particularly manuscript printing and oil production.

Research Details and Cost

Category Detail
Prerequisite Combustion is unlocked after researching Industrialization.
Cost Researching Combustion requires 115,000 Science and 45 Blueprint.
Next Technology Combustion directly unlocks the Ecology science.
Progression Path This technology is part of the progression toward the Concrete era: Physics $\to$ Electricity $\to$ Industrialization $\to$ Combustion or Mechanization.

Key Workshop Upgrades

Combustion unlocks three important Workshop upgrades designed to enhance energy and production processes:

  1. Oil Refinery
    • This upgrade improves the effectiveness of Oil Wells by 35% (additive with other effects). This is vital for addressing the oil bottleneck as the game progresses.
  2. Offset Press
    • This upgrade significantly boosts the automated production of Manuscripts by Steamworks. It increases the Printing Press production rate by a factor of 4, raising the output to 0.01 manuscripts per second.
  3. Fuel Injector
    • This upgrade helps mitigate the energy cost of industrialisation by reducing the coal consumption of Steamworks by 20%.

Strategic Context

Combustion is one of the important industrial technologies to target in the mid-game, alongside Metallurgy and Electronics. The upgrades unlocked by Combustion are crucial for maintaining the resource flow necessary for space and high-tier infrastructure development, particularly by improving oil supply for structures like Oil Wells and Kerosene production.
