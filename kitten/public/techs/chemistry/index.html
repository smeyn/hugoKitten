Chemistry is a pivotal mid-game science technology that initiates the “Alloy Era”, providing the necessary foundation for advanced industrialisation and early space exploration.

Costs and Prerequisites

To research Chemistry, you must first unlock Physics. The technology itself requires the following resources:

  • Science: 60,000
  • Compendium: 50

Unlocked Buildings and Resources

Chemistry unlocks the ability to produce Alloy, a high-end craftable material made from Steel and Titanium. It also enables the construction of two critical industrial buildings:

  • Oil Well: Your primary source of terrestrial oil production, providing +0.02 oil per tick.
  • Calciner: A complex industrial structure that consumes minerals and oil to produce iron and small amounts of Titanium. While slow, Calciners provide a steady domestic alternative to trading with Zebras for Titanium.

Workshop Upgrades

Researching Chemistry makes several high-impact Alloy-tier upgrades available in the Workshop:

  • Alloy Barns: Increases the storage capacity of Barns, Warehouses, and Harbours for wood, minerals, and iron by 100%.
  • Alloy Warehouses: Boosts the storage of wood, minerals, coal, iron, gold, and titanium in your facilities by 45%.
  • Alloy Axe: Makes Woodcutters 50% more effective.
  • Alloy Armour: Provides a +0.5 hunting bonus, making each hunt yield roughly 40–50% more furs and ivory.

Strategic Importance

  • Breaking Resource Caps: The Alloy-tier storage upgrades are essential for reaching the high science and resource limits required for the Concrete Era and Mechanization.
  • Transition to Space: Chemistry is a mandatory step in the technology tree leading toward Electricity, Biology, and eventually the Rocketry needed for space missions.
  • Titanium Management: Strategic guides recommend building several Calciners immediately after researching Chemistry to begin accumulating the Titanium needed for advanced Workshop upgrades before domestic production becomes more efficient through later Metallurgy upgrades.
  • Village Progression: Players typically aim to acquire all Alloy buildings when they have approximately 90–100 kittens, using the expanded storage to push toward a population of 130+ for an efficient first reset.
