Rocketry is a high-level late-game technology that acts as the primary gateway to the Space era. It transitions your civilization from ground-based industrialisation to interstellar exploration and resource acquisition.

Costs and Prerequisites

  • Science Cost: 175,000.
  • Resource Cost: 125 Blueprints.
  • Prerequisite: You must first research Electronics.

Immediate Unlocks

Researching Rocketry provides several critical features and further technological paths:

  • Space Tab: This new tab becomes available, allowing you to build rockets and launch missions.
  • Technologies: It unlocks the Satellites and Oil Processing sciences.
  • Workshop Upgrade: You gain access to Oil Distillation, which costs 175k Science and 5,000 Titanium, improving Oil Well output by 75%.
  • Missions: It allows you to undertake the first space mission, Orbital Launch (Cost: 250 Starcharts, 5,000 Catpower, 100k Science, and 15k Oil).

Strategic Importance

Rocketry is the mandatory starting point for the “Space Race.” Its primary strategic functions include:

  • The Path to Unobtainium: Completing the missions unlocked by Rocketry leads to the Moon Mission, which is the only way to build Lunar Outposts and begin generating Unobtainium.
  • Oil Economy Scaling: The Oil Distillation upgrade is vital for meeting the massive oil demands of subsequent space missions, such as the Moon Mission (45k Oil) and Moon Bases (70k Oil).
  • Starchart Automation: By unlocking the Satellites technology, it allows you to begin automating the production of Starcharts, which are required in large quantities for all further space exploration.

Special Challenge Effects

In the Black Sky challenge, where astronomical events do not occur, the initial costs for missions and buildings unlocked by Rocketry are significantly altered. For example, the Orbital Launch mission removes the starchart requirement entirely but increases the Science, Catpower, and Oil costs by 11x.
