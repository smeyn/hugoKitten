Construction is a critical early-to- mid-game technology research that fundamentally transforms your village by introducing new resource management tools and advanced housing options. It is essential for continuing scientific progression and scaling your infrastructure.

Technology Details

Category Detail
Cost 1300 Science.
Prerequisite Unlocked by Animal Husbandry.
Unlocks Engineering (next research).

Key Buildings Unlocked

Researching Construction unlocks four key buildings that help expand your village capabilities:

  1. Log House: This is a tier-up housing structure that costs 200 Wood and 250 Minerals. It provides +1 maximum kitten and +50 maximum catpower. Log Houses are often prioritised early to continue maximizing population.
  2. Lumber Mill: This industrial structure costs 100 Wood, 50 Iron, and 250 Minerals. It increases Wood production by +10%.
  3. Warehouse: This is an important storage building costing 1.5 Beam and 2 Slab. Warehouses initially do not store Catnip but increase the maximum limits of Wood, Minerals, Iron, Coal, Gold, and Titanium. They eventually upgrade to Spaceports.
  4. Ziggurat: This is a mega-structure building that serves as a prerequisite for the Unicorn/Time Crystal Era. It costs 50 Megalith, 50 Scaffold, and 1 Blueprint and increases Max Culture by +8%.

Effects and Strategic Importance

The research also provides crucial operational enhancements:

  • Enables Crafting: Construction formally enables crafting operations in the Workshop. This allows basic resource inputs to be converted into complex materials.
  • Workshop Upgrades: It unlocks several useful upgrades:
    • Composite Bow: Provides a permanent +50% boost to Catpower production from Hunters.
    • Catnip Enrichment: Improves Catnip refinement efficiency by reducing the cost of wood crafting from 100 Catnip to 50 Catnip.
    • Reinforced Saw: Improves Lumber Mill efficiency by an additional +20%.

Strategically, players are advised to push for Construction science early to establish Log Houses for population growth and Lumber Mills to improve wood output, as wood accumulates slowly before this point.

As a general action, commencing construction on a civilization begins when you start gathering catnip to expand the game. Buildings are often kept hidden until you have at least 30% of the necessary resources to construct the first one.
