Navigation is a pivotal technology in Kittens Game, marking the transition into the trade and titanium accumulation phase by unlocking essential infrastructure, advanced crafting, and further scientific branches.

Technological Details

  • Prerequisite and Cost: Navigation is unlocked by researching Astronomy. Researching it requires 35,000 Science and 100 Manuscript.
  • Scientific Path: Successfully researching Navigation unlocks the next three critical technology branches: Architecture, Physics, and Geology.
  • Strategic Timing: Players are advised to research Navigation Science once they have accumulated 25 Starcharts. The policy Clear Cutting is often recommended beforehand to provide the necessary Wood for the buildings and ships unlocked by Navigation.

Key Unlocks

Navigation introduces the ability to build advanced transportation and storage structures:

1. Trade and Crafting

Navigation enables the crafting of Trade Ships.

  • Trade Ships are essential for discovering new civilizations through the “Explore” function.
  • Trade Ships are critical for reaching the Titanium era, as they are the major upgrade for obtaining more Titanium through trading with Zebras.
  • Crafting a Trade Ship initially requires 100 Scaffold, 150 Plate, and 25 Starchart.

2. Building Unlocks

Navigation unlocks the Harbour building.

  • Harbours provide substantial increases to storage capacity for several key common resources:
    • Catnip: +2500.
    • Wood, Minerals, Iron, Coal, Gold, and Titanium storage limits are all increased.
    • The Expanded Cargo upgrade (unlocked by Navigation) further increases Harbour capacity by 1% per trade ship, up to a limit of 225%.

3. Workshop Upgrades

The technology also unlocks several important Workshop upgrades focused on resource efficiency and trade relations:

  • Caravanserai: This upgrade improves race relations by giving each Tradepost a flat 0.35% chance decrease to the hostile event probability and a 0.175% increase to the friendly event probability.
  • Titanium Axe: Improves Woodcutters’ output by 50%.
  • Astrolabe: Improves Observatory effectiveness.
  • Titanium Reflectors: Increases the maximum science storage bonus derived from Libraries/Data Centers based on the number of Observatories you have.

Navigation acts as a necessary gateway, like plotting a course to a new continent, enabling the building of the ships (Trade Ships) and port infrastructure (Harbours) needed to access lucrative resources (Titanium and Iron) from distant, often hostile, civilizations.
