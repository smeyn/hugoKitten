In Kittens Game, Antimatter is a late-game science technology that introduces powerful upgrades and leads into the endgame Terraformation phase.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Prerequisite Technology: You must first research Superconductors.
  • Resource Requirements:
    • Science: 500,000.
    • Relic: 1.

Unlocked Content

Researching Antimatter unlocks the subsequent Terraformation science, as well as several high-impact Workshop upgrades:

  • Antimatter Bases: Reduces the energy consumption of your Moon Bases by 50% (from 10 Watts down to 5 Watts).
  • Antimatter Reactors: Makes your Research Vessels and Space Beacons 95% more effective, significantly boosting your maximum science cap.
  • Antimatter Fission: Increases your Engineer kittens’ effectiveness at crafting eludium by 25%.
  • Antimatter Drive: A powerful rocket engine upgrade that drastically speeds up space travel, dividing travel time by 25.

The Antimatter Resource

Alongside the technology, the Antimatter resource itself becomes a critical bottleneck in the late game.

  • Production: It is generated by Sunlifters once every game year, provided you have a positive energy balance.
  • Storage: Its storage capacity is extremely limited and must be expanded using Containment Chambers and Heatsinks.
  • Strategic Use: Amassing 5,000 Antimatter is a major endgame milestone required to build the Relic Station upgrade, which allows your Space Beacons to passively generate Relics.
