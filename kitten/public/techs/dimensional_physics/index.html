Dimensional Physics is a high-level late-game technology in Kittens Game that focuses on expanding resource storage and scientific capacity through the enhancement of Accelerators. It is part of the progression path toward time manipulation and antimatter technologies.

Costs and Prerequisites

  • Science Cost: 235,000.
  • Prerequisite: You must first research Particle Physics.
  • Technology Path: It is unlocked following Nuclear Fission and Particle Physics.

Unlocked Workshop Upgrades

Researching Dimensional Physics is primarily done to access two powerful upgrades for the Accelerator building:

  • Energy Rifts:

    • Cost: 200k Science, 7,500 Titanium, and 250 Uranium.
    • Effect: Transforms Accelerators into storage facilities by creating “rifts to pocket dimensions”.
    • Capacity Bonuses: Each Accelerator provides substantial storage for several resources: +30,000 Catnip, +20,000 Wood, +25,000 Minerals, +7,500 Iron, +2,500 Coal, +250 Gold, and +750 Titanium.
    • Strategic Value: This is a major late-game tool for bypassing the storage bottlenecks of standard buildings like Barns and Warehouses.

  • LHC (Large Hadron Collider):

    • Cost: 250k Science, 100 Unobtainium, and 150 Alloy.
    • Effect: Adds a flat bonus to your maximum science capacity for every Accelerator owned.
    • Bonus: +2,500 Max Science per Accelerator.
    • Strategic Value: This helps reach the massive science thresholds required for deep-space and temporal technologies, such as Chronophysics and Antimatter.

Strategic Utility

Dimensional Physics is often described as a turning point for resource management. By investing in Energy Rifts, players can significantly increase their Titanium and Gold caps, which are essential for mass trading with civilisations like the Zebras or Nagas. Furthermore, the LHC provides a scalable way to increase science storage without relying solely on expensive Observatories or Bio Labs.
