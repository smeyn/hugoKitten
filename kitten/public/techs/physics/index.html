Physics is a crucial mid-game technology research that opens up highly advanced industrial and temporal mechanics, setting the stage for the transition into the titanium and space eras.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the Physics technology:

Unlocking and Cost

  • Prerequisite: Physics is unlocked after researching Navigation.
  • Cost: Researching Physics requires 50,000 Science and 35 Compendiums.

Strategic Importance and Subsequent Research

Physics is a key stepping stone in the scientific progression, particularly because it enables the construction of high-tier industrial buildings and unlocks critical next-tier science branches.

  • Technology Unlocks: Physics immediately unlocks three advanced sciences:
    1. Chemistry
    2. Electricity
    3. Metaphysics
  • Industrial Path: Physics is the first step in the crucial path toward the Concrete era. This path continues through Electricity $\to$ Industrialization $\to$ Mechanization, which ultimately unlocks the Factory building and the ability to craft Concrete.
  • Space Transition: Physics is a prerequisite on the path to Rocketry, which is necessary to access the Space tab.

Upgrades and Resources Unlocked

  • Craftable Resource: Researching Physics enables the ability to craft Blueprints. Blueprints are essential for constructing advanced industrial and utility buildings like Steamworks and Reactors.
  • Workshop Upgrades: Physics unlocks several valuable Workshop upgrades:
    • Steel Saw: Improves Lumber Mill efficiency by an additional +20%.
    • Pyrolysis: Boosts Coal output by +20% (multiplies with other effects, though Smelters are unaffected).
    • Pneumatic Press: Expands Steamworks automation to include converting iron to plates when resources cap out.

Context in the Progression Loop

The decision to research Physics is part of the move past the Theology and Astronomy hurdle. Once unlocked, Physics leads into a phase where the player focuses on generating the titanium needed for construction and acquiring Blueprints (which are convertible from Compendiums after Physics is researched) for technology advancements. Players focused on paragon farming often prioritize this research path to accelerate technological development.
