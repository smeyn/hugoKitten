Terraformation is a high-level endgame science technology in Kittens Game that serves as a vital gateway to scaling your population and energy production through deep-space engineering.

Costs and Prerequisites

  • Science Cost: 750,000.
  • Resource Cost: 5 Relics.
  • Prerequisite: You must first research Antimatter.

Unlocked Technologies and Structures

Researching Terraformation is required to unlock several critical endgame components:

  • Hydroponics (Technology): Unlocks the Hydroponics structure, which is mandatory for massive late-game food scaling.
  • Terraforming Station (Building): Located on the planet Yarn in the Space tab.
  • Tectonic (Building): Located in the Centaurus System in the Space tab.

The Terraforming Station (Yarn)

This structure represents the process of melting Yarn’s ice and oxygenating its atmosphere to support life.

  • Cost: 25 Antimatter, 5,000 Uranium, and 5,000 Kerosene.
  • Effect: Each station increases your maximum kitten population by +1.
  • Synergy: Every unit of Hydroponics you own provides a +1.0% bonus to the kitten capacity of your Terraforming Stations, allowing them to eventually house more than one kitten each.

Tectonic (Centaurus System)

This structure involves ripping open the planet near the star Centaurus to harvest magma energy.

  • Cost: 600,000 Science, 500 Antimatter, and 75,000 Thorium.
  • Effect: Each Tectonic provides +25 Energy.
  • Upgrades: Every level of Molten Core (requires Exophysics) improves Tectonic energy production by an additional 5%.
  • Challenge Goal: Building at least one Tectonic is a requirement for completing the Energy Challenge.

Strategic Utility

Terraformation is a cornerstone of the Endgame Paragon phase. By “Terraforming Yarn,” players can build a vast amount of housing (potentially support up to 60,000 kittens), which is essential for generating massive amounts of Paragon per reset. Additionally, Tectonic structures provide the high-density energy required to power late-game facilities like AI Cores and Entanglement Stations without relying solely on ground-based reactors.
