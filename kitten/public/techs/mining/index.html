Mining is a crucial early-game technology research in Kittens Game, foundational to developing your industrial economy by unlocking the Miner profession and the Mine building, which produce Minerals.

Unlocking and Costs

  • Prerequisite: Mining is unlocked after researching Agriculture (100 Science).
  • Cost: Researching Mining requires 500 Science.

Effects and Functionality

Mining enables kittens to produce minerals and unlocks critical early buildings and effects:

  • Job Unlocked: It unlocks the Miner job, allowing kittens to accumulate Minerals at a base rate of +0.05 Minerals per Tick.
  • Building Unlocked: It unlocks the Mine building.
    • Mines cost 100 Wood to build and have a price ratio of 1.15.
    • Each Mine provides a +20% Minerals bonus.
  • Resource Source: Mining enables Meteors, which are events that grant a small, automatic boost to your Minerals.
  • Workshop Upgrade: It unlocks the Bolas Workshop upgrade, which improves hunting effectiveness.

Strategic Significance (Early Game)

Mining is essential for transitioning from a simple catnip and wood economy to one capable of producing processed materials:

  • Mineral Access: Minerals become increasingly important for advanced buildings. Mining allows you to secure a consistent supply of Minerals early on.
  • Progression Path: Mining is typically researched after Agriculture, along with Archery. While Hunting (unlocked by Archery) starts weak, Mining immediately yields a useful amount of Minerals, even with a single Miner.
  • Next Technology: Mining unlocks Metal Working, which is necessary to build Smelters for converting Minerals into Iron.
  • Policy Conflict: Mining also unlocks the Strip Mining environmental policy (costing 2000 Science), which increases mineral production by 30% but adds 5% pollution and incurs a -2% happiness penalty.

Interactions in Iron Will Mode

In the self-imposed restriction mode, Iron Will (where housing cannot be built), Mining is essential for gaining minerals:

  • Mineral Source: In Iron Will mode, meteors that appear after researching Mining are one of the only initial ways to gain Minerals. Building more Mines increases the Mineral bonus received from these meteors.
  • Policy Effect: The Strip Mining policy is highly recommended in Iron Will mode because it grants a +10% bonus minerals from meteors.
  • Coal Production: The Deep Mining upgrade (unlocked by Steel) allows Mines to produce 0.003 Coal per Tick.
