In Kittens Game, Quantum Cryptography is a high-level technology that marks your entry into the final stages of the scientific and digital era. It represents an evolution in data security and processing that is essential for managing your late-game infrastructure.

Key Effects

Quantum Cryptography is primarily a “gateway” technology that unlocks several advanced upgrades and buildings:

  • Entanglement Station: This is the most critical building unlocked by this tech. It is your primary defense against the AI Apocalypse. Entanglement Stations consume Gigaflops generated by your AI Cores, allowing you to keep your AI level stable while still benefiting from the massive science boosts they provide.
  • Quantum Computing: A Workshop upgrade that significantly increases your Science cap and the efficiency of your Data Centers. This is vital for reaching the multi-million science requirements of Relic and Void tech.
  • AI Core Efficiency: It improves the synergy between your computers and your kittens, allowing for more precise resource management in the late game.

Unlock Requirements & Cost

To research Quantum Cryptography, you must have a mastery of both advanced physics and computing:

Requirement Type Cost / Detail
Artificial Intelligence Technology The base requirement for all digital upgrades.
Particle Physics Technology Required for the “Quantum” aspect of the tech.
Science Resource 200,000 Science
Blueprints Resource 500 Blueprints

Strategic Importance

Given your current focus on Enriched Uranium and Temporal Distortion, Quantum Cryptography is the technology that will allow you to scale your AI Cores safely.

  1. AI Apocalypse Prevention: As we discussed regarding Machine Learning, AI Cores are powerful but dangerous. Quantum Cryptography provides the Entanglement Stations needed to “burn” your AI’s Gigaflops, keeping the AI level below 15 and preventing the destruction of your resources.
  2. The Science Ceiling: By unlocking Quantum Computing, you can push your Science cap high enough to research Chronotheology and Void Space, which are the keys to managing the Distortion you’ve been seeing.
  3. Late-Game Crafting: The Blueprint cost (500) is steep, but by this point, your Nuclear Smelters and Geologists should be providing enough gold and minerals to trade with Leviathans or craft the blueprints manually.

Pro Tip: Do not build more than 1-2 AI Cores until you have researched Quantum Cryptography and built at least one Entanglement Station. The AI level rises quickly, and without a way to reduce it, you risk losing your entire Uranium stock.
