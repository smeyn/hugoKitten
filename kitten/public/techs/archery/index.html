Archery is a fundamental early-game scientific technology that serves as the gateway to the hunt system, rare resource acquisition, and the specialised Iron Will progression path. It costs 300 Science and is unlocked after researching Agriculture.

Core Unlocks and Mechanics

Researching Archery introduces several critical components to the colony’s development:

  • The Hunter Job: This technology enables you to assign kittens as Hunters, who produce 0.06 catpower per tick.
  • The Hunt System: Once you have accumulated 100 catpower, you can send your kittens on a hunt. This is the primary method for obtaining rare resources like Furs and Ivory, and it offers a 5% chance to find Unicorns.
  • Technological Branching: Archery is the mandatory prerequisite for Animal Husbandry, which leads to housing improvements and the “Unicorn Tree”.
  • Zebra Buildings: For advanced players, Archery unlocks Zebra-specific structures including the Zebra Outpost, Zebra Workshop, and Zebra Forge.

Workshop Enhancements

The effectiveness of the systems unlocked by Archery can be drastically improved through specific Workshop upgrades:

  • Catpower Production: You can boost the rate at which Hunters generate catpower using the Composite Bow (+50%), Crossbow (+25%), and Railgun (+25%).
  • Hunting Bonuses: To increase the yield of Furs and Ivory per hunt, you can research various tiers of gear, starting with Bolas (+1.0) and Hunting Armour (+2.0), followed by smaller increments from Steel Armour, Alloy Armour, and Nanosuits. These bonuses are additive.

Strategic Utility

In the early game, there is often a choice between researching Archery or Mining. Most strategic guides recommend prioritising Mining first, as hunting starts very weak and the colony initially lacks the spare kittens to compensate for the rapid consumption of Furs and Ivory. However, hunting is essential long-term because Furs and Ivory provide a +10% happiness boost, which improves the overall production efficiency of all kittens.

Iron Will and Zebra Accumulation

Archery is the strategic pivot point for the specialised “Iron Will” (IW) mode, where the player progresses without any kitten housing:

  • Zebra Discovery: In IW mode, Zebras serve as the primary source of catpower (acting as passive Hunters) and begin to appear only after Archery is researched.
  • The Reset Loop: A common strategy involves researching Archery and then immediately resetting the game. Doing this while in Iron Will increases your maximum Zebra limit for the next run.
  • Prestige Setup: Players often perform 6 to 10 of these short “Archery resets” to accumulate 50–60 Zebras. This provides a permanent, efficient catpower source that bypasses the need to feed kittens, significantly accelerating mid-game economic stability.
