Architecture is a key mid-game technology research that advances your infrastructure by unlocking new buildings, particularly high-tier housing and structures crucial for gold management.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the Architecture technology:

1. Requirements and Cost

  • Prerequisite: Architecture is unlocked after researching Navigation.
  • Cost: Researching Architecture requires 42,000 Science and 10 Compendium.
  • Progression: Architecture is part of the research path following Astronomy and Navigation.

2. Buildings Unlocked

Architecture unlocks two important mid-game structures: the Mansion and the Mint.

Building Cost and Purpose Details
Mansion High-tier housing. Costs 185 Slab, 75 Steel, and 25 Titanium. Provides +1 maximum kitten and +50 maximum catpower.
Mint Structure for managing gold and generating rare resources. Costs 5,000 Minerals, 200 Plate, and 500 Gold. Mints increase gold storage capacity and can generate Furs and Ivory in exchange for Catpower and Gold consumption.

3. Strategic Importance

  • Population Growth: Mansions provide additional population capacity, helping sustain kitten growth.
  • Resource Management: Mints are useful for increasing your gold storage limit, which helps unlock useful culture and catpower bonuses available in the Religion tab. Running Mints temporarily when idling is helpful to stockpile furs.
  • Progression: Architecture is required to unlock the next technology, Acoustics.

In the accelerated progression for short runs, Architecture is one of the technologies targeted early on, costing a total of 70 Compendia (when combined with Acoustics).
