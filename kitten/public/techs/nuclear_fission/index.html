Nuclear Fission is a pivotal technology research in the late mid-game, crucial for advancing your industrial infrastructure, especially regarding energy production, titanium refinement, and unlocking access to Uranium trade.

Unlocking and Cost

  • Prerequisite: Nuclear Fission is unlocked after researching Electronics.
  • Cost: Researching Nuclear Fission requires 150,000 Science and 100 Blueprint.

Key Unlocks and Effects

Nuclear Fission provides both industrial capacity and scientific progression:

  1. Building Unlocked: Reactor

    • It unlocks the Reactor mega-structure.
    • The Reactor costs 3500 Titanium, 5000 Plate, 50 Concrete, and 25 Blueprint.
    • It provides a substantial +5% Global Production bonus.
    • It generates +10 Energy but consumes Uranium at a rate of 0.001 per Tick.
    • It also increases maximum Uranium storage by +250.

  2. Workshop Upgrades

    • Nuclear Smelters: This upgrade allows existing Smelters to produce Titanium at a rate of 0.0015 per tick. This provides a domestic source for the critical resource Titanium.
    • Reactor Vessel: This upgrade improves the ship potential for expanded Harbour capacity, specifically increasing the Harbour capacity limit by 5% per active Reactor past the 225% limit.

  3. Technology Progression

    • Nuclear Fission unlocks two major scientific pathways: Nanotechnology and Particle Physics.

Strategic Importance

This technology is a mandatory gateway for the Uranium and space exploration phases:

  • Unlocking Dragons: Nuclear Fission is the prerequisite for establishing trade relations with the Dragons. Dragons are essential because they sell Uranium in exchange for Titanium (250 units). Uranium is a vital resource for advanced structures and technologies.
  • Uranium Management: The Reactor, unlocked here, helps manage the newly introduced Uranium resource by increasing its storage capacity.
  • Global Production: The Reactor’s +5% Global Production bonus makes it an attractive investment, helping to boost all resource outputs.

The research path proceeds as part of the broader industrialisation effort: Physics $\to$ Electricity $\to$ Industrialization $\to$ Electronics $\to$ Nuclear Fission.
