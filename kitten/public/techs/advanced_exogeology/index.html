Advanced Exogeology, now primarily referred to as Exometallurgy, is an essential late-game science technology that serves as the gateway to the Eludium Era. It represents a significant shift in production scaling, focusing on the refinement of space-based resources and the drastic expansion of population and scientific limits.

Costs and Prerequisites

  • Science Cost: 325,000.
  • Blueprint Cost: 350 Blueprints.
  • Prerequisite: Researching Exogeology is mandatory before this technology becomes available.

Unlocking Eludium

The primary function of this technology is enabling the crafting of Eludium in the Workshop. Eludium is an extremely rare and expensive composite resource required for deep-space missions and advanced storage structures.

  • Crafting Recipe: 1,000 Unobtainium and 2,500 Alloy.

Enabled Workshop Upgrades

Researching Advanced Exogeology/Exometallurgy provides access to four high-impact Workshop upgrades that optimize the late-game economy:

  • Microwarp Reactors:
    • Cost: 150k Science and 50 Eludium.
    • Effect: Increases the Unobtainium production of Lunar Outposts by 75%. This is considered a vital upgrade for scaling UO production to meet endgame demands.
  • Eludium Huts:
    • Cost: 275k Science and 125 Eludium.
    • Effect: Reduces the Hut price ratio by 10%. This reduction allows for significantly higher kitten populations, which is critical for maximizing Paragon gains upon resetting.
  • Planet Busters:
    • Cost: 275k Science and 250 Eludium.
    • Effect: Doubles the effectiveness of Planet Crackers (Hissmeowra), making them twice as efficient at producing Uranium.
  • Eludium Reflectors:
    • Cost: 250k Science and 15 Eludium.
    • Effect: Improves the maximum science bonus provided by Observatories to Libraries and Data Centers by an additional 2% each.

Strategic Utility

Advanced Exogeology is the threshold where Unobtainium transitions from a rare mission requirement to a primary industrial input. The technology and its associated upgrades are designed to overcome the production bottlenecks of the space era. Specifically, Microwarp Reactors are a priority for any run lasting longer than five minutes, as they provide a massive boost to UO velocity. While Eludium Huts are expensive, they are essential for players pushing toward the highest possible population caps (such as 500+ kittens) to maximize the return on investment for each reset.
