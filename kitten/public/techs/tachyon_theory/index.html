Tachyon Theory is a high-level science technology that marks a major turning point in the late game, transitioning your civilization from standard industrialization into the era of time manipulation and void exploration. It is a mandatory step for unlocking the most advanced endgame mechanics.

Costs and Prerequisites

Researching Tachyon Theory is expensive and requires resources that can only be obtained through advanced trading or religious sacrifices.

  • Science Cost: 750,000.
  • Resource Cost: 25 Time Crystals and 1 Relic.
  • Prerequisite: You must first research Chronophysics.

Unlocked Technology and Upgrades

Researching this technology unlocks the Void Space science and provides access to three powerful Workshop upgrades:

  • Chronoforge: Unlocks the “Chronoforge” section in the Time tab. This allows you to combust (shatter) Time Crystals to skip ahead to the next spring, advancing the calendar year instantly. This is the foundation of the late-game “Shatter Engine,” used to rapidly generate antimatter and resources.
  • Tachyon Accelerators: A massive storage upgrade for Accelerators, increasing the capacity they provide for catnip, wood, minerals, iron, coal, gold, and titanium by 500%.
  • Chronoengineers: An efficiency upgrade that allows Engineers to continue performing crafts during a time skip (provided you have Resource Retrieval levels).

Strategic Importance

Tachyon Theory is primarily used as a gateway to the Endgame Reset Loop. By unlocking the Chronoforge, it allows you to manipulate time to stay within beneficial cycles, such as the Redmoon cycle, which provides a massive boost to Unobtainium production.

Furthermore, it is a required prerequisite for Void Space, which is necessary to attempt the Atheism Challenge and to begin collecting Void, a resource essential for reaching the game’s theoretical limits.
