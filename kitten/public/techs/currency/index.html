In Kittens Game, “currency” can refer to the Currency technology, the primary economic resource Gold, or the prestige-based currencies used for long-term progression.

The Currency Technology

Currency is a mid-early game science technology that costs 2,200 Science and is unlocked after researching Civil Service. Researching it provides the following:

  • Building Unlock: Enables the construction of Tradeposts, which reduce luxury resource demand and improve trading effectiveness.
  • Workshop Upgrade: Unlocks Gold Ore, which allows your Smelters to produce a small amount of gold (0.001 per tick) while they are active.

Gold (Primary Economic Currency)

Gold is the standard currency used for domestic improvements and international relations.

  • Production: Initially generated by Smelters with the Gold Ore upgrade. Later, Geologist kittens can produce it once the Geodesy workshop upgrade is purchased. In Iron Will mode, hunting also has a 25% chance of yielding gold.
  • Usage:
    • Trading: Used as a cost for discovering new civilizations and executing trades with certain races.
    • Promotion: Required to Promote kittens, which increases their rank and production bonuses.
    • Construction: A mandatory material for building Temples, Tradeposts, and Mints.
    • Religion: Essential for purchasing high-tier Order of the Sun upgrades and executing faith resets via Apocrypha.

Prestige Currencies (Resets)

These are earned by resetting the game and provide permanent, stacking benefits for future runs.

  • Karma: Earned if you have more than 35 kittens at reset. It primarily increases Kitten Happiness, which provides a global production bonus.
  • Paragon: Earned if you have more than 70 kittens at reset (1 point for every kitten over 70). It provides a global production bonus and increases resource storage caps.
  • Burned Paragon: Regular Paragon can be “burned” to provide a smaller but separate production boost that is highly effective during the Dark Future (Year 40,000+).

Special and Late-Game Currencies

  • Catpower: While technically a resource, it functions as a currency for Sending Explorers (1,000 Catpower) and initiating Hunts (100 Catpower).
  • Blackcoin: A speculative cryptocurrency bought and sold from Leviathans in exchange for relics. Its price fluctuates over time.
  • Time Crystals: A rare currency used to purchase advanced temporal technologies like Chronospheres and to skip years via time shattering.
