Void Space is a high-tier endgame science technology and a dedicated section in the Time tab that marks the transition into Phase 3 of Kittens Game, focusing on advanced temporal mechanics and the use of the Void resource.

Technological Unlock

  • Cost: 800,000 Science, 30 Time Crystals, and 100 Void.
  • Prerequisite: You must first research Tachyon Theory.
  • Immediate Unlocks: Researching this technology grants access to the Atheism challenge, the Paradox Theory science, and the Cryochambers and Void Aspiration workshop upgrades.

The Atheism Challenge

Void Space is the mandatory requirement to attempt the Atheism Challenge. In this challenge, the Order of the Sun is unavailable, meaning you must play without any faith bonuses. The goal is to reset the game with at least one Cryochamber constructed.

Void Space Structures (Time Tab)

Once the technology is researched, several advanced structures become available in the Void Space section of the Time tab:

  • Cryochambers: Each chamber houses one kitten and allows you to carry that kitten (and its experience) over to your next game. You cannot build more cryochambers than your current number of active Chronospheres.
  • Void Hoover: Unlocked via Void Aspiration; increases the maximum amount of void you can gain during a Temporal Paradox day by 1.
  • Void Rift: Unlocked via Void Aspiration; each rift increases your global resource storage ratio by 2% and boosts HR Harvester energy production by 10%.
  • Chronocontrol: Unlocked via Paradox Theory; increases the number of days a Temporal Paradox lasts.
  • Void Resonator: Unlocked via Paradox Theory; improves faith production and allows Order of the Void to trigger during a time skip.

Strategic Importance

Void Space is a critical gatekeeper for the Endgame Reset Loop. It is the prerequisite for Paradox Theory, which unlocks Resource Retrieval—the mechanic that allows you to gain a percentage of your yearly resources every time you shatter a Time Crystal. Furthermore, building a “Void Seed” through numerous Void Rifts is a primary strategy for reaching the massive Unobtainium caps (over 8 billion) required for self-sustaining positive resets in the late game.
