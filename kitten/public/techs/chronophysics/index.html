Chronophysics is a pivotal mid-to-late game technology that marks the transition from simple space exploration to the manipulation of time itself. It is the primary gateway to carrying resources over through resets and unlocking the advanced “Void” and “Relic” phases of the game.

Requirements and Costs

Chronophysics is often the first technology players encounter that requires Time Crystals rather than standard resources.

  • Prerequisite: You must first research Particle Physics.
  • Science Cost: 250,000.
  • Special Cost: 5 Time Crystals.

Strategic Note: For your first run, obtaining these 5 Time Crystals usually requires trading 5,000 Unobtainium with the Leviathans or sacrificing 25 Alicorns at the Ziggurat.

Key Unlocks

Researching Chronophysics unlocks three critical components for endgame progression:

Unlock Function
Chronosphere A mega-structure that allows you to carry over resources to a new reset. Each sphere preserves 1.5% of your non-crafted resources.
Flux Condensator A Workshop upgrade that allows Chronospheres to also preserve crafted resources (like Blueprints, Eludium, and Steel).
Stasis Chambers An upgrade that nearly doubles the storage capacity of your Accelerators.
Tachyon Theory The subsequent technology required to unlock the Chronoforge, Void Space, and the Atheism Challenge.

Synergy with Anachronomancy

One of the most powerful interactions in the game involves the Anachronomancy metaphysics upgrade.

  • Permanent Unlock: If you own Anachronomancy, Chronophysics is saved across resets.
  • Immediate Access: This means you start every future run with Chronophysics already researched. Combined with carried-over Time Crystals and Unobtainium, this allows you to build Chronospheres immediately at the start of a new run, drastically accelerating your recovery speed.

Strategic Context

You previously asked about resets and Iron Will. Chronophysics is the technology that makes resetting highly profitable rather than a complete restart. By building Chronospheres (unlocked here) and the Flux Condensator, you can carry over millions of blueprints and science resources, effectively bypassing the early-game “grind” entirely.
