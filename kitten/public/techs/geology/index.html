Geology is a crucial mid-game technology that opens up advanced mineral extraction, job specialization, and boosts industrial resource production, particularly Coal and Gold.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the Geology technology:

Technology Details and Cost

  • Prerequisite: Geology is unlocked by researching Navigation.
  • Cost: Researching Geology requires 65,000 Science and 65 Compendium.
  • Unlocks: Geology unlocks the Geologist job, the Quarry building, and the Geodesy upgrade. It also unlocks the Biology technology.

Key Unlocks and Effects

Geology introduces new ways to gather important base resources, accelerating industrial capacity:

Unlock Type Production/Effect Details Source
Geologist Job Coal per Tick +0.015 This kitten job is enabled by Geology.
Quarry Building Minerals +35%, Coal per Tick +0.015 This building is unlocked by Geology.
Geodesy Upgrade Geologists gain +0.0075 coal/tick and +0.0008 gold/tick This workshop upgrade makes Geologists more effective and allows them to find gold.
Mineralogy Department Upgrade Not detailed, but listed as a Zebra Upgrade associated with Geology This is an upgrade for the Academy building.

The Quarry building requires 50 Scaffold, 125 Steel, and 1,000 Slab to construct.

Strategic Importance

Geology and its associated upgrades are highly valuable for boosting key industrial inputs, especially Gold, which becomes a bottleneck resource during the Titanium era:

  • Coal and Gold Production: Strategically, Geology improves both coal and gold production.
  • Gold Generation (Geodesy): The Geodesy upgrade is considered a major milestone. While the upgrade itself is costly (90,000 Science, 250 Titanium, 500 Starchart), acquiring it GREATLY increases gold production. Gold is essential for trading (including the vital trade for Titanium with Zebras) and for funding Temple construction.
  • Resource Bottleneck: In the Titanium era, once you have enough Observatories to get Starcharts at a healthy rate, players are advised to focus on researching Geology and pushing for Geodesy.

In essence, researching Geology provides the necessary specialization (Geologists and Quarries) to rapidly scale the production of fundamental inputs (Minerals and Coal) and provides a pathway (Geodesy) to alleviate the mid-game bottleneck associated with Gold, ensuring smoother trade and cultural progression.
