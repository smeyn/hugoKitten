Biochemistry is an advanced scientific technology research in Kittens Game, situated on the technology tree after Biology. It is essential for unlocking critical infrastructure related to oil production and catnip refinement.

Here is a breakdown of the technology:

Unlocking and Cost

  • Prerequisite: Biochemistry is unlocked by successfully researching Biology.
  • Cost: Researching Biochemistry requires a high amount of resources: 145,000 Science and 500 Compendiums.

Key Unlocks and Effects

Researching Biochemistry opens access to the next scientific field and provides a crucial Workshop upgrade:

  1. Next Technology: Biochemistry unlocks Genetics.
  2. Workshop Upgrade (Biofuel Processing): The research enables the Biofuel Processing Workshop upgrade. This upgrade provides a significant functional benefit to the Bio Lab building (unlocked by Biology).
    • Bio Labs, with the Biofuel Processing upgrade, can automatically convert catnip into oil.
    • This conversion consumes 5 catnip per second (or 1 catnip per tick), and uses 1 Watt of Energy.
    • In exchange, the Bio Lab produces 0.10 oil per second (or 0.02 oil per tick),.

Strategic Significance

Biochemistry plays a critical role in mitigating the oil production bottleneck in the late mid-game, particularly for players facing resource constraints or engaging in specialized runs:

  • Oil Supply: By enabling Bio Labs to convert catnip to oil, Biochemistry helps players utilize massive catnip surpluses to generate industrial fuel. Oil is a common resource needed for structures like Oil Wells, Calciners, and Kerosene production,,.
  • Kerosene Production: The Oil Processing technology, unlocked by Rocketry, enables the production of Kerosene. Kerosene is a crucial fuel for space missions (like Dune Mission and Moon Mission) and the Orbital Array,,. A steady supply of oil via Biofuel Processing is fundamental to maintaining Kerosene production velocity.
  • Production Scaling: The subsequent research unlocked by Genetics is the GM Catnip upgrade, which further improves the oil yield from Bio Labs by 60%,.
  • Run Planning: Due to the high science and compendium costs, the Biology research pathway (which leads to Biochemistry) is sometimes advised to be skipped in initial runs until players can easily craft Alloy.
