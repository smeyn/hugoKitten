The research Electricity is a critical mid-game technology in Kittens Game, enabling the expansion of your industrial and energy infrastructure.

Unlocking and Cost

  • Prerequisite: Electricity is unlocked after researching Physics. Physics itself requires 50,000 Science and 35 Compendiums.
  • Cost: Researching Electricity requires 75,000 Science and 85 Compendiums.

Strategic Role and Progression

Electricity is a vital step in the technological chain, leading to higher levels of industrial and space technology:

  • Building Unlocked: Researching Electricity unlocks the Magneto building. Magnetos are crucial industrial structures that provide a +2% Global Production bonus and +5 Energy.
  • Next Technology: Electricity unlocks the subsequent research branch, Industrialization.
  • Late-Game Path: Electricity is part of the progression required to reach advanced technologies such as Mechanization (which enables Concrete). The technology line progresses through Physics $\to$ Electricity $\to$ Industrialization $\to$ Mechanization.
  • Electronics Unlock: The Industrialization branch, unlocked by Electricity, eventually leads to the Electronics science. Electronics unlocks critical utility upgrades, including SETI (which automates astronomical events for Starchart acquisition) and enables the upgrade of Libraries to Data Centers.

In the mid-game, Electricity is listed alongside Chemistry, Acoustics, and Geology as a research option, noted for advancing the player “one step closer to something useful”.
