In Kittens Game, Festivals are temporary global events that provide significant growth and production bonuses. They are unlocked via the Drama and Poetry technology, which requires 90,000 Science and 5,000 Parchment.

Base Effects and Duration

A standard festival lasts for one game year (400 days, which is approximately 13 minutes and 20 seconds of real-time).

  • Kitten Growth: The primary benefit of a festival is doubling the rate at which new kittens arrive (from roughly 0.16 to 0.32 kittens per second). This is essential for quickly filling housing to reach population milestones for Paragon.
  • Happiness: Festivals provide a base happiness boost, which improves overall village productivity.

Advanced Mechanics (Numeromancy)

Once you research the Numeromancy Metaphysics upgrade, festivals grant powerful Cycle-specific bonuses. These are often used to resolve specific late-game resource bottlenecks:

  • Redmoon: Doubles Unobtainium income (essential for the space race).
  • Cath: Doubles Catpower income (ideal for mass trading or hunting).
  • Piscine: Doubles Science income.
  • Helios: Doubles Faith income and increases Unicorn income by 25%.
  • Kairo: Increases Starchart income by 5x.

Building and Policy Synergies

  • Brewery: This building is specifically designed to enhance festivals. Each Brewery provides +1% to Festival Happiness and the Cycle Bonus, while slightly increasing kitten arrival speed (+0.1%).
  • Carnivals (Metaphysics): This upgrade allows you to stack festivals, adding to their duration so you do not have to manually restart them every year.
  • Carnivale (Policy): Increases the kitten growth rate during festivals by an additional 30%, though it also increases luxury resource consumption.
  • Extravagance (Policy): Makes rare resources provide significantly more happiness during festivals but consumes them twice as fast.

Strategic Utility

  • Early Game: Most guides recommend skipping Drama and Poetry (and thus festivals) on your first few runs because the 5,000 Parchment cost is too high for the marginal early-game benefit.
  • Late Game Paragon Farming: Festivals are mandatory for “short runs” (approx. 30 minutes) where the goal is to max out population as fast as possible to reset for Paragon.
  • Resource Spikes: In the mid-to-late game, players often “shatter” Time Crystals to reach the Redmoon Cycle and then hold a festival to maximize Unobtainium production.
