Several buildings and structures increase your maximum science capacity, ranging from early-game terrestrial buildings to late-game space structures.

Standard Terrestrial Buildings

  • Libraries: The first science building, providing +250 Max Science.
  • Academies: Provides +500 Max Science.
  • Observatories: Provides +1,000 Max Science.
  • Bio Labs: Provides +1,500 Max Science.
  • Data Centers: An upgraded version of the Library that provides +750 Max Science.

Buildings Requiring Specific Upgrades

  • Temples: Initially provide no science, but after purchasing the Scholasticism religion upgrade, each Temple provides +500 Max Science. Each additional level of Scholasticism purchased via Transcendence increases this by an extra +100.
  • Accelerators: Normally used for uranium, but once the LHC (Large Hadron Collider) workshop upgrade is researched, each Accelerator provides +2,500 Max Science.

Space Structures

  • Research Vessels: Found on the Piscine mission, each provides +10,000 Max Science.
  • Space Beacons: Found on the Kairo mission, each provides a massive +25,000 Max Science.

Craftable Resources

  • Compendiums: While not a building, crafting this resource is vital for science storage. Each Compendium adds +10 to your Max Science. This bonus is capped at 100% of your maximum science from buildings (or 1000% if playing in Iron Will mode).

Important Scaling Upgrades

Your base maximum science can be multiplied and scaled through several Workshop and Space upgrades:

  • Astrolabe: Increases the capacity of Observatories by 50% (to +1,500).
  • Satellites: Each active satellite in space increases the science cap of your Observatories by 5%.
  • Reflectors (Titanium, Unobtainium, Eludium): These upgrades allow your Observatories to boost the effectiveness of your Libraries and Data Centers.
  • Uplink & Starlink: These upgrades create a synergy where your Bio Labs and Data Centers provide percentage-based max science boosts to each other.
  • Antimatter & Void Reactors: These endgame upgrades massively multiply the science capacity of your Space Beacons and Research Vessels by up to +400%.
