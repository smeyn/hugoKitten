Tempus Fugit is a powerful toggleable time-manipulation ability that accelerates calendar time and increases your overall production tick rate by 50%.

Here are the core mechanics and strategies regarding its use:

  • Activation and Upkeep: Turning on the ability costs 1 Temporal Flux, and it aggressively consumes 5 Temporal Flux for every real-time second it is active.
  • Production Multiplier: The 50% boost applies globally to your resource generation, acting as a massive multiplier for late-game resources like Unobtainium.
  • Endgame Paragon Farming: Tempus Fugit is highly valued in the deep endgame for rapid Paragon grinding. Players will activate it alongside thousands of Breweries and stacked Festivals to make kittens arrive incredibly fast, allowing them to rapidly cap their population and reset the game for Paragon.
  • Strategic Limitations: Importantly, Tempus Fugit does not affect the amount of resources you receive from Resource Retrieval when shattering Time Crystals. Additionally, if you are playing actively without automation (especially in Iron Will mode), accelerating calendar time gives you less real-world time to manually click on fleeting astronomical events.
  • Sustainment Challenges: Because it drains flux so quickly, maintaining 100% uptime on Tempus Fugit is difficult and often impractical. While you can purchase Temporal Batteries to stockpile a larger reserve of flux while offline, trying to permanently sustain Tempus Fugit using the Chronosurge upgrade is often considered uneconomical due to the massive amounts of Void required to unlock and power it.
