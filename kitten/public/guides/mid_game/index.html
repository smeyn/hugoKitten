n Kittens Game, the mid-game spans the transition from initial industrialization to your first Moon landing and early Paragon farming—roughly between Navigation / Chemistry and Rocketry / Unobtainium. During this phase, progress shifts from manually gathering base resources to establishing production loops, balancing the power grid, and mitigating bottlenecks like Titanium, Coal, and Oil.

The Three Critical Mid-Game Engines

  1. The Magneto + Steamworks Synergy This is your civilization’s primary production engine:
  • Base Mechanic: Each Magneto grants a +2% global production bonus.
  • The Synergy: Each active Steamworks boosts the bonus of all Magnetos by +15%.
  • Formula: Bonus = Magnetos * 0.02 * (1 + Steamworks * 0.15).
  • A setup of 10 Magnetos and 10 Steamworks multiplies every resource in your settlement by +50%, scaling Catnip, Minerals, Wood, Coal, and Iron simultaneously.
  1. The Trade Fleet (Zebra Titanium Automation)
  • Manual Titanium mining is far too slow to sustain mid-game construction.
  • Build hundreds of Trade Ships using Starcharts and Scaffolds.
  • Trade with the Zebras using Catpower and Gold. A high Trade Ship count guarantees large shipments of Titanium and Iron per trade caravan, easily funding Calciners, Magnetos, and Warehouses.
  1. Power Grid & Clean Energy
  • Turning on Magnetos (-5 W each) and Calciners (-1 W each) without enough energy triggers brownouts, slashing efficiency across the village.
  • Prioritize Hydro Plants (upgrades Aqueducts) and Solar Farms to establish at least +20 to +30 W of clean baseline power before adding heavy consumers.

Essential Mid-Game Workshop Upgrades

  • Pneumatic Bellows / Blast Furnace: Automatically converts raw Coal and Iron into Steel via Calciners and Smelters.
  • Offset Press: Upgrades Steamworks to automatically generate Manuscripts each spring, cutting the Culture bottleneck on Compendiums.
  • Pumpjack & Oil Processing: Boosts Oil Well production and storage caps to prepare rocket fuel reserves. Concrete Huts / Concrete Barns: Uses Concrete (Slabs + Steel) to break ground-level housing and storage caps.

The Mid-Game Reset Target

If you are on your first 1–3 runs, the mid-game is your reset staging ground:

  • Target: Push housing until you hit 130–170 kittens (via Concrete Huts and early Space Stations).
  • Paragon Harvest: Earning 60–100 Paragon per reset unlocks the early Metaphysics price-ratio discounts (Golden Ratio, Divine Proportion), making subsequent runs significantly faster.
