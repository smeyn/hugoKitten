The decision of when is a good time to trade in Kittens Game depends heavily on your current stage of progression, what specific resource you need, and which civilization you are trading with.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of trading timelines and strategic considerations:

Early Game Trading (After Year 20)

You can begin trading early, as the Trade tab unlocks after 20 years of gameplay when a Lizard arrives, offering to trade Wood for Minerals. This can be decreased to Year 5 if you have more than 0 Karma, and even to Year 1 with the Diplomacy Metaphysics upgrade.

Trading is immediately useful for:

  • Acquiring needed starting resources: For example, Lizards sell Wood for Minerals.
  • Hunting for Blueprints: Any successful trade has a 10% chance of including one Blueprint as an extra resource. This is fixed and cannot be increased by other trade bonuses. Because Blueprints have an incredibly steep production cost, players are advised to keep Trading until they “luck out and get a Blueprints for free”.
  • Acquiring Spice: Spice (a luxury resource) has a 35% chance of being included in a successful trade.

You must first create Gold before you can trade with anyone, which is accomplished by developing the Gold Ore upgrade in the Workshop.

Mid-to-Late Game Trading (Titanium and Blueprint Acquisition)

Trading becomes central during the mid-game, particularly in the Titanium era (after researching Astronomy and Navigation).

  • Trading for Titanium and Iron (Zebras):

    • Once you learn Navigation and build Trade Ships (which require Starcharts), you can Send Explorers to discover additional civilizations, including the hostile Zebras.
    • Zebras trade Slabs for Titanium and Iron.
    • The focus becomes trading with Zebras non-stop to acquire Titanium and Iron.
    • This is especially important in the Concrete era, where Blueprint requirements for sciences start becoming a bottleneck. Trading allows you to stockpile blueprints while also acquiring titanium and steel.

  • Trading for Minerals/Automation (Nagas and Griffins):

    • Nagas are discovered once you achieve 1500 Culture Cap. They sell Minerals for Ivory. This trade can be used in a loop: trading Ivory for Minerals, then putting Minerals back into Smelters to produce Gold, which is needed for trade costs and other buildings.
    • The sources also suggest a trading loop involving Sharks and Griffins to collect Wood, Steel, Gears, and Blueprints for Steamworks.

Seasonal Considerations (Optimizing Trade Timing)

Trade rates are affected by seasons, making it optimal to trade certain resources during specific times of the year:

Race/Resource Season for Optimal Trade Modifier Strategic Note
Lizards (Wood) Summer +35% (Also good in Autumn: +15%, and Winter: +5%) Wood is generally useful for construction.
Sharks (Catnip) Winter +45% (Also good in Spring: +20%, and Autumn: +15%) Good for supplementing food supply, especially during harsh winters.
Griffins (Iron) Autumn +35% Useful for supporting Smelters and Steel production.
Nagas (Minerals) Spring +25% Useful for early building materials and supporting Smelters. The guide suggests trading with Nagas only in Spring.
Zebras (Iron) Summer +15% Zebras generally buy Slabs, making them an import source for Iron and Titanium.
Zebras (Plate) Winter +25% (Also good in Spring/Autumn: +5%) Plate is important for advanced storage (Harbours).
Spiders (Coal) Autumn +15% Useful for powering industrial buildings.

End-Game Trading (Blueprints and Final Push)

In the end-game, trading remains the fastest way to acquire large amounts of Blueprints and facilitates rapid resets:

  • Fastest Reset Method: Trading becomes the fastest reset possible once you have a massive Catpower stockpile. You can trade, acquire necessary Blueprints (which are needed for Chronospheres), and reset rapidly.
  • Leviathans (Relics and Time Crystals): Once you acquire a Black Pyramid, Leviathans appear and trade Unobtainium for advanced resources like Time Crystals, Relics, and Sorrow. This trade has no seasonal adjustment.

In summary, you should start trading as soon as you meet the prerequisites (Gold and Currency technology), prioritize it in the Titanium era to secure Blueprints and Titanium from Zebras, and time your trades with Nagas, Griffins, and Sharks according to the seasons to maximize resource returns.

Trading often acts as a supply chain bridge, like using a freight train to move large quantities of bulk goods needed for heavy industry, rather than trying to hand-carry every component.

How Tradeposts Affect the TradeRatio

Tradeposts affect the {TradeRatio} through three distinct mechanisms:

A. Direct Building Effect (Base Trade Ratio) Each built Tradepost (tradepost.val) adds to the global building effect tradeRatio.

Bonus per Tradepost: +0.015 (or +1.5%) Formula contribution: {Base Tradeposts Bonus} = 0.015 * {Number of Tradeposts}$$

B. Synergy with Policies (Phantom Tradeposts)

The diplomatic policy bonus (calculateTradeBonusFromPolicies) treats certain policy effects as “phantom tradeposts” that are multiplied by the base tradepost efficiency 0.015.

Formula:{Policy Bonus} = 0.015 * {PhantomTradeposts}

Phantom Tradepost Sources (for non-zebra races like Leviathans):

  • Liberalism: +10 Phantom Tradeposts
  • Outer Space Treaty: +10 Phantom Tradeposts
  • Zebra Relations Bellicosity: +5 Phantom Tradeposts
  • Max Contribution: Up to +0.375 (+37.5%) to {TradeRatio} from policies.

Note: The number of physically built Tradeposts does not scale this policy bonus; it only utilizes the base tradepost multiplier coefficient of 0.015.

C. Pacifism Challenge Completion Synergy

If the Pacifism challenge has been completed one or more times, it unlocks a permanent trade ratio bonus when the challenge is inactive:

{Pacifism Bonus} = 0.015 * \min\Big({TradepostLimit}, {Number of Tradeposts} * {TradepostRatioLimit}\Big)

Where:

{TradeKnowledge}: Typically 1.0 per completion of the Pacifism challenge.

{TradepostLimit}: (7 + 3 * {TradeKnowledge}) * (0.99 + 0.01 * {TradeKnowledge})

{TradepostRatioLimit}$: A diminishing returns factor capped at 0.25.

This means completed Pacifism runs directly scale the efficiency of your built Tradeposts, allowing your physical Tradepost count to provide an extra multiplicative boost to the {TradeRatio}.
