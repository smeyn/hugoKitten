In Kittens Game, Burning Paragon is a way to squeeze more efficiency out of your prestige bonuses. It converts your “live” Paragon into Burned Paragon, which exists in a separate pool and stacks with your regular Paragon.

Since you are currently struggling with Faith storage for Sunspires, understanding how burning affects your storage is critical.

1. The Immediate Trade-off

When you burn Paragon, the effect on your current run is usually negative unless you are very far into the game:

  • Storage: Regular Paragon gives 0.1% storage per point. Burned Paragon only gives 0.05% (half as much) before Year 40,000.
  • Production: Regular Paragon caps its bonus at 150 points. Burned Paragon caps its bonus at 75 points.

Why do it then? Because they stack. 150 regular Paragon + 75 burned Paragon gives you a higher production bonus than 1,000 regular Paragon alone, because regular Paragon hits diminishing returns very quickly.

2. When to Burn for Your Faith Problem

Burning Paragon is a “reset” for your storage, which might actually make your Sunspire problem worse in the short term. However, it is a powerful long-term strategy:

  • The “75/150” Rule: The most efficient setup for a mid-game player is to have 75 Burned Paragon and 150+ Regular Paragon. This maximizes your production bonus before diminishing returns cripple you.
  • The Best Time to Burn: Always burn right before you reset your game. If you burn in the middle of a run, your storage caps (including Faith) will instantly drop, potentially breaking your production lines.
  • Dark Future (Year 40,000+): If you are doing a very long run, Burned Paragon becomes 4x more powerful for storage than regular Paragon. If you reach Year 40k, burning becomes your primary way to massive storage increases.

3. Other Benefits of Burned Paragon

  • Leader Bonus: Burned Paragon provides a unique boost to your Leader’s traits (e.g., if your leader is a Manager, they get even better at reducing hunting costs).
  • Karma/Cryochambers: It reduces the amount of Karma needed to build Cryochambers, which helps you carry more kittens into the next run. Burned Paragon is a permanent prestige resource in Kittens Game created by “burning” regular Paragon. While burned paragon provides smaller initial production bonuses than regular paragon, it stacks with regular paragon and becomes significantly more powerful during the late-game Dark Future (Year 40,000+).

4. Core Effects

  • Production Bonus: Burned paragon provides a global production boost that is exactly half as effective as regular paragon before reaching Year 40,000. However, because it is tracked as a separate pool, it stacks additively with regular paragon, allowing you to bypass the severe diminishing returns that regular paragon hits at high levels (starting around 150–200).
  • Storage Bonus: Each point of burned paragon increases your resource storage capacity.
    • Pre-Dark Future: +0.05% storage capacity per point.
    • During Dark Future: +0.2% storage capacity per point, making it twice as effective as regular paragon for storage in the deep endgame.
  • Leader Trait Boost: Burned paragon provides a small multiplicative boost to the effectiveness of your Leader’s Trait (e.g., Artisan crafting or Scientist research discounts).
  • Trading Bonus: When using a Merchant leader, burned paragon increases the number of Time Crystals received per trade with the Leviathans.

5. Strategic Utility and Timing

  • Early Mid-Game (400 Threshold): Many players choose to burn their first 400 paragon. This is considered an optimal “sweet spot” because 400 burned paragon provides production bonuses roughly equivalent to the peak efficiency of regular paragon (around 200) before heavy diminishing returns set in.
  • Iron Will Strategy: Burned paragon is vital for Iron Will mode because it provides essential production and storage boosts that cannot be easily acquired through normal buildings.
  • Relic Era Prep: It is recommended to have at least 200 to 400 burned paragon before focusing heavily on Relics and Antimatter to ensure your industrial base is strong enough to handle the costs.
  • Late-Game “Seed”: In the extreme late game, players aim for massive stockpiles (up to 250,000+ burned paragon) to maximize storage for the push toward 100 Chronospheres and “Positive Resets.”

6. The “Dark Future” Interaction

The primary reason to accumulate large amounts of burned paragon is to mitigate the penalties of the Dark Future, which begins at Year 40,000. During this period, regular paragon production bonuses are suppressed, but burned paragon effects are amplified. Its storage bonus quadruples (from 0.05% to 0.2%), which is critical for reaching the multi-billion Unobtainium caps required for endgame progression.

7. How to Burn

  • Burning is done via the Metaphysics tab (unlocked with Unobtainium).
  • Caution: Once paragon is burned, it cannot be turned back into regular paragon. It is generally advised to buy your desired Metaphysics upgrades (like price ratio reductions) before burning your remaining spare paragon.
  • Note: Burned paragon always carries over across resets, even if you are not using Chronospheres.

Summary Recommendation

If you are stuck at 6 Sunspires:

  1. Don’t burn right now if you are in the middle of a run; you will lose the storage you need.
  2. Wait until you are ready to reset. Just before you click “Reset,” go to the Metaphysics tab and click Burn Paragon.
  3. On your next run, you will have the Burned Paragon bonus plus the new Paragon you get from your kittens. This combined total will make reaching that 7th Golden Spire much easier.
