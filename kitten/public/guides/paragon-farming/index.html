When you are speed-farming Paragon, your primary goal is to bypass the early-game crawl as fast as possible so you can scale your kitten population to ~160 and reset. Because your Chronospheres carry over raw resources and Time Crystals, you won’t be starting from absolute zero, but your tech tree path still needs to be laser-focused on housing, population scaling, and space access.

Here is the most efficient sequence of technologies to research after a fresh paragon-farming reset:

Phase 1: Securing the Basics & Resource Generation

  • Agriculture & Animal Husbandry: Mandatory first steps to unlock catnip fields, pastures, and basic food stability so your initial few kittens don’t starve.
  • Mining & Metalworking: Essential for getting your iron and titanium production online so you can start constructing basic buildings and workshops.
  • Writing: Unlocks Temples and manuscripts. (If you picked Tradition earlier, this is where your manuscript costs drop).

Phase 2: Unlocking Population & Building Expansion

  • Construction & Architecture: Unlocks concrete, warehouses, and better housing options to expand your storage caps so your Chronosphere carryovers don’t instantly overflow and get wasted.
  • Philosophy: Unlocks Temples further (for Apocrypha/Solar Revolution faith boosts) and your core government policies (like getting your happiness and production modifiers locked in).
  • Civil Service: Crucial for farming. This unlocks your Embassies and foreign trade routes. Trade is your lifeline for bypassing tedious manual resource gathering—specifically trading with the Zebras for Titanium.

Phase 3: Scaling Industry & Energy

  • Machinery & Electronics: Unlocks steamworks, factories, and the energy grid. You need electricity to power advanced housing (Mansions) and space facilities.
  • Industrialization: Accelerates your raw resource conversion rates.

Phase 4: Reaching Space & the Finish Line

  • Rocketry & Satellites: Your gateway to the Space tab. Once you unlock space, you can immediately start building:

  • Lunar Outposts and Space Elevators to flood your economy with Unobtainium and Titanium.

  • Chronospheres (if you have the Unobtainium from your carryover/shatters), which ensure your next farming run starts with even more resources.

  • Biology / Chemistry / Physics: Grab these as needed to support your space program and unlock high-tier workshop upgrades, but don’t waste precious science early on researching deep, non-essential tech trees that don’t help you push your kitten population.

Pro-Tips for Speed-Farming Tech Progression

  1. Rely on Your Carryovers: Thanks to your Chronospheres, don’t waste time manually gathering early-game wood and minerals if your carryover cache already paid out. Drop your wood burners, mines, and libraries immediately.
  2. Skip the Fluff: When speed-farming, ignore deep scientific techs that don’t feed directly into housing, space, or resource multipliers. You don’t need every single late-game tech if your only goal is hitting 160 kittens and resetting.
  3. Keep Festival Active: Run festivals back-to-back as soon as you have the culture and catnip to spare. It supercharges your production and kitten arrival rate, cutting hours off your farm loop.
