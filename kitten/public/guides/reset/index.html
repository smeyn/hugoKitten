Resetting is the primary mechanic used to exchange current progress for permanent prestige currencies—Karma and Paragon—which provide global multipliers to production and resource storage capacity.

Timing Your Reset

Deciding when to reset depends on your current game phase and prestige goals:

  • The First Reset: It is strategically recommended to reset only after researching Concrete Huts and reaching at least 130 kittens. This ensures you gain roughly 60 Paragon, enough to purchase essential early Metaphysics upgrades like Diplomacy or Golden Ratio.
  • Subsequent Early Runs: Aim for 170+ kittens on your second run to secure 100 Paragon for the Divine Proportion upgrade.
  • Prestige Minimums: You must have more than 35 kittens to gain Karma and more than 70 kittens to receive Paragon (one point per kitten over 70).
  • The “Slow-Down” Rule: Generally, you should reset when progress becomes “too slow” or when building costs exceed your current production and storage limits.

The Pre-Reset Checklist (BMF)

Experienced players use the BMF mnemonic (Backup, Metas, Faith) to ensure no progress is wasted before a reset:

  • Backup: Manually save and export your game data to prevent accidental loss.
  • Metas (Metaphysics): Use your accumulated Paragon to buy permanent upgrades in the Metaphysics tab, particularly price ratio reductions like Enlightenment or Renaissance, which make future buildings cheaper.
  • Faith (The TAP Sequence): To maximize spiritual carry-over, perform the TAP sequence: Transcendence (to increase your tier), then Adore the Galaxy (to convert worship into Epiphany), and finally Praise the Sun.
  • Population Push: Max out all housing, including Huts, Log Houses, and Mansions, to squeeze out every possible Paragon point.
  • Resource Conversion: Sacrifice all unicorns for tears and alicorns for Time Crystals before they are lost.

Carry-over Mechanics

Understanding what survives the reset is vital for planning your next run:

  • Permanent Carry-over: Karma, Paragon, Metaphysics upgrades, Epiphany, Transcendence tiers, Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS), and Cryptotheology buildings are preserved.
  • Resources Lost: You lose all standard buildings, technology, and luxury resources like furs, ivory, and spice.
  • Conditional Carry-over:
    • Chronospheres: Each sphere preserves 1.5% of your non-crafted resources.
    • Flux Condensator: This workshop upgrade is required to carry over crafted resources like steel, plates, and blueprints.
    • Cryochambers: These allow you to carry over one kitten per chamber, retaining their job skills and experience.

Advanced Reset Strategies

  • Iron Will (IW) Mode: In this kittenless mode, the goal is to reset immediately after researching Archery to accumulate Zebras, which provide passive Catpower for future runs.
  • Positive Resets: In the endgame, the goal is to build at least 73 Chronospheres. At this level, the resources carried over exceed the cost of the spheres themselves, allowing for infinite, self-sustaining growth loops.

Checklist

To maximize the benefits of a reset and ensure your next run starts with a massive economic foundation, you should follow a thorough pre-reset preparation sequence. The community often uses the acronym BMF (Backup, Metas, and Faith) to remember the core steps. Here is a comprehensive pre-reset checklist:

  1. Maximize Your Population (Paragon Preparation)

    Before anything else, cap out all of your housing structures (Huts, Log Houses, Mansions, Space Stations). Because Paragon rewards are based directly on your kitten population at the time of reset (granting 1 Paragon for every kitten over 70), you should push your population as high as your storage caps allow.

  2. Backup and Metaphysics (The “B” and “M” in BMF)

    • Backup: Always export and save a backup of your game just in case you forget a step or realize you didn’t have enough resources stockpiled.
    • Metaphysics: If you have enough Paragon, purchase any planned Metaphysics upgrades (such as price ratio reductions). If you plan to burn any Paragon for its unique production and storage bonuses, do so now while you have a small spare amount.

  3. Optimize Your Religion (The “F” in BMF) To ensure your faith bonuses carry over effectively, execute the TAP sequence:

    • Transcend: Spend your accumulated epiphany to buy Transcendence tiers.
    • Adore the Galaxy: Convert all of your worship into epiphany. The game will do this automatically if you forget, but manually clicking the button grants an extra 1% bonus.
    • Praise the Sun: Convert your remaining faith pool into worship.

  4. Refine Luxury Resources:

    Do not leave valuable resources behind, as luxuries do not carry over on reset. Sacrifice all of your accumulated Alicorns to turn them into Time Crystals, ensuring you have the Anachronomancy metaphysics upgrade so the crystals survive the reset. Refine any spare unicorn tears into Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS), which is permanently retained.

  5. Chronosphere and Resource Stockpiling:

    Chronospheres allow you to carry over 1.5% of your standard resources per sphere into the next run to optimize this:

    • Turn off automation: Disable any scripts or autoclickers (like auto-praise or auto-crafting) so they don’t consume your carefully balanced stockpiles right before you reset.
    • Buy Flux Condensator: Purchase this workshop upgrade (costing 5,000 unobtainium and 5 Time Crystals) so your crafted resources, such as blueprints, alloy, and compendiums, will also carry over.
    • Stockpile everything: Cap out your non-crafted resources (like gold, faith, and starcharts). Heavily stockpile crafted resources; carrying over a massive amount of Compendiums is especially critical, as they will vastly increase your science cap at the very start of your next run, allowing you to instantly rebuy key technologies.
    • Liquidate Buildings: Right before buying your Chronospheres, you can sell off expensive buildings like Orbital Arrays and Space Elevators at a 50% refund to squeeze out extra resources for your carryover pool.
    • Buy Chronospheres: Finally, purchase as many Chronospheres as your unobtainium allows.

Once all these steps are complete, you are ready to hit the reset button.

Progression

Paragon progression in Kittens Game follows a compounding curve. Paragon functions as both a raw production and storage multiplier when stockpiled and a currency spent in Metaphysics. The central rule of farming it is: never spend down to zero, because your banked reserve is what makes subsequent runs fast.

How Paragon Is Earned

When you reset via the Time tab:

  • You gain 1 Paragon per kitten over 70 (Kittens - 70).
    • 130 kittens = 60 Paragon
    • 200 kittens = 130 Paragon
    • 250 kittens = 180 Paragon
  • You also earn Karma, which increases kitten happiness and scales with total historical kittens sacrificed across resets.

The Golden Ratio: The 150–200 Banked Floor

  • Production Bonus: Each banked Paragon grants +1% global production, capped at +200% with diminishing returns past 200 (hard cap asymptotic behavior).
  • Storage Bonus: Each banked Paragon grants a flat +0.1% to storage caps, with no diminishing returns.
  • The Spending Rule: When buying Metaphysics upgrades, always maintain at least 150 to 200 unspent Paragon in reserve. Spending all your Paragon to buy an upgrade sets your early-game production back to ground zero.

Step-by-Step Reset Progression Strategy

The Exact Reset Point Rule

The trap in Kittens Game is overextending a run hoping for “just a few more kittens.”

Reset immediately when:

Time to unlock the next 2 kitten housing slots > Time to rebuild to your current kitten count on a fresh run

More specifically, reset when all three conditions are met:

  1. Housing Caps Hard-Stall: You have capped all available Log Cabins, Mansions, and Huts, and the next housing structure requires either days of waiting or a massive tech tree leap (e.g., waiting days to mine enough Unobtainium for another Space Station).
  2. Kitten Count is at its Natural Bracket:
  • Early Game: Reset around 130 kittens.
  • Mid Game (Post-Concrete/Unobtainium Huts): Reset around 180–220 kittens.
  • Post-Renaissance: Reset around 250–270+ kittens.
  1. Pre-Reset Checklist is Done:
  • Burn excess Catpower into Hunts (maximize furs/ivory/parchment).
  • Trade all spare resources for maximum storage fill.
  • Praise the Sun and perform an Apocrypha reset if you have excess faith stored, to permanently increase your Solar Revolution multiplier.
  • Fill every housing slot to capacity before hitting the reset button.
