Karma is a permanent prestige resource in Kittens Game that is primarily used to boost kitten happiness and overall production efficiency across game resets.

Earning Karma

  • Threshold: You begin earning karma points when you reset your game with more than 35 kittens.
  • Scaling: The amount of karma gained is based on your total population at the time of reset; more kittens result in a higher karma yield.
  • Challenge Modifier: In the Anarchy challenge, kittens count double toward karma gains when you reset.

Core Effects

  • Happiness Boost: Karma’s primary mechanical benefit is providing a +1% happiness modifier per point.
  • Production Bonus: Since kitten productivity is tied to happiness, karma acts as a global production multiplier for all working kittens.
  • Pre-Space Utility: In early runs, karma is essential for mitigating the “population penalty” to happiness, which otherwise acts as a “production killer” as your village grows.
  • Late-Game Scaling: When combined with the Holy Genocide cryptotheology building, karma can contribute to “karmakittens,” which increases the paragon earned per kitten during resets.

Usage and Costs

  • Cryochambers: Karma is consumed as a resource to build Cryochambers (costing 1 karma each), which allows you to carry individual kittens over into your next run.
  • Diminishing Returns: While regular paragon production bonuses suffer from diminishing returns, the happiness bonus from karma scales linearly.

Persistence

Unlike buildings or standard resources, karma is one of the few items that always carries over when you reset your game, providing a permanent foundation for future growth. Strategic advice often suggests pushing for at least 130 kittens in early runs to ensure a healthy amount of karma and paragon for subsequent progression.
