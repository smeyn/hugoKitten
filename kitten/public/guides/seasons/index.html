In Kittens Game, Seasons are a core cyclical mechanic that dictates the efficiency of your resource production, particularly catnip, and influences the success of trade with other civilisations.

The Seasonal Cycle

Each year consists of four seasons: Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter.

  • Duration: Each season lasts 100 days, which equates to 1,000 game ticks or approximately 3 minutes and 20 seconds in real-time.
  • Calendar: The progression of seasons is hidden until you research the Calendar technology (30 Science).

Catnip Production and Survival

The primary impact of seasons is on Catnip Fields, which receive a production boost in Spring and a massive penalty in Winter.

Season Catnip Multiplier (Average Weather) Impact on Strategy
Spring +50% Best time to expand your population and build up stocks.
Summer +0% Standard production; a good time to focus on non-food resources.
Autumn +0% Focus on accumulating large catnip reserves for the coming cold.
Winter -75% Production nosedives; kittens are at high risk of starvation.

Key Exception: Farmers are vital because their catnip production is not affected by seasonal modifiers. Reallocating kittens to the Farmer job during Winter is a standard survival tactic.

Weather Variations

Starting in Year 4, seasons have a 35% chance to be “abnormal”—either Warm or Cold. This adds a further modifier to your catnip fields:

  • Warm Weather: Provides an additional boost (e.g., Spring becomes +65%).
  • Cold Weather: Makes penalties harsher (e.g., Winter drops to -90%).

The Communism policy makes cold winters even more dangerous by setting catnip field production to -100%.

Seasonal Trade Strategy

Different races offer better deals or different resource amounts depending on the time of year.

  • Spring: The best time to trade with Nagas for Minerals (+25%).
  • Summer: Lizards provide a +35% bonus to Wood.
  • Autumn: Griffins are most efficient, providing +35% Iron.
  • Winter: Sharks are the MVP of Winter, providing a +45% bonus to Catnip, which can be refined into Wood to survive the season.

Advanced Seasonal Mechanics

  • Energy Generation: Solar Farms are more efficient in Summer (+2.67 Energy) than in Winter (+1.5 Energy).
  • Space Production: Once Numerology is researched, different years in a 10-year Cycle (like Redmoon or Helios) provide additional multipliers to space resources.
  • “Winter Has Come” Challenge: This challenge forces the game into a permanent, endless Winter, requiring players to rely entirely on Farmers for food.
