Solar Chant is a fundamental religious upgrade found within the Order of the Sun section of the Religion tab. It serves as a primary driver for scaling your colony’s religious economy by directly increasing the rate at which Faith is generated.

Cost and Requirements

  • Initial Cost: 100 Faith.
  • Prerequisite: You must have accumulated at least 150 Worship (formerly known as “praised faith”) to unlock the option to purchase it.
  • Price Ratio: It has a price ratio of 2.5, meaning that if you unlock the ability to purchase multiple levels, each subsequent tier will cost 2.5 times more than the last.

Core Effects and Stacking

  • Base Bonus: Upon the first purchase, Solar Chant provides a permanent 10% boost to your overall faith generation rate.
  • Transcendence Scaling: Once you unlock the Transcendence upgrade (which requires 125,000 Worship, 7,500 Faith, and 7,500 Gold), Solar Chant becomes a tiered upgrade.
  • Incremental Growth: For every additional level of Solar Chant purchased using Transcendence, the faith production bonus increases by an additional +10%.

Strategic Utility

  • Late-Game “Priest Strategy”: As players enter the late game and find themselves waiting for slow-moving resources like Unobtainium or Eludium, a common strategy is to reassign the majority of the kitten workforce to the Priest job. Solar Chant is critical here, as multiple levels of the upgrade significantly amplify the output of these [Priests] ( /job/priest/).
  • Faith Reset Loops: High levels of Solar Chant facilitate a positive feedback loop. By dramatically increasing faith production, players can more rapidly rebuild their Solar Revolution production bonus (which caps at 1000%) after performing a faith reset or “Adoring the Galaxy”.
  • Transcendence Progression: Accelerating faith production via Solar Chant is essential for reaching the extremely high epiphany requirements of later Transcendence Tiers, such as Tier 15, which requires approximately 3.04 million percent epiphany.
