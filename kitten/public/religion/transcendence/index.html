Transcendence is a permanent religious progression system in the sources that allows players to stack tiered upgrades and boosts the efficiency of “Adoring the stars”. It is unlocked in the religion tab for 7,500 Faith and 7,500 Gold once you have reached a prerequisite of 125,000 Worship.

Mechanics of Transcending

Once the initial upgrade is purchased, a permanent “Transcend” button appears. This allows you to spend Epiphany—a hidden resource converted from Worship when you “Adore the Galaxy”—to increase your Transcendence Tier.

  • Carry Over: Transcendence tiers are among the few things that permanently carry over through game resets.
  • Tier Thresholds: The cost in Epiphany increases exponentially; for example, Tier 1 requires ~1.97% Epiphany, while Tier 15 requires ~3.04M%.

Key Benefits of Increasing Tiers

  • Epiphany Multiplier: Each Transcendence Tier increases the amount of Epiphany you receive when you “Adore the Galaxy”. The conversion multiplier is (TT+1)^2 (e.g., at Tier 10, your conversion is 121x stronger).
  • Cryptotheology Unlocks: Specific tiers are required to purchase advanced endgame buildings using Relics:
    • Tier 1: Black Obelisk (increases Solar Revolution cap).
    • Tier 7: Event Horizon (increases global resource storage).
    • Tier 15: Blazar (massively boosts Time Crystal and Relic production).
    • Tier 25: Holy Genocide (unlocks the Post-Apocalypse challenge).
  • Multi-Level Upgrades: Researching the Transcendence upgrade allows you to purchase religious abilities like Solar Chant, Scholasticism, and Golden Spire multiple times, with each level further boosting faith production and storage.
  • Atheism Challenge: In the Atheism challenge, your Solar Revolution cap is increased by +100% per Transcendence Tier.

Progression and Costs

Increasing your Transcendence Tier requires a set amount of Epiphany. The cost escalates exponentially, with Tier 1 requiring only ~1.97% Epiphany bonus, while Tier 15 requires over 3.04 million%.

Strategic Progression: BMF and TAP

The sources provide two specific methods for managing Transcendence to avoid losing progress or production bonuses:

  1. The BMF Reset Protocol: To maximize prestige gains before a game reset, players should follow the Backup, Metas, Faith (BMF) order. Within the Faith step, you must Transcend before you Adore the stars. Because higher Transcendence tiers increase the conversion multiplier for Adoration, this order ensures you receive the maximum possible Epiphany for your next run.
  2. The Mid-Run TAP Method: If you wish to Transcend without resetting the entire game, the sources suggest the TAP (Transcend, Adore, Praise) procedure. A rule of thumb is to have at least 1.5 times the Epiphany threshold listed for the next tier before doing this, otherwise, you may severely decrease your praising bonus and harm your current production.

Endgame Benefits and Unlocks

Reaching higher Transcendence tiers is essential for late-game and “Phase 3” progression:

  • Cryptotheology: Tiers are required to unlock specific endgame buildings. For instance, Black Obelisks require Tier 1, while Blazars—which are vital for time crystal and relic production—require Tier 15.
  • Atheism Challenge: Completing this challenge grants a permanent reward where the Solar Revolution cap increases by 100 percentage points for every Transcendence tier you possess.
  • Tiered Upgrades: Each tier allows you to purchase additional levels of religious upgrades like Solar Chant (+10% faith generation), Scholasticism (+ science), and Sun Altar (+ happiness/faith storage).
  • Unicorn Tears Challenge: In this mode, Unicorn Tombs increase your maximum faith specifically based on your Transcendence Tier.

Pro-Tip: Strategists recommend waiting until you have 1.5x to 2x the required Epiphany bonus for the next tier before Transcending mid-run to avoid a significant temporary drop in your Solar Revolution production bonus.
