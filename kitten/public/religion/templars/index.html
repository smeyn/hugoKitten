Templars is a high-tier religious upgrade available under the Order of the Sun in the Religion tab. It specifically enhances the utility of Temples by increasing the colony’s maximum Catpower capacity.

Acquisition and Costs

To unlock the Templars upgrade, you must first research Theology and accumulate a significant amount of religious capital.

  • Prerequisites: You must have at least 75,000 Worship accumulated through “Praising the Sun”.
  • Cost: The initial upgrade costs 3,500 Faith and 3,000 Gold.
  • Price Ratio: Like most religious upgrades, it has a price ratio of 2.5, meaning subsequent levels (if unlocked via Transcendence) become significantly more expensive.

Effects and Scaling

The primary function of Templars is to turn your Temples into storage facilities for Catpower.

  • Base Effect: Each Temple provides +75 max catpower.
  • Transcendence Scaling: If you have purchased Transcendence, you can buy additional levels of the Templars upgrade. Each additional level increases the bonus by a further +25 max catpower per Temple.

Strategic Utility

While it may seem like a minor bonus early on, Templars is a critical component for late-game industrial and trade optimization:

  • Blueprint Farming: In the endgame, players often rely on Mints to generate the massive quantities of blueprints needed for high-tier structures. Because Mints scale their output based on your maximum Catpower capacity, the boost provided by Templars directly increases your blueprint production speed.
  • Policy Synergies: The Liberalism policy is specifically noted for allowing players to acquire more levels of the Templars upgrade more easily, which in turn assists with ivory generation and international trade.
  • Resource Management: By significantly raising the Catpower cap, Templars allows you to store enough energy to fund massive space missions, such as Orbital Launches (which require 5,000 Catpower) or Rorschach Missions (which require 80,000 Catpower), without being limited by standard housing caps.
