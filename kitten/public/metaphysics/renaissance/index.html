Renaissance is the final and most powerful Metaphysics upgrade in the building price ratio reduction chain (following Enlightenment, Golden Ratio, Divine Proportion, and Vitruvian Feline).

Core Effects and Cost

  • Effect: Renaissance provides a massive 2.25% reduction to all building price ratios. This makes buildings significantly cheaper to construct, allowing you to build many more structures to boost your population and production before hitting your resource storage caps. It is widely considered one of the most powerful (“OP”) metaphysics upgrades in the game because once you acquire it, you will rarely have issues generating enough paragon for anything else.
  • Cost: Purchasing the upgrade requires a substantial investment of 750 Paragon.

Strategy and Timing

  • Progression: It is highly recommended that you prioritise acquiring all price ratio reduction upgrades, culminating in Renaissance, before making serious investments into other late-game mechanics like end-game unicorn ziggurats.
  • When to Buy: Players typically aim to purchase Renaissance around their fifth to seventh reset. A common strategy is to keep resetting at Unobtainium Huts until you have generated enough paragon to comfortably afford it.
  • Challenges: If you plan on taking on the game’s difficult challenge modes, such as the Anarchy challenge, it is highly advised to secure Renaissance beforehand to ensure your economy can withstand the harsh penalties.
