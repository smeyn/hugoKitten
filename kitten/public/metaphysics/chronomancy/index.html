Chronomancy is a mid-game Metaphysics upgrade that serves as a vital bridge toward advanced temporal and astronomical technologies. It costs 25 Paragon to purchase and is primarily used to increase the frequency of celestial phenomena.

Core Effects and Unlocks

  • Event Multiplier: It provides a 1.1x multiplier to the daily chance of both meteors and astronomical events. The sources explicitly note that this is a multiplicative bonus to the base chance, not a flat +10% addition.
  • Technological Prerequisites: Purchasing Chronomancy is required to unlock two essential high-tier Metaphysics:
    • Anachronomancy: Allows Time Crystals and Chronophysics to persist across game resets.
    • Astromancy: Further doubles the chance of star events and increases the effectiveness of Observatories.

Strategic Utility

The value of Chronomancy depends on your current stage of progression:

  • The Starchart Solution: In early runs where Starchart production is a bottleneck, the combination of Chronomancy and Astromancy is described as “pure magic”. It more than doubles event frequency, allowing you to build a larger fleet of Trade Ships for Titanium acquisition.
  • Late-Game Priority: In very advanced “Endgame” or “Iron Will” runs, some strategies suggest prioritising price-ratio reductions (like Renaissance) first, treating Chronomancy as a “less vital” upgrade to be picked up once your economic foundation is stable.

Advanced Synergies

The effectiveness of the Chronomancy bonus can be further amplified through late-game structures and upgrades:

  • Temporal Accelerators: These items in the Time tab improve Chronomancy’s time-related effects by 1.25%.
  • Blazars: As a Cryptotheology building, each Blazar improves Chronomancy effectiveness by 2.5%.
  • Observatory Integration: Within the Bonfire tab, Chronomancy is listed as an upgrade for the Observatory, specifically increasing the Astronomical Event Chance by the 1.1x multiplier.
