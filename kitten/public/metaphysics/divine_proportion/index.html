Divine Proportion is a highly recommended Metaphysics upgrade that directly improves your civilization’s economy by lowering the escalating costs of buildings. It serves as the third major upgrade in the crucial price ratio reduction chain, bridging the gap between Golden Ratio and Vitruvian Feline.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Prerequisite: You must first purchase the Golden Ratio metaphysics upgrade to unlock Divine Proportion.
  • Cost: Purchasing the upgrade requires an investment of 100 Paragon.

Core Effects

  • Price Ratio Reduction: Divine Proportion reduces all building price ratios by ~1.77% (exactly 16/9%). This means subsequent buildings will escalate in cost much slower, allowing you to construct significantly more structures before hitting your resource storage caps.
  • Progression: Acquiring Divine Proportion directly unlocks Vitruvian Feline, the next tier of price ratio reduction.

Strategy and Timing

  • Prioritization: It is highly advised that you prioritize buying all of the price ratio reduction upgrades (Enlightenment, Golden Ratio, Divine Proportion, Vitruvian Feline, and Renaissance) as early as possible in your playthroughs, as making everything in your village cheaper is vital for rapid expansion.
  • When to Buy: A common community strategy is to aim for purchasing Divine Proportion around your third reset. Players typically try to acquire it when they reach a population of about 200 kittens, and then continue pushing their population to 220+ kittens before resetting again. This ensures you generate enough Paragon (around 150) to comfortably afford your next set of upgrades in the following run.
