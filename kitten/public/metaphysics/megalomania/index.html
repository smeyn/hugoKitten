Megalomania is a mid-game Metaphysics upgrade that serves as the essential gateway to advanced religious structures and the “Unicorn Era”. It costs 10 Paragon to purchase.

Unlocks and Benefits

By purchasing Megalomania, you gain access to high-tier structures and mechanics that are otherwise hidden:

  • Black Pyramids: These are necessary to attract Leviathans, allowing you to trade Unobtainium for Time Crystals and Relics.
  • Markers: These strange structures initiate the corruption process, which slowly transforms Alicorns into Necrocorns.
  • Black Codex: Unlocks further religious upgrades, such as the Unicorn Graveyard, which boosts your maximum culture based on the number of Ziggurats you possess.

Acquisition Requirements

To unlock Megalomania in the Metaphysics menu, you must meet the following criteria:

  • Science Tech: Research the Metaphysics technology (55k Science and 5 Unobtainium).
  • Metaphysics Prerequisite: You must first purchase Enlightenment (formerly Engineering) for 5 Paragon.

Strategic Timing: When to Buy

While its cost is relatively low, strategic guides offer specific warnings regarding when to invest your Paragon:

  • The “Early Game Trap”: Megalomania is often considered a trap for new players. Because the structures it unlocks (like Black Pyramids) are “really expensive” and belong to the endgame resource line, it is not recommended for your first few runs.
  • Priority Order: You should prioritise all price ratio reduction upgrades—specifically Golden Ratio, Divine Proportion, and Renaissance—before buying Megalomania.
  • The 40k Run Exception: If you are attempting a very long run (such as the 40k years milestone), Megalomania is considered necessary to begin working toward Time Crystals as early as possible.

Role in the Unicorn Era

Megalomania is the catalyst for transitioning from simple unicorn gathering to a full Time Crystal engine. Once purchased, you can refine Unicorn Tears into Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS); five units of BLS allow you to build your first Black Pyramid, which officially begins the process of trading with Leviathans for the temporal resources we discussed previously, such as Anachronomancy and Chronospheres.
