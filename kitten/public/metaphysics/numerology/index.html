Numerology is an early-to-mid Metaphysics upgrade under the Science -> Metaphysics tab that ties your village’s production to the calendar’s celestial cycle.

Cost & Prerequisites

  • Tab: Science $\to$ Metaphysics
  • Cost: 16 Paragon
  • Prerequisites: Unlocked after researching Megamania.
  • Tree Position: Serves as the gateway requirement for downstream temporal metaphysics upgrades: Numeromancy (50 Paragon) and Carnivals / Pawgan calendar enhancements.

What It Does: Celestial Cycle Modifiers

The game’s calendar progresses through 10 celestial cycles, each lasting 5 calendar years (a full cycle loop takes 50 game years).

Without Numerology, these cycle signs are purely cosmetic. Once Numerology is purchased, each celestial sign grants a specific $+35%$ production bonus to a designated resource, paired with minor penalties to others:

Celestial Sign Primary Boost (+35%) Secondary Modifiers
Charon Catnip Winter penalties slightly harsher
Umber Coal Minor boost to fossil fuels
Kharon Iron Slight reduction to mineral gathering
Yarn Culture Boosts parchment/manuscript generation
Zeus Minerals Boosts baseline ground extraction
Hermes Trade Ratio Improves caravan transaction returns
Ares Catpower / Hunting Higher Fur and Ivory yields
Helios Solar & Faith Boosts Solar Farms and base Faith accumulation
Tithonus Wood Improves logging and beam production
Ananke Oil Speeds up oil wells and fuel conversion

Why It Matters

  1. Cycle Awareness: During early and mid-game runs, aligning your big build phases with beneficial signs (e.g., waiting for Kharon to mass-forge Steel or Ares for major hunt batches) provides free efficiency boosts.
  2. Gateway to Numeromancy: Its primary long-term importance is unlocking Numeromancy (50 Paragon), which applies similar cycling percentage multipliers to Space missions, deep-space projects, and festival durations.
  3. Synergy with Time Shattering: In the late game, when combusting Time Crystals via Resource Retrieval, you skip through years instantly. The cycle modifiers cycle rapidly, evening out into a solid, passive net boost across all resources.

When to Buy It

Buy it around Runs 3–5, after you have secured your baseline 150–200 banked Paragon floor and picked up the first two price-ratio discounts (Golden Ratio and Divine Proportion).

At only 16 Paragon, it is a cheap pickup that paves the way toward late-game temporal metaphysics.
