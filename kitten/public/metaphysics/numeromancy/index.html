Numeromancy is a late-game Metaphysics upgrade that grants your civilisation unique production bonuses during Festivals based on the current active Cycle of the Cath solar system.

Key Statistics and Requirements

  • Cost: 250 Paragon.
  • Prerequisite: Numerology (which costs 50 Paragon and applies cycle-based effects to space production).
  • Unlocks: The Malkuth metaphysics upgrade.

Festival Effects by Cycle

When a Festival is active, Numeromancy applies the following multipliers depending on the current 5-year cycle:

Cycle Resource Bonus (x) Primary Focus
Charon 1.5x Catnip, Wood, Minerals
Umbra 1.5x Coal, Iron, Titanium, Gold
Yarn 2.0x Culture
Helios 2.0x / 1.25x Faith / Unicorns
Cath 2.0x Catpower
Redmoon 2.0x Unobtainium
Dune 2.0x Uranium
Piscine 2.0x Science
Terminus 2.0x Oil
Kairo 5.0x Starcharts

Strategic Synergies

The most powerful application of Numeromancy is during the Redmoon cycle to resolve the Unobtainium bottleneck.

  • The Redmoon Engine: By running a festival during the Redmoon cycle, you receive a base 2x boost to Unobtainium income. When combined with other endgame bonuses, this can reach nearly 2.5x total production.
  • Brewery Scaling: Each Brewery you construct increases the “Festival Cycle Bonus” by 1%. For example, having 50 breweries would increase the Redmoon festival bonus from 2.0x to 2.5x.
  • Temporal Arbitrage: Experienced players use a Chronoforge to “shatter” Time Crystals, skipping less profitable years to land precisely on the Redmoon cycle and maximise the Numeromancy festival bonus.

Why It Matters

Numeromancy is essential for the mid-to-late game transition (Phase 3 and 4). It transforms Festivals from simple happiness-boosting events into high-leverage tools for resource generation. This is particularly vital for reaching Transcendence Tier 15, as the faith income bonus in the Helios cycle and the Unobtainium boost in Redmoon accelerate the accumulation of Time Crystals and Relics.
