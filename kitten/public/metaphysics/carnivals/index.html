Metaphysics: Carnivals The Carnivals upgrade is an essential step in the temporal and religious Metaphysics tree. • Cost: 25 Paragon. • Primary Effect: It allows your Festivals to stack in duration. Without this, clicking “Hold a Festival” while one is already active simply wastes resources; with Carnivals, the time is added to the remaining duration. • Progression: It is the direct prerequisite for Numerology. It is also considered a mandatory part of the path to Order of the Void and reaching high Transcendence Tiers in the late game.
