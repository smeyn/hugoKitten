Unicornmancy is a mid-to-late-game Metaphysics upgrade that increases the frequency of rare unicorn-related astronomical events. It is a critical component for players entering the “Unicorn Era”, as it accelerates the acquisition of Unicorns and Ivory, which are the foundational resources for generating Time Crystals and Relics.

Acquisition and Cost

To access Unicornmancy, you must first research the Metaphysics technology (requiring 5 Unobtainium) to unlock the Metaphysics purchase menu.

  • Paragon Cost: 125 Paragon.
  • Strategic Timing: Sources recommend purchasing Unicornmancy once you have secured your primary price-reduction upgrades (up to Renaissance) and are ready to begin serious progression into time manipulation.

Primary Effects

The upgrade provides a 10% increase to the daily chance of the following phenomena:

  • Unicorn Rifts: Random events enabled by Ivory Towers that grant a bulk amount of unicorns.
  • Ivory Meteors: Random events enabled by Ivory Citadels that provide a burst of ivory.

Synergies and Scaling

The benefits of Unicornmancy can be significantly amplified by late-game infrastructure:

  • Blazars: Each level of this Cryptotheology building improves the effectiveness of Unicornmancy by 2.5%.
  • Ivory Infrastructure: Because the frequency of Rifts and Ivory Meteors is fundamentally tied to the number of Ivory Towers and Ivory Citadels you possess, Unicornmancy becomes exponentially more valuable as your religious infrastructure grows.
  • Solar Revolution: While Unicornmancy increases the frequency of events, the Solar Revolution bonus is one of the only multipliers that boosts the actual production of unicorn resources.

Strategic Importance

In the late game, Ivory often becomes a major bottleneck because its production does not benefit from standard paragon or building bonuses. By increasing the frequency of Ivory Meteors, Unicornmancy provides a necessary “AFK” source of ivory. This allows you to build the vast numbers of Ziggurats required to refine Unicorn Tears and eventually trade with Leviathans for the resources needed to reach the “Infinity” stage of the game.
