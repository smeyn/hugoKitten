The ten time cycles in Kittens Game, which are named after space missions, each have a unique astronomical symbol or glyph that appears to the left of your current year.

Here are the glyphs for each of the cycles:

  1. Charon: ⍙
  2. Umbra: ⍦
  3. Yarn: ⍧
  4. Helios: ⌒
  5. Cath: ⌾
  6. Redmoon: ⍜
  7. Dune: ⍫
  8. Piscine: ⎈
  9. Terminus (T-minus): ⍝
  10. Kairo: ℣

Understanding the Glyph Colors

Each cycle lasts for five years. The active glyph will dynamically change color to let you know exactly which year of the current cycle you are in:

  • 🔴 Red: Year 0
  • 🟡 Orange-Yellow: Year 1
  • 🟢 Green: Year 2
  • 🔵 Blue: Year 3
  • 🟣 Violet: Year 4

Once you research the Numerology and Numeromancy metaphysics upgrades, these cycles will actively alter your space structures’ production rates and grant powerful, unique bonuses whenever you hold festivals.
