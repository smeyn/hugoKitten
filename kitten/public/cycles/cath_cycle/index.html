The Cath cycle is the fifth of ten cycles in the Kittens Game temporal system, following the Helios cycle and preceding the Redmoon cycle. Each cycle lasts for five game years (20,000 ticks), which translates to approximately 1 hour, 6 minutes, and 40 seconds of real-time.

Indicators and Identification

  • Glyph: The cycle is identified by the ⌾ symbol appearing to the left of the year in the calendar.
  • Color Coding: The color of the glyph changes to denote the specific year within the five-year Cath cycle: Red (Year 0), Orange-Yellow (Year 1), Green (Year 2), Blue (Year 3), and Violet (Year 4).

Resource Multipliers (Requires Numerology)

If you have researched the Numerology metaphysics upgrade, the Cath cycle provides specific bonuses and penalties to space-based production:

  • Space Elevators: Provides a +100% bonus to the transferred production bonus, specifically for Oil.
  • Satellites: Provides a +100% bonus to starchart production and a +100% bonus to the Satellite observatory science bonus.
  • Space Stations: Provides a +50% bonus to science production.
  • Space Beacons: Imposes a -50% penalty to starchart production.

Festival Effects (Requires Numeromancy)

When the Numeromancy metaphysics upgrade is researched, holding a Festivals during the Cath cycle grants a unique global modifier:

  • Catpower Boost: All Catpower income is doubled (x2) for the duration of the festival. This effect can be further enhanced by building Breweries.

Strategic Utility

In the late game, players often use the Chronoforge to “shatter” Time Crystals, skipping through less desirable cycles to reach those that benefit their current bottleneck. While Redmoon is often the primary target for Unobtainium farming, the Cath cycle is strategically valuable for players needing to maximize Oil transfer via Space Elevators or those looking to quickly cap Science through boosted Satellites and Space Stations. Additionally, the doubled Catpower income from festivals makes Cath an ideal time for mass trading or hunting.
