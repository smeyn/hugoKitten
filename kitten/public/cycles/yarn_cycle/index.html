The Yarn cycle is one of the ten five-year cycles in the Kittens Game time system. Each cycle is associated with a specific glyph and provides various production modifiers and festival bonuses once certain late-game technologies are researched.

Cycle Mechanics and Effects

The effects of the Yarn cycle become active only after researching specific Metaphysics technologies:

  • Numerology Effect: When Numerology is researched, the Yarn cycle provides a +100% boost to catnip production from Hydroponics. Conversely, it imposes a -50% penalty to starchart production from Research Vessels.
  • Numeromancy Effect: If Numeromancy is researched, holding a festival during the Yarn cycle results in doubled culture income (x2).
  • Timing: As the third cycle in the sequence, it begins at Year 10 of a millennium and repeats every 50 years (Years 10–14, 60–64, etc.).

Space Exploration Context

The cycle is named after the planet Yarn, a Class M planet with a moderate climate and oxygen atmosphere.

  • Mission: The Yarn Mission is unlocked after the Helios mission and requires 7,500 starcharts, 35,000 titanium, 350,000 science, and 12,000 kerosene.
  • Planet Structures: Once reached, you can build Terraforming Stations (which increase your maximum kitten population by +1) and Hydroponics systems.
  • Hydroponics: These structures provide a +10% bonus to maximum catnip and a +2.5% boost to catnip production. During the Yarn cycle, this production bonus is significantly enhanced by the Numerology modifier.

Strategic Importance

In the extreme endgame, terraforming Yarn is a key strategy for maximizing kitten population. By building a massive number of Terraforming Stations, players can reach population levels high enough to generate “unreasonable” amounts of Paragon (up to 120,000 per RL-day) during long runs. Additionally, the cycle’s festival bonus makes it an ideal time to grind culture if you are still using it for early-to-mid-game crafts like Manuscripts or Compendiums.
