The Charon cycle is the first in the sequence of ten five-year cycles within the Kittens Game time system. Each cycle is associated with a specific glyph and provides production modifiers or festival bonuses once certain late-game Metaphysics technologies are researched.

Cycle Mechanics and Effects

The effects of the Charon cycle are only active after researching specific Metaphysics technologies:

  • Numerology Effects: Researching Numerology activates modifiers for space-based structures during this cycle:
    • Lunar Outposts: Unobtainium output is reduced by 10%.
    • Entanglement Stations: Gigaflops consumption is increased by 100%.
  • Numeromancy (Festival) Effects: If Numeromancy is researched, holding a festival during the Charon cycle provides a 1.5x multiplier to the income of three basic resources: Catnip, wood, and minerals.
  • Visual Indicator: The cycle is represented by the glyph ⍙. The color of this glyph changes annually to indicate progress through the five-year period: red (Year 0), orange-yellow (Year 1), green (Year 2), blue (Year 3), and violet (Year 4).

Relationship to Space Exploration

The cycle is named after the planet Charon, which is the destination of a high-tier late-game space mission.

  • Charon Mission: Unlocked after the Umbra mission, it requires 75,000 starcharts, 750,000 science, 35,000 kerosene, and 35,000 thorium.
  • Planet Charon: Described as an incredibly dense dwarf planet at the edge of the Helios system, sometimes called “The Gates of Death”.
  • Entanglement Station: Once Charon is reached, you can construct Entanglement Stations, which generate hashes for reverse-engineering relics but consume significant energy and gigaflops. As noted above, their gigaflops consumption is doubled during the Charon cycle.

Strategic Context

In the late game, the Charon cycle is generally considered a “fallow” period for industrial production, particularly because of the penalty to unobtainium output from Lunar Outposts. Advanced strategies for farming unobtainium often involve using a Chronoforge to skip through the Charon and Umbra cycles to reach more profitable years, such as those in the Redmoon cycle. Conversely, if a player is still in the early-to-mid game and needs to build up stocks of wood or minerals, the Numeromancy festival bonus makes Charon a useful time to run festivals.
