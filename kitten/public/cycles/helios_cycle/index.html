The Helios cycle is the fourth in the sequence of ten five-year cycles within the Kittens Game time system. Represented by the glyph ⌒, it repeats every 50 years, beginning at Year 15 of every millennium (e.g., Years 15–19, 65–69, etc.).

Cycle Mechanics and Effects

The modifiers for the Helios cycle are only activated once specific late-game Metaphysics technologies have been researched:

  • Numerology Effects: Researching Numerology activates modifiers for energy and space storage:
    • Sunlifter: Energy output is increased by +50%.
    • Cryostations: Storage capacity is reduced by -10%.
  • Numeromancy (Festival) Effects: If a festival is active during this cycle and Numeromancy is researched, your village receives significant bonuses to religion-based resources:
    • Faith income x2 (doubled).
    • Unicorn income x1.25.

Space Exploration Context

The cycle is named after the star Helios, a G2V spectral type star at the center of the Cath solar system. Reaching it requires the Helios Mission, which is unlocked after the Dune mission and costs 3,000 starcharts, 15,000 titanium, 250,000 science, and 1,250 kerosene. Completing this mission unlocks several critical late-game structures:

  • Sunlifter: Generates Antimatter (1 per year) and high energy (30 Watts base).
  • Containment Chamber: Increases maximum antimatter storage.
  • Heatsink: Enhances the storage capacity of Containment Chambers by +2% and increases their power consumption.
  • Sunforge: Provides a +1% bonus to the storage capacity of all base metals (iron, titanium, gold, uranium, and unobtainium).

Strategic Importance

  • Energy Maximisation: Because Sunlifters receive a +50% production boost during this cycle, it is one of the best times to run energy-intensive infrastructure, such as high numbers of AI Cores or Entanglement Stations.
  • Religion Farming: The doubled faith income from festivals makes the Helios cycle an ideal window for “Praising the Sun” to maximize the Solar Revolution bonus or for pushing toward higher Transcendence Tiers.
  • Storage Penalties: Players must be cautious of the -10% penalty to Cryostation storage. Since Cryostations are one of only two buildings that provide Unobtainium storage, this cycle can temporarily lower your resource cap, which may cause you to lose surplus unobtainium if you are near your maximum capacity.
  • Antimatter Production: While some early strategy guides consider Helios missions a low priority, acquiring Sunlifters is mandatory for entering the Relic era, as they are the primary source of the antimatter needed for Relic Stations.
