The Dune cycle is the seventh in the sequence of ten five-year cycles within the Kittens Game time system. Represented by the glyph ⍫, it repeats every 50 years (starting at Year 30 of a millennium, then Year 80, 130, etc.). Its primary function is to significantly boost industrial outputs related to the planet for which it is named.

Cycle Mechanics and Effects

Like other cycles, Dune’s effects are activated only after researching specific late-game Metaphysics technologies:

  • Numerology Effects: This technology activates modifiers for space-based structures located on the planet Dune and the Umbra black hole:
    • Planet Crackers: Uranium output is increased by +10%.
    • Hydraulic Fracturers: Oil output is increased by +50%.
    • HR Harvesters: Energy production is reduced by -25%.
  • Numeromancy (Festival) Effects: If a festival is held while the Dune cycle is active and Numeromancy is researched, your village receives a 2x multiplier (doubled income) for Uranium.

Relationship to Planet Dune

The cycle is tied to the Dune Mission, which is unlocked after the Moon Mission. Reaching Dune requires 1,000 starcharts, 7,000 titanium, 175,000 science, and 75 kerosene. Once reached, you can construct the structures affected by the cycle’s modifiers:

  • Planet Cracker: The primary source of automated Uranium production.
  • Hydraulic Fracturer: Provides a high-pressure stream of Oil.
  • Spice Refinery: Refines sand into Spice, a rare resource that improves happiness.

Strategic Importance

The Dune cycle is a critical window for resource management in the late mid-game and early space eras:

  • Uranium Farming: Uranium is essential for powering Reactors, funding Nuclear Smelters (for titanium), and fueling Lunar Outposts (to produce unobtainium). The 2x festival bonus makes this cycle the most efficient time to stockpile uranium.
  • Oil Stabilization: Because Hydraulic Fracturers receive a 50% production boost during this cycle, it is an ideal time to fill your oil caps to build expensive space structures like Research Vessels or Moon Bases.
  • Energy Management: Players heavily reliant on HR Harvesters for power (common in the deep late game) must be wary of the -25% energy penalty during this cycle, which could lead to an energy deficit and global production penalties.
  • Progression Flow: In the sequence of cycles, Dune acts as a mirrored opposite to the Umbra cycle, which penalizes Dune structure output while boosting Umbra structures. Shattering Time Crystals is often used to skip Umbra and land in cycles like Dune or Redmoon to maximize resource yield.
