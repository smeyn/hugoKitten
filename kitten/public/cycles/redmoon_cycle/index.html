The Redmoon cycle is the sixth phase in the ten-cycle system of Kittens Game and is widely considered the most strategically important period for late-game resource farming. Its primary purpose is to provide a massive multiplier to Unobtainium production, which is the critical bottleneck for space exploration and time manipulation.

The character used for the Redmoon cycle is the glyph ⍜.

Core Effects

The impact of the Redmoon cycle depends on your researched Metaphysics upgrades:

Upgrade Required Effect during Redmoon
Numerology +20% Unobtainium output from Lunar Outposts.
Numerology -50% Gigaflop consumption for Entanglement Stations.
Numeromancy Holding a festival provides an additional x2 Unobtainium income.

The “Shatter into Redmoon” Strategy

Because the Redmoon cycle is so much more productive than other cycles, endgame players use Time Crystals to skip less profitable years and land exactly on the Redmoon phase.

  • Timing: The first Redmoon cycle begins at Year 25 and lasts for five years (approximately 66.67 minutes of real-time play).
  • The Shatter Engine: By “shattering” (combusting) Time Crystals in the Chronoforge, players can advance the calendar instantly. It takes 45 skips to get from the end of one Redmoon cycle back to the start of the next one.
  • Maximising Returns: Strategic guides recommend staying in Redmoon as much as possible to fuel the “Type 0 Relic Engine”. When combined with Breweries, the festival bonus in Redmoon can reach nearly 2.5x total production.

Why It Matters for Progression

The Redmoon cycle is the foundation of the self-sustaining positive chrono-reset loop.

  • Unobtainium Accumulation: Players need billions of Unobtainium to build the 70+ Chronospheres required to carry over enough resources to make a reset profitable.
  • Arbitrage: By timing their most intensive production and trading (specifically with the Elders/Leviathans) during Redmoon, players can generate the massive amounts of Unobtainium and Time Crystals needed to reach the Endgame.
