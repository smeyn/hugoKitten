Metal Plates (often simply referred to as Plates) are a foundational mid-game craftable resource refined from iron, primarily used for expanding storage and established industrial infrastructure.

Crafting and Acquisition

  • Workshop Crafting: A single Metal Plate costs 125 Iron. Because the default iron storage limit is only 50, you must first expand your capacity using Barns or other storage buildings before you can craft them.
  • Trading with Zebras: Once you have discovered the Zebras, they provide a reliable alternative source for plates. There is a 65% base chance to receive 2 plates per successful trade. This probability can be increased to 100% by reaching Embassy level 54.
  • Automation: Researching the Pneumatic Press workshop upgrade allows Steamworks to automatically convert a portion of your iron into plates twice per year whenever your iron is near its storage limit.
  • Kitten Traits: Assigning a kitten with the Metallurgist trait as your Leader provides a +0.1 bonus to the craft ratio for plates.

Primary Uses

Metal plates are mandatory for progressing into the “Titanium Era” and establishing advanced energy systems:

  • Trade Ships: Building your first fleet requires 150 plates (along with scaffolds and starcharts). These ships are essential for discovering Zebras and beginning titanium acquisition.
  • Storage Buildings: They are a core component for Harbours (75 plates) and Mints (200 plates).
  • Industrial Structures: High-tier buildings require massive quantities, including Factories (2,500 plates) and Reactors (5,000 plates).
  • Religious Infrastructure: Each Temple requires 15 plates.
  • Workshop Upgrades: Several critical upgrades depend on plates, such as Reinforced Warehouses (50 plates) and Alloy Barns (750 plates).

Strategic Utility

In the early-to-mid game, plates are often a bottleneck for expanding storage. Strategic guides suggest that if your Coal production is too slow to craft Steel, you should instead focus your iron on crafting plates to build more Harbours. This increases your global storage caps, allowing you to eventually afford the more expensive upgrades and buildings.

Persistence and Resets

As a crafted resource, Metal Plates are normally lost upon resetting the game. To preserve a portion of your stockpile for the next run, you must possess at least one Chronosphere and the Flux Condensator workshop upgrade. The amount carried over is determined by the number of active Chronospheres and the square root of your total plate count at the time of reset.
