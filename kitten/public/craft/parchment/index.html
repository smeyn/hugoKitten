Parchment is a vital mid-game craftable resource in Kittens Game that serves as the foundation for your colony’s cultural and religious expansion. It is primarily used to construct culture-generating buildings and to craft more advanced intellectual resources.

Acquisition and Crafting

  • Unlocking: You gain the ability to craft parchment after researching the Writing technology (3,600 Science).
  • Workshop Crafting: A single unit of parchment is crafted from 175 Furs in the Workshop.
  • Trading: Once you reach Embassy level 5 with the Sharks, they have a 25% chance of selling you parchment in exchange for iron. This chance can be increased by +25% if you enact the Shark Relations: Scribes policy.
  • Automation: While there is no direct early-game automation for parchment itself, maximizing your Workshops provides a multiplicative boost to the number of parchments produced per craft.

Primary Uses

Parchment is a mandatory requirement for transitioning out of the early game and establishing a “cultural engine”:

  • Resource Conversion: It is the primary input for crafting Manuscripts. Each manuscript requires 25 parchment (reduced to 20 with the Tradition policy).
  • Building Construction: It is a required material for several essential structures:
    • Amphitheatres: 3 Parchment (essential for reducing unhappiness and generating early culture).
    • Chapels: 250 Parchment (the primary structure for generating faith).
    • Breweries: 375 Parchment (required for holding Festivals).
  • Scientific Research: A massive stockpile of 5,000 parchment is required to research Drama and Poetry, which enables the festival system.

Strategic Management

  • Early-Game Grind: Strategic guides recommend beginning to craft parchment as often as possible once the Workshop is unlocked, as you will need vast quantities for the “Theology hurdle”.
  • The “Hunter Dump” Strategy: To efficiently accumulate parchment before automation is available, players should temporarily reassign all kittens to the Hunter job. This allows you to mass-produce the furs necessary for a bulk parchment craft session.
  • Policy Synergy: Enacting the Tradition policy is highly recommended because it reduces the parchment cost for manuscripts and doubles their cultural effectiveness, significantly speeding up mid-game scientific progress.

Special Constraints

  • Pacifism Challenge: In this challenge, hunting is disabled. You cannot obtain furs, and therefore cannot craft parchment, until you build a Mint to generate a passive supply of luxury resources.
  • Iron Will: Since you lack kittens for standard jobs in this mode, your parchment supply depends entirely on the furs gathered by Zebras acting as passive hunters.
  • Persistence: As a crafted resource, parchment is lost upon resetting the game unless you possess Chronospheres and the Flux Condensator workshop upgrade.
