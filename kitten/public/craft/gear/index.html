Gears are a critical mid-to-late-game craftable resource in Kittens Game, functioning as an integral component for buildings and upgrades that increase automation and industrial efficiency.

Production and Acquisition

  • Recipe: One Gear is crafted in the Workshop using 15 Steel.
  • Prerequisites: You must first research the Steel science technology to unlock Gear crafting.
  • Crafting Efficiency: Kittens with the Metallurgist trait provide a specific +0.1 bonus to your craftRatio for Gears when assigned as the village leader.

Primary Building Requirements

Gears are a mandatory material for constructing several industrial and resource-gathering structures:

  • Steamworks: Requires 20 Gears.
  • Magnetos: Requires 5 Gears.
  • Oil Wells: Requires 25 Gears.

Key Workshop Upgrades

Gears are heavily utilized in the Workshop, particularly for upgrades that automate manual tasks or enhance industrial output:

  • Printing & Pressing: Upgrades like the Printing Press (45 Gears), Offset Press (250 Gears), and Photolithography (1,250 Gears) allow Steamworks to automatically generate manuscripts.
  • Industrial Automation: Workshop Automation (25 Gears) and Advanced Automation (75 Gears) enable Steamworks to automatically craft slabs and beams.
  • Production Boosts: Upgrades such as Steel Plants (750 Gears) and Rotary Kiln (500 Gears) improve the efficiency of Calciners.
  • Environmental & Efficiency: Carbon Sequestration (125 Gears) reduces factory pollution, while Robotic Assistance (10,000 Gears) significantly reduces kitten catnip consumption.

Strategic Importance

Gears represent the transition from manual resource management to automated industrial cycles. Because Gears are crafted from Steel (which is itself a refined resource), it is highly recommended to maximize your Workshops and Factories to achieve a high craft ratio. This ensures you produce multiple Gears for the same amount of Steel, which is vital for overcoming the “Theology hurdle” and establishing a robust industrial base.
