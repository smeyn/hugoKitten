Alloy is a crucial craftable resource in Kittens Game, marking a mid-to-late game phase of infrastructure development.

Resource Details

Alloy is a composite material refined from other metals and is categorised as a craftable resource.

Detail Specification
Prerequisite Technology Chemistry, which itself requires Physics.
Crafting Cost 75 Steel and 10 Titanium.
Unlocks The crafting of Alloy is unlocked by the Chemistry technology.

Uses and Strategic Importance

Alloy is necessary for constructing advanced buildings, structures, and various Workshop upgrades:

  1. Buildings:
    • Bio Lab: Requires 25 Alloy and 100 Slab to build, significantly boosting Science production and storage.
    • Magneto: Requires 10 Alloy, 5 Gear, and 1 Blueprint to construct, providing a crucial +2% Global Production boost and generating 5 Energy.
  2. Workshop Upgrades: Alloy is the prerequisite material for several high-tier upgrades:
    • Alloy Saw: Requires Alloy and improves Lumber Mill efficiency by $+15%$.
    • Alloy Axe: Requires Alloy and makes Woodcutters $50%$ more effective.
    • Alloy Barns/Warehouses: Required for various storage upgrades.
    • Alloy Armour/Nanosuits: Required for advanced Hunting bonuses.

Progression in the “Alloy Era”

The point where Alloy becomes readily available signals a major checkpoint in the game’s progression.

  • Technology Path: Getting to the Alloy era requires a specific scientific path: Navigation $\to$ Physics $\to$ Chemistry.
  • Resource Bottlenecks: Alloy requires substantial amounts of Titanium, which must be acquired primarily through trading slabs with Zebras. This titanium requirement mandates ongoing construction of Trade Ships and Observatories (to acquire Starcharts).
  • Strategic Focus: Once Alloy is unlocked, strategic focus shifts to purchasing Alloy buildings and the associated upgrades to boost storage and production. The next major goal after the Alloy era is typically achieving Concrete Huts.
  • Trait Bonus: Kittens with the Metallurgist trait, when assigned as a leader, provide a specific bonus to metal crafting efficiency, increasing the craft ratio for resources including Alloy by $+0.1$.

Finally, Alloy is also a component, alongside Unobtainium, in crafting the late-game resource Eludium.
