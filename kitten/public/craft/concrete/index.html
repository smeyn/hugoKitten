Concrete is a critical mid-to-late-game craftable resource used primarily for advanced infrastructure, storage expansion, and space exploration. Its acquisition marks the beginning of the “Concrete Era,” which is a major milestone in a player’s first run.

Production and Unlocking

  • Prerequisite: You must first research the Mechanization science technology (115K Science, 45 Blueprints) to unlock the ability to craft concrete and assign Engineers to automate its production.
  • Recipe: Crafting one unit of concrete requires 2,500 Slabs and 25 Steel.
  • Trait Bonus: Kittens with the Chemist trait provide a specific bonus to crafting efficiency for concrete when assigned as a leader.

Major Upgrades

Concrete is essential for several Workshop upgrades that significantly boost village capacity:

  • Concrete Huts: Reduces the price ratio of Huts by 0.3 (30%), allowing for a much larger kitten population.
  • Concrete Pillars: Increases the maximum storage of wood, minerals, and iron in Barns and Warehouses by 5%, and gold/titanium by 5%.
  • Concrete Barns: Increases the storage capacity of Barns by 75%.
  • Concrete Warehouses: Increases the storage capacity of Warehouses by 35%.

Construction Uses

Many advanced buildings and space structures require concrete as a primary material:

  • Industrial/Science: Factories (15), Reactors (50), Accelerators (125), Hydro Plants (100), and Data Centers (10).
  • Space Infrastructure: Lunar Outposts (150), Moon Bases (250), and Cryostations (1,500).

Strategic Importance

The “Concrete Era” is often the target for a player’s first reset. Strategy guides generally recommend pushing until you can afford Concrete Huts and reach at least 130 kittens. This ensures you gain at least 60 Paragon, providing a substantial production and storage boost for your next run. To reach the 125K science cap required for Concrete Huts, players often research Biology to build Bio Labs.
