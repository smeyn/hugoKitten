A Wooden Beam (often simply called a Beam) is a foundational mid-game craftable resource in Kittens Game refined from wood. It serves as a primary support structure required for advanced construction and the development of the colony’s industrial and religious infrastructure.

Acquisition and Production

  • Workshop Crafting: A single wooden beam costs 175 Wood. Because this cost is relatively low compared to later-game items, players often mass-craft beams once their wood storage is expanded.
  • Trading with Lizards: Once you reach Embassy level 5 with the Lizards, they have a 15% chance of selling you 10 beams in exchange for 1,000 minerals.
  • Automation: Researching Workshop Automation allows Steamworks to automatically convert a portion of your wood into beams once per year whenever your wood reaches 98% of its storage cap. This frequency increases to twice per year with the Advanced Automation upgrade.

Primary Uses in Construction

Beams are a mandatory material for several critical buildings and higher-tier resources:

  • Storage Expansion: They are required to build Warehouses (1.5 beams each), which are essential for increasing the global caps for multiple resources.
  • Mega Structures: Beams are a component for crafting Megaliths (25 beams each), which are used to build Ziggurats and other religious structures.
  • Naval Infrastructure: They are the primary input for crafting Scaffolds (50 beams each), which are needed for building Trade Ships and Harbours.

Workshop Upgrades

Several essential Workshop upgrades require a stockpile of beams to improve the colony’s efficiency:

  • Reinforced Barns (25 Beams): Increases the storage space for wood, minerals, and iron by 80%.
  • Coal Furnace (2,000 Beams): Allows Smelters to produce coal while burning wood.
  • Deep Mining (1,200 Beams): Allows Mines to produce a small amount of coal.

Strategic Utility and Resets

In the early-to-mid game, beams are often the first major “crafting hurdle” players face as they transition from raw wood collection to organised manufacturing. Strategic guides suggest that once the Construction science is researched, players should prioritise building enough warehouses to raise their storage caps for iron and science.

As a crafted resource, wooden beams are normally lost upon resetting the game. To carry them over into a new run, a player must possess at least one Chronosphere and the Flux Condensator Workshop upgrade. The amount preserved follows a square root formula based on the total quantity held at the time of reset.
