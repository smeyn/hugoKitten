Bloodstone is classified as an uncommon resource in Kittens Game that plays a role in late-game crafting and unlocks specific buildings associated with the Zebras,. It is poetically described as a strange jewelry claimed to be made of time and a blood of elder gods.

Acquisition Methods

Bloodstone can be acquired through hunting and crafted using advanced resources:

  1. Hunting: Bloodstone has a chance to be found when engaging in hunting, provided you have at least 10 Zebras,. In the Iron Will challenge mode, the chance to yield a bloodstone during a hunt is 0.05% if you already have it. However, this chance increases significantly to 5% if you do not already have bloodstone.

  2. Crafting: Bloodstone is a craftable item unlocked by the Zebra Forge. The crafting cost is high, requiring temporal and elder resources: 5,000 Time Crystal and 10,000 Relic.

Uses and Buildings

Bloodstone is a prerequisite resource for several Zebra structures and other craftable materials:

  • Zebra Outpost: Requires 1 Bloodstone.
  • Zebra Workshop: Requires 5 Bloodstone.
  • Zebra Forge: Requires 50 Bloodstone. The Zebra Forge itself unlocks the ability to craft Bloodstone, as well as T-Mythril.
  • T-Mythril: Bloodstone is a component in crafting T-Mythril,.

Persistence

As a craftable resource, Bloodstone is retained across game resets, but only conditionally. It is carried over if the player has Chronospheres built and the Flux Condensator workshop upgrade researched. The amount carried over is subject to diminishing returns.
