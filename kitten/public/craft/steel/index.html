Steel is a mid-game technology and a critical craftable resource in Kittens Game, marking a major step toward industrialization and advanced building construction.

Here is a comprehensive overview of Steel based on the sources:

Steel Technology

The technology named Steel has the following characteristics:

  • Prerequisite: It is unlocked after researching Writing.
  • Cost: Researching Steel requires 12,000 Science.
  • Strategic Timing: Steel is one of the three “specialist Techs” available after Writing (alongside Philosophy and Machinery). Strategic advice suggests researching Steel first among these three options to acquire useful upgrades that strengthen your economy.

Steel Resource (Crafting and Composition)

Steel is a Craftable Resource.

  • Composition: Steel is made by combining 100 Iron and 100 Coal.
  • Acquisition: The research of the Steel technology enables the resource Steel.
  • Trading: Steel can also be acquired through trade, notably from Griffins, who sell 25 Steel per trade once you reach Embassy Level 5. Griffins buy Wood.

Key Unlocks and Upgrades

Researching Steel unlocks critical Workshop Upgrades, many of which focus on resource efficiency and early Coal production:

Upgrade Name Cost Components (Examples) Primary Effect
Coal Furnace Science, Minerals, Iron, Beam Allows Smelters to produce 0.005 Coal per tick while burning wood.
Deep Mining Science, Iron, Beam Allows Mines to produce 0.003 Coal per tick.
Steel Axe Science, Steel Makes Woodcutters 50% more effective.
Reinforced Warehouses Science, Plate, Steel, Scaffold Increases resource storage in Warehouses, Barns, Harbours, and Mints by 25%.
Steel Armour Science, Steel Provides a +0.5 Hunting bonus.
Steel Saw Science, Steel Improves Lumber Mill efficiency by 20%.
High Pressure Engine Science, Gear, Blueprint Reduces Steamworks coal consumption by 20%.

Usage in Buildings and Crafting

Steel is crucial for moving beyond basic construction into industrial and specialized buildings:

  • Advanced Materials: Steel is required for crafting Gears.
  • Industrial Buildings:
    • The Steamworks building requires 65 Steel (along with 20 Gear and 1 Blueprint).
    • The Quarry building requires 125 Steel.
    • The Calciner building requires 100 Steel.
  • Housing: It is required for the mid-game Mansion building (75 Steel).
  • Crafting: Steel is a component of Concrete, which is crafted from 25 Steel and 2500 Slab.

Strategic Notes

  • Early Coal: Steel is essential because it unlocks the upgrades (Coal Furnace and Deep Mining) necessary to begin accumulating Coal. Coal is required to craft Steel itself, and to power industrial structures like the Steamworks.
  • Iron Priority: Once Steel is researched and the coal-producing upgrades are acquired, players are often advised to let Coal production max out. This is because the Iron used to produce Coal is highly valuable and better prioritized for building Lumber Mills and Observatories.
  • Trade Importance: Trading with Zebras yields Iron, which can then be converted into Steel. This trade route helps maintain a supply of Steel, especially once it becomes a bottleneck for future buildings.
