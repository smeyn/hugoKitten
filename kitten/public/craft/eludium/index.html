Eludium is an extremely rare and expensive high-tier crafted resource in Kittens Game, described as a composite of Unobtainium and Alloy.

Acquisition and Production

  • Unlocking: Eludium is unlocked through the Exometallurgy science (formerly known as Advanced Exogeology).
  • Crafting Recipe: Crafting one unit of eludium requires 1,000 Unobtainium and 2,500 Alloy.
  • Production Multipliers: The yield is significantly boosted by your overall Workshop craft ratio. Furthermore, kittens with the Chemist trait provide a specific crafting bonus (+0.075) for eludium, and the Antimatter Fission workshop upgrade makes Engineers 25% more effective at producing it.

Strategic Infrastructure and Buildings

Eludium is a critical component for late-game population scaling and advanced industrial structures:

  • Eludium Huts: This bonfire upgrade reduces the price ratio of Huts by 10%, allowing for much higher populations. While powerful, some strategic guides note that the 125 eludium cost may only provide a marginal (~1%) increase in paragon-per-hour efficiency in very short runs.
  • Cryostation: These supermassive storage facilities cost 25 eludium and are essential for reaching the high resource caps required for the deep endgame.
  • Spaceport: Eludium is required to build Spaceports, which enhance the capacity of other space structures like Moon Bases and Cryostations.
  • Sunlifter: Each Sunlifter costs 225 eludium and is the primary structure used to generate Antimatter.
  • Entanglement Station: These late-game structures require 5,000 eludium each and are used for quantum computing and reverse-engineering relics.

Workshop and Advanced Upgrades

Many high-tier upgrades are gated by eludium costs:

  • Eludium Reflectors: This upgrade increases your maximum Science capacity by +2% for every Observatory you own.
  • Microwarp Reactors: Costing 50 eludium, this is a vital upgrade that increases the Unobtainium production of Lunar Outposts by 75%.
  • Scientific Utility: It is required for several advanced workshop techs including Cryocomputing (Data Center efficiency), Machine Learning, Antimatter Reactors, and Chronoengineers.

Preservation and Reset Strategy

  • Preservation: Eludium is a crafted resource and can be carried over through game resets if you have Chronospheres and the Flux Condensator upgrade.
  • Diminishing Returns: The amount preserved follows a square root formula: $1.5* {number of Chronospheres}* sqrt(current eludium amount)$.
  • Efficiency Tipping Point: A common strategy for rapid resets is to stockpile 1,111 eludium before resetting with one Chronosphere. This ensures you start the next run with the 50 eludium needed to immediately purchase Microwarp Reactors and accelerate your Unobtainium production.
