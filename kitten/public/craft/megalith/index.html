Megaliths are a mid-to-late game craftable resource used primarily for the construction of megastructures and the advancement of the unicorn religion.

Acquisition and Crafting

  • Workshop Crafting: Once the prerequisite technologies are met, a single megalith can be crafted using 50 Slabs, 25 Beams, and 5 Plates.
  • Trading with Nagas: Megaliths can also be obtained through trade with the Nagas. This requires 500 Ivory per trade and has a 10% chance of yielding a megalith, provided the player has reached an Embassy level of 15.

Primary Use: Ziggurats

The most critical application of megaliths is building Ziggurats, which cost 50 Megaliths, 50 Scaffolds, and 1 Blueprint each.

  • Unlocks: Ziggurats are the gateway to the unicorn religion system, enabling the sacrifice of unicorns to obtain Unicorn Tears.
  • Culture Boost: Each Ziggurat provides a +8% increase to maximum culture.
  • Visibility: While unlocked by the Construction technology, the Ziggurat will not appear in the Bonfire tab until the player possesses approximately 5 Megaliths, 5 Scaffolds, and 1 Blueprint.

Endgame and Religious Structures

In the late game, megaliths are a mandatory component for advanced structures within the Cryptotheology and unicorn trees:

  • Sky Palace: 5 Megaliths.
  • Marker: 750 Megaliths.
  • Unicorn Graveyard: 1,000 Megaliths.
  • Unicorn Necropolis: 2,500 Megaliths.
  • Black Pyramid: 2,500 Megaliths.

Strategic Considerations

Strategic guides recommend building a significant number of Ziggurats (ideally 25–30) before heavily investing in unicorn sacrifices, as the number of tears received per sacrifice is directly determined by the total number of Ziggurats owned.

Persistence and Resets

As a crafted resource, megaliths are normally lost upon resetting the game. To carry them over into a new run, a player must have at least one Chronosphere and the Flux Condensator Workshop upgrade. The amount preserved follows a square root formula based on the total quantity held before the reset.
