Manuscripts are a vital mid-game craftable resource that serves as a sum of modern knowledge for your kittens, acting as a primary gate for advanced scientific research and cultural expansion.

Crafting and Acquisition

  • Unlocked By: You gain the ability to craft manuscripts after researching the Writing technology.
  • Standard Cost: Each manuscript costs 25 Parchment and 400 Culture.
  • Policy Efficiency: By enacting the Tradition policy, the cost is reduced to 20 Parchment and 300 Culture, and the manuscript’s effect on maximum culture is doubled.
  • Trading: Manuscripts can be obtained through trade with the Sharks (15% chance) once you have an Embassy level of at least 10.

Automation and Scaling

The “Theology hurdle”—the period where you must grind manuscripts manually—is a notorious progression bottleneck. To overcome this, players typically rely on the Steamworks building for automated production:

  • Printing Press: This workshop upgrade allows Steamworks to automatically print 0.0025 manuscripts per second.
  • Offset Press: Increases Steamworks production to 0.01 per second.
  • Photolithography: Further boosts production to 0.04 per second.

Primary Uses

Manuscripts are mandatory for transitioning into the religious and celestial eras of the game:

  • Technology Requirements: They are required to research Theology (35), Astronomy (65), and Navigation (100).
  • Building Construction: Each Temple requires 10 manuscripts to build.
  • Resource Conversion: They are the base material for crafting Compendiums, requiring 50 manuscripts each.
  • Culture Boost: Owning manuscripts provides a minor bonus to your maximum culture cap, though this effect suffers from diminishing returns.

Strategic Advice

  • Grinding Tactic: Before automation is available, the most efficient way to acquire manuscripts is to assign all kittens to the Hunter job to mass-produce parchment, then wait for culture to max out before using the “craft all” manuscript button.
  • Metaphysics Synergy: The Codex Vox upgrade adds a +25% bonus to your craft ratio for manuscripts and provides a 1.05x multiplier to all manuscript crafting.
  • Religious Pivot: If you enact the Lizard Relations: [Priests] ( /job/priest/) policy, manuscripts will grant maximum faith instead of a portion of the maximum culture they usually provide.
