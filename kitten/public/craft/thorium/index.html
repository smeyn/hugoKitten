Thorium is a high-tier, highly radioactive craftable resource in Kittens Game that serves as a critical fuel for late-game energy production and deep-space exploration. It is primarily used to power advanced reactors and fund missions to the furthest reaches of the star system.

Acquisition and Production

  • Research Requirements: To unlock Thorium, you must research the Thorium science node, which costs 375,000 Science and 375 Blueprints. This technology becomes available after researching Orbital Engineering.
  • Workshop Crafting: Once researched, Thorium can be crafted in the Workshop at a base cost of 250 Uranium per unit.
  • Trading: You can also acquire Thorium by trading with Dragons. They have a 50% chance to sell Thorium in exchange for titanium, provided you have reached Embassy level 5.
  • Crafting Bonuses: Assigning a kitten with the Chemist trait as your Leader provides a +0.075 bonus to the craft ratio for Thorium.

Energy and Infrastructure Upgrades

Thorium is central to scaling your energy grid and scientific capacity through several key Workshop upgrades:

  • Thorium Reactors: This upgrade increases the energy output of your Reactors by 25%, but introduces a consumption rate of 0.05 Thorium per tick (0.25 per second) per active reactor.
  • Enriched Thorium: This vital efficiency upgrade reduces the Thorium consumption of your reactors by 0.0625 per tick, helping to stabilise your stockpiles.
  • Quantum Dot Cells: Costs 175k Science, 200 Eludium, and 1000 Thorium. It improves Solar Farm energy production by 15% in winter and 30% in spring and autumn.
  • Thorium Drive: A high-tier engine that divides space travel time by 50 (or 75 if combined with an Antimatter Drive).

Space Missions and Structures

As you progress into the late-game “Umbra” and “Centaurus” eras, Thorium becomes a mandatory requirement for exploration:

  • Missions: Significant quantities are required for missions to Umbra (15,000), Charon (35,000), Centaurus (50,000), and the Furthest Ring (75,000).
  • Heatsinks: Essential for increasing antimatter storage, each Heatsink costs 12,500 Thorium.
  • Tectonics: These space structures, which generate 25 Watts of energy, cost 75,000 Thorium each.

Strategic Utility

  • The Antimatter Seed: In long “seed runs” aimed at reaching a 5,000 antimatter capacity, Thorium is often a major bottleneck. Reaching the milestone of 34 Heatsinks requires over 526,000 Thorium.
  • Energy Management: Strategic guides recommend turning off Thorium Reactors during most of the run to conserve fuel, only activating them when time skipping or when a massive energy surplus is required for antimatter generation.
  • Preservation: As a crafted resource, Thorium is normally lost upon resetting. To carry it over into a new run, you must possess Chronospheres and the Flux Condensator Workshop upgrade. The amount preserved is determined by the square root of your total Thorium at the time of reset.
