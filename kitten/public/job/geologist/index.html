Geologists are a specialized job you can assign to your kittens that primarily provides a steady source of coal, and later, gold.

Acquisition and Prerequisites

  • Unlock: The Geologist job becomes available once you research the Geology science technology.
  • Cost: Reaching Geology requires progressing past the Navigation technology and saving up 65,000 Science and 65 Compendiums. Because of the high science capacity required, it is generally considered a mid-game job.

Core Production

  • Base Output: A Geologist natively produces +0.015 coal per tick.
  • Strategic Use: Early in the mid-game, coal can be a major bottleneck (especially since active Steamworks consume it heavily), making Geologists an excellent way to increase coal income without relying solely on Smelters or Mines. If you lack coal for vital mid-game structures like Oil Wells, assigning more Geologists is a standard strategy.

Upgrades and Boosts

Geologists can be significantly improved through specific Workshop upgrades, effectively turning them into the backbone of your late-game trade economy:

  • Geodesy: Arguably the most important upgrade for this job. Costing 90,000 Science, 250 Titanium, and 500 Starcharts, Geodesy increases coal output by +0.0075 per tick and allows Geologists to passively generate gold at a rate of +0.0008 per tick (or +0.004 per second). Once you reach the Space phase, you will likely assign the vast majority of your kittens as Geologists, using their gold production to fuel massive trades with Zebras for titanium and iron.
  • Mining Drill: Costs 100k Science, 1,750 Titanium, and 750 Steel. It increases Geologist coal production by +0.01 per tick, and gold production by +0.0005 per tick (provided you have already researched Geodesy).
  • Unobtainium Drill: Costs 250k Science, 250 Unobtainium, and 1,250 Alloy. It adds another +0.015 coal per tick and +0.0005 gold per tick.
