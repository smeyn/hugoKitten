[Priests] ( /job/priest/) are a specialized job for your kittens that becomes available after you research the Theology science technology. Their primary role is to generate Faith, which is the fundamental resource used in the game’s Religion tab.

Core Production

  • Base Output: A kitten assigned as a Priest natively produces 0.0015 faith per tick.

Strategic Importance: The Late Game

While [Priests] ( /job/priest/) are introduced in the mid-game, they become arguably the most important job assignment in the deep late game. As you progress into the Relic and Unicorn eras, most conventional resources matter less, and your primary goal shifts to maximizing your global production via the Solar Revolution religion upgrade.

To fuel this, you will eventually assign almost all of your kittens as [Priests] ( /job/priest/). The massive amounts of faith they generate are continually converted into Worship (by using the “Praise the Sun” action), which can exponentially boost your entire civilization’s production by up to +1000%.

Upgrades and Boosts

You can improve the effectiveness of your [Priests] ( /job/priest/) through specific upgrades:

  • Solar Chant: This religion upgrade increases your total faith production by +10%. You can purchase additional levels of Solar Chant after you unlock Transcendence, with each level granting another +10% boost.
  • Order of the Void: This Metaphysics upgrade (which costs 75 Paragon) fundamentally changes how [Priests] ( /job/priest/) work. Once purchased, all of your [Priests] ( /job/priest/) (and any other faith-producing structures) will automatically generate an additional 10% of their faith output directly as Worship. This acts as a powerful passive engine for building your Solar Revolution bonus.
