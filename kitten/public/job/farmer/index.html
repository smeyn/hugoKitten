Farmers are one of the earliest specialized jobs you can assign to your kittens. Unlocked by researching Agriculture, they serve as your civilization’s primary defense against starvation during the early game.

Core Production and Mechanics

  • Base Output: A Farmer produces 1 catnip per tick (which equates to 5 catnip per second).
  • Net Gain: Because every working kitten natively consumes 0.85 catnip per tick, assigning a kitten as a Farmer yields a net gain of 0.15 catnip per tick.

Strategic Importance: Surviving Winter

The most critical attribute of Farmers is that their production is completely immune to seasonal and weather penalties. While your Catnip Fields will suffer a devastating production drop of up to -75% (or -90% in a Cold Winter), Farmers will continue producing at their full, regular rate. In the early stages of the game, a standard and vital strategy is to reallocate all or most of your kittens to the Farmer job just before Winter arrives to ensure your village does not starve.

As you construct more Catnip Fields, Pastures, and Aqueducts to bolster your passive catnip generation, you can gradually reduce your reliance on Farmers. Eventually, you may only need to keep a single kitten employed as a Farmer to maintain a positive food balance.

Upgrades and Boosts

You can increase the efficiency of your Farmers through several Workshop upgrades and buildings:

  • Mineral Hoes: Increases Farmer production by +50%.
  • Iron Hoes: Increases Farmer production by an additional +30%.
  • Note on Stacking: These two hoe upgrades stack additively with each other.
  • Aqueducts: Building an Aqueduct provides a +3% boost to your total catnip production, which directly scales the catnip generated by your Farmers.
  • Leadership: If you assign a Farmer as your Leader, their rank will provide a multiplier to your farming output, meaning you will need far fewer kittens working as Farmers to feed your population.
