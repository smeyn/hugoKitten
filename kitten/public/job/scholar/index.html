Scholars are a specialized job for your kittens that becomes available after you construct your first Library (which requires 25 Wood). Their primary function is to generate Science, which is essential for purchasing new technologies and unlocking Workshop upgrades.

Core Production

Each kitten actively assigned as a Scholar generates a base output of +0.035 Science per tick.

Upgrades and Late-Game Utility

While their main purpose is Science generation, Scholars gain a massive secondary utility in the late game through the Astrophysicists workshop upgrade.

  • Astrophysicists: Costing 250,000 Science and 350 Unobtainium, this upgrade enables each of your Scholar kittens to passively produce +0.0005 starcharts per second.

Because starcharts can become a major bottleneck for space exploration and ship building, reallocating extra kittens to the Scholar job is an effective late-game strategy to boost your starchart generation without relying solely on manual astronomical events.
