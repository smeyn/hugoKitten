Miners are a specialized job you can assign to your kittens, serving as your civilization’s primary workforce for generating Minerals.

Acquisition and Prerequisites

To employ Miners, you must first research the Mining science technology, which grants you the ability to construct a Mine. Building your first Mine officially unlocks the Miner job, while also providing a permanent +20% boost to your overall mineral production.

Core Production

Each kitten actively assigned as a Miner natively generates 0.05 minerals per tick.

Strategic Importance

Minerals are a fundamental resource necessary for purchasing and crafting a wide variety of advanced buildings and expansions for your village. When balancing your workforce during the early stages of the game, a highly recommended strategy is to employ about half as many Miners as you have Woodcutters. Because wood is required to build the Mines that boost your mineral output, maintaining this specific ratio typically results in a stable and equal production rate of both wood and minerals.
