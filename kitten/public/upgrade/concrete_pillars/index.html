Concrete Pillars is a Workshop upgrade that serves as the foundation for the “Concrete Era” of storage and housing. It is generally the first concrete-based upgrade players purchase after researching the Mechanization technology.

Costs and Requirements

  • Science Cost: 100,000 Science.
  • Resource Cost: 50 Concrete.
  • Prerequisite: You must research the Mechanization technology.

Core Effects

Unlike the massive +75% boost from Concrete Barns, this upgrade provides a smaller, universal reinforcement to your storage infrastructure:

  • General Storage: It increases the storage capacity of Barns, Warehouses, and Harbors by +5%.
  • Gold Storage: It increases the Max Gold capacity provided by Mints by +5%.
  • Catnip Synergy: If you have the Silos upgrade, the +5% bonus also applies to the Catnip storage of your Barns and Warehouses.

Strategic Context

While a 5% boost may seem small compared to other upgrades, Concrete Pillars is a critical stepping stone:

  • Gateway Upgrade: It is a direct prerequisite for unlocking Concrete Barns and Concrete Warehouses.
  • Path to Concrete Huts: It is part of the essential chain required to unlock Concrete Huts, which is the primary goal for maximizing your population before your first reset.
