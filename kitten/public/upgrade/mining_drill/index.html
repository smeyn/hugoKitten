In Kittens Game, the Mining Drill is a vital mid-game Workshop upgrade that significantly boosts your mineral production, which is essential as you begin to scale your industrial infrastructure.

Key Effects

The Mining Drill provides a massive efficiency boost to your Miners:

  • Miner Efficiency: It increases the effectiveness of your Miners by +20%.
  • Production Scaling: This is a multiplicative bonus that affects the base production of every kitten assigned to the Miner job. As you stack more global production bonuses (like Magnetos or Solar Revolution), this 20% increase becomes a significant source of raw materials.

Unlock Requirements & Cost

To see the Mining Drill in your Workshop tab, you must have advanced through the early industrial technologies:

Requirement Type Cost / Detail
Coal Mining Technology The primary scientific prerequisite
Workshop Building Required to craft the upgrade
Science Resource 750 Science
Steel Resource 100 Steel

Strategic Context

Given your progress with Enriched Uranium and Machine Learning, the Mining Drill is a legacy upgrade that likely fueled your early transition into the Steel Age. Here is how it impacts your current high-level play:

  1. Steel Production: Since minerals are a primary ingredient for Steel (and later Alloy), the Mining Drill helps ensure your Smelters and Calciners never run out of raw ore.
  2. Concrete & Skyscrapers: In the mid-to-late game, your mineral needs skyrocket as you build Concrete Huts and Skyscrapers. The Mining Drill helps keep your mineral income high enough to support these massive construction projects.
  3. Geologist Synergy: While the Mining Drill specifically helps Miners, it works alongside Geologist upgrades (like Geodesy) to ensure your ground-based resource production remains competitive with your space-based income.

Pro Tip: If you find your mineral production is still the bottleneck for crafting Alloy, check if you have also purchased the Unobtanium Drill upgrade. It provides an even larger boost and is essential for the late-game resource grind.
