Electrolytic Smelting is a powerful mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that significantly boosts the output of your terrestrial Smelters.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Prerequisite Technology: You must first research the Metallurgy science.
  • Resource Requirements:
    • Science: 100,000
    • Titanium: 2,000.

Core Effects

This upgrade makes Smelters “twice as effective” by providing a massive boost to their primary outputs:

  • Iron Production: Increases iron yield from Smelters by +95%.
  • Coal Production: Increases coal yield from Smelters (assuming you have the Coal Furnace upgrade) by +95%.

Strategic Importance

  • Doubling Output: Electrolytic Smelting effectively doubles your Smelter production rate for two critical industrial resources.
  • Resource Bottlenecks: This upgrade is essential for scaling your industrial base. Iron is constantly needed for building components and Observatories, while Coal is the primary ingredient for Steel, which becomes a major bottleneck as you approach the Space era.
  • Synergy: It works alongside other Smelter upgrades like Gold Ore (+0.001 gold/tick) and Nuclear Smelters (+0.0015 titanium/tick), transforming simple early-game buildings into advanced multi-resource refineries.
