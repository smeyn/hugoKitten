The Alloy Saw is a late-mid-game Workshop upgrade that enhances the passive wood production of your Lumber Mills.

Costs and Prerequisites

To unlock the Alloy Saw, you must first research the Chemistry science technology. Once unlocked, it is available in the Workshop for the following resources:

  • Science: 85,000
  • Alloy: 75
  • Prerequisite Upgrade: You must have already purchased the Titanium Saw.

Core Effect

The primary function of this upgrade is to boost the efficiency of every Lumber Mill in your village:

  • Production Bonus: It increases the wood production bonus of each Lumber Mill by an additional 15%.
  • Scaling: This bonus is additive with previous saw upgrades (Reinforced, Steel, and Titanium Saws). A base Lumber Mill provides +10% wood production; with the full chain of upgrades including the Alloy Saw, each mill becomes significantly more productive.

Strategic Utility

  • Late-Game Wood Velocity: As the costs for advanced structures like Observatories and Harbours escalate, maintaining a high wood production rate is critical. The Alloy Saw allows you to continue scaling wood income without needing to constantly build more mills, which eventually become prohibitively expensive.
  • Efficiency Benchmark: Mathematically, moving from 40 to 50 Lumber Mills with an Alloy Saw provides approximately a 21.4% relative increase in wood production, compared to only a 20% increase if no saws were researched.
  • Crafting Chain: Because wood is a foundational resource for second-order crafts like Beams and Scaffolds, this upgrade helps speed up the production of materials required for early space exploration and the transition into the Titanium era.
