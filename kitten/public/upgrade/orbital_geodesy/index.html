In Kittens Game, Orbital Geodesy is a high-level Workshop upgrade that significantly enhances the effectiveness of your Geologists. It represents the application of satellite data to terrestrial mining, allowing your kittens to locate and extract minerals with surgical precision.

Key Effects

The primary benefit of Orbital Geodesy is a massive increase in your resource production, specifically targeting the rarest ground-based minerals:

  • Geologist Efficiency: It provides a +50% bonus to all production from Geologists.
  • Gold Production: Geologists are your main source of Gold on the ground. This upgrade is essential for funding the late-game Gold requirements for trading and high-level buildings.
  • Unobtainium Synergy: While it doesn’t produce Unobtainium directly, the increased Gold production allows you to trade more frequently with Leviathans, which is the fastest way to get Time Crystals and Relics for your Cryptotheology.

Unlock Requirements & Cost

To see Orbital Geodesy in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the Orbital Engineering and Geology branches:

Requirement Type Cost / Detail
Orbital Engineering Technology Prerequisite for all satellite-based upgrades.
Geodesy Workshop Upgrade You must have purchased the standard Geodesy first.
Science Resource 150,000 Science
Alloy Resource 1,000 Alloy
Starcharts Resource 1,000 Starcharts

Strategic Context

Given your current progress with Enriched Uranium and Temporal Distortion, Orbital Geodesy is a critical “economic” upgrade:

  1. Trading Mastery: As you move into the Dark Future, you will need vast amounts of Gold to bribe the Leviathans. Orbital Geodesy ensures your Gold income can support dozens of trades per year.
  2. Resource Balancing: By the time you reach this upgrade, your Miners and Smelters are already highly efficient. Orbital Geodesy brings your Geologists up to that same elite level, rounding out your terrestrial economy.
  3. Path to the Exotic: This upgrade is often a stepping stone toward the most advanced resource-gathering technologies in the Farther Ring, ensuring you have the “old world” resources needed to build “new world” colonies.

Pro Tip: Pair this upgrade with the Mining Drill we discussed. The combined bonus to both Miners and Geologists will effectively end any mineral-related bottlenecks you are currently facing.
