In Kittens Game, GM (Game Master) Catnip is an incredibly rare, “cheat-tier” resource that functions differently than standard catnip. It is not part of the normal progression and is typically only encountered through specific developer-related events, game-testing modes, or save-file manipulation.

What is GM Catnip?

Unlike regular catnip, which is the foundational food and wood source for your civilization, GM Catnip represents an infinite or hyper-efficient version of the resource.

  • Production: It cannot be produced by Farmers or Catnip Fields in a standard game.
  • Purpose: It is primarily used by the developer (Bloodrizer) or testers to bypass early-game bottlenecks when debugging new late-game mechanics.
  • Storage: It usually does not respect standard storage limits, allowing for “impossible” amounts of resources.

How it Relates to Your Progress

Since you are currently managing advanced mechanics like Enriched Uranium and Distortion, GM Catnip likely has two points of relevance for you:

  1. Testing/Debug Mode: If you have enabled “Cheat Mode” or used the console to test building ratios, GM Catnip is the fuel used to keep kittens alive without needing thousands of Farmers.
  2. Resource Conversions: In some very specific (often older or modded) versions of the game, GM Catnip was a placeholder for “perfect” efficiency.

Standard Catnip in the Late Game

At your stage of the game (likely Year 40,000+ or deep into Chronospheres), standard catnip should be a non-issue.

  • BioLabs and Hydroponics should be providing more than enough catnip to support your population.
  • If you are actually running low on catnip while focusing on Enriched Uranium, ensure your Reactors aren’t consuming too much energy, as a power deficit can sometimes stall your automated production buildings.

Note: If you found “GM Catnip” in your save data or a guide, it’s a sign of a modified game state. In a “clean” run, you will never need it to reach the end-game.
