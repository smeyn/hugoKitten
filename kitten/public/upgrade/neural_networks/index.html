In Kittens Game, the Neural Network is a high-tier Workshop upgrade that significantly enhances your village’s automation capabilities by improving the Engineer job.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Prerequisite Technology: You must first research the Artificial Intelligence science (which requires 250,000 Science and 150 Blueprints).
  • Resource Requirements:
    • Science: 200,000
    • Titanium: 7,500

Core Effects

The upgrade provides two primary benefits, one mechanical and one thematic:

  • Doubled Engineer Effectiveness: The primary mechanical benefit is that it doubles the efficiency of all Engineer kittens. Since Engineers are responsible for automating the crafting of resources (such as converting Oil to Kerosene or Iron to Plates), this upgrade effectively doubles your passive crafting rate.
  • Noosphere Learning: Thematically, the upgrade allows kittens to “learn from the noosphere”. This means kittens can learn job skills from other kittens employed in different roles, facilitating faster skill acquisition across your population.

Strategic Importance

The Neural Network is a critical component of the late-game industrial engine. By doubling the effectiveness of your Engineers, it allows your civilization to handle much higher resource throughput without hitting storage caps, which is essential for maintaining the production of advanced materials like Eludium or Kerosene while idling. It is often purchased alongside other AI-related upgrades like Machine Learning and AI Engineers to maximize the utility of your technical workforce.
