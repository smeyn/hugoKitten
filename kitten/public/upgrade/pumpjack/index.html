The Pumpjack is a crucial mid-game Workshop upgrade in Kittens Game that enhances the efficiency of Oil Wells while introducing a modest energy requirement.

Key Details and Effects

Category Specification Citation
Type Workshop Upgrade for Oil Wells ,
Prerequisite Technology Mechanization ,
Cost 100,000 Science, 250 Titanium, and 125 Gear
Effect Improves Oil Well effectiveness by +45% (additive with other effects like Oil Refinery and Oil Distillation) ,
Energy Oil Wells will now consume 1 Watt (Wt) Energy each ,
Functionality It enables automation, allowing you to activate or deactivate individual Oil Wells.

Strategic Context

The Pumpjack is essential for accelerating oil production, which is a key bottleneck resource for several subsequent upgrades and buildings, including Kerosene production (rocket fuel),, and space missions such as the Moon Mission.

  • Production Boost: The $+45%$ boost to oil output significantly increases your supply, especially when combined with other oil production upgrades like Oil Refinery and Oil Distillation.
  • Energy Management: While it boosts production, the upgrade introduces a base energy cost (1 Wt) to Oil Wells. This reinforces the need for stable energy infrastructure, achieved through buildings like Steamworks and Magnetos or renewable upgrades like Solar Farms and Hydro Plants.

To achieve the necessary technological prerequisite, Pumpjack requires researching Mechanization,, which itself is unlocked via the Industrialization technology path,.

You can think of the Pumpjack as standardising and improving the oil extraction process: it makes your Oil Wells yield significantly more resource, but requires electricity to run the machinery.
