Factory Processing is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that significantly enhances your civilization’s ability to refine oil into Kerosene by leveraging your industrial infrastructure.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Prerequisite Technology: You must first research Oil Processing, which is unlocked after Rocketry.
  • Resource Requirements:
    • Science: 195,000
    • Titanium: 7,500
    • Concrete: 125.

Core Effects and Mechanics

The primary function of Factory Processing is to link your Factory buildings to your Kerosene production:

  • Scaling Bonus: Each Factory built provides a +5% bonus to oil refinement effectiveness (the amount of Kerosene produced from Oil).
  • Multiplicative Stacking: This effectiveness bonus stacks multiplicatively with your global crafting ratio bonuses, allowing for massive Kerosene yields as your industrial base grows.
  • Workshop Interaction: While primarily categorized as a Factory upgrade, it is purchased in the Workshop and listed as improving “Kerosene Crafting”.

Strategic Importance

  • Fuel for Space Exploration: Kerosene is a critical resource for late-game progression, used as fuel for Spaceports and for sending high-tier space missions like Piscine, Helios, and T-Minus.
  • Managing Oil Caps: Oil production is often slow but hits its storage cap frequently. By using Engineers to automate the conversion of Oil to Kerosene, players can prevent Oil production from stalling; Factory Processing ensures this conversion is as efficient as possible.
  • Infrastructure Synergy: This upgrade transforms Factories from simple crafting boosters into vital components of your energy and fuel supply chain, making large factory networks essential for sustained space operations.
