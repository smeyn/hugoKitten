Concrete Warehouses is a mid-game Workshop upgrade that further expands your storage infrastructure during the “Concrete Era.” It serves as a vital companion to Concrete Barns, helping you reach the resource caps necessary for advanced housing and space technologies.

Core Effects

  • Storage Boost: It increases the storage capacity of Warehouses, Barns, Harbors, and Mints by +35%.
  • Resources Affects: This multiplier applies to your maximum Wood, Minerals, Coal, Iron, Gold, and Titanium.
  • Catnip Synergy: If you have the Silos upgrade, Concrete Warehouses also increases the Catnip capacity of your storage buildings (contributing 25% of its total effect, or roughly +8.75%).

Costs and Requirements

  • Science Cost: 100,000 Science.
  • Resource Cost: 35 Concrete and 1,250 Titanium.
  • Prerequisite: You must have researched Mechanization and purchased the Concrete Pillars upgrade first.

Strategic Utility

  • The Concrete Suite: This upgrade is part of the critical trio of concrete upgrades (Pillars, Barns, Warehouses). While Concrete Barns offers a higher percentage boost (+75%), Concrete Warehouses is essential because it scales your Harbors, which become your primary source of storage in the mid-to-late game.
  • Pre-Reset Push: Players often purchase this alongside Concrete Huts to maximize their storage limits, allowing them to build a few extra Log Houses or Mansions to squeeze out more kittens (and thus more Paragon) before resetting.
  • Gold and Titanium: Unlike early barn upgrades, Concrete Warehouses boosts Gold and Titanium storage (via Harbors and Warehouses), which is critical for affording the 125,000 Science cap required for technologies like Electronics and Robotics.
