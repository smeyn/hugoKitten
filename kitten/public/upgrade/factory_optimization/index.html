Factory Optimization is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that enhances the effectiveness of the Engineer kitten job, facilitating better automation of the crafting process.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Prerequisite Technology: Researching Electronics is required to unlock the upgrade.
  • Resource Requirements:
    • Science: 75,000
    • Gears: 250
    • Titanium: 1,250.

Effects on Engineer Know-How

The primary function of this upgrade is to increase the “know-how” (efficiency) of Engineers based on their class:

  • Class 1 Engineer: Know-how increases to 10.
  • Class 2 Engineer: Know-how increases to 2.

This improvement allows a smaller number of Engineers to perform a higher volume of automated crafts, which is critical for managing resources like Oil or Iron that would otherwise hit their storage caps while the player is away.

Related Industrial Optimizations

While “Factory Optimization” specifically targets Engineer efficiency, several other “Factory” upgrades and mechanics are essential for maximizing industrial output:

  • Factory Logistics: This upgrade increases the global craft bonus provided by each Factory building by 1% (moving the base bonus from 5% to 6%).
  • Factory Processing: This upgrade links your industrial base to your fuel production, providing a +5% bonus to oil refinement effectiveness (Kerosene production) for every Factory built.
  • Space Manufacturing: A game-changing upgrade that allows Factories to provide a bonus to space structures like Space Elevators and Orbital Arrays. This bonus is calculated as 75% of your total factory craft ratio.
  • Full Industrialization (Policy): This policy makes all Factories 5% more powerful (boosting their crafting and space bonuses) at the cost of increased pollution.

Strategic Importance

In the mid-to-late game, optimizing factories is a top priority because their effects are multiplicative and provide global multipliers. High-level players typically focus on maxing out their Factory count to drive massive Unobtainium production through Space Elevators, as this is the primary gateway to Phase 4 (Endgame) content like Time Crystals and Chronospheres.
