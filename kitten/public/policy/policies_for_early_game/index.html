The Policy/ available early in Kittens Game are crucial for optimizing resource accumulation, accelerating the cultural and scientific development required to reach later phases, and preparing for the first major reset (Phase 1/2: Village, Order of the Sun, chasing Paragon).

The overall strategy in the early game is to maximize global production through kittens and happiness, while overcoming specific resource bottlenecks like wood and manuscripts.

Here are the recommended policy choices during early game cycles, generally unlocked after researching Engineering:

1. Environment Policy/

Policy Choice Rationale & Effect
Clear Cutting Clear Cutting is highly recommended over its alternatives.
It provides a substantial +30% wood production bonus.
This boost is critical because wood becomes a “huge help” for constructing major mid-game buildings, such as observatories, harbours, and ships (required after researching Navigation).
Note: Although it introduces a small penalty of -2% happiness and +5% pollution, the resource gain outweighs these drawbacks during this developmental phase. The alternative, Environmentalism, is considered “pretty weak and doesn’t do much for you”.

2. Government Policy/ (Tier 1: Unlocked by Writing)

Policy Choice Rationale & Effect
Tradition Tradition is generally preferred.
This policy accelerates the essential manuscript grind required to advance the science tree (especially toward Theology and Astronomy).
It reduces the manuscript cost ($-100$ culture and $-5$ parchment) and doubles the manuscript maximum culture effect.
Note: While Liberty increases the production effect of happiness, experts favour Tradition for accelerating the major cultural bottleneck. For early runs where unhappiness is a major factor, some players might consider Liberty to offset the production hit, but typically suggest switching to Tradition later.

3. Government Policy/ (Tier 2: Unlocked by Writing + Prior Policy)

Policy Choice Rationale & Effect
Monarchy Monarchy is considered an “AMAZING” choice due to its flexibility and scaling potential.
It makes the leader’s trait twice as powerful. For example, a Leader with the Artisan trait will gain a +10% craft ratio instead of +5% (ignoring other bonuses).
This is powerful because a highly promoted leader can provide a massive boost to whatever resource is currently bottlenecking production, such as wood, science, or crafting efficiency.
Contrast: Alternatives like Republic and Autocracy offer small bonuses that are generally dwarfed by Monarchy’s multiplicative leader effect.

4. Philosophy Policy/ (Unlocked by Philosophy)

Policy Choice Rationale & Effect
Epicureanism Epicureanism is often preferred because it focuses on boosting overall production.
Luxury resources (Furs, Ivory, Spice) will provide 11% happiness instead of the base 10%. Higher happiness is critical for increasing kitten productivity across all jobs.
By the time this policy is reached, trade and hunting systems should be robust enough to manage luxury consumption, making Stoicism (which halves consumption) less necessary.

5. Foreign Policy/ (Unlocked by Currency)

Policy Choice Rationale & Effect
Diplomacy Diplomacy is a standard early pick in the Foreign tree.
Each trade costs 5 less Catpower. Catpower is a foundational resource needed for hunting, exploration, and trade.
Furthermore, choosing Diplomacy leads to the subsequent policy Cultural Exchange, which boosts culture income.

In summary, for early game cycles, the optimal choices centre on Clear Cutting (for wood/infrastructure), Tradition (for manuscript/science velocity), and Monarchy/Epicureanism (for maximizing kitten production via happiness and leader efficiency).
