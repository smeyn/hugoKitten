Order of the Stars is a late-game Government policy that focuses on accelerating religious progression by increasing Faith production. It represents a shift where [Priests] ( /job/priest/) take control of the government after the horrors of deep space are discovered.

Core Mechanics and Costs

  • Cost: 150,000 Culture.
  • Primary Effect: Provides a +20% bonus to Faith production.
  • Leadership Restriction: Once enacted, only kittens with the Priest job are allowed to be assigned as the village leader.
  • Prerequisites: You must have reached Dune and currently have a kitten assigned to the Priest job.
  • Mutual Exclusivity: This policy is mutually exclusive with Technocracy (+20% science cap) and Cosmological Libertarianism (+15% unobtainium production).

Strategic Utility

Choosing Order of the Stars is a highly specific strategic decision depending on your current run goals:

  • The Religion Grind: This policy is the ideal choice for runs where your primary objective is to work on religious bonuses and grind Transcendence tiers. Because faith production is naturally slow, the +20% boost is significant for reaching high tiers like TT15, which is a major gateway for late-game time crystal production.
  • Leadership Flexibility: Although the policy restricts leaders to [Priests] ( /job/priest/), it is noted in endgame guides that the policy remains effective even if you choose to have no leader assigned at all.
  • Comparison with Alternatives:
    • Most players prefer Cosmological Libertarianism because the need for Unobtainium is often more pressing than Faith in the late game.
    • Technocracy is generally avoided because science storage can be easily increased through other buildings and upgrades, making it less valuable than a production boost.

When to Enact

You should consider Order of the Stars if you have already optimized your Unobtainium production and are now hitting a bottleneck with your Apocrypha bonus or trying to break through higher Transcendence levels to fuel your Time Crystal engine.
