Rationality is a mid-game Philosophy policy in Kittens Game that provides a modest boost to scientific and industrial production. It represents the societal view that the world can be fundamentally understood through logic and reason.

Core Effects and Costs

  • Effect: Increases both Science and Iron production by 5%.
  • Cost: 3000 Science.
  • Prerequisites: You must have researched the Philosophy technology and enacted either the Stoicism or Epicureanism policy.
  • Mutual Exclusivity: Choosing Rationality permanently locks you out of the Mysticism policy for the remainder of the run.

Strategic Comparison: Rationality vs. Mysticism

Because these policies are mutually exclusive, you must choose between boosting techs/Iron or Culture/Faith.

Policy Production Bonuses Strategic Context
Rationality +5% Science, +5% Iron Focuses on technical and industrial throughput.
Mysticism +5% Culture, +5% Faith Focuses on religious and cultural scaling.

Expert Recommendation

Strategic guides generally recommend choosing Mysticism over Rationality for the following reasons:

  • Science is “Easy”: Science production and storage are relatively easy to manage through buildings like Libraries, Academies, and Observatories.
  • Culture/Faith Bottlenecks: Culture and Faith accumulate much more slowly, especially in the early-to-mid game, making percentage bonuses for these resources far more valuable.
  • Iron Scaling: While a 5% Iron boost is helpful, industrial production is often better scaled through Workshops, Smelters, and Calciners rather than a dedicated philosophy policy.
