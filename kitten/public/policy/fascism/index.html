The policy Fascism is an advanced Government policy in Kittens Game, primarily focused on aggressively boosting population by reducing housing costs and eliminating population-related happiness penalties.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of the Fascism policy:

Policy Details and Effects

Category Detail
Cost 15,000 Culture.
Description The policy’s description is “Lebensraum!”.
Prerequisites You must Build a factory and have previously chosen Autocracy or Monarchy.
Locks Choosing Fascism locks out both the Communism and Liberalism Policy/.
Key Effects Log cabins are 50% cheaper. Log Houses are the housing structure that follows Huts.
Eliminates population penalties to happiness.

Strategic Evaluation

Fascism is one of three major government policy choices (Liberalism, Fascism, or Communism) available after meeting the Factory prerequisite.

  • First Run Population Push: This policy is strategically useful on the first run. The main goal of the initial run is often to accumulate sufficient population to gain Paragon upon reset. You might select Fascism for a couple of extra log houses to help reach 130+ kittens before the first reset.
  • Population Boost: The policy gives a nice population boost because the reduced cost of log cabins makes expanding housing capacity easier.
  • Happiness Management: By eliminating population penalties to happiness, it allows players to grow their workforce faster without suffering the associated production drop caused by unhappiness.
  • Policy Alternatives: In later runs, Policy/ that offer long-term industrial boosts or trade relations are often prioritized:
    • Liberalism may be preferred if you are struggling with zebra hostility (which impacts titanium acquisition).
    • Communism may be preferred if zebra hostility has been negated, as it provides a strong boost to industry (+25% iron, coal, and titanium production). Communism is also suggested to give better time crystal results in self-imposed restriction runs.
  • Paragon Scaling: As your Paragon grows, the Fascism policy can be unlocked to aid in population growth.
