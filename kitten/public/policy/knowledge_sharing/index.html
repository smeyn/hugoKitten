In Kittens Game, Knowledge Sharing is a mid-game Workshop upgrade that significantly streamlines your science progression by allowing your kittens to “learn from each other” more effectively.

Key Effects

The primary function of Knowledge Sharing is to increase your Science production based on your population and infrastructure:

  • Science Generation: It provides a +10% bonus to all Science production.
  • Scholar Efficiency: It specifically boosts the effectiveness of Scholars. In the mid-game, Scholars are your primary way of reaching high Science caps for technologies like Electricity and Industrialization.
  • Cultural Synergy: It improves the rate at which Manuscripts are consumed or utilized for certain scientific breakthroughs, making it easier to maintain your “Culture” levels.

Requirements to Unlock

To see Knowledge Sharing in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the basic educational technologies:

Requirement Type Cost / Detail
Philosophy Technology The primary academic prerequisite
Academy Building You must have at least one Academy built
Science Resource 15,000 Science
Manuscripts Resource 100 Manuscripts

Strategic Value

Given that you are managing Enriched Uranium and Distortion, Knowledge Sharing is a fundamental building block you likely acquired to scale your Science cap. Here is why it remains relevant in the late game:

  1. Reaching the Antimatter Era: To unlock Antimatter and Relic-based technologies, you need Science caps in the millions. Knowledge Sharing acts as a multiplier that makes every Library, Academy, and BioLab more potent.
  2. Reset Efficiency: When you reset (especially with Chronospheres), Knowledge Sharing is one of the early upgrades you want to grab to quickly regain your tech tree progress.
  3. Leader Synergy: If your Leader is a Scientist, the Knowledge Sharing bonus stacks with the Leader’s rank bonus, allowing for massive bursts of Science generation.

Pro Tip: If you find yourself “Science-capped” (unable to afford the next tech even when your storage is full), focus on building more BioLabs and Compendiums. Knowledge Sharing will then apply its percentage bonus to that higher base.
