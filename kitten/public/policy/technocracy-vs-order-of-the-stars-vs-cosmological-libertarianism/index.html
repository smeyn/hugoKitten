Technocracy, Order of the Stars, and Cosmological Libertarianism are mutually exclusive high-tier government Policy/ unlocked in the Science tab once you reach Dune. Each costs 150,000 Culture and forces you to choose between prioritising science capacity, faith production, or the generation of the critical late-game resource, unobtainium.

Comparison of Dune-Tier Policy/

Policy Effect Key Prerequisite Strategic Focus
Cosmological Libertarianism +15% Unobtainium production Reach Dune General progression and overcoming the unobtainium bottleneck.
Order of the Stars +20% Faith production; leaders must be [Priests] ( /job/priest/) Reach Dune + assigned Priest leader Religious-focused runs and grinding Transcendence tiers.
Technocracy +20% Science cap Reach Dune Reaching massive science thresholds for high-tier technologies.

Strategic Deep Dive

1. Cosmological Libertarianism (The “Meta” Choice)

This is widely considered the strongest option for most players.

  • Unobtainium is King: Unobtainium is the primary bottleneck for advanced space missions and time manipulation. A 15% boost to its production is significant because few other modifiers affect it directly.
  • Efficiency: Strategic guides recommend this over the others because “you REALLY need more unob” to progress through Phase 4 and establish a positive reset loop.

2. Order of the Stars (The Religious Choice)

This policy shifts your government’s focus to the heavens and the Order of the Sun.

  • Faith Grinding: It is most useful during long runs where your primary goal is increasing your epiphany and religion bonuses.
  • Leader Restriction: Note that while it provides a 20% faith boost, it mandates that only [Priests] ( /job/priest/) can be assigned as leaders. However, the policy bonus itself still functions even if you do not have an active leader assigned.

3. Technocracy (The Niche Choice)

Technocracy provides a flat 20% increase to your Science storage capacity.

  • Redundancy: Strategy guides often label Technocracy as unnecessary because players have numerous other options to expand science storage—such as Compendiums, Observatories, and Data Centers—making a policy slot for science cap a lower priority.
  • Specific Utility: It is only truly beneficial if you are struggling to hit a specific, massive science threshold for a high-tier technology that other storage buildings cannot reach.

Choosing the Right Path

Because these Policy/ are mutually exclusive, selecting one will permanently lock the other two for the remainder of your current run.

  • For most runs: Choose Cosmological Libertarianism to speed up the acquisition of time crystals and eludium.
  • For deep-endgame/40k runs: You might consider Order of the Stars if your unobtainium production is already self-sustaining and you wish to focus purely on religious scaling.
