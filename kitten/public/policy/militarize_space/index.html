Militarize Space is a late-game Foreign policy that focuses on aggressive scientific research to expand your knowledge limits. It specifically enhances the synergy between your orbital infrastructure and ground-based research buildings.

Core Effects and Requirements

  • Cost: 10,000 Culture.
  • Prerequisite: You must have successfully launched at least one Satellite.
  • Scientific Bonus: It makes the Satellite science bonus to Observatories 10% more powerful.
  • Mutual Exclusivity: Choosing this policy permanently locks you out of the Outer Space Treaty for the remainder of your current run.

Strategic Analysis: Militarize Space vs. Outer Space Treaty

Deciding between these two policies depends on whether your current bottleneck is Science storage or Trade efficiency.

Policy Primary Benefit Best Used When…
Militarize Space Increases maximum Science storage via Satellites. All your space structures and ground buildings are consistently hitting their science caps.
Outer Space Treaty Improves trade relations with all races (equivalent to 10 Tradeposts). You need to maximize trade yields (especially for Titanium or Unobtainium).

Expert Recommendation

In most standard playstyles, Outer Space Treaty is considered the superior choice because trade remains a vital part of resource acquisition even in the late game. Science storage can often be expanded through other means—such as building more Data Centers, Bio Labs, or Research Vessels—without sacrificing a policy slot.

However, if you are deep into the space era and find that your science limit is preventing you from researching the next multi-million science technology (like Exophysics or Quantum Cryptography), Militarize Space can provide that final necessary push.
