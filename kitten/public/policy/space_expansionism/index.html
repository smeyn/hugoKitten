Space expansionism in Kittens Game refers to the phase where your civilisation leaves its home planet, Cath, to colonise the solar system. This era is officially unlocked by researching the Rocketry technology, which enables the Space tab and allows you to build rockets for interstellar missions.

Initial Unlocking and Missions

To begin your expansion, you must first navigate the “Titanium Era” to secure the necessary materials and storage caps. The first milestone is Orbital Launch, which requires a significant investment in Starcharts, Catpower, Science, and Oil.

Key initial missions include:

  • Orbital Launch: Unlocks the ability to deploy Satellites and build Space Elevators.
  • Moon Mission: The most critical early goal, as it provides access to Unobtainium through Lunar Outposts and significantly expanded storage via Moon Bases.
  • Dune Mission: Provides a source of Uranium (via Planet Crackers) and high-pressure Oil (via Hydraulic Fracturers).
  • Piscine Mission: Unlocks Research Vessels, which are essential for generating large quantities of Starcharts in the mid-to-late game.

Critical Resources for Space

Successful space expansion relies on several specific resources that become the primary bottlenecks of the late game:

  • Starcharts: Found by observing astronomical events or produced by Satellites and Research Vessels; these are the “currency” of space travel.
  • Unobtainium (UO): A rare resource refined from Uranium at Lunar Outposts. It is the gateway to Time Crystals, Eludium, and the endgame storage loops.
  • Oil and Kerosene: Oil is used for initial launches, while advanced missions require Kerosene, a fuel processed from oil.
  • Antimatter: Produced by Sunlifters (Helios), it is used for high-tier upgrades and increasing resource caps.

Strategic Optimization

Space expansion is not just about exploring; it is a mechanism for scaling your civilisation’s resource caps geometrically.

  • Space Manufacturing: This is a vital workshop upgrade that allows your Factories to improve the production of space buildings. Without this, space structures often provide marginal returns.
  • Global Multipliers: Buildings like the Space Elevator (reduces oil costs) and Orbital Array (provides a 2% bonus to all space structures) are essential for making expansion efficient.
  • Storage Caps: Late-game progression is gated by the 8.14 billion Unobtainium storage requirement needed for 100 Chronospheres. This is achieved through massive investment in Moon Bases, Cryostations, and AI Bases.
