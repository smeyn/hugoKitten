Technocracy is a high-tier Government policy that provides a 20% increase to your maximum science storage capacity. While this sounds substantial, it is generally considered the least effective option in its tier because there are many other ways to expand science storage that do not require sacrificing a policy slot.

Key Statistics

  • Cost: 150,000 Culture.
  • Requirement: You must have reached Dune to unlock it.
  • Mutual Exclusivity: Enacting Technocracy permanently locks you out of Cosmological Libertarianism and Order of the Stars for the remainder of the run.

Strategic Analysis: Why it is often Skipped

In the late game, science storage is rarely the primary bottleneck. Players have access to numerous structures and items that expand this cap, making the 20% boost from Technocracy redundant:

  • Science Buildings: Libraries (and their upgrade, Data Centers), Academies, Observatories, and Bio Labs all provide flat and percentage-based science cap increases.
  • Compendiums: Every Compendium you craft increases your science storage by 10 units.
  • Late-Game Structures: Research Vessels and Space Beacons provide massive science cap boosts (+10k and +25k respectively).

Superior Alternatives

Because you can only choose one policy at this tier, the community generally recommends the other two options depending on your current goals:

  • Cosmological Libertarianism: Provides +15% Unobtainium production. Unobtainium is a much harder bottleneck to clear than science storage, making this the “meta” choice for most players.
  • Order of the Stars: Provides +20% Faith production. This is ideal for long runs focused on grinding Transcendence tiers and religious bonuses.

When to Consider Technocracy

Technocracy is only truly useful if you are attempting to reach a specific, massive science threshold for an endgame technology (like Blackchain or Exophysics) and have already maxed out all other science storage options without reaching the required amount.
