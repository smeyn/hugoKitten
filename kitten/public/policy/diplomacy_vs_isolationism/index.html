Both Diplomacy and Isolationism are Foreign Policy/ available in Kittens Game, costing 1,600 Culture each and requiring the Currency technology as a prerequisite. They represent a choice in your society’s foreign relations path, with impacts on trade costs and future policy options.

Here is a comparison of the two Policy/:

Diplomacy

Aspect Details
Cost Culture 1600
Prerequisite Currency
Effect Each trade costs 5 less catpower.
Policy Path Leads to Cultural Exchange or Knowledge Sharing (after researching Astronomy).
Strategic Value This policy is generally preferred because it unlocks the path to Cultural Exchange, which provides a bonus to Culture, a resource that is often slow to acquire.
Related Meta The Diplomacy Metaphysics upgrade improves the trade process by reducing the unlock year for races from Year 5 to Year 1, and increases standing ratio.

Isolationism

Aspect Details
Cost Culture 1600
Prerequisite Currency
Effect Each trade costs 1 less gold.
Policy Path Leads to Big Stick Policy or City On A Hill (after researching Astronomy).
Strategic Value While the gold discount might “feel good,” the subsequent Policy/ in this path are generally considered less beneficial (“aren’t any good”).

Strategic Recommendation

For most runs, Diplomacy is the recommended choice.

The primary advantage of Diplomacy is accessing the later Policy/:

  • Cultural Exchange (+5% culture income) is highly valuable because Culture is a resource that accumulates slowly.
  • Knowledge Sharing (+5% science income) is the alternative, although Science is generally easier to obtain than Culture, making Cultural Exchange the common preference.

Conversely, choosing Isolationism grants a Gold discount on trades, but the resulting Policy/ (Big Stick Policy and City On A Hill) are considered weaker options. Big Stick Policy makes Embassies 15% cheaper, while City On A Hill increases Culture cap by 5%.

In summary, choosing Diplomacy prioritises long-term resource boosts (Culture) via future Policy/, whereas Isolationism provides a minor, immediate reduction in gold expenditure during trade.
