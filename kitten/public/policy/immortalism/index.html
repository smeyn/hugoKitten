In Kittens Game, Immortalism is a high-level Metaphysics upgrade that fundamentally changes how your kittens interact with the cycle of life and death—specifically concerning their rank and experience.

Key Effects

The primary function of Immortalism is to preserve the progress of your kittens across certain game events:

  • Experience Retention: Normally, when a kitten dies or a reset occurs without certain upgrades, their accumulated experience and rank are lost. Immortalism allows kittens to retain their rank and XP even after they “die” from age or other factors (though they still need to be replaced by new kittens).
  • Leader Longevity: It is particularly useful for maintaining high-level Leaders. Since leaders provide bonuses based on their rank (like production boosts or price reductions), keeping those ranks intact is vital for efficiency.
  • Reduced Micromanagement: You won’t have to spend as much time re-assigning and re-leveling leaders in very long runs.

How to Unlock

To access Immortalism, you must have unlocked the Metaphysics tab (which requires the Astronomy and Philosophy technologies and at least one Ziggurat).

  • Cost: 150 Paragon.
  • Prerequisite: You must first purchase the malkuth (or similar lower-tier) Metaphysics upgrades.

Strategic Context: Is it worth it?

Since you are currently managing complex systems like Enriched Uranium and Distortion, here is how Immortalism fits into your late-game strategy:

  1. Paragon Efficiency: Early on, 150 Paragon is better spent on “Price Ratio” upgrades (like Engineering or Golden Ratio). However, once you have your core ratio upgrades, Immortalism is a great “quality of life” purchase.
  2. Long Runs (Dark Future): In runs where you are pushing toward Year 40,000+, your kittens will inevitably go through many cycles. Immortalism ensures that your workforce remains “elite” without constant intervention.
  3. Synergy with Cryptotheology: As you build more Black Obelisks, your production bonuses skyrocket. Having high-ranked kitten engineers and scientists further multiplies these bonuses, making Immortalism a solid late-game investment.

Expert Tip: If you are planning a Reset soon to farm Paragon, make sure you’ve spent your current Paragon on these permanent upgrades first, as they persist across all future timelines!
