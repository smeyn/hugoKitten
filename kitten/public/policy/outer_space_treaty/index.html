The Outer Space Treaty is a high-tier Foreign policy in Kittens Game that improves global trade relations by declaring space a domain for all races. It is primarily used to boost the effectiveness of trade with all civilisations once you have reached the space era.

Requirements and Costs

To enact this policy, your civilisation must meet the following criteria:

  • Cost: 10,000 Culture.
  • Prerequisite: You must have launched at least one Satellite.
  • Mutual Exclusivity: Choosing this policy permanently locks out the Militarize Space policy for the remainder of your current run.

Core Effects

  • Global Trade Bonus: It provides a permanent boost to relations with all trading partners, equivalent to having 10 extra Tradeposts.
  • Pacifism Challenge Goal: Researching this policy and subsequently resetting the game is the primary objective and win condition for the Pacifism Challenge.

Strategic Comparison

When choosing between the available space policies, the Outer Space Treaty is widely considered the superior choice for most playstyles.

Policy Primary Benefit Strategic Context
Outer Space Treaty Better trade relations (+10 Tradeposts). Generally useful most of the time to resolve resource bottlenecks through trade.
Militarize Space Boosts the science bonus that Satellites provide to Observatories. Only recommended if your space structures are consistently hitting their science storage caps.

Role in the Endgame

In the late game, the Outer Space Treaty serves as a vital component for players focusing on system arbitrage and trade efficiency. Because trade is one of the most effective ways to acquire restricted resources like Titanium and Iron without relying on slower production buildings, the relationship bonus provided by the treaty helps ensure consistent, high-yield exchanges with all discovered races.
