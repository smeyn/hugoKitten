The choice between the Liberty and Tradition Policy/ in Kittens Game represents an early decision regarding how your civilization addresses population growth, resource management, and scientific advancement. Both Policy/ become available once you have researched Writing and both cost 150 Culture.

This choice is particularly important because once one is selected, the other is locked out, and this initial decision influences the subsequent Government Policy/ you can pursue.

Here is a comparison of their purpose, effects, and strategic recommendations:

1. Tradition Policy

Purpose and Effects:

  • Manuscript Efficiency: Tradition is described as being “Good for small culture oriented societies”. Its primary effect is reducing the cost and increasing the impact of Manuscripts.
    • Cost Reduction: Manuscript cost is reduced by 100 Culture and 5 Parchment.
    • Culture Cap Boost: It doubles the manuscript max culture effect.
  • Expert Recommendation: “Monstrous Advice” strongly recommends Tradition (“all the way!”) because manuscripts are crucial for progression, and making them cheaper and more effective is highly beneficial.

Progression Path:

  • Choosing Tradition allows you to later select Monarchy or Autocracy (both costing 1500 Culture).

2. Liberty Policy

Purpose and Effects:

  • Happiness Focus: Liberty is intended for societies focused on happiness, addressing the natural unhappiness that arises from increasing population.
    • Happiness Bonus: It increases the production effect of happiness by 10%.
    • Population Bonus: It adds free housing for one kitten.
  • Contextual Use: In early runs, the Liberty policy can be obtained to offset a “big production hit due to happiness”.

Strategic Critique:

  • “Monstrous Advice” labels Liberty as a “noob trap” because it promises to solve a problem (low happiness/production) that can often be managed through other means, such as accumulating Karma from resets or building Amphitheatres.
  • The “Kittensgame starter guide,” however, suggests that in early runs, if progression is slowing due to low happiness/high population penalty, obtaining Liberty can be useful to mitigate that production hit. However, in later runs (once Karma is acquired), switching to Tradition may be preferable.

Progression Path:

  • Choosing Liberty allows you to later select Republic or Autocracy (both costing 1500 Culture).

Summary of Differences

Policy Primary Benefit Recommended Strategy Subsequent Governments
Tradition Reduces the cost of manuscripts and increases their culture effect. Highly recommended by experts for improving long-term progress and managing the manuscript bottleneck. Monarchy, Autocracy.
Liberty Increases kitten production based on Happiness and provides one free housing unit. Useful in specific early runs if happiness penalties are severely impacting production. Republic, Autocracy.

In essence, Tradition invests in the efficiency of your scientific engine (Manuscript production), while Liberty attempts to mask the symptoms of low kitten Happiness to maintain production. For most players seeking rapid progress, the permanent resource efficiency gained from Tradition is favored, as manuscripts quickly become a bottleneck required for advanced technologies like Theology and Astronomy.
