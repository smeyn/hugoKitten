In Kittens Game, Transkittenism, Necrocracy, and Radical Xenophobia are mutually exclusive late-game policies available after you have traded with the Leviathans. You can only pick one per run, and they cannot be changed once selected.

Because they are exclusive, choosing one requires identifying your primary bottleneck: storage capacity, raw production, or specific resource pacts.

Comparison Overview

Policy Primary Focus Key Benefit
Transkittenism Storage Increases Moon Base storage capacity (which effectively allows for more Chronospheres).
Necrocracy Production Global production bonus based on your Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS) levels.
Radical Xenophobia Diplomacy/Pacts Increases Mausoleum efficiency and grants 5 free Pacts.

Deep Dive

1. Transkittenism (The “Storage” Choice)

  • Best for: Players aiming for “Chronosphere amplification” or pushing to reach higher kitten counts through storage-based building limits.
  • The Effect: It doubles Gigaflop output. Because Gigaflops are used to maintain Entanglement Stations (which boost relic output) and affect Moon Base storage, it helps you manage higher building caps.
  • Why pick it: If you are planning a very long run where you want to maximize the number of Chronospheres you can build, the extra storage from Moon Bases provided by Transkittenism is often the superior choice.

2. Necrocracy (The “Production” Choice)

  • Best for: Players who have significant Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS) and want to maximize Unobtainium, ivory, and alicorn production.
  • The Effect: Provides a global production bonus of 0.1% per 1% of BLS. It also boosts your Leviathan Energy Cap by 5%.
  • Why pick it: If your main limitation is the speed at which you generate resources (Unobtainium being the most common), Necrocracy is mathematically superior in the late game. As your BLS increases over multiple runs, this bonus scales significantly, potentially tripling your Unobtainium production.

3. Radical Xenophobia (The “Niche” Choice)

  • Best for: Very specific scenarios where you need Pacts or want to maximize the efficacy of your Mausoleums.
  • The Effect: Increases the efficacy of all Mausoleums and gives 5 free Pacts.
  • Why pick it: This is generally considered the weakest of the three for most standard gameplay goals. It is mostly used if you specifically need the Pacts for a challenge or a very unconventional setup.

Strategic Recommendation

  • Early/Mid-Late Game: If you don’t have much BLS built up yet, Transkittenism is often preferred for the storage flexibility.
  • Deep/Infinite Runs: Once you have high levels of BLS (usually 250+), Necrocracy typically outperforms Transkittenism by providing a massive, multiplicative production boost to your entire economy, including space structures.
  • Note on AI Apocalypse: Be aware that Necrocracy can make managing the AI Apocalypse more complex because it increases your reliance on the resources that trigger it. If you want to avoid the headache of managing AI cores vs. Entanglement stations, some players stick with Transkittenism purely for the “lazier” late-game management.

Summary: If you aren’t sure, Transkittenism is generally safer and more flexible for storage, while Necrocracy is the “power gamer” move for when your production speed (specifically Unobtainium) becomes your primary blocker.
