City On A Hill is a mid-game Foreign policy in Kittens Game that provides a small +5% increase to your maximum culture cap. It represents an isolationist approach where your village seeks to be a “shining example” to the rest of the world.

Quick Stats

Attribute Details
Cost 4,000 Culture
Primary Effect +5% Culture Cap
Prerequisites Astronomy (Tech) and Isolationism (Policy)
Mutually Exclusive Big Stick Policy

Strategic Utility

While a larger culture cap can be helpful for researching advanced technologies like Theology, this policy is generally considered a suboptimal choice for several reasons:

  • Weak Bonus: Compared to other global multipliers, a 5% increase to the culture cap is relatively minor and often described as “kinda sucking” in strategic guides.
  • The Big Stick Alternative: By choosing City On A Hill, you permanently lock yourself out of the Big Stick Policy for the remainder of the run. Big Stick Policy provides a 15% discount on Embassies, which is widely viewed as more practically useful for progressing trade relations with other civilisations.
  • Isolationist Drawbacks: Because this policy requires the Isolationism prerequisite, you are already trading away the benefits of the Diplomacy policy branch, which offers better trade efficiency and faster discovery of other races.

Strategic Recommendation

Most experienced players advise skipping City On A Hill in favour of the Big Stick Policy if you have committed to the Isolationism branch. If your goal is to increase culture throughput or cap, investment in Amphitheatres, Temples, and Academies typically provides more substantial results without the opportunity cost of a policy slot.
