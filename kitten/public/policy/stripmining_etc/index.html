In Kittens Game, Strip Mining, Clear Cutting, and Environmentalism are mutually exclusive environmental policies unlocked after researching the Engineering technology. You may only choose one of these policies per run.

Direct Comparison of Effects

Policy Cost Primary Effects Pollution Impact
Clear Cutting 2,000 Science +30% wood production, -2% happiness +5%
Strip Mining 2,000 Science +30% mineral production, -2% happiness +5%
Environmentalism 2,000 Culture +3% happiness -5%

Iron Will Bonuses

In Iron Will mode (where you have no kittens), these policies provide unique bonuses to your zebras and celestial events:

  • Clear Cutting: +10% bonus to catnip refinement (equivalent to one un-upgraded Lumber Mill).
  • Strip Mining: +10% bonus minerals from meteors.
  • Environmentalism: No specific Iron Will bonus is listed for the base policy, but it leads to Open Woodlands, which provides a 4.16% bonus to both refinement and meteor minerals.

Strategic Advice from the Sources

  • The Case for Clear Cutting: This is widely considered the strongest choice for early-to-mid-game runs. Wood is a major bottleneck when transitioning into the “Titanium Era,” as you need massive amounts of it for Observatories, Harbours, and Ships.
  • The Case for Strip Mining: Generally viewed as the least useful of the production-based choices. Minerals are typically easier to accumulate than wood through standard progression, making the 30% boost less impactful than the wood boost from Clear Cutting.
  • The Case for Environmentalism: Sources describe this policy as “pretty weak” because the 3% happiness boost is often outweighed by the raw production benefits of the other two. However, it is the only option that directly reduces pollution, which otherwise reduces catnip production and Solar Revolution bonuses.

Downstream Policy Unlocks

The choice you make here determines which advanced policies you can research later in the run after reaching Industrialization or Ecology:

Following Clear Cutting or Strip Mining:

  • Sustainability (10k Culture): Removes the -2% happiness penalty from your chosen production policy and provides a further -5% pollution reduction.
  • Full Industrialization (10k Culture): Makes Factories 5% more powerful (boosting their crafting bonus), but increases pollution by another +5%.

Following Environmentalism:

  • Conservation (10k Culture): Increases the happiness bonus from Environmentalism to +5% and provides another -5% pollution reduction.
  • Open Woodlands (10k Culture): A compromise policy that provides +12.5% wood and mineral production at the cost of +5% pollution.
