The Helios Mission is a significant mid-to-late game space project that involves launching a rocket to the G2V spectral type star at the centre of the Cath solar system. Completing this mission is a prerequisite for moving further into the star system, specifically unlocking the Yarn Mission.

Mission Requirements and Logistics

To launch the Helios mission, you must have first successfully completed the Dune Mission. The specific resource requirements are:

  • Starcharts: 3,000
  • Titanium: 15,000
  • Science: 250,000
  • Kerosene: 1,250

The travel time for the mission is 1,200 game-days, which equates to approximately 40 minutes in real-time. This duration can be reduced significantly by researching drive upgrades such as the Antimatter Drive or Thorium Drive.

Unlocked Structures and Infrastructure

The primary importance of the Helios mission is the gateway it provides to advanced energy and antimatter production. Successfully completing the mission unlocks four critical space structures:

  • Sunlifter: The primary source of antimatter, producing 1 unit per year while also generating 30 Watts of energy.
  • Containment Chamber: Provides base storage for up to 100 units of antimatter, though it consumes 50 Watts of energy.
  • Heatsink: Increases the antimatter storage capacity of Containment Chambers by +2%.
  • Sunforge: Enhances the storage capacity of base metals (iron, titanium, gold, uranium, and unobtainium) by 1% per building.

The Helios Cycle

If you have researched Numerology, the “Helios” cycle (one of ten 5-year cycles in the game) provides specific benefits to your infrastructure. During this cycle:

  • Sunlifter energy output is increased by 50%.
  • Cryostation storage is reduced by 10%.
  • If Numeromancy is also researched, holding a festival during the Helios cycle provides double Faith income and a 1.25x multiplier to Unicorn income.

Strategic Utility

The Helios mission marks the transition into the Relic Era. To obtain the 5,000 antimatter capacity required for Relic Stations, players must typically invest heavily in Helios-based structures, specifically building enough Heatsinks (often up to 34 or 37) and Containment Chambers to reach the necessary cap. Furthermore, building a Sunlifter is a mandatory goal for completing the Energy Challenge.
