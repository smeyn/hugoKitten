The Moon Mission is a pivotal space project in Kittens Game that involves launching a rocket to Redmoon, a satellite of the planet Cath. It represents the first major step into space exploration beyond orbital deployment and is the primary gateway to the Unobtainium Era.

Mission Requirements and Logistics

  • Prerequisites: You must first research Rocketry and successfully complete the Orbital Launch mission.
  • Resource Costs: The launch requires 500 Starcharts, 5000 Titanium, 125k Science, and 45k Oil.
  • Travel Time: The journey to Redmoon takes 30 game-days, which is approximately 1 minute in real-time. This can be further reduced by drive upgrades like the Antimatter or Thorium Drives.

Unlocks and Infrastructure

Successfully completing the Moon Mission is essential for unlocking the infrastructure required to produce and store advanced materials:

  • Lunar Outpost: A nuclear-powered mining facility that is the primary source of Unobtainium. It consumes 1.75 uranium per second to produce 0.035 unobtainium per second.
  • Moon Base: A vital storage building that provides a base capacity of +150 Unobtainium, along with significant boosts to other resources such as Catnip (+45k), Wood (+25k), and Titanium (+1250).
  • Subsequent Missions: Completing the Moon Mission is a prerequisite for launching missions to Dune and Piscine.

Strategic Importance

  • The Unobtainium Bottleneck: Progressing past the mid-game is heavily gated by Unobtainium, which is required for Metaphysics upgrades and the high-tier Eludium alloy.
  • Oil Management: A major hurdle for this mission is oil storage. While the mission itself costs 45k oil, building the first Moon Base (which is necessary to store enough unobtainium for Unobtainium Huts) requires 70k oil. Players often need to build numerous Oil Wells or tankers to reach these caps.
  • Resource Scaling: The effectiveness of Moon Bases can be significantly boosted by the AI Bases upgrade, which adds a 10% storage bonus per AI Core.
  • Challenges: In the Black Sky Challenge, the starchart requirement for the preceding Orbital Launch is removed, but science and oil costs are significantly increased, making the path to the Moon Mission more difficult.

Economic Utility

Beyond unobtainium, the Catnip storage provided by Moon Bases (45k) is critical for surviving the harsh food penalties of Winter in the late game, especially during challenges like “Winter Has Come” where the seasonal cycle is permanently frozen in winter.
