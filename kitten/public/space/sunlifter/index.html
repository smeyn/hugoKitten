A Sunlifter is a high-tier space structure built in the center of the Helios solar system, serving as the primary engine for antimatter production and late-game energy generation. It is a critical component for transitioning into the Relic era and completing high-level challenges.

Acquisition and Costs

To construct a Sunlifter, you must first complete the Helios Mission (3,000 starcharts, 15k titanium, 250k science, and 1,250 kerosene).

  • Structure Cost: 500k Science, 225 Eludium, and 2,500 Kerosene.
  • Price Ratio: 1.15.
  • Challenge Requirement: Building at least one Sunlifter is a mandatory goal for completing the Energy Challenge.

Core Functions

The Sunlifter provides two essential resources simultaneously:

  1. Energy Production: It generates 30 Watts of energy. Alongside Hydro Plants, Sunlifters are the main focus for power generation in the late game.
  2. Antimatter Generation: It produces 1 antimatter per year. Antimatter is only generated at the change of the calendar year and only if your global energy production is positive.

Celestial Cycle Interactions

The effectiveness of Sunlifters is heavily influenced by the ten-cycle system if you have researched Numerology:

  • Helios Cycle: Provides a +50% bonus to Sunlifter energy output.
  • T-minus Cycle: Imposes a -50% penalty to Sunlifter energy output.

Strategic Utility

  • The Relic Era: Sunlifters are vital for reaching the 5,000 antimatter cap required for Relic Stations. To achieve this cap, you will typically need to power a vast array of Heatsinks and Containment Chambers, which require massive energy surpluses provided by Sunlifters.
  • Time Skipping: Because antimatter is granted only once per year, players often use Time Crystals to “shatter” or skip years. This triggers the year-change logic repeatedly, allowing you to harvest antimatter from your Sunlifters much faster than waiting in real-time.
  • Late-Game Power: While Reactors provide significant power, they consume Thorium; Sunlifters provide a stable, “clean” alternative that is essential for maintaining the energy required for Heatsinks and Containment Chambers.
