The Dune Mission is a mid-to-late game space project that involves launching a rocket to explore Dune, described as a large, lifeless planet covered by sand and volcanic rock. It serves as a critical bridge for industrial scaling, particularly in stabilizing the uranium and oil supplies required for advanced space exploration.

Mission Requirements and Logistics

To access the Dune mission, you must have first successfully completed the Moon mission. The specific resource costs for the launch include:

  • Starcharts: 1,000.
  • Titanium: 7,000.
  • Science: 175,000.
  • Kerosene: 75.

The base travel time for the mission is 356 game-days, which equates to approximately 11 minutes and 52 seconds in real-time. This duration can be reduced significantly by researching drive upgrades such as the Antimatter Drive or Thorium Drive.

Unlocked Infrastructure and Resources

Successfully exploring Dune unlocks the Helios mission and provides access to several vital space structures:

  • Planet Cracker: A massive mining vessel that produces 1.5 uranium per second and increases uranium storage by 1,750. This is a primary source of uranium needed to power Lunar Outposts for unobtainium production.
  • Hydraulic Fracturer: Produces oil at a rate of 2.5 per second. While helpful for oil management, these structures are notably more effective once you have researched Space Manufacturing.
  • Spice Refinery: Refines the planet’s sand into Spice at 0.125 per second. Spice is a rare luxury resource that provides a +10% happiness boost but is gradually consumed over time.

Technological and Policy Unlocks

Reaching Dune is a prerequisite for advanced societal and scientific developments. It allows the research of Cosmological Technocracy and unlocks the ability to enact high-tier Policy/:

  • Technocracy: Increases the science cap by 20%.
  • Cosmological Libertarianism: Increases unobtainium production by 15%.
  • Order of the Stars: Increases faith production by 20% but restricts leaders to the priest job.

The Dune Cycle and Synergy

Dune is also one of the ten celestial cycles (represented by the glyph ⍫). If you have researched Numerology, the Dune cycle provides specific modifiers to your space production:

  • Planet Cracker uranium output: +10%.
  • Hydraulic Fracturer oil output: +50%.
  • HR Harvester energy production: -25% penalty.
  • Festival Effect: If Numeromancy is researched, holding a festival during the Dune cycle provides a 2x multiplier to uranium income.

Strategic Utility

The mission marks the transition into the Eludium Era. Because Planet Crackers provide a more stable and faster uranium supply than terrestrial Accelerators or trading with Dragons, the Dune mission is essential for players looking to mass-produce unobtainium and eventually eludium. Additionally, the spice produced here provides a reliable way to maintain kitten happiness as populations grow larger.
