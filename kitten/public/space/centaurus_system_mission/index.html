The Centaurus System Mission is a high-tier late-game space project that allows your kittens to travel to a warm, faraway star system. It represents one of the final stages of stellar exploration before reaching the theoretical “end of the universe”.

Mission Logistics and Costs

  • Unlock Prerequisite: You must first successfully complete the Rorschach Mission to unlock Centaurus.
  • Resource Requirements: The launch requires 100k Starcharts, 40k Titanium, 400k Science, 50k Kerosene, and 50k Thorium.
  • Travel Time: The base travel time is 120,000 game-days, which is approximately 2 days, 18 hours, and 40 minutes in real-time. This can be reduced by a factor of 75 (bringing it down to roughly 53 minutes) if you have researched both Thorium and Antimatter Drives.

Unlocked Infrastructure

Successfully reaching the Centaurus System provides access to powerful energy-generating structures:

  • Tectonic: A massive industrial project that rips open the planet near the Centaurus star to collect magma energy. It costs 600k Science, 500 Antimatter, and 75k Thorium to build and provides a base +25 Energy Production. It requires the Terraformation technology.
  • Molten Core: An advanced upgrade that costs 25M Science and 5M Uranium. Each level of Molten Core improves the effectiveness of Tectonic structures by 5%. It requires the Exophysics technology.

Strategic Progression

  • Challenge Unlocks: Completing the Centaurus Mission is the mandatory prerequisite to unlock the Energy Challenge. This challenge doubles energy consumption but rewards players with a permanent reduction in production penalties caused by negative energy.
  • Furthest Ring: Reaching the Centaurus System is the final requirement before you can launch the mission to the Furthest Ring, described as the end of the universe.
  • Endgame Utility: Because this mission and its associated buildings require massive amounts of Thorium, Antimatter, and Science, it is typically undertaken only after the player has established a robust “Shatter Engine” and significant science storage through structures like Space Beacons.
