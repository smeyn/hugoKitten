The Yarn Mission is a late-game space exploration project that involves travelling to Yarn, a Class M planet within the Helios star system. It is described as having a moderate climate, oxygen atmosphere, and seas.

Mission Logistics and Unlocks

  • Prerequisites: Players must successfully complete the Helios Mission before the Yarn Mission becomes available in the “Ground Control” section of the Space tab.
  • Launch Costs: The mission requires a significant investment of resources: 7,500 Starcharts, 35,000 Titanium, 350,000 Science, and 12,000 Kerosene.
  • Travel Time: The base travel time is 3,800 game-days, which is approximately 2 hours, 6 minutes, and 40 seconds in real-time. This duration can be reduced significantly by researching Thorium or Antimatter Drives.
  • Sequential Unlocks: Completing this mission unlocks the Yarn Planet and the subsequent mission to Umbra.

Yarn Infrastructure

Once the planet is reached, two specialized structures can be developed to scale the colony’s population and agricultural efficiency:

  • Terraforming Station: Costs 25 Antimatter, 5,000 Uranium, and 5,000 Kerosene. It provides +1 maximum kitten capacity.
  • Hydroponics: Requires the Hydroponics technology and costs 1 Unobtainium and 500 Kerosene. It increases maximum catnip by 10%, catnip production by 2.5%, and provides a 1.0% bonus to the kitten capacity of Terraforming Stations. Hydroponics also increases the overall catnip storage capacity by 10%, which stacks additively with the Refrigeration upgrade.

The Yarn Cycle

“Yarn” is also the name of one of the ten five-year cycles in the game (represented by the glyph ⍧). If Numerology has been researched, the Yarn cycle provides the following modifiers:

  • Production Bonuses: Hydroponics catnip production is increased by 100%.
  • Production Penalties: Research Vessel starchart production is reduced by 50%.
  • Festival Effect: If Numeromancy is also researched, holding a festival during the Yarn cycle provides a 2x multiplier to culture income.

Strategic Importance

In the late game, terraforming Yarn is a primary strategy for players aiming to maximize their kitten population (potentially reaching upwards of 60,000 kittens) to farm larger amounts of Paragon during long runs. This is achieved by stacking Terraforming Stations and leveraging the scaling bonuses from Hydroponics and AI Cores.
