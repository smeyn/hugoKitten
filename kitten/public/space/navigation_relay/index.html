Part of the locked Space Exploration Tee.

In Kittens Game, the Navigation Relay is a mid-to-late game Workshop upgrade that significantly enhances your space-based logistics. It is designed to work in tandem with your Satellites to make space exploration and resource management much more efficient.

Key Effects

The Navigation Relay serves as a force multiplier for your orbital network:

  • Satellite Efficiency: It provides a +15% bonus to the effectiveness of your Satellites.
  • Starchart Generation: Because it boosts Satellite efficiency, it indirectly increases your Starchart production. This is crucial for launching the “Long Range Spaceships” and outer-system missions like Jupiter and Saturn that we discussed earlier.
  • Energy Management: By making your existing Satellites more effective, you can often generate the starcharts you need without having to build as many individual units, saving you on the scaling Alloy and Science costs.

Unlock Requirements & Cost

To see the Navigation Relay in your Workshop, you must have advanced into the Space era:

Requirement Type Cost / Detail
Satellite Space Building You must have at least one Satellite deployed.
Orbital Engineering Technology The primary scientific prerequisite.
Science Resource 150,000 Science
Starcharts Resource 1,500 Starcharts
Alloy Resource 1,000 Alloy

Strategic Context

Given your current focus on Enriched Uranium and managing Temporal Distortion, the Navigation Relay is a “maintenance” upgrade that ensures your foundation remains solid as you push into the Dark Future.

  1. Scaling for the Farther Ring: As missions to Jupiter and beyond require thousands of Starcharts, the 15% boost from the Navigation Relay (especially when stacked with the Hubble Space Telescope) is essential to prevent a “Starchart drought.”
  2. Antimatter Preparation: To build Containment Chambers for Antimatter, you need massive Science caps. Since Satellites contribute to your Science cap via the Satellite Radio upgrade, the Navigation Relay helps push those caps even higher.
  3. Efficiency: It is a relatively “cheap” upgrade compared to the cost of building 10 additional Satellites in the late game, making it a high-priority purchase after a reset.

Expert Tip: If you have already started farming Void, the Navigation Relay helps stabilize your Starchart income so you can keep your Research Vessels and Space Beacons active even when the timeline is experiencing Distortion.
