Tectonic is an advanced, power-generating Space structure located in the Centaurus System. It allows your feline civilization to tap into massive amounts of geothermal energy by literally ripping open the planet near the star Centaurus.

1. Cost and Scaling

Constructing a Tectonic structure represents a major milestone due to its high, premium resource requirements:

  • Base Cost: 600,000 Science, 500 Antimatter, and 75,000 Thorium.
  • Prerequisite: You must first research the Terraformation technology to unlock it.
  • Price Ratio: 1.25.
  • The Space Penalty: Like all Space structures, Tectonics do not benefit from Metaphysics price ratio reductions (such as Golden Ratio or Renaissance). Each subsequent level will strictly cost 25% more than the last.

2. Effects and Synergies

  • High-Yield Power Generation: Each active Tectonic structure produces a flat +25 Watts (Wt) of energy to help power your grid.
  • Molten Core Synergy: As we recently discussed, every level of Molten Core you purchase in the Centaurus System permanently improves the energy output of your Tectonic structures by +5%.
  • Energy Challenge Requirement: Building at least one Tectonic structure is a mandatory milestone to successfully complete the Energy Challenge.

3. Strategic Value and the Late-Game Transition

In the mid-to-late game, Tectonics are highly sought after because they represent one of the most concentrated, reliable power sources available. However, their relative value shifts as you transition into the deep endgame:

  • The Production Bottleneck: Mass-building Tectonics can become highly taxing on your Antimatter storage and Thorium crafting pipelines.
  • The Hawking Radiation (HR) Shift: Once you establish a high-level Shatter Engine and begin time-skipping heavily, your calendar will reach the “Dark Future” (Year 40,000+). Because of the extreme calendar depth, the energy output of your HR Harvesters on Umbra will scale so massively that they will eventually eclipse your Tectonic structures, rendering Tectonics less critical for raw power in the deep endgame.
