Building on our look at Yarn’s space housing, Hydroponics is an advanced, late-game space structure constructed on the planet Yarn. It acts as a massive scaling mechanism for both your food supply and population capacity in the late game.

1. Unlocking & Construction Costs

To build Hydroponics, you must first complete the Yarn Mission and research the corresponding technology on your Science tree:

  • Hydroponics Technology: Costs 1M Science and 25 Relics (unlocked by Terraformation).
  • Structure Construction Cost: Each Hydroponics building costs 1 Unobtainium and 500 Kerosene.
  • Price Ratio: 1.15. Because it is a space structure, it does not benefit from Bonfire metaphysics price reductions.

2. Core Effects & Mechanics

Each Hydroponics station provides three distinct multipliers:

  • Catnip Production (+2.5% per building): Grants a global multiplier to your catnip generation.
  • Catnip Storage Capacity (+10% per building): Expands your maximum catnip cap. This bonus stacks additively with the Refrigeration workshop upgrade (+75% max catnip). For example, if you have researched Refrigeration and own 50 Hydroponics, your global catnip storage capacity is boosted by +575%.
  • Terraforming Station Synergy (+1.0% per building): Permanently increases the kitten housing bonus of your Terraforming Stations by +1.0% per structure.

3. Strategic Late-Game Value

Taming the Happiness Consumption Spike

As your civilization matures, keeping your kittens happy is vital to scaling your global output. However, keeping happiness high (e.g., above 100%) dramatically increases catnip consumption. A community sitting at 420% happiness suffers a massive 320% increase in kitten catnip consumption.

By mass-producing Hydroponics, you can compound your passive food production so heavily that you can easily sustain hundreds of hyper-productive, highly happy kittens without risking sudden winter starvations.

The Yarn & Piscine Cycle Factors

If you have researched Numerology, your active planetary cycle will directly scale the efficiency of your hydroponic farms:

  • Yarn Cycle (⍧): Temporarily boosts Hydroponics catnip production by +100%.
  • Piscine Cycle (): Temporarily penalises Hydroponics catnip production by -50%.

Orbital Population Scaling

By pairing your Hydroponics with Terraforming Stations, you can squeeze maximum housing out of orbit. Compounding this loop is one of the premier ways to push your overall kitten population well past standard limits. This populates your priestly ranks in the late game, enabling you to farm massive amounts of Faith and Paragon during resets.
