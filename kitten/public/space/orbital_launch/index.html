The Orbital Launch mission serves as the critical gateway in the sources, transitioning the colony from terrestrial development to the Space era. It is the first project available in the “Ground Control” section of the Space tab once the Rocketry technology has been researched. For a standard game run, the launch requires a stockpile of 250 Starcharts, 5,000 Catpower, 100,000 Science, and 15,000 Oil.

Requirements and Challenge Variations

The resource demands for this mission change significantly depending on the active game mode:

  • Standard Costs: As noted, the primary hurdles are the science and oil caps.
  • Black Sky Challenge: In this mode, the Starchart requirement is removed entirely (0). However, to compensate, the mission requires 1.1 million Science, 55,000 Catpower, and 165,000 Oil, representing an 11-fold increase for non-starchart resources.

Unlocks and Infrastructure

Successfully completing the Orbital Launch provides access to the foundational structures and locations of the star system:

  • Missions and Locations: It unlocks the Moon Mission and makes the planet Cath accessible.
  • Satellite Deployment: Players can begin deploying Satellites, which consume energy to provide a passive income of Starcharts (0.005/sec) and increase the science storage capacity of Observatories by 5%.
  • Space Elevator: This structure is essential for long-term expansion as it provides a 1% bonus to all space structures and reduces the oil requirements for future space missions and buildings by 5% (up to a 75% discount with diminishing returns).
  • Space Stations: These provide a 50% science bonus and housing for +2 kittens.

Strategic Considerations

  • The Oil Bottleneck: Strategic guides highlight that Oil management is the dominant constraint at this stage. While the launch itself costs 15,000 Oil, the next mission requires 45,000, and the first Moon Base requires 70,000, necessitating a rapid increase in Oil Wells and storage.
  • Resource Pivot: This milestone marks the end of “Phase 1” and “Phase 2” of the game; once space is unlocked, common terrestrial resources begin to matter less as the economy shifts toward Uranium, Unobtainium, and Antimatter.
  • Iron Will Mode: In this specialized mode, achieving an Orbital Launch is a prerequisite for obtaining the Space Oddity gold star achievement. Players in this mode often find the Catpower and Gold requirements for space missions particularly challenging due to the lack of a kitten workforce.
