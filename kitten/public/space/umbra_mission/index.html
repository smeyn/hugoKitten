The Umbra Mission is a late-game space project focused on exploring the supermassive black hole located at the centre of the Helios system. It represents a significant step into the furthest reaches of the star system, transitioning the colony into the deepest “Phase 3” of developmental progression.

Mission Logistics and Prerequisites

To launch the Umbra mission, your kittens must have already successfully explored Yarn. The resource requirements are substantial, reflecting the mission’s late-game status:

  • Starcharts: 25,000
  • Science: 500,000
  • Kerosene: 25,000
  • Thorium: 15,000

Successfully completing the launch unlocks the Umbra Planet and the subsequent Charon Mission.

The Umbra Cycle and Festivals

“Umbra” is also one of the ten five-year celestial cycles (represented by the glyph ⍦). If you have researched Numerology and Numeromancy, the cycle provides the following modifiers:

  • Production Penalties: During this cycle, Planet Cracker uranium output is reduced by 10% and Hydraulic Fracturer oil output decreases by 25%.
  • Production Boosts: Energy production from HR Harvesters (located on the Umbra planet) is increased by 50%.
  • Festival Multipliers: Holding a festival during the Umbra cycle provides a 1.5x income multiplier for coal, iron, titanium, and gold.

Umbra Infrastructure: HR Harvesters

The primary structure available on the Umbra planet is the HR Harvester (Hawking Radiation Harvester). These are unique energy-producing structures with the following characteristics:

  • Cost: 25 Relics and 1,250 Antimatter.
  • Energy Generation: They generate energy based on black hole evaporation, providing a base of 1 Watt that increases over time.
  • Scaling: The output is boosted by Dark Future years (years beyond 40,000) and Void Rifts, which provide a +10% boost per rift.

Strategic Context

In the broader scope of the sources’ endgame guides, the Umbra mission is part of the “Mid-Game” transition into chasing relics and established cryptotheology. Because the mission requires 15,000 Thorium, it necessitates a stable uranium-to-thorium refinement pipeline and high science caps, which are typically supported by earlier structures like Space Beacons and Research Vessels. Furthermore, the mission marks the point where Antimatter becomes a primary construction material for planetary infrastructure.
