Rorschach is a late-game celestial body in the Helios star system, specifically described as the biggest comet near Helios.

The Rorschach Mission

To explore Rorschach, you must first successfully complete the Kairo Mission. The mission represents a significant investment in late-game resources:

  • Starcharts: 15,000
  • Titanium: 80,000
  • Science: 500,000
  • Kerosene: 25,000.

While travel times for space missions can be significantly reduced by researching Thorium Drives (divided by 50) or Antimatter Drives (divided by 25), the base travel time for Rorschach is not explicitly listed alongside the other missions in the provided technical logs.

Strategic Impact and Unlocks

The primary function of the Rorschach mission is to serve as a gateway to the furthest reaches of the star system:

  • Expanded Vision: Upon completion, the sources note that while Rorschach is “impenetrable,” its discovery allows your kittens to “see farther”.
  • Centaurus Mission: Successfully exploring Rorschach is the mandatory prerequisite to unlock the Centaurus Mission, which leads to a warm, faraway star system.

Context in Late-Game Progression

In the broader “Phase 4” of game progression, Rorschach is part of the deep-space exploration arc that follows the stabilization of the Unobtainium and Antimatter economies. Because the mission requires a substantial 80,000 Titanium, players often need to have reached the “Titanium Era” high-storage milestones—such as building numerous Moon Bases, Cryostations, or Accelerators with the Energy Rift upgrade—to satisfy the mission’s resource caps.
