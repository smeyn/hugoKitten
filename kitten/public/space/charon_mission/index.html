The Charon Mission is a significant late-game space project that takes your kittens to a small, incredibly dense dwarf planet on the far edge of the Helios system. Known colloquially as “The Gates of Death” due to its hostile and barren environment, it represents one of the final frontiers of planetary exploration before reaching the Centaurus system.

Mission Requirements and Unlocks

  • Prerequisite: You must successfully complete the Umbra Mission before the Charon Mission becomes available.
  • Launch Costs: This project requires a massive stockpile of advanced resources: 75,000 Starcharts, 750,000 Science, 35,000 Kerosene, and 35,000 Thorium.
  • Unlocks: Successfully landing on the planet unlocks the Charon Planet tab and the ability to construct the Entanglement Station.

Entanglement Stations

Once Charon is reached, you can build Entanglement Stations, which are essential for advanced quantum computing and hashrate generation.

  • Cost: Each station costs 1,250 Relics, 5,250 Antimatter, and 5,000 Eludium.
  • Function: These stations consume 25 Wt of energy and 0.1 gigaflops per tick (produced by AI Cores) to generate 0.1 hashes per tick.
  • Strategic Utility: Hashes are used to increase your hashrate level, which provides a +25% bonus to relic production from Space Beacons for every level achieved. This is a core component of the “Antimatter Phase” of the endgame, where players aim to automate and scale relic generation.

The Charon Cycle

“Charon” is also the first of the ten five-year celestial cycles, represented by the glyph ⍙. If you have researched Numerology and Numeromancy, the cycle introduces the following global modifiers:

  • Production Penalties: During this cycle, Lunar Outpost unobtainium output is reduced by 10%.
  • Operational Costs: Gigaflop consumption for Entanglement Stations is increased by +100% (doubled).
  • Festival Bonus: Holding a festival during the Charon cycle provides a 1.5x income multiplier for catnip, wood, and minerals.

Strategic Considerations

The Charon Mission is often viewed as a major resource gate because of its Thorium and Starchart requirements. By this stage, players generally rely on a robust “Shatter Engine” to skip through non-productive years and harvest enough antimatter to afford the Entanglement Stations. Additionally, because Entanglement Stations consume gigaflops, players must carefully balance the number of AI Cores and stations to prevent an AI Apocalypse, ensuring that gigaflop production stays ahead of consumption without allowing the AI level to reach critical thresholds.
