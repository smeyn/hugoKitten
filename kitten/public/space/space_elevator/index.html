The Space Elevator is a foundational space structure in the sources, situated on the planet Cath, that serves as a critical bridge between terrestrial industry and orbital expansion. It is unlocked following a successful Orbital Launch and requires the research of both Orbital Engineering and Nanotechnology.

Costs and Core Effects

The structure requires 6,000 Titanium, 75,000 Science, and 50 Unobtainium to construct, featuring a price ratio of 1.15. Its primary benefits include:

  • Oil Efficiency: It reduces the oil requirements for space missions and buildings by 5% per level. This effect is subject to diminishing returns and is capped at a 75% reduction.
  • Space Structure Bonus: It provides a flat 1% bonus to the effectiveness of all other space structures.
  • Production Transfer: It transfers 0.1% of your global production specifically to Hydraulic Fracturers and Spice Refineries on Dune.

Strategic Synergies and Scaling

The true utility of Space Elevators depends heavily on late-game technological integration. The sources emphasize that Space Elevators are not particularly worth their cost until the Space Manufacturing workshop upgrade is researched.

  • Space Manufacturing Interaction: Once unlocked, each Factory provides a bonus to space production (3.75% per factory, or 4.5% with Factory Logistics) that amplifies the base bonus provided by Space Elevators.
  • Cycle Modifiers: If Numerology is researched, the Cath cycle doubles the transferred production bonus for oil, while the Kairo cycle reduces the overall transferred production bonus by 50%.

Endgame Utility and Management

In the late game, Space Elevators become a primary driver for Unobtainium and Time Crystal farming.

  • Unobtainium Production: They are essential for boosting the output of Lunar Outposts. Strategic advice suggests prioritizing the purchase of Space Elevators over Moon Bases and Orbital Arrays during short runs.
  • Profitability Calculations: Advanced players use a “break-even” formula to determine if building another elevator is worth it based on the remaining time in a run. For example, if the calculated gain in unobtainium is greater than half the construction cost, it is profitable to build and later sell the structure.
  • Reset Procedure: Because Space Elevators do not carry over during a reset, the sources recommend selling them all immediately before resetting to reclaim 50% of the invested resources.
