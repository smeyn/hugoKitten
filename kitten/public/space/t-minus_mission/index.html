The T-minus mission is a significant mid-to-late-game space project in Kittens Game that involves travelling to Terminus, described as a supermassive ice giant located at the far end of the Helios solar system. This mission serves as a critical bridge between the mid-game industry and the advanced resource management required for late-game space exploration.

Mission Logistics and Costs

To launch a mission to Terminus, you must first successfully complete the Piscine mission. The resource requirements for the launch are as follows:

  • Starcharts: 2,500
  • Titanium: 12,000
  • Science: 225,000
  • Kerosene: 750

The base travel time for the mission is 2,500 game-days, which is approximately 1 hour, 23 minutes, and 20 seconds in real-time. As with other missions, this duration can be divided by 25 with an Antimatter Drive, 50 with a Thorium Drive, or 75 if both are researched.

Critical Unlocks: Cryostations

Successfully completing the T-minus mission unlocks the Kairo mission and the T-Minus planet, but its most strategically vital unlock is the Cryostation. Cryostations are massive storage facilities that provide essential boosts to your global caps:

  • Storage Gains: Each station increases the maximum capacity for Wood and Minerals (+200K), Iron (+50K), Titanium (+7,500), Uranium (+5,000), Oil (+7,500), Unobtainium (+750), and Coal (+25K).
  • Titanium Bottleneck: Cryostations are identified as one of the most effective ways to increase titanium storage, which is necessary to reach the 125K cap required for the Space Manufacturing upgrade.
  • Unobtainium Progression: Because they provide 750 unobtainium storage each, they are also a primary method for reaching the 1,000-unit threshold required for the Eludium Era.

The T-minus Cycle

“T-minus” is also one of the ten five-year cycles within the Helios system, represented by the glyph ⍝. If you have researched Numerology or Numeromancy, this cycle introduces specific global modifiers:

  • Production and Storage: During this cycle, Sunlifter energy output is reduced by 50%, but Cryostation storage capacity is increased by 20%.
  • Festival Effect: Holding a festival during the T-minus cycle provides an Oil income x2 multiplier.

Strategic Utility

In the broader progression of the game, the T-minus mission is a gateway to “Phase 4,” where the player begins to focus on chrono-optimization and geometric growth. By providing a massive leap in storage capacity through Cryostations, it allows kittens to store the high-tier materials (like Eludium and Unobtainium) needed to build Chronospheres and prepare for self-sustaining resets.
