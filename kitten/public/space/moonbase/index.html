A Moon Base is a highly valuable, mid-to-late game Space structure located on Redmoon. Its primary function is to act as a massive storage locker, letting you scale your resource caps to bypass the harsh limits of Cath-based buildings.

1. Unlocking & Mission Requirements

To unlock the Moon Base, you must first complete the Moon Mission under your Space tab, which costs:

  • 500 Starcharts
  • 5,000 Titanium
  • 125,000 Science
  • 45,000 Oil

Completing this mission permanently unlocks both the Lunar Outpost (for gathering Unobtainium) and the Moon Base under the Redmoon celestial section.

2. Construction Costs & Price Ratio

Constructing your first Moon Base requires a steep entry investment of mid-game materials:

  • 700 Starcharts
  • 9,500 Titanium
  • 250 Concrete
  • 100,000 Science
  • 50 Unobtainium
  • 70,000 Oil
  • Price Ratio: 1.12 (and 1.05 for Oil). Because it is a Space structure, it does not benefit from Bonfire metaphysics price-reduction upgrades like Golden Ratio or Renaissance.

3. Core Storage Effects

Each Moon Base provides a flat, massive upgrade to your maximum caps across almost all major resources:

  • Maximum Catnip: +45,000
  • Maximum Wood: +25,000
  • Maximum Minerals: +30,000
  • Maximum Coal: +3,500
  • Maximum Iron: +9,000
  • Maximum Titanium: +1,250
  • Maximum Oil: +3,500
  • Maximum Unobtainium: +150

4. Energy Consumption & Deficit Behavior

  • Base Power Draw: Each Moon Base consumes 10 Watts (Wt) of active power.
  • The Deficit Penalty: If your global energy grid slips into a deficit (negative energy), your active Moon Bases will still function, but if you manually disable them to save power, you will instantly lose their entire storage capacity, potentially capping your resources and causing excess stockpiles to evaporate.

5. Key Late-Game Synergies & Upgrades

Because Moon Bases are one of only two structures in the entire game that expand your base Unobtainium capacity (the other being Cryostations), scaling them is critical to executing the late-game Chronosphere loop. You can exponentially boost their effectiveness using these key upgrades:

  • Antimatter Bases (Workshop Upgrade): Costs 15 Eludium and 250 Antimatter. This permanently slashes the energy consumption of your Moon Bases by 50%, bringing their power draw down to 5 Wt each.
  • AI Bases (Workshop Upgrade): Unlocked after Antimatter Bases. This links your Moon Bases to your quantum computing grid, causing every active AI Core to increase the storage capacity of your Moon Bases by +10% (which rises to +11% if you have the Transkittenism policy active).
  • Spaceports: Upgrading your Warehouses into Spaceports provides a passive +0.85% bonus to all Moon Base storage capacities per Spaceport built.
  • Global Multipliers: In the endgame, the flat storage additions of your Moon Bases are multiplied heavily by your Paragon reserves (+0.1% per point), Event Horizons (+10% global storage per level), and Void Rifts (+2% global storage per level).
