In Kittens Game, Jupiter is a pivotal location in the Space tab that marks your transition from basic lunar exploration to heavy industrialization. It is the primary gateway to the “Farther Ring” of the solar system.

Key Buildings & Missions

  • Jupiter Mission: Launching this mission unlocks the planet. It is relatively expensive in the mid-game, requiring a significant amount of Starcharts and Rocket Fuel.
  • Saturn Mission: Reaching Jupiter is a strict prerequisite for advancing to Saturn and the outer reaches of the system.
  • HE-Fuel (Hydro-Electric Fuel): Jupiter allows you to start utilizing hydrogen-based resources more effectively, which is essential for scaling your Rocketry efforts.

Strategic Importance

For a player at your stage—managing Enriched Uranium and Distortion—Jupiter serves a few specialized roles:

  1. Science Cap Scaling: Jupiter-related missions and buildings are often the key to pushing your Science Max into the range required for Antimatter and Relic technologies.
  2. Unobtainium Boost: While the Moon is your first source, Jupiter (and its moons in certain versions/mods) provides the infrastructure needed to automate and accelerate your Unobtainium grind.
  3. Space Elevators: Improving your presence at Jupiter often complements your Space Elevators, reducing the friction of moving resources between the ground and your orbital stations.

Managing the Starchart Bottleneck

If you are struggling to afford the Jupiter mission or its upgrades, remember the Hubble Space Telescope upgrade we discussed. It will provide the 30% boost to your Satellites and Research Vessels needed to generate the thousands of starcharts required for Jupiter operations.
