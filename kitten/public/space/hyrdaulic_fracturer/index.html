A Hydraulic Fracturer is a high-tier space structure situated on the planet Dune that produces a high-pressure stream of oil. It is a critical component of late-game resource management, specifically for sustaining the heavy oil requirements of the Eludium Era.

Acquisition and Costs

To access this building, you must first reach the Space tab by researching Rocketry and successfully complete the Dune Mission.

  • Mission Requirements: 1,000 Starcharts, 7,000 Titanium, 175k Science, and 75 Kerosene.
  • Building Cost: Each Hydraulic Fracturer costs 750 Starcharts, 1,025 Alloy, 150k Science, and 100 Kerosene.
  • Price Ratio: The building has a price ratio of 1.18.

Production and Scaling

The base effect of a Hydraulic Fracturer is the production of 2.5 oil per second. While powerful, its true utility comes from several late-game scaling mechanisms:

  • Space Manufacturing: Sources indicate that Hydraulic Fracturers are “not particularly effective” until the Space Manufacturing workshop upgrade is researched. Once active, Factories provide a bonus to space production (equivalent to 75% of the factory craft ratio), which significantly amplifies the output of these structures.
  • Space Elevators: These terrestrial structures provide a unique synergy, granting 0.1% of your global production specifically to Hydraulic Fracturers and Spice Refineries.
  • Orbital Arrays: These provide a flat 2% bonus to all space structures, including the Fracturer.

Environmental and Cyclical Modifiers

If you have researched Numerology, the current celestial cycle will drastically alter the efficiency of your oil production on Dune:

  • Dune Cycle: Provides a massive +50% boost to oil output.
  • Umbra Cycle: Imposes a -25% penalty to production.
  • Festivals: Under the Numeromancy upgrade, holding a festival during the T-minus cycle provides a 2x multiplier to oil income, which further synergises with your Fracturer infrastructure.

Strategic Utility

As you transition into the late game, oil becomes a significant bottleneck for producing Kerosene and maintaining Research Vessels. While early-game oil management relies on Oil Wells and Bio Labs, the Hydraulic Fracturer is the primary method for scaling oil production to the levels required for deep-space exploration and Antimatter generation. Strategic advice suggests that if your oil is constantly hitting its cap, you should consider using Engineers to automate the conversion of oil into Kerosene to ensure your Fracturers never halt production.
