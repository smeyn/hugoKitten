The Amphitheatre is an important early-to-mid-game building in Kittens Game, crucial for managing kitten happiness and initiating Culture production, which is vital for later scientific and religious progression.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of the Amphitheatre:

Unlocking and Cost

  • Prerequisite Technology: The Amphitheatre is unlocked after researching Writing. Writing costs 3600 Science and is unlocked by Engineering.
  • Resource Cost: Constructing an Amphitheatre requires a mix of common and crafted resources:
    • Wood: 200 units
    • Minerals: 1200 units
    • Parchment: 3 units (Parchment is a craftable resource made from Furs)
  • Price Ratio: The Amphitheatre has a price ratio of 1.15.

Effects and Function

The Amphitheatre serves two critical functions for societal development: culture production and unhappiness mitigation.

  • Culture Production: Each Amphitheatre produces +0.005 Culture per Tick. Culture is a special resource necessary to convert Parchment into Manuscripts in the Workshop.
  • Culture Storage: Each Amphitheatre increases the maximum Culture storage capacity by +50.
  • Happiness/Unhappiness: Amphitheatres reduce population penalties to happiness, providing -4.8% Unhappiness mitigation.

Strategic Importance

The decision to build Amphitheatres is a major early-game strategic step, as it addresses a key bottleneck in the technology tree:

  • Culture Grind: Amphitheatres are one of the earliest buildings to generate Culture. Culture is required to craft Manuscripts, which are necessary to research Theology and subsequent high-tier sciences like Astronomy. This resource requirement creates a significant hurdle, forcing players to focus on culture generation.
  • Managing Population Happiness: As your kitten population grows (by building Huts and Log Houses, which you should keep maxed out), they become unhappy. Since Happiness dictates the overall efficiency of your Kittens in their work, Amphitheatres are extremely important in the early game to reduce unhappiness and maintain high production levels.
    • It is advised to build Amphitheatres until they stop noticeably improving Happiness, which can be monitored via the ‘population penalty’ percentage on the top bar of the game.
  • Preparation for Temples: Amphitheatres help make obtaining Temples easier, as Temples are another structure that generates Culture.
  • Building Priority: When happiness starts becoming a serious problem, players should consider pushing for the Writing science to unlock Amphitheatres. You may need to temporarily employ all kittens as Hunters to stockpile the Parchment required to craft them.

Upgrades and Progression

  • Upgrade Path: The Amphitheatre can be upgraded to a Broadcast Tower. This upgrade is unlocked by researching Electronics.
  • Broadcast Tower Conversion: The Broadcast Tower upgrade offers a significant boost to culture production and unhappiness reduction, with a single tower potentially replacing an entire stock of Amphitheatres for happiness purposes. A single tower increases the culture cap as much as six Amphitheatres, and increases culture production as much as “two hundred”. There is generally little reason to delay this upgrade once the metal is available to build a tower.
  • Alternative Culture Buildings: Culture can also be generated by Temples and Chapels.
