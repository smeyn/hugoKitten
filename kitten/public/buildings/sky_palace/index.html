The Sky Palace is an advanced religious building that plays a crucial role in the late Phase 2 (the Unicorn/Time Crystal Era) progression by generating the unique resource, Alicorns.

Functionality and Effects

  • Prerequisite: The Sky Palace is unlocked after building at least one Ivory Citadel (a preceding unicorn religious upgrade).
  • Cost: It requires a substantial investment of 500 Tears, 125,000 Ivory, and 5 Megalith.
  • Unicorn Production: It provides a significant boost to unicorn generation by +50%.
  • Alicorn Generation: The Sky Palace enables a random event that grants one alicorn. The frequency of this event increases by 0.01% per game-day for each Sky Palace, meaning it takes 100 Palaces to increase the chance by 1% per day. Additionally, each Sky Palace produces a very slow passive rate of 0.00002 alicorns per tick (or 0.08 per year).
  • Other Bonuses: It also improves gold storage by +1% and increases ivory gained from Ivory Meteors by 5%.

Strategic Context

The Sky Palace is strategically important because it is a prerequisite for subsequent powerful religious upgrades and resources:

  • Progression Path: Building a Sky Palace is necessary to unlock the Unicorn Utopia upgrade.
  • Time Crystal Economy: The alicorns generated by the Sky Palace are essential because sacrificing 25 alicorns yields one Time Crystal [3, 4]. Alicorns are therefore a key input for the temporal economy [5].
  • Ivory Acquisition: The Sky Palace is part of a series of unicorn buildings (including Ivory Tower and Ivory Citadel) [1, 6] that are targeted early in the Unicorn Era to provide a passive, AFK source of ivory (via Ivory Meteors) [1, 5, 7], which is needed to trade for minerals with the Nagas [8].

In general late-game strategy, obtaining the Sky Palace and continuing the unicorn pathway (Unicorn Utopia, Sunspire) is advised to maximize the alicorn summon chance and conversion efficiency for generating Time Crystals [2, 9].
