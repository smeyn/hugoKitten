The Ivory Citadel is an advanced building within the Unicorn religious progression, critical for scaling late-game luxury resource production.

Building Details and Effects

The Ivory Citadel is unlocked after constructing at least one Ivory Tower.

  • Cost: Building an Ivory Citadel requires 50 Tears and 50,000 Ivory.
  • Effect on Unicorns: It significantly improves unicorn generation by +25%.
  • Ivory Generation: The Ivory Citadel enables a random event called Ivory Meteors, which generate ivory. The frequency of this event increases by 0.05% per game-day for each Ivory Citadel.

Strategic Context

The Ivory Citadel sits on the path toward the most powerful religious buildings, playing a key role in setting up the Unicorn/Time Crystal Era:

  1. Religious Progression: The Ivory Citadel is a prerequisite for constructing the Sky Palace.
  2. Unicorn Economy: In the late game, the overall religious path relies on generating Unicorns, which are then sacrificed to produce Tears [2, 3]. Tears are then refined into Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS) to produce Black Pyramids, which unlock Leviathan trade [4-6].
  3. Ivory Source: Strategically, Ivory Citadels are built primarily because they provide a valuable, passive, AFK source of ivory via the Ivory Meteor event [1, 7]. Ivory is a necessary resource for trading with the Nagas for minerals [8, 9] and is also needed to construct further unicorn production buildings [10]. In runs focused on maximizing ivory returns early, the strategy recommends building as many Ivory Citadels as possible before moving on to higher-tier buildings like Sky Palaces, Unicorn Utopias, and Sunspires [7].
