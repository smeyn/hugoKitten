The Unicorn Tomb is an early building within the Unicorn religious progression chain, serving as a direct upgrade to the Unicorn Pasture.

This structure is vital for establishing the infrastructure necessary to scale luxury and temporal resources like Tears and Time Crystals in the mid-to-late game.

Details and Effects

Category Detail
Prerequisite The Unicorn Tomb is an upgrade available after successfully building a Unicorn Pasture [1, 2].
Cost Building a Unicorn Tomb requires 5 Tears and 500 Ivory [1, 3].
Effect It provides a 5% improvement to unicorn generation (Unicorns per Tick +5%) [1, 3].
Progression Completing at least one Unicorn Tomb is the direct prerequisite for unlocking the Ivory Tower.

Context in Religious Progression

The Unicorn Tomb is placed early in the sequence of unicorn buildings that scale unicorn production and eventually lead to the coveted Time Crystal economy:

Unicorn Pasture –> Unicorn Tomb –> Ivory Tower to Ivory Citadel –> Sky Palace –> Unicorn Utopia –> Sunspire

The entire chain is essential because the Unicorns generated are later sacrificed in Ziggurats to produce Tears, which are required to build the Unicorn Tomb itself and subsequent structures.

Challenge Reward

The Unicorn Tomb gains a special utility if you complete the Unicorn Tears challenge. As the base reward for completing this challenge, Unicorn Tombs receive the additional benefit of increasing maximum faith based on Transcendence Tier.
