The Void Rift is an advanced time-manipulation building that provides powerful, permanent boosts to both your storage capacity and energy production in the late game.

Acquisition and Costs

  • Prerequisite: To unlock the Void Rift, you must first acquire the Void Aspiration upgrade.
  • Cost: The first Void Rift requires an investment of 75 Void.
  • Price Ratio: The cost scales with a 1.3 price ratio for each subsequent purchase.

Core Effects

  • Global Storage Boost: Each Void Rift permanently increases all of your global resource storage capacity by 2%. This specific storage bonus stacks additively with the 10% storage bonus provided by Event Horizons, and scales multiplicatively alongside other storage bonuses like those from Paragon.
  • Energy Production: Each Void Rift also increases the energy production of your HR Harvesters by 10%. Because HR Harvesters scale infinitely with Dark Future years, accumulating Void Rifts is an excellent way to drastically multiply your late-game power output.

Endgame Strategy

In the deep endgame, acquiring dozens of Void Rifts becomes a primary objective to accelerate your resource velocity. A common strategic milestone is to amass around 200 million Void to purchase 50 Void Rifts.

This massive expansion to your global storage capacity is critical for establishing a self-sustaining perpetual reset loop. By expanding your storage limits, you can afford to build 70 to 100+ Chronospheres, which allows you to carry over exponentially more resources every time you reset your game and guarantees that each run begins with a larger foundation of capital.
