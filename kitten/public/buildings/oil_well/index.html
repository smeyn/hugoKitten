The Oil Well is a significant resource-producing building in Kittens Game, marking a progression into the era of advanced fuel and energy infrastructure.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of the Oil Well based on the sources:

1. Construction and Unlocks

  • Prerequisite Technology: The Oil Well building is unlocked by researching the Chemistry technology.
  • Cost: The base cost to construct an Oil Well is 50 Steel, 25 Gears, and 25 Scaffold.
  • Energy Consumption: Once the Pumpjack upgrade is applied, the Oil Well consumes 1 Energy per tick to operate.

2. Purpose and Production

The Oil Well’s primary function is to introduce and produce Oil, which is crucial for subsequent advanced crafting and energy production.

  • Output: The Oil Well produces +0.02 Oil per Tick.
  • Storage: Each Oil Well provides +1500 Max Oil capacity.

Oil is classified as a Common Resource, produced by Oil Wells or refined from catnip by upgraded Bio Labs. Oil is vital for constructing Calciners and is later processed into Kerosene, a rocket fuel.

3. Upgrades and Strategic Importance

Oil Wells are critical, as Oil quickly becomes a bottleneck resource during the transition to space exploration.

The output of Oil Wells can be boosted by several Workshop upgrades:

Upgrade Name Effect Prerequisite
Pumpjack Improves effectiveness by +45% (additive with other effects). Also adds automation and causes the Oil Well to consume 1 Energy. Mechanization.
Oil Refinery Improves effectiveness by +35%. Combustion.
Oil Distillation Output is improved by +75%. Rocketry.

The need for Oil Wells is frequently emphasized:

  • Early Space Era: In the pre-Metaphysics, “early Space” phase, Oil is slow to produce, making it necessary to build Oil Wells quickly and let them “fill up” their production/storage.
  • Grinding: Grinding out the necessary Steel for Oil Wells is recommended over relying on Tankers for storage cap increases.
  • Coal Management: When grinding coal for Oil Wells, players are advised to turn off Steamworks or anything else consuming coal to maximize the available coal.
  • Overbuilding: Players should build more Oil Wells than they initially need to help increase the oil production rate.

4. Policy Connection

The Full Industrialization policy, which becomes available after researching Industrialization and choosing either Strip Mining or Clear Cutting, boosts the production of Iron, Coal, and Titanium. Although it doesn’t directly boost the Oil Well, it is generally preferred over Sustainability in runs where many factories (which consume Oil indirectly via Kerosene crafting) are built.
