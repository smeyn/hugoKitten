The Harbour is a crucial mid-game storage building in Kittens Game, essential for expanding resource limits, especially as your civilization begins advanced production and trading operations.

Here is a detailed overview of the Harbour:

Unlocking and Cost

The Harbour is unlocked after reaching the Navigation stage of your technology tree, and it requires crafted materials to build:

  • Prerequisite Technology: Navigation.
  • Resource Cost: Building a Harbour costs a combination of processed materials:
    • Scaffold: 5 units
    • Slab: 50 units
    • Plate: 75 units. Plate is listed as a craftable resource required for early and mid-game buildings, especially Harbours.
  • Price Ratio: The Harbour has a price ratio of 1.15.

Primary Function: Resource Storage

Harbours are categorized as general storage buildings designed to increase the maximum capacity of numerous common and uncommon resources:

Resource Storage Increase per Harbour
Catnip +2,500
Wood +700
Minerals +950
Iron +150
Coal +100
Gold +25
Titanium +50

Scaling and Upgrades

The effectiveness of Harbours can be enhanced through various Workshop and technology upgrades:

  • Trade Ship Capacity Bonus (Expanded Cargo): The Expanded Cargo upgrade, unlocked by Navigation, grants a bonus of +1% to Harbour capacity per Trade Ship. This bonus is subject to diminishing returns past 168.75% and is capped at 225%.
  • Reactor Capacity Boost (Reactor Vessel): The Reactor Vessel upgrade increases the Expanded Cargo limit by +5% per Reactor.
  • Coal Storage (Barges): The Barges upgrade increases the maximum Coal storage provided by Harbours by 50%.
  • General Storage Upgrades: Harbours also benefit from global storage upgrades initially designed for Barns and Warehouses, such as Expanded Barns, Reinforced Barns, Titanium Barns, Alloy Barns, and Concrete Barns, which increase the storage capacity for wood, minerals, and iron.

Strategic Role

Harbours are vital for maintaining economic momentum in the mid-game:

  • Resource Management: They address the strategic need to increase resource storage limits (or “caps”). If progress is stalled because resource storage limits are too low, building storage buildings like Harbours is necessary.
  • Progression Path: The early policy choice Clear Cutting is recommended because the resulting wood production boost is crucial for constructing Harbours, Observatories, and Trade Ships.
  • Titanium Buff: Harbours are among the structures that provide an early boost to Titanium storage capacity, which is necessary before more advanced storage options become available.
