The Ivory Tower is an advanced religious building within the Unicorn progression pathway that helps establish the production of unicorns and associated resources needed for late-game advancement.

Here is a detailed summary of the Ivory Tower:

Building Details and Effects

Category Detail
Cost 25 Tears and 25,000 Ivory.
Prerequisite You must complete at least one Unicorn Tomb before you can build an Ivory Tower.
Unicorn Generation It improves unicorn generation by 10%.
Alicorn/Time Crystal Progression The Ivory Tower unlocks the Unicorn Rifts random event, which generates unicorns. Each Ivory Tower increases the frequency of this event by 0.05% per game-day (or 1% per day per 20 towers). It also provides a boost to your unicorn rift amount equal to 10% of your unicorn ziggurat bonus to unicorn production.

Strategic Context

The Ivory Tower is a key step in the religious pathway focused on Unicorns and Time Crystals (Phase 2 progression) [1, 2]:

  1. Religious Hierarchy: The Ivory Tower is the prerequisite structure that leads to the construction of the Ivory Citadel [3].
  2. Unicorn Resource Chain: The primary religious path involves obtaining Unicorns (often initially through Hunting) [4, 5] and then sacrificing them in a Ziggurat to produce Tears [1, 6, 7]. These Tears are then used along with Ivory to construct the Ivory Tower. This process is crucial because it ultimately leads to the production of Alicorns, which are refined into Time Crystals [6, 8].
  3. Efficiency in Specialized Runs: In long runs, such as the Iron Will 40K challenge, players focus on building the unicorn production chain for resources like Ivory, which is then traded for minerals with the Nagas [9, 10]. The unicorn resources produced by Ivory Towers (Unicorns via Rifts) and the subsequent Ivory Citadels (Ivory via Meteors) are often prioritized as a passive, AFK source of these luxury goods [11].
