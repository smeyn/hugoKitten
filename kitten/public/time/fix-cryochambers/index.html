The Fix Cryochambers action allows you to repair a Used Cryochamber, making it as good as new after it breaks from carrying a kitten through a game reset.

Costs and Requirements

  • Repair Cost: Fixing a single cryochamber costs a flat rate of 100 Time Crystals, 500 Void, and 3,000 Temporal Flux.
  • Prerequisites: The repair button will only appear on your menu if you currently possess a Used Cryochamber and have already purchased the Chronoforge upgrade.

Strategic Mechanics

  • Bypassing Build Limits: By fixing broken chambers rather than just building new ones, you can actually accumulate more Cryochambers than your active number of Chronospheres. However, you should note that the maximum number of kittens you can bring back with you on your next reset is still strictly limited by your number of active Chronospheres.
  • Challenge Complications: If you use cryochambers to bring kittens into certain restrictive challenges (such as Atheism or Pacifism), you will not be able to reclaim your reserve resources to help pay for their repairs. This means you will be forced to generate all of the necessary Time Crystals, Void, and Temporal Flux entirely from scratch during that challenge run, or risk losing those kittens on your next reset.
