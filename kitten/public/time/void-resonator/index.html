Continuing our journey through late-game temporal structures, the Void Resonator is an advanced, game-changing building located under the Void Space section of your Time tab. It is unlocked after researching the Paradox Theory technology (which costs 1,000,000 Science, 40 Time Crystals, and 250 Void).

Its primary function is to transform how you generate religious prestige in the late game by creating a powerful bridge between time-skipping and faith.

The Order of the Void Synergy

The Void Resonator works hand-in-hand with the Order of the Void metaphysics upgrade. Normally, Order of the Void passively converts 10% of your faith production directly into Worship.

When you build Void Resonators, they multiply this effect. The game calculates the conversion using the following formula: {Worship Generated} = {Faith Production} * 0.1 * (1 + ({Void Resonators} * 0.1))

In other words, each Void Resonator you construct adds a flat +10% bonus to your passive Order of the Void conversion rate.

The Game-Changer: Time-Skip Integration

Crucially, Void Resonators allow the Order of the Void conversion to trigger every time you combust a Time Crystal to skip a year.

Normally, time-skipping bypasses passive production ticks. But with Void Resonators active:

  1. Every time you shatter a Time Crystal, you advance the calendar by one year.
  2. The game calculates your massive yearly faith production.
  3. The Void Resonators instantly inject a huge portion of that faith directly into your Worship pool.

Strategic Late-Game Value

When you establish a high-level Shatter Engine (Resource Retrieval + Chrono Furnaces), you will be skipping thousands of years in rapid succession. Void Resonators turn these time-skips into a passive faith-farming machine.

Instead of waiting on manual praises, simply skipping years will passively flood your pool with billions of units of Worship. This makes pushing your civilization up to Transcendence Tier 20 and beyond incredibly fast and effortless, permanently compounding your global production multipliers via the Solar Revolution bonus.
