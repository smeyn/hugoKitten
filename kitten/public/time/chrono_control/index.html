Chronocontrol (or Chrono Control) is an advanced, late-game Time structure under the Void Space section of your Time tab. It plays a crucial role in managing time skips, temporal flux, and anomalies.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of its prerequisites, costs, effects, and late-game synergies:

1. Prerequisites & Costs

To unlock Chronocontrol, you must first research the Paradox Theory technology, which costs 1,000,000 Science, 40 Time Crystals, and 250 Void.

Once unlocked, constructing your first Chronocontrol costs:

  • 30 Time Crystals
  • 500 Void
  • 3,000 Temporal Flux
  • Price Ratio: 1.25 (meaning subsequent structures cost 25% more)
  • Energy Consumption: Consumes 15 Watts (Wt) of active power

2. Core Effects & Synergies

  • Temporal Paradox Extension: Chronocontrol directly increases the duration (in days) of a Temporal Paradox, an event occasionally triggered by active Chronospheres.
  • Distortion Synergy: Once you purchase the Distortion workshop upgrade (unlocked by Paradox Theory), each level of Chronocontrol adds an extra +3 days to your Temporal Paradoxes.
  • Unlocking Chronosurge: Building at least one Chronocontrol is a hard requirement to unlock the Chronosurge upgrade in your Workshop.

3. Strategic Importance: The Active Flux Loop

Unlocking and building Chronocontrol is the vital gateway to transitioning your temporal flux generation from passive to active.

By building Chronocontrol and purchasing Chronosurge (which costs 25 Time Crystals, 100k Unobtainium, 750 Void, and 6,500 Temporal Flux), your Chronospheres will automatically gather 1 temporal flux per year. Since shattering (combusting) a Time Crystal immediately advances the calendar by one year, every single time skip you perform will instantly generate flux. This is absolutely essential for the late-game “Cryo Engine” strategy, allowing you to instantly generate the massive amounts of flux (3,000 per chamber) required to repair used Cryochambers and carry over hundreds of kittens across resets to farm millions of Paragon.
