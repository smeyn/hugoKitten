What is Controlled Delay?

In Kittens Game, Controlled Delay is an advanced upgrade located in the Chronoforge section of your Time tab. It is designed to help you pace out automated time-skips in the late-to-end game.

Prerequisites & Unlocking

Because Controlled Delay is a Chronoforge upgrade, it is hidden until you unlock its parent section:

  1. Tachyon Theory: You must first research the Tachyon Theory technology on your Science tree (which requires researching Chronophysics beforehand).
  2. Chronoforge Workshop Upgrade: Once Tachyon Theory is researched, you must purchase the Chronoforge upgrade in your Workshop. This costs 500,000 Science, 5 Relics, and 10 Time Crystals and officially unlocks the Chronoforge sub-section on your Time tab.

Once the Chronoforge section is unlocked, Controlled Delay will be visible and available for purchase.

Cost & Price Ratio

  • Time Crystals: 1
  • Gears: 10
  • Price Ratio: 1.0 — Crucially, because the price ratio is a flat 1.0, the cost of this upgrade never increases as you purchase subsequent levels.

Effects & Strategic Value

  • Paced Automation: Each purchased level of Controlled Delay forces your Chrono Furnace automation to wait 0.2 seconds between using 500% fuel to trigger a time-skip.
  • Active Gameplay Window: This upgrade was explicitly created to allow you to perform meaningful in-game actions (such as praising, adoring, or trading) during Chrono Furnace automation. Without a Controlled Delay, a massive backlog of accumulated chrono furnace fuel would fire instantly, skipping time too quickly for you to safely interact with the game.
  • Limitations: Toggling the automation button on the Controlled Delay itself has no gameplay effect, and it has no impact on batch uses of the manual “Use Temporal Energy” button.
