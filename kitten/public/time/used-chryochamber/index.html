A Used Cryochamber is an “Unsealed Cryochamber” that is currently “Not in operating condition”. In this broken state, the structure does absolutely nothing, but it can be repaired.

Cryochambers become “used” after you perform a game reset and successfully utilize them to carry your kittens over into your new run.

To restore a Used Cryochamber and make it as good as new, you must use the Fix Cryochambers action, which costs a flat rate of 100 Time Crystals, 500 Void, and 3,000 Temporal Flux. Keep in mind that the option to repair them will only appear on your menu if you have already purchased the Chronoforge upgrade.
