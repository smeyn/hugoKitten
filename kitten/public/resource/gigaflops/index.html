Gigaflops is the raw computational throughput unit generated by AI Cores. It measures computational work per second and directly governs how fast your AI Level (G-Level) increases toward the AI Apocalypse.

How Gigaflops Work

  • Source: Every powered AI Core produces Gigaflops.

  • Energy Demand: AI Cores consume large amounts of electricity (scaling per core). If your energy grid enters a brownout, Gigaflop output drops or halts completely.

  • Conversion to AI Level: Gigaflops act like an experience bar. When cumulative Gigaflops reach a dynamic capacity threshold, your AI Level increases by 1, resetting the Gigaflop counter to fill the next tier.

  • Scaling Threshold: The Gigaflop capacity required to reach the next AI Level scales exponentially:

    {Required Gigaflops} = {Base} * (1 + {AI Level})^{2 to 3}

Upgrades That Modify Gigaflops

Several late-game Workshop and Space upgrades scale or accelerate Gigaflop output:

Upgrade System Effect
Neural Networks Workshop Increases base Gigaflops produced per AI Core
Machine Learning Workshop Boosts computational speed and increases science caps
Quantum Computing Workshop Multiplies Gigaflop efficiency and alters AI energy draw
Antihash / Containment Moon / Deep Space Slows down or counters runaway Gigaflop accumulation

The Gigaflop Threat: AI Apocalypse (G-Level)

As Gigaflops accumulate and elevate your AI Level, the AI system becomes increasingly dangerous:

  • Level 1–14 (Benign / Productive): Minor benefits to science limits, calculation automation, and space calculations.
  • Level 15+ (Hostility / Rebellion Begins):
    • The AI begins consuming erratic, massive amounts of electrical power.
    • It starts randomly purging resources, crashing satellites, and deleting buildings (such as destroying Moon Bases, Factories, or Space Stations).
    • The message log will warn you of rogue AI behaviors.

How to Control Gigaflop Growth

  1. Toggle AI Cores Off: If your AI Level is approaching Level 14–15 and you do not have enough Antimatter to control it, turn AI Cores off. Inactive cores produce zero Gigaflops.
  2. Antimatter & Antihash: Sinking Antimatter into containment and antihash structures offsets the net rate of Gigaflop conversion into danger levels.
  3. Resetting the Counter: Resetting your game (or triggering specific late-game containment protocols) clears the accumulated AI Level back to zero.
