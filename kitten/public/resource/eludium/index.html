Eludium is a highly valuable, late-game craftable resource in Kittens Game. Its primary purpose is to enable the construction of the game’s final tier of housing buildings and to unlock powerful technological upgrades.

Characteristics and Acquisition

  1. Crafting: Eludium is a composite alloy created by combining two other advanced resources:

    • 2,500 Alloy
    • 1,000 Unobtainium
    • The crafting ability is unlocked by researching the Exometallurgy science (formerly Advanced Exogeology) [1, 3].

  2. Prerequisites for Crafting:

    • Exometallurgy Science: Costs 325K Science and 350 Blueprints. This technology is unlocked after researching Exogeology.
    • Unobtainium Storage: Achieving Eludium status requires substantial Unobtainium storage (1,000 Unobtainium) which means building multiple Moon Bases or a Cryostation.

Primary Uses

Eludium is required for constructing the highest tier of housing and for high-level upgrades:

  • Eludium Huts (Housing): The Eludium Huts upgrade reduces the price ratio of Huts by 10% [5, 6]. To purchase this upgrade, you need 275K Science and 125 Eludium [3, 6].
  • Workshop Upgrades: Eludium is necessary for numerous advanced Workshop upgrades, often enhancing high-tier buildings:
    • Dark Energy: Increases Max Catnip, Wood, Minerals, Coal, Iron, Titanium, and Gold storage from Accelerators by +250% [6].
    • Tachyon Accelerators: Increases the same Accelerator storage capacities by +500% [6].
    • Tachyon Drive: A rocket engine that sets space travel speed to 50 (or 75 combined with Antimatter Drive) [7].
    • Cryocomputing: Reduces Data Center energy consumption from 2Wt to 1Wt [8].
    • Quantum Dot Cells: Improves Solar Farm energy production by +15% in winter and +30% in spring and autumn [6].
    • Antimatter Fission/Reactors/Drive: Required for various Antimatter-related upgrades [8, 9].
    • Chronoengineers: Improves Engineer effectiveness and allows them to work during time skips [7].
  • Space Structures: Eludium is used in the construction of the Spaceport [10].
  • Cryptotheology: Eludium is a cost component for advanced Cryptotheology buildings such as the Relic Station [8].

Strategic Context

The process of acquiring Eludium defines a major phase of the game progression, known as the Eludium Era.

  • This era requires increasing the Unobtainium Production Rate to a reasonable level, primarily through Planet Crackers (to generate Uranium) and Lunar Outposts (to convert Uranium to Unobtainium) [11].
  • Achieving Eludium Huts is considered the next major checkpoint after Concrete Huts [12, 13]. Once Eludium Huts are built, the player is considered ready for the subsequent Unicorn/Time Crystal Era, provided the Unobtainium generation rate is high enough (around 1 Unobtainium per second or 1,100 Paragon plus the Renaissance upgrade) [14].

Analogy: If Alloy is the steel frame of a skyscraper, and Unobtainium is the unique, high-strength glass that allows it to reach impossible heights, then Eludium is the final, specialized rivet that binds them together, enabling construction of structures (like Eludium Huts) that define the architectural apex of the kitten civilization.
