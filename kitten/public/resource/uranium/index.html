Uranium is a critical, late-game resource primarily used for nuclear power, advanced material refinement, and storage enhancement in Kittens Game.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of its acquisition, storage, and uses:

Acquisition and Production

Uranium is an uncommon resource that can be acquired through three main methods, with one being an advanced crafting method and the others relying on trade and late-game buildings:

  1. Trade (Primary Source): Uranium can be bought from the Dragons in exchange for Titanium [1, 2]. Dragons will sell 1 Uranium with a 95% chance of success. The required technology to unlock the Dragons is Nuclear Fission [3, 4].
  2. Refinement: Uranium can be refined from Titanium using the Accelerator building.
    • One Accelerator produces $0.0025$ Uranium per tick (or $0.0125$ per second) but consumes $0.015$ Titanium per tick [5, 6].
    • In comparison, a Lunar Outpost consumes Uranium 140 times faster than a single Accelerator produces it, highlighting the importance of multiple accelerators or trade [6, 7].
  3. Quarry Upgrade: After researching Orbital Geodesy (unlocked by Satellites technology), Quarries will produce a small amount of Uranium at a rate of $0.0005$ per tick [5, 8]. This rate is boosted by the Enriched Uranium workshop upgrade (+25%) [5, 9].
  4. Space Production: Uranium is produced by Planet Crackers [1, 10]. Production from Planet Crackers is affected by the Dune cycle (-10% effect without Numerology, +10% effect with Numerology) [11].

Uses and Upgrades

Uranium is essential for progressing into the space and atomic eras of the game:

  • Refining Unobtainium: Uranium is refined into Unobtainium by a Lunar Outpost [1, 6]. This is a critical step for unlocking Metaphysics [6, 12].
  • Fueling Reactors: Uranium is consumed by the Reactor building at a rate of $0.001$ per tick to generate energy [13]. The Enriched Uranium upgrade reduces this consumption by 25% [9, 13].
  • Refining Thorium: Uranium is used to craft Thorium at a rate of 250 Uranium per unit of Thorium [14]. This is unlocked by the Thorium science [15].
  • Advanced Buildings: Uranium is a cost component for several advanced buildings, including the Accelerator (25 Uranium) [5] and the Reactor (25 Blueprints, 50 Concrete, 5,000 Plate, and 3,500 Titanium) [13].
  • Workshop Upgrades: Uranium is required for several high-tier upgrades, such as:
    • Energy Rifts (from Accelerators) [16].
    • Stasis Chambers [16].
    • Void Energy [16].
    • Nuclear Plants [8].
    • Augmentations [9].

Storage Capacity

The default maximum storage capacity for Uranium is 250 [17].

  • This capacity is mainly increased by building Reactors, as each Reactor increases the maximum Uranium storage by +250 [13].
  • The Accelerator building is also a key component for storage upgrades, as Accelerators, with the Energy Rifts upgrade, provide substantial storage capacity for common resources, including Uranium [16].

Uranium represents a bottleneck resource in the mid-to-late game [18]. Its acquisition and refinement pathway is critical because Unobtainium, the resource it creates, is necessary to unlock the powerful and permanent upgrades found in the Metaphysics system [6, 19].

What Produces Uranium

Uranium, a critical resource in the later stages of the game, is primarily acquired through three methods: trading with the Dragons, production via upgraded Quarries, and refinement using Accelerators.

Here is a breakdown of what produces or provides Uranium:

1. Production via Buildings and Upgrades

Uranium can be produced directly using advanced buildings and upgrades:

  • Quarry (with Orbital Geodesy): Quarries will provide a small amount of uranium once the Orbital Geodesy upgrade is purchased [1, 2]. Quarries produce uranium at a rate of 0.0005 per tick.
    • Orbital Geodesy requires Satellites technology and costs 150,000 Science, 1,000 Alloy, and 35,000 Oil.
  • Accelerator: This building refines titanium into uranium. Accelerators produce 0.0025 uranium per tick (or 0.0125 per second) [3, 4].
    • The Accelerator building is unlocked by researching the Particle Physics science [3, 5]. It costs 7,500 Titanium, 125 Concrete, and 25 Uranium.
  • Planet Cracker: Uranium is produced by the Planet Cracker structure [1, 6]. This structure’s output is affected by the Dune cycle, which can increase its uranium output by +10% [7].
  • Reactor (Consumption Reduction): While Reactors consume Uranium [8], the Enriched Uranium upgrade reduces this consumption by 25% and also provides a +25% uranium production bonus that applies only to Quarries [9].

2. Acquisition via Trade

The most straightforward way to initially acquire Uranium is through trade:

  • Dragons: Uranium can be bought from the Dragons civilization, in exchange for Titanium.
    • Dragons sell 1 Uranium and the trade has a 95% chance of success (reaching 100% chance with 6 Embassy levels) [10, 11].
    • Dragons are unlocked after researching Nuclear Fission [12].

3. Production Boosts

Uranium production can be boosted by:

  • Cycles (Dune): The Dune cycle doubles Uranium income when the Numerology metaphysics upgrade is researched [7].
  • Enriched Uranium: This Workshop upgrade increases Uranium production by +25% (but only applies to Quarries) [9].
  • Planet Busters: This upgrade doubles the output of the Planet Cracker structure [13].

Uranium is an important resource, particularly in the later stages of the game, as it is needed to refine Unobtainium via a Lunar Outpost [1, 4].
