Tears are a critical resource associated with the Unicorn progression pathway, categorizing as a Unique Resource and a Luxury Resource in Kittens Game [1, 2]. They are essential inputs for escalating the religious infrastructure and advancing toward the mid-to-late-game economy, particularly by leading to the highly valuable Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS) [3-5].

Acquisition and Persistence

Tears are acquired exclusively through a specific religious action:

  • Production via Sacrifice: Tears are gained by sacrificing unicorns [1, 6].
  • Scaling with Ziggurats: Each time unicorns are sacrificed (an action costing 2500 unicorns) [6, 7], the amount of tears received is equal to the total number of Ziggurats the player has built [6, 7]. Therefore, players are advised to construct a minimum of 10, or preferably 25–30, Ziggurats before converting unicorns to maximize the efficiency of Tear generation [7, 8].
  • Loss on Reset: As a luxury resource, Tears are lost upon game reset, alongside Furs, Ivory, Spice, Unicorns, Alicorns, and Necrocorns [1, 2].

Uses and Strategic Importance

Tears are consumed for two primary purposes: investing in powerful religious infrastructure and producing the required resources for the endgame:

  1. Building Unicorn Structures: Tears are necessary for constructing increasingly potent religious buildings that scale unicorn production and further the resource chain:

    • Unicorn Tomb: Costs 5 Tears and 500 Ivory [9].
    • Ivory Tower: Costs 25 Tears and 25,000 Ivory [10].
    • Ivory Citadel: Costs 50 Tears and 50,000 Ivory [11].
    • Sky Palace: Costs 500 Tears, 125,000 Ivory, and 5 Megalith [11].
    • Unicorn Utopia: Costs 5,000 Tears and 1,000,000 Ivory [12].
    • Marker: Costs 5,000 Tears (along with Megalith, Spice, and Unobtainium) [13].

  2. Refinement to Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS): Tears are refined to create Sorrow, also known as Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS) [4, 5]. This conversion costs 10,000 Tears per 1 Sorrow [4]. BLS is a prerequisite for constructing Black Pyramids [4, 14], which are necessary to unlock the ability to trade with the Leviathans for advanced resources like Time Crystals and Relics [3, 14, 15].

This resource forms a critical loop in the game’s late progression: Unicorns $\to$ Tears $\to$ BLS $\to$ Black Pyramids, which then allows access to Time Crystals and Relics for temporal progression [3, 4].
