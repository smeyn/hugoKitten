Coal is a Common Resource in Kittens Game that is essential for progressing into the industrial phase of the game.

Here is a comprehensive overview of how Coal is produced, its strategic importance, and its consumption:

Acquisition and Production

Coal production is unlocked after researching the Steel technology. Once unlocked, Coal can be generated in several ways:

  1. Mines (Deep Mining Upgrade): The Deep Mining upgrade, unlocked by Steel, allows Mines to produce Coal.

    • Mines produce Coal at a rate of 0.003 Coal per tick (0.015 per second).
    • This upgrade requires 5,000 Science, 1,200 Iron, and 50 Beam.

  2. Smelters (Coal Furnace Upgrade): The Coal Furnace upgrade, also unlocked by Steel, enables Smelters to produce Coal.

    • Smelters produce Coal at a rate of 0.005 Coal per tick (0.025 per second) while burning wood.
    • This upgrade costs 5,000 Science, 5,000 Minerals, 2,000 Iron, and 35 Beam.

  3. Geologists (Job): The Geologist job, unlocked by the Geology technology, directly generates Coal at a rate of 0.015 per tick.

    • The Mining Drill upgrade further boosts Geologist Coal production by 0.01 Coal per tick (0.05/sec).
    • The Unobtainium Drill upgrade provides an additional boost of 0.015 Coal per tick (0.075/sec).

  4. Quarries: The Quarry building, unlocked by Geology, automatically produces Coal at a rate of 0.015 Coal per tick.

  5. Trading (Spiders): Coal can be acquired by trading Scaffolds with Spiders.

    • Spiders sell 350 Coal per trade.
    • Spiders are a Friendly civilization (+15% trade bonus) and are unlocked once you have 100 Trade Ships and 125k Max Science.
    • Trade with Spiders for Coal is optimally performed during Autumn (+15% amount modifier).

  6. Production Multipliers: The Pyrolysis Workshop upgrade boosts general Coal output by 20%, though this bonus does not apply to Smelters.

Strategic Importance and Consumption

Coal is essential for crafting advanced resources and powering industrial buildings:

  • Crafting Steel: Coal is needed, alongside Iron, to craft Steel (100 Iron and 100 Coal). Steel is required for Gears and complex machinery.
  • Powering Steamworks: Coal is primarily used to power the Steamworks building, which is crucial for automation.
    • Steamworks consumes Coal. The base consumption rate is -80% (i.e., they consume 80% of the normal consumption value, though the precise base consumption amount is not listed in the provided sources).
    • Upgrades like the High Pressure Engine and Fuel Injector can reduce Steamworks’ coal consumption by 20% each.
  • Early Game Strategy: Once the Steel technology is researched, players are generally advised to acquire both the Coal Furnace and Deep Mining upgrades to start accumulating Coal. However, shortly after acquiring the upgrades, players should often allow Coal to max out, as the Iron used to produce it is prioritized for building Lumber Mills and Observatories.

Storage

The default storage limit for Coal is 60.

  • Storage buildings like Barns and Warehouses increase the capacity for Coal.
  • The Barges upgrade increases the maximum Coal stored by Harbours by 50%.

Coal is one of the foundational resources needed for the mid-game, as its production and utilization facilitate the shift from a basic agrarian economy to an industrial one, similar to how the early days of a growing town rely on charcoal or timber before progressing to the efficiency of larger fuel sources.
