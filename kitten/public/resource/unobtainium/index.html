Unobtainium is a rare, high-tier resource that marks the transition into the late game (Phase 4). It is primarily refined from uranium and is essential for space exploration, time manipulation, and advanced engineering.

Production and Acquisition

Unobtainium is generated using Lunar Outposts on Redmoon, which convert 1.75 uranium into 0.035 unobtainium per second. Production can be significantly enhanced through several methods:

  • Global Multipliers: Productivity is boosted by Space Elevators (+1%), Orbital Arrays (+2%), and the Cosmological Libertarianism policy (+15%).
  • Space Manufacturing: This workshop upgrade allows Factories to improve the effectiveness of Space Elevators and Orbital Arrays, further scaling output.
  • Celestial Cycles: During the Redmoon cycle, unobtainium output increases by 20%. If Numeromancy is researched, holding a festival during this cycle provides a 2x multiplier to unobtainium income. Conversely, the Charon cycle imposes a 10% production penalty.

Storage and Capacity

Managing unobtainium storage is a major bottleneck in late-game progression. The base capacity is 150 units, which must be expanded to reach milestones like the “Eludium Era”.

  • Storage Buildings: Moon Bases provide +150 capacity, while Cryostations (located on Terminus) provide a much larger +750 capacity.
  • Storage Enhancements: Capacity is further boosted by Paragon (0.1% per point), AI Bases (+10% per AI Core), Sunforges (+1% for base metals), Event Horizons (+10%), and Void Rifts (+2%).
  • T-Minus Cycle: This cycle provides a +20% storage bonus specifically for Cryostations.

Strategic Uses

Unobtainium is a prerequisite for the most advanced technologies and upgrades in the game:

  • Metaphysics: Unlocking the Metaphysics technology requires 5 units of unobtainium.
  • Eludium: This advanced alloy is crafted using 1000 unobtainium and 2500 alloy.
  • Trading with Leviathans: Once a Black Pyramid is built, you can trade 5000 units of unobtainium to the Leviathans for Time Crystals and Relics.
  • Infrastructure Upgrades: It is required for Unobtainium Huts (which reduce hut price ratios), Unobtainium Reflectors (increasing science caps), and the construction of Markers.
  • Chronospheres: Building a Chronosphere costs 2500 units. These are vital for resets, as each Chronosphere preserves 1.5% of your unobtainium for the next run.

The “Positive Reset” Goal

For endgame players, the ultimate objective is to establish a self-sustaining reset loop. This is achieved by reaching an unobtainium capacity of at least 8.14 billion units, allowing for the construction of 100 Chronospheres. At this level, the resources carried over during a reset exceed the costs of the Chronospheres built, enabling exponential growth in subsequent runs.
