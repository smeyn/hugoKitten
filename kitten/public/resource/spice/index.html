Spice is primarily used as a rare luxury resource and a building component for advanced structures in Kittens Game [1-3].

Uses and Effects

  1. Happiness Bonus (Luxury Resource) Spice is categorized as a rare luxury resource. Every rare resource, including spice, provides a +10% bonus to happiness.
  2. Building Material Spice is a resource required for constructing late-game religious buildings:
    • The Brewery requires 5 Spice, along with Wood, Culture, and Parchment.
    • The Marker, a strange structure made of unknown metal, costs 50,000 Spice.
    • The Black Pyramid, a dark relic of unspeakable horrors that unlocks trade with Leviathans, costs 150,000 Spice.

Characteristics and Management

  • Consumption and Degradation: Spice is a resource that gradually degrades over time [3, 5].
    • Tradeposts help decrease the consumption rate of spice, though with diminishing returns.
    • The Stoicism policy can be chosen to halve the consumption of luxury resources, which includes Spice, and also reduces Brewery consumption by 25% [6].
    • Once a Brewery is built, it consumes 0.1 Spice per tick.

Acquisition

Spice is acquired during successful trade transactions [3, 5].

  • Any successful trade performed has a chance of receiving spices [7].
  • There is a 35% chance of one Spice being included in the trade, with a minimum amount of 25, which can be increased by your trade ratio value [7].
  • The chance of receiving spice increases by 1 percentage point per embassy built, and this increase has no diminishing returns [7, 8].
