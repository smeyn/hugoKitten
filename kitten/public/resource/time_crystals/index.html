Time Crystals (TC) are a crucial, uncommon resource that marks the transition into the late-game phase, often referred to as Phase 2 or the Unicorn/Time Crystal Era. They are primarily used for time manipulation, unlocking advanced physics and void technologies, and are the precursor to Relics.

Acquisition and Generation

Time Crystals cannot be produced by standard buildings or jobs; they must be acquired through specific late-game conversions and trade:

  1. Sacrificing Alicorns: You receive 1 Time Crystal by sacrificing 25 Alicorns [3, 4]. This conversion ratio is improved by Unicorn Utopia (+5%) and Sunspire (+10%) .
  2. Trading with Leviathans: Leviathans sell Time Crystals (minimum amount 0.25) with a 98% chance of success . They require 5,000 Unobtainium in exchange [6]. The amount received per trade is increased by the Leviathan’s energy level (which is maintained by feeding them Necrocorns) and by the number of Tradeposts you have .
  3. Shatter Engine: In the late game, Time Crystals can become self-sustaining through the Type 2 Relic Engine (Shatter Engine). This involves purchasing Resource Retrieval upgrades (unlocked by Paradox Theory) high enough that shattering a Time Crystal yields enough Unobtainium and Alicorns to Civilisations/sacrifice for more Time Crystals [9-11].

Primary Uses

Time Crystals are essential for advanced technological and religious progression, especially concerning time mechanics and void resources:

  • Refining into Relics: 25 Time Crystals can be refined into 1 Relic . Relics are necessary for buying Cryptotheology buildings like the Black Obelisk and Black Nexus .
  • Time and Void Science: TCs are prerequisites for key late-game technologies:
    • Chronophysics: Costs 5 Time Crystals.
    • Tachyon Theory: Costs 25 Time Crystals and 1 Relic.
    • Void Space: Costs 30 Time Crystals and 100 Void.
    • Paradox Theory: Costs 40 Time Crystals and 250 Void.
  • Time Manipulation and Antimatter: Time Crystals are integral to the Chronoforge system (unlocked by Tachyon Theory).
    • They are combusted in the Chrono Furnace to advance the year. Shattering TCs is done to skip time, triggering Antimatter gain from Sunlifters faster.
    • Shattering TCs is also required to reach the goal of the 1000 Years Challenge.
  • Advanced Structures and Upgrades: TCs are components for various high-tier structures and workshop upgrades:
    • Chronosphere: Costs 1 Time Crystal, among other resources, and is a prerequisite for generating Void and Temporal Flux [14, 21].
    • Flux Condensator: Requires 5 Time Crystals and is crucial for preserving a fraction of crafted resources across resets via Chronospheres.
    • Stasis Chambers (1 TC) and Void Energy (2 TCs) are upgrades for Accelerators.
  • Zebra Crafting: Time Crystals are also needed to craft Bloodstone (5K TCs and 10K Relics), which is used to craft T-Mythril.

Strategic Significance

Time Crystals play a unique role in game persistence and efficiency:

  • Reset Retention: The Metaphysics upgrade Anachronomancy (Cost: 125 Paragon) ensures that Time Crystals and the Chronophysics technology are saved across resets. This capability is critical for accelerating future runs. quick paragon farming runs
  • Engine Goal: Due to their importance in resets and late-game systems (Relics/Void), players aim to build up a large stockpile (20-30K TCs) to acquire upgrades like Resource Retrieval immediately after a reset, eliminating the slow initial grind for them.
  • Challenging Use: The 1000 Years Challenge is unlocked by shattering a Time Crystal and requires constant shattering (and associated TC costs) to advance the game’s calendar to Year 1000.

Time Crystals act as the key to temporal mechanics in Kittens Game. If Unobtainium is the material that unlocks space, Time Crystals are the resource that unlocks control over the flow of time itself, allowing kittens to either speed up their civilization or carry advantages across dimensional resets.
