Antimatter is a high-tier late-game resource in Kittens Game that serves as the primary currency for the Relic Era and advanced space engineering. It is a non-crafted resource, meaning a portion of it can be preserved across resets if you possess Chronospheres.

Acquisition and Production

Antimatter is primarily generated through orbital infrastructure in the Helios system:

  • Unlocking: It requires the Superconductors technology (225k Science, 175 Blueprints) to access the Antimatter science node, which costs 500k Science and 1 Relic.
  • Sunlifters: These are the primary source of production. Each Sunlifter produces 1 antimatter per year.
  • The Energy Prerequisite: Antimatter production only occurs if your colony has a positive energy surplus. If energy is negative, Sunlifters will not generate antimatter.
  • Temporal Acceleration: Because standard production is tied to the game year (which lasts roughly 13 minutes and 20 seconds), players often use the Chronoforge to combust/shatter Time Crystals. Each shatter advances the calendar to the next year, triggering an immediate antimatter deposit.

Storage and Capacity Milestones

The default storage capacity for antimatter is 100 units. Expanding this cap is mandatory for high-level progression:

  • Containment Chambers: Constructed in the Kairo system, these provide +100 max antimatter each, but consume a heavy 50 Watts of energy.
  • Heatsinks: These increase the storage effectiveness of Containment Chambers by +2% per building.
  • The 5,000 Cap Goal: A major strategic milestone is reaching a 5,000 unit capacity, which is the cost of a Relic Station. To achieve this, a typical configuration requires approximately 34 Heatsinks and 29 Containment Chambers, supported by significant Paragon levels. If your maximum antimatter is below 5,000, your relic production efficiency is reduced proportionally.

Primary Uses

Antimatter is essential for transitioning into the endgame “Relic Engine” and optimizing space travel:

  • Relic Era Infrastructure: It is the core requirement for Relic Stations (5,000 AM) and Space Beacons (50 AM), which automate the generation of Relics.
  • Quantum Computing: Entanglement Stations, which generate hashes for cryptographical attacks on the elders, cost 5,250 AM.
  • AI and Automation: It is required to build AI Cores (125 AM) and purchase the Machine Learning upgrade (125 AM).
  • Space Upgrades: Advanced space missions and structures depend on it, including Terraforming Stations (25 AM), Tectonics (500 AM), and Antimatter Drives (125 AM).
  • Reactor Enhancements: It is used to upgrade standard infrastructure, such as Advanced AM Reactors (1,750 AM) and Void Reactors (2,500 AM).

Strategic Considerations

During the late game, players often engage in a “Seed Run”—a very long game session focused specifically on maximizing energy and containment to build a massive antimatter and void stockpile. Once the cap is high enough, you can perform Positive Resets with 70+ Chronospheres to exponentially grow your antimatter reserves across subsequent runs. To maximize production during these loops, players often turn off Thorium Reactors except when actively time-skipping to ensure the energy surplus remains positive for antimatter generation.
