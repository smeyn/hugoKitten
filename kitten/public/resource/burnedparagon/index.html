Burned Paragon provides several crucial bonuses to your civilization’s production, storage capacity, and leader traits. It operates similarly to regular Paragon and stacks with it, but with different scaling rules:

  • Production Bonus: Burned paragon provides a global production boost, but it generally has half the effectiveness of regular paragon. It stacks directly with your normal paragon bonuses. For example, having 400 regular paragon and 400 burned paragon yields a combined 285% production increase. It is highly recommended to acquire around 200 to 400 burned paragon to significantly boost your production capabilities.
  • Storage Capacity Bonus: By default, each burned paragon grants a 0.05% bonus to your storage capacity, which is half the value of regular paragon. However, this bonus undergoes a massive change when you enter the Dark Future phase. During the Dark Future, the storage bonus from burned paragon is multiplied by four, increasing to 0.2% per burned paragon. This makes it twice as effective as regular paragon for expanding storage in the deep endgame. These bonuses stack additively with your standard paragon storage bonuses.
  • Leader Trait Boost: Burned paragon also slightly increases the effectiveness of your assigned Leader’s specific trait. For instance, if you have a Merchant leader, possessing burned paragon will directly improve the formula that dictates how many Time Crystals you receive per trade with the Leviathans.
