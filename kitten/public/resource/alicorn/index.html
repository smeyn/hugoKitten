Alicorns are a unique, late-game resource in Kittens Game, categorized as a Unique Resource. They are crucial for progressing into the Time Crystal era of the game.

Here is a detailed overview of their mechanics, acquisition, and uses:

1. Resource Type and Persistence

Alicorns are classified as a Luxury Resource, which means they are always lost upon reset (along with Furs, Ivory, and Unicorns) [1, 2].

2. Acquisition and Production

Alicorns are not produced by standard jobs or early buildings; they are acquired through late-game religious structures and rare events:

  • Sky Palaces: This religious building, which requires Tears, Ivory, and Megalith to construct, is the main way to gain Alicorns [3].
    • Building a Sky Palace enables a random event that grants one Alicorn, and each palace increases the frequency of this event by $0.01%$ per game-day [3].
    • Sky Palaces also passively produce $0.00002$ alicorns per tick ($0.08$ per year) [3].
  • Unicorn Utopia: This subsequent unicorn religious upgrade improves alicorn summon chance by $0.015%$ per game-day and increases passive production to $0.000025$ alicorns per tick [4].
  • Pacifism Challenge Reward: Completing the Pacifism challenge provides an increasing reward, including a $+10%$ Alicorn chance per completion (up to $120%$) and a $10%$ increase to Alicorn per tick production (with no diminishing returns) [5].

3. Primary Uses and Conversion

Alicorns are primarily consumed to generate Time Crystals (TC), a valuable prestige resource:

  • Sacrifice for Time Crystals: You can Sacrifice 25 Alicorns to receive 1 Time Crystal [6].
    • The conversion ratio is improved by the religious upgrades Unicorn Utopia (+5%) and Sunspire (+10%) [4, 6].
    • The act is described as “Banish the alicorns to the Bloodmoon” [6].
  • Resource Retrieval Strategy: In the end game, acquiring enough levels of the Resource Retrieval upgrade means shattering a Time Crystal will yield enough unobtainium and alicorns to allow the player to Civilisations/sacrifice immediately for new Time Crystals, sustaining the flow of this resource [7].

4. Consumption and Corruption

Alicorns are consumed or corrupted in three ways:

  • Necrocorn Corruption: The religious building Marker starts a process that slowly changes alicorns into Necrocorns [1, 8].
  • Unicorn Necropolis: The Unicorn Necropolis building, an advanced religious structure, requires 100 Alicorn (along with Necrocorn, Megalith, and Void) [9].
  • Ivory Temple Consumption: The Ivory Temple, a building related to Zebras, consumes $0.00002$ alicorns per tick while converting Ivory to Minerals [10].

In terms of strategy, it is advised to conserve your alicorn supply until the “last moment” to maximize the Time Crystal conversion ratio, particularly during a final run before a reset [11].
