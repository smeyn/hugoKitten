Thorium is a late-game resource primarily used as a highly radioactive and unstable fuel, and its function is integrated with power generation, specialized upgrades, and trading.

Here is a comprehensive overview of Thorium:

Acquisition and Crafting

Thorium is a Craftable Resource:

  • Source: It is crafted from Uranium.
  • Crafting Cost: 250 Uranium are required to craft one unit of Thorium.
  • Unlocking: The ability to craft Thorium is unlocked by researching the Thorium science [1, 2].
    • The Thorium science costs 375K Science and 375 Blueprints, and is unlocked by Orbital Engineering.
  • Trade: Thorium can also be acquired through trade with Dragons. Dragons sell Thorium (minimum amount 1) with a 50% chance of success, provided you have at least Embassy Level 5 with them.

Uses and Upgrades

Thorium is essential for specific Reactor upgrades and advanced space travel:

  • Thorium Reactors (Upgrade): This Workshop upgrade increases Reactor energy output by $0.25$ Wt (+25%) [4, 5]. However, this upgrade also causes the Reactor to consume Thorium at a rate of $0.25$ per tick per reactor.
    • The upgrade costs 400K Science and 10K Thorium.
    • The Enriched Thorium upgrade further reduces this consumption by $0.0625$ per tick.
    • It is generally advised in specialized runs (like the IW 40K endless run) to turn off Thorium Reactors when purchased and only use the upgrade when time skipping for antimatter [6].
  • Thorium Drive (Upgrade): This Workshop upgrade, which costs 400K Science, along with Ships, Gears, Alloy, and 100K Thorium, enhances space travel speed by setting the multiplier to 50 (travel time is divided by 50) [7].
  • Quantum Dot Cells (Upgrade): This upgrade, requiring Thorium and other resources, improves Solar Farm energy production by +15% in winter and +30% in spring and autumn [8].

Strategic Context

Thorium plays a role in late-game resource management, particularly in the process of building up Antimatter Cap [9]:

  • A major goal in the end-game is to obtain 5,000 Antimatter capacity for Relic Stations [9, 10]. Achieving this may require building Containment Chambers and Heatsinks, which heavily utilize Thorium [9].
  • For instance, obtaining the 5,000 Antimatter cap often requires approximately 34 Heatsinks (costing 526K Thorium) [9].

Thorium’s relationship with Uranium is similar to how early resources are refined: just as Wood is refined from Catnip [11], Thorium is a refined fuel source derived from Uranium, enabling higher-tier energy production and propulsion mechanisms in the late game. However, its use in Thorium Reactors presents a classic trade-off: increased power output in exchange for continuous consumption of a rare, specialized resource.
