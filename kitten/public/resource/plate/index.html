Plate is a core mid-game resource in Kittens Game that is produced entirely through crafting using Iron as the primary input.

Here is a detailed breakdown of plate production, the required input, and mechanisms for acquisition:

1. Primary Production: Crafting

Plate (specifically Metal Plate) is categorized as a craftable resource.

  • Input Cost: One Metal Plate is crafted in the Workshop by consuming 125 Iron.
  • Purpose: Plate is required for advanced construction in the early and mid-game, particularly for buildings like Harbours.

2. Supporting Iron Production (The Prerequisite)

Since Plate production is capped by your Iron supply, effective production requires robust Iron generation, which is achieved through refining and trade:

  • Smelters: Iron is refined from wood and minerals using Smelters. Smelters unlock after researching Metal Working.
  • Calciners: Iron can also be refined using Calciners from oil and minerals.
  • Upgrades: Several Workshop upgrades boost Iron production from these buildings:
    • Electrolytic Smelting increases Smelter output of iron (and coal) by +95%.
    • Fluidized Reactors boost Calciner output by +100% more iron.
    • Oxidation increases Calciner iron production by +95%.
    • The Rotary Kiln also provides a +75% increase to Calciner iron output.
  • Policy/: The Communism policy grants a +25% increase to iron production. The Rationality policy grants a +5% iron production bonus.
  • Trade: Iron can be acquired directly by trading with other races. For example, Griffins sell Iron for Wood, and Zebras sell Iron for Slab.

3. Automated Conversion and Trade Acquisition

  • Automation: The Pneumatic Press Workshop upgrade, unlocked via Physics, allows Workshop Automation to automatically convert iron to plates when iron resources are near their capacity limit.
  • Trade Acquisition: Plate can be acquired through trade with Zebras. Zebras sell Plate (2 units per trade, with a 65% chance) in exchange for 50 Slab. Building Embassies increases the chance of receiving plates from Zebras, reaching 100% chance at Embassy level 54.

4. Uses of Plate

Plate is crucial for further expanding infrastructure and developing advanced resources:

  • Storage: Plate is needed to build a Harbour (75 Plate), a storage building essential for increasing capacity for Catnip, Wood, Minerals, and Iron, among others.
  • Culture/Gold: Plate is required for Temples (15 Plate) and Mints (200 Plate). Mints increase gold storage and produce rare resources.
  • High-Tier Structures: Significant amounts of Plate are needed for Reactors (5000 Plate) and Factories (2500 Plate).
  • Late-Game Crafting: Plate is an input for crafting Megaliths (5 Plate).

Plate serves as an early example of vertical production integration in the game, where continuous management of raw resources (Wood, Minerals) is required to ensure a steady supply of an intermediary resource (Iron) which is then crafted into Plate for infrastructure expansion.
