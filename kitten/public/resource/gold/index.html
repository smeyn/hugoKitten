Gold is a strategic metal in Kittens Game primarily used for international trade, religious advancement, and leadership development. It is categorized as a common resource that can be produced through industrial refinement or specialized worker roles.

Production and Acquisition

Gold acquisition shifts from manual hunting in specialized modes to automated industrial production as your village advances:

  • Smelting: Once the Gold Ore workshop upgrade is researched, Smelters begin producing $0.001$ gold per tick ($0.005$ per second) as a byproduct of smelting iron.
  • Geologists: After researching Geodesy, kittens assigned as Geologists can find gold at a rate of $0.0008$ per tick. This rate can be further enhanced by the Mining Drill and Unobtainium Drill upgrades.
  • Hunting (Iron Will): In Iron Will mode, where there are no kittens, hunting has a 25% chance to yield gold once Gold Ore is researched.
  • Trade: While most civilizations require gold from the player to trade, gold can be obtained through specific loops, such as trading with the Nagas for minerals to feed smelters.

Strategic Uses

Gold is a critical bottleneck for mid-game progression due to its diverse applications:

  • Trade: Each trade transaction with civilizations like the Lizards, Sharks, or Zebras typically costs 1 unit of gold.
  • Religion: Gold is a required component for building Temples and purchasing many Order of the Sun upgrades, such as Solar Revolution, Golden Spire, and Sun Altar.
  • Village Infrastructure: It is necessary for constructing Tradeposts (which improve trade and happiness) and Mints (which automate the gathering of furs and ivory).
  • Leadership: Promoting a kitten to a higher rank requires both experience and gold. Higher-ranked leaders provide significant production bonuses, especially if the Monarchy or Republic policies are enacted.

Strategic Gold Cap Benchmarks

Expanding gold storage is critical for scientific and religious progression:

  • Solar Revolution: Requires a gold cap of 500.
  • Apocrypha: Requires a gold cap of 5,000.
  • Transcendence: Requires a gold cap of 7,500.
  • Leadership Promotions: Higher ranks for your kitten leaders require significant gold amounts, ranging from 25 to 200+ gold.

Policies and Efficiency

Several government and foreign policies directly impact your gold economy:

  • Liberalism: This policy is highly valued as it reduces all gold costs by 20% and improves trade relations.
  • Zebra Relations (Appeasement): While this policy improves titanium trade, it imposes a -5% penalty to gold income.
  • Monarchy: This policy doubles the power of your leader’s trait but consumes 10% of your gold production as upkeep for the royal court.

The initial storage limit for Gold is 10 units. This capacity can be increased through the construction of specific storage buildings, as well as production buildings that feature storage benefits, such as the Mint [1, 2].

Here are the ways to increase your maximum Gold storage:

1. Dedicated Buildings and Upgrades

Several buildings directly contribute to your maximum Gold capacity:

  • Mint: The Mint building increases your Max Gold by +100. Mints are unlocked by researching Architecture and are constructed using 5,000 Minerals, 200 Plate, and 500 Gold. Building more Mints is suggested as a method to increase your Gold storage limit.
  • Barn: The Barn, unlocked by Agriculture, provides a base increase of +10 Max Gold [1, 5].
  • Warehouse: The Warehouse, unlocked by Construction, provides a base increase of +5 Max Gold.
  • Harbour: The Harbour, unlocked by Navigation, provides a base increase of +25 Max Gold.
  • Sky Palace: The Sky Palace, a religious building, improves gold storage by +1%.

2. Storage Upgrades

Various Workshop upgrades applied to storage buildings will further increase the Gold capacity of Mints, Warehouses, and Harbours:

  • Reinforced Warehouses: Increases the maximum limit for several resources, including Gold, from Warehouses, Barns, Harbors, and Mints by +25% [7]. This upgrade is unlocked by Steel [7, 8].
  • Titanium Warehouses: Increases Gold capacity from the specified storage buildings by +50% [4, 7].
  • Alloy Warehouses: Increases Gold capacity from the specified storage buildings by +45% [7].
  • Concrete Pillars: Increases Gold capacity from the specified storage buildings by +5% [7].
  • Concrete Warehouses: Increases Gold capacity from the specified storage buildings by +35% [7].
  • Storage Bunkers: Increases Gold capacity from the specified storage buildings by +20%. This upgrade is unlocked by Exogeology [7, 9].

3. Advanced Mechanisms

Late-game advanced production and prestige mechanics also influence Gold capacity:

  • Paragon: High levels of Paragon (gained by resetting with more than 70 kittens) improve resource caps, and this effect on storage never diminishes [10, 11].
  • Accelerator Upgrades: Advanced Accelerator Workshop upgrades, associated with pocket dimensions, also increase the maximum capacity of Gold:
    • Energy Rifts (Requires Dimensional Physics) [12].
    • Stasis Chambers (Requires Chronophysics) increases max Gold from Accelerators by +95% [12].
    • Void Energy (Requires Stasis Chambers) increases max Gold from Accelerators by +75% [12].
    • Dark Energy (Requires Void Energy) increases max Gold from Accelerators by +250% [12].
    • Tachyon Accelerators (Requires Tachyon Theory) increases max Gold from Accelerators by +500% [12].
  • Event Horizon: This Cryptotheology upgrade, available at Transcendence-Tier 7, increases the global resource storage ratio by +10% [13].

Strategic Importance

Increasing Gold storage is necessary because Gold is used extensively:

  • Trading: Gold is required for trading with civilizations [14], and the Isolationism policy reduces the Gold cost per trade by 1 [15].
  • Building: Gold is needed to construct high-tier buildings like Temples [16, 17], Tradeposts [2, 18], and Mints.
  • Upgrades: Gold is a component cost for several religious upgrades, such as Golden Spire, Sun Altar, Stained Glass, Solar Revolution, Basilica, Templars, Apocrypha, and Transcendence [19-22].

In the “Titanium era,” Gold becomes a major bottleneck for trade and building Temples, emphasizing the need to maximize its accumulation and storage [23, 24].
