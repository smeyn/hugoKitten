Dragons are a neutral trading civilization in Kittens Game that are crucial for acquiring Uranium and Thorium, essential resources for progressing into the space and atomic phases of the game.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the Dragons and their strategic importance:

Prerequisites and Attitude

  • Attitude: Dragons are a Neutral civilization, starting with a 0% attitude towards your kittens.
  • Unlocking: Dragons are unlocked once you have researched the Nuclear Fission technology.
  • Trade Requirements: To trade with Dragons, you must buy Uranium or Thorium by offering Titanium. The minimum cost they require is 250 Titanium.

Resources Traded

Dragons specialize in selling advanced radioactive resources:

Resource Sold Minimum Amount Chance to Receive Strategic Note
Uranium 1 95% Uranium is a Common Resource.
Thorium 1 50% Thorium is sold once you reach Embassy Level 5.

Dragon trades do not have any seasonal adjustments that affect the amount of resources received.

Strategic Role and Uranium Acquisition

The primary function of trading with Dragons is to gain Uranium, which is necessary for creating Unobtainium (UO) and for powering Reactors.

  1. Unobtainium Production: The Lunar Outpost converts Uranium into Unobtainium. Since the Lunar Outpost consumes Uranium at a rate 140 times faster than a single Accelerator can produce it internally, trading Titanium for Uranium with Dragons provides a vital alternative source for this bottleneck resource.
  2. Thorium Acquisition: Dragons are a source for Thorium, a highly radioactive and unstable fuel. Thorium is used for the Thorium Reactors upgrade and the Thorium Drive space engine upgrade.
  3. Embassy Bonus: If you build Embassies with the Dragons, the chance to receive Uranium increases, reaching a guaranteed 100% chance at Embassy Level 6.

In the progression towards Metaphysics, where you need Unobtainium, you will need to research Nuclear Fission to unlock Dragons. Trading with Dragons to acquire Uranium, which then converts to Unobtainium via the Lunar Outpost, is a standard path forward.

The reliance on Dragons for Uranium highlights the constant trade-offs in resource management: you exchange your hard-won Titanium (acquired mainly through trading with the hostile Zebras) for the prerequisite resource Uranium needed for the next technological jump to Unobtainium.
