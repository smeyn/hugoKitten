The Zebras are a crucial civilization in Kittens Game, primarily known as who provide essential industrial resources during the mid-game, particularly in the Titanium era.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of the Zebras, their role in trade, and their unique function in the Iron Will challenge:

Trading and Strategic Importance

Zebras are primarily sought after for their critical resource exports:

  • Acquisition and Attitude: Zebras are a Hostile civilization with an initial attitude of -30%. They are unlocked once you have 1 Trade Ship. Since they are hostile, they have a chance of giving you no resources at all during a trade.
  • Trade Mitigation: The penalty from Zebra hostility can be negated by building a sufficient number of Tradeposts (85 posts, or 58 with the Diplomacy Metaphysics upgrade).
  • Resources Traded: Zebras buy Slab (50 minimum). In return, they sell three critical resources:
    • Iron (300 minimum amount).
    • Plate (2 minimum, with a 65% chance).
    • Titanium (amount is calculated based on the number of Trade Ships you possess: (0.03 * Trade Ships) + 1.5).
    • They also offer Alloy (0.25 minimum, 5% chance) once you reach Embassy Level 5.
  • Titanium Trade Mechanic: Unlike most resources gained through trade, the amount of Titanium received from the Zebras (such as the bonus received from Tradeposts).
  • Seasonal Optimization: Trades are best conducted during Summer for Iron (+15% amount modifier) and Winter for Plate (+25% amount modifier).
  • Strategic Goal: In the Titanium era, the focus often becomes trading with Zebras non stop to acquire Titanium and Iron. This trade is crucial for unlocking titanium-heavy technologies and buildings.

Policy/ and Upgrades

Zebras are associated with several Policy/ and specialized upgrades:

  • After meeting the Zebras, you must choose between two foreign Policy/: * Zebra Relations: Appeasement (5000 Culture): This policy is generally preferred because it improves relations (equivalent to 15 extra tradeposts) and provides a multiplicative boost to the titanium trade ratio (equivalent to 15 tradeposts). It is highly recommended because players are often desperate for titanium. * Zebra Relations: Bellicosity (5000 Culture): This policy worsens relations with Zebras but slightly improves relations with other races.
  • There are several buildings associated with this race, including the Zebra Outpost, Zebra Workshop, and Zebra Forge. Additionally, the workshop upgrade Mineralogy Department is a Zebra upgrade for Academies.

Role in Iron Will (IW) Mode

In the specialized Iron Will challenge (a mode played without kittens or housing), Zebras adopt a unique and critical function:

  • Job Replacement: In IW mode, Zebras are categorized as a Special Resource that acts as hunters to replace the absent kitten population, generating Catpower. Catpower is essential for hunting, which yields Gold (due to the Gold Ore upgrade effect in IW mode) and Ivory.
  • Acquisition: You gain one zebra after researching Archery. Players often perform short reset runs (6 to 10 runs) solely to rush Archery and accumulate .
  • Zebra Limit: The maximum zebra limit can be increased by 1 every time you research Archery and reset (up to 10 or 11 initially). The limit also increases based on the amount of science spent in the previous run.
  • While a higher zebra count is desired to speed up hunting and catpower generation, diminishing returns on catpower production start to set in .
  • Bloodstone: If you have at least in IW mode, hunting gains a 0.05% chance of yielding Bloodstone.
  • IW Carryover: The Zebra counter carries over when you reset the game. The Zebra Diplomacy and Zebra Covenant Metaphysics upgrades allow a portion of your Zebras (10% and 50% of cap, respectively) to remain if you choose to end Iron Will mode by building housing.

Analogy: Zebras act as an offshore supplier for heavy manufacturing materials. They are a hostile, high-risk trade partner, but they are the only reliable source for large quantities of Titanium—the resource needed to build your space program and advanced factories. In the unique constraints of Iron Will, however, they become a primary workforce, serving as essential substitute hunters to generate the gold and ivory required for civilization building.
