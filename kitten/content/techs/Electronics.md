[Electronics]( {{< relref "techs/Electronics.md" >}}) is a crucial mid-game science technology that introduces significant building upgrades, sets up your transition into the Space phase, and unlocks powerful automation tools. 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first research the [Mechanization]( {{< relref "techs/Mechanization.md" >}}) technology.
*   **Cost:** 135,000 Science and 70 Blueprints.

### **Core Unlocks: Building Upgrades**
Researching Electronics directly enables you to upgrade two of your standard terrestrial buildings into more powerful variants:
*   **Libraries into Data Centers:** Data Centers provide a larger boost to your Science (+10%), Max Science (+750), Max Culture (+250), and Compendium Cap (+1000) at the cost of 2 Watts of energy.
*   **Amphitheatres into Broadcast Towers:** Broadcast Towers drastically reduce unhappiness (-75%) and generate Culture based on your energy surplus.

### **Unlocked Technology and Sciences**
Electronics acts as the gateway to the space age and advanced automation, unlocking the following science technologies:
*   [Robotics]( {{< relref "techs/Robotics.md" >}})
*   [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}})
*   [Rocketry]( {{< relref "techs/Rocketry.md" >}}) (which enables the Space tab and space missions)

### **Unlocked Workshop Upgrades**
Electronics grants access to a massive suite of Workshop upgrades:
*   [SETI:]( {{< relref "upgrade/SETI.md" >}}) One of the most highly recommended upgrades to rush. For 125K Science and 250 Titanium, SETI makes all astronomical events automatic and silent, completely removing the need to manually click meteors to gather Science and Starcharts.
*   [Refrigeration:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Refrigeration.md" >}}) Expands your maximum catnip limit by +75%.
*   [CAD System:]( {{< relref "upgrade/CAD_System.md" >}}) Science buildings improve your blueprint crafting ratio.
*   [Factory Logistics:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Logistics.md" >}}) Increases the craft bonus of your Factories.
*   [Factory Optimization:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Optimization.md" >}}) Improves your Engineer kittens' effectiveness.
