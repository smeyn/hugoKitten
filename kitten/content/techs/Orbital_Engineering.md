In **Kittens Game**, [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) is a pivotal mid-to-late game technology that transitions your civilization from surface-based industry to a true spacefaring empire. It is the primary gateway to the **Space tab**'s most influential infrastructure.

### Key Effects
Unlocking Orbital Engineering allows you to begin building and upgrading your orbital presence, specifically focusing on science and logistics:

* [Space Elevator:]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) This is the most critical building unlocked by this tech. It drastically **reduces the oil cost** of launching space missions. Without it, the oil requirements for reaching the Farther Ring (like [Jupiter]( {{< relref "space/jupiter.md" >}})) quickly become impossible to meet.
* [Satellite Radio:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Satellite_Radio.md" >}}) A Workshop upgrade that allows your [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) to increase your **Science cap**. This is vital for reaching the science levels needed for [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}).
* [Navigation Relay:]( {{< relref "space/Navigation_Relay.md" >}}) As we discussed earlier, this upgrade boosts Satellite effectiveness by **+15%**, further accelerating your Starchart and Science generation.

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To research Orbital Engineering, you must have a solid foundation in both electronics and rocketry:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Electronics]( {{< relref "techs/Electronics.md" >}}) | Technology | Base tech for advanced circuitry. |
| [Rocketry]( {{< relref "techs/Rocketry.md" >}}) | Technology | Required to access the Space tab. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **150,000 Science** |
| [Blueprints]( {{< relref "resource/Blueprint.md" >}}) | Resource | **250 Blueprints** |

### Strategic Importance
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Temporal Distortion**, Orbital Engineering is the technology that likely enabled your current progress.

1.  **Breaking the Oil Barrier:** Before this tech, your space expansion is limited by how much oil you can store and produce. The [Space Elevator]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) (unlocked here) is what allows you to bypass that bottleneck and launch the heavy missions required for [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}).
2.  **Science Scaling:** By allowing Satellites to contribute to your Science cap, this technology provides the "storage" needed to research [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}) and [Nanotechnology]( {{< relref "techs/Nanotechnology.md" >}}).
3.  **Path to the Moon:** While you can reach the Moon with just Rocketry, Orbital Engineering makes [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) much more sustainable by reducing the logistical overhead of getting there.

> **Pro Tip:** Once you have this tech, prioritize building at least 5-10 [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}). The cumulative oil reduction is what makes late-game missions to [Charon]( {{< relref "planet/Charon.md" >}}) or **Centaurus** feasible.
