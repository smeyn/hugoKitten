[Particle Physics]( {{< relref "techs/Particle_Physics.md" >}}) is a pivotal mid-to-late-game science technology that serves as the gateway to uranium refining and advanced temporal mechanics.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Science Cost:** 185,000 Science.
*   **Blueprint Cost:** 135 Blueprints.
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first research [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}).
*   **Strategic Tip:** Assigning a **Scientist leader** can reduce the science cost of this technology by 5%, bringing it down to approximately **175,750 Science**.

### **Unlocked Buildings: The Accelerator**
Researching Particle Physics is the mandatory prerequisite for constructing [Accelerators]( {{< relref "buildings/Accelerator.md" >}}).
*   **Function:** Accelerators provide a steady source of [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) by refining Titanium (at a rate of -0.015 Titanium and +0.0025 Uranium per tick).
*   **Energy Impact:** Each active Accelerator consumes **2 Watts** of energy.
*   **Storage Potential:** Once the [Energy Rifts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Energy_Rifts.md" >}}) upgrade is researched (via Dimensional Physics), Accelerators provide massive storage capacity for various resources, including catnip, wood, minerals, coal, iron, titanium, and gold.

### **Unlocked Technologies**
Particle Physics is required to unlock two high-tier science branches:
*   [Chronophysics:]( {{< relref "techs/Chronophysics.md" >}}) The branch dealing with time manipulation, [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}), and reset preservation.
*   [Dimensional Physics:]( {{< relref "techs/Dimensional_Physics.md" >}}) The branch that unlocks the [LHC]( {{< relref "upgrade/LHC.md" >}}) (Large Hadron Collider) and [Energy Rifts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Energy_Rifts.md" >}}) for expanded storage.

### **Unlocked Workshop Upgrades**
*   [Enriched Uranium:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) Reduces the Uranium consumption of [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) by 25% and provides a +25% Uranium production bonus to **Quarries**.
*   [Railgun:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Railgun.md" >}}) A permanent military upgrade that increases the [Catpower]( {{< relref "resource/cat_power.md" >}}) production of Hunter kittens by **25%**.

### **Strategic Importance**
Particle Physics marks the transition into the "Metaphysics" era. It is essential for generating the initial [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) needed for space exploration; because [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) consume uranium to produce unobtainium, players often rely on Accelerators (and trading with Dragons) to maintain their uranium supply until more efficient space-based uranium sources like Planet Crackers are built.
