[Genetics]( {{< relref "techs/Genetics.md" >}}) is an advanced Science technology in Kittens Game that focuses on biological enhancements for your civilization's resource production. 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** To unlock the Genetics technology, you must first research the [Biochemistry]( {{< relref "techs/Biochemistry.md" >}}) science.
*   **Cost:** Researching Genetics requires an investment of **190,000 Science and 1,500 Compendiums**.

### **Workshop Upgrades Unlocked**
Researching Genetics does not unlock any new buildings directly; instead, it grants access to two highly impactful Workshop upgrades:

**1. Unicorn Selection**
*   **Cost:** 175,000 Science and 1,500 Titanium.
*   **Effect:** This upgrade improves the effectiveness of your Unicorn Pastures, providing a **25% multiplier to your overall unicorn production**. This is a crucial upgrade when you are transitioning into the mid-to-late game unicorn religion phase, as maximizing unicorn production is necessary for acquiring tears, alicorns, and eventually Time Crystals.

**2. GM Catnip (Genetically Modified Catnip)**
*   **Cost:** 175,000 Science, 1,500 Titanium, and 1,000,000 Catnip.
*   **Effect:** This upgrade creates genetically modified catnip that improves your Bio Labs' oil yield by **60%**. To benefit from this, you must already have the *Biofuel Processing* upgrade active (which automatically converts catnip into oil). Importantly, GM Catnip provides this massive 60% boost to oil production without increasing the amount of catnip your Bio Labs actually consume.
