In *Kittens Game*, [Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Engineering.md" >}}) is an early-game science technology that acts as a gateway to both physical infrastructure and societal governance.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Science Cost:** 1,500 Science.
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first research [Construction]( {{< relref "techs/Construction.md" >}}).
*   **Leads to:** Researching Engineering is the mandatory prerequisite for [Writing]( {{< relref "techs/Writing.md" >}}).

### **Unlocked Features**
Researching Engineering provides two primary unlocks:
*   [Aqueducts:]( {{< relref "buildings/Aqueduct.md" >}}) A building that costs 75 Minerals (with a 1.12 price ratio) and increases **catnip production by 3%**. This bonus applies to both Catnip Fields and Farmer kittens. Strategically, Aqueducts are often more cost-effective than building additional Catnip Fields once the fields' prices have escalated.
*   **Policies:** Engineering unlocks the **Policy system**, allowing you to officially sponsor systems of government, foreign relations, philosophy, and environmental management. 

### **Strategic Importance**
Engineering marks a transition from simple resource gathering to more complex management:
*   **Food Stability:** Along with Agriculture, Engineering is vital for establishing a food surplus that can withstand "Cold Winters". 
*   **Societal Direction:** The policy system unlocked here allows for early-game optimization. For example, you can choose the [Clear Cutting]( {{< relref "Policy/Clear_Cutting.md" >}}) or [Strip Mining]( {{< relref "Policy/Strip_Mining.md" >}}) policies to boost wood or mineral production by 30% at the cost of some happiness and increased pollution.
*   **Path to Culture:** Because it is required for Writing, Engineering is an essential step toward unlocking [Amphitheatres]( {{< relref "buildings/Amphitheatre.md" >}}), which generate the [Culture]( {{< relref "resource/culture.md" >}}) needed for advanced mid-game research like Theology.

### **Naming Note and Potential Confusion**
It is important to distinguish the Engineering technology from other similarly named mechanics:
*   [Metaphysics:]( {{< relref "techs/Metaphysics.md" >}}) The "Enlightenment" metaphysics upgrade (which reduces all price ratios by 1%) was **formerly named Engineering**.
*   **Engineer (Job):** The Engineer kitten profession is **not** unlocked by this technology; it is unlocked much later via [Mechanization]( {{< relref "techs/Mechanization.md" >}}).
*   **Artisan (Trait):** The Artisan kitten trait was also formerly known as the "Engineer" trait before being renamed to avoid confusion with the automation job.
