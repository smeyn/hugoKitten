[Paradox Theory]( {{< relref "Paradox_Theory.md" >}}) is a high-tier late-game science technology that acts as a fundamental gateway to advanced temporal mechanics and the "Shatter Engine" in *Kittens Game*.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Science Cost:** 1,000,000 (1M)
*   **Resource Requirements:** 40 Time Crystals and 250 Void
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first research [Void Space]( {{< relref "techs/Void_Space.md" >}})

### **Unlocked Upgrades and Structures**
Researching Paradox Theory is required to unlock several critical late-game features:
*   **Resource Retrieval (Time Tab):** This is perhaps the most vital unlock; it allows you to retrieve 1% of your yearly resources (per level) whenever you shatter a Time Crystal. This is the foundation for infinite Time Crystal loops.
*   **Chronocontrol (Void Space Structure):** A structure that increases the duration (number of days) of a [Temporal Paradox]( {{< relref "guides/temporal_paradox.md" >}}) event. It also serves as the prerequisite for unlocking [Chronosurge]( {{< relref "upgrade/Chronosurge.md" >}}).
*   **Distortion (Workshop Upgrade):** This upgrade enhances the effectiveness of Chronocontrol, adding **+3 days** to the Temporal Paradox for every level of Chronocontrol built.
*   **Void Resonator:** An upgrade that allows the **Order of the Void** metaphysics bonus to trigger during a time skip. It also improves faith-to-worship production based on the number of resonators owned.

### **Strategic Importance**
*   **The Shatter Engine:** Paradox Theory is the cornerstone of Phase 4 (Endgame) progression. By unlocking [Resource Retrieval]( {{< relref "upgrade/resource-retrieval.md" >}}), it enables a self-sustaining cycle where shattering a Time Crystal generates enough Unobtainium to buy back more than one crystal through Leviathan trading.
*   **Void Accumulation:** Because it unlocks structures that extend the duration and effectiveness of Temporal Paradoxes, Paradox Theory is essential for farming the massive quantities of [Void]( {{< relref "resource/void.md" >}}) required for endgame buildings like [Void Rifts]( {{< relref "buildings/void_rift.md" >}}).
*   **Faith Scaling:** Through the **Void Resonator**, players can continue to build their religious "Apocrypha" bonus while time skipping, ensuring that their production multipliers remain high even when bypassing thousands of years of normal game time.
