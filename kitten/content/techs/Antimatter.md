In *Kittens Game*, [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) is a late-game science technology that introduces powerful upgrades and leads into the endgame Terraformation phase.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research [Superconductors]( {{< relref "techs/Superconductors.md" >}}).
*   **Resource Requirements:** 
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 500,000.
    *   [Relic:]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) 1.

### **Unlocked Content**
Researching Antimatter unlocks the subsequent [Terraformation]( {{< relref "techs/Terraformation.md" >}}) science, as well as several high-impact Workshop upgrades:

*   [Antimatter Bases:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Antimatter_Bases.md" >}}) Reduces the energy consumption of your Moon Bases by 50% (from 10 Watts down to 5 Watts).
*   [Antimatter Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Antimatter_Reactors.md" >}}) Makes your Research Vessels and Space Beacons 95% more effective, significantly boosting your maximum science cap.
*   [Antimatter Fission:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Antimatter_Fission.md" >}}) Increases your Engineer kittens' effectiveness at crafting eludium by 25%.
*   [Antimatter Drive:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Antimatter_Drive.md" >}}) A powerful rocket engine upgrade that drastically speeds up space travel, dividing travel time by 25.

### **The Antimatter Resource**
Alongside the technology, the [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) resource itself becomes a critical bottleneck in the late game. 
*   **Production:** It is generated by [Sunlifters]( {{< relref "space/sunlifter.md" >}}) once every game year, provided you have a positive energy balance. 
*   **Storage:** Its storage capacity is extremely limited and must be expanded using **Containment Chambers** and **Heatsinks**. 
*   **Strategic Use:** Amassing 5,000 Antimatter is a major endgame milestone required to build the [Relic Station]( {{< relref "upgrade/Relic_Station.md" >}}) upgrade, which allows your Space Beacons to passively generate Relics.
