[Hunters]( {{< relref "job/Hunter.md" >}}) are a specialized job for your kittens that becomes available after you research the [Archery]( {{< relref "techs/Archery.md" >}}) technology. Their primary function is to slowly generate [Catpower]( {{< relref "resource/cat_power.md" >}}), which they produce at a base rate of **0.06 per tick**. 

### **The Hunting Mechanic**
Whenever you accumulate 100 Catpower, you can initiate a hunt. Interestingly, you do not actually need to have any kittens actively assigned to the Hunter job to perform a hunt; you only need to have the required 100 Catpower saved up. 

Hunting is essential for progressing through the game because it yields several rare and valuable resources:
*   **Furs and Ivory:** These are the most common drops you will receive. Simply possessing these luxury items grants a **+10% boost to your kittens' happiness** for each resource type. However, Furs and Ivory will gradually degrade over time, so it is best to use them (such as crafting Furs into Parchment) rather than hoarding them.
*   **Unicorns:** Every hunt has a **5% chance** to yield a Unicorn, which eventually allows you to build Unicorn Pastures.
*   **Gold (Iron Will Mode):** If you are playing the kitten-less Iron Will mode and have the Gold Ore upgrade, each hunt has a **25% chance** to yield gold.
*   **Bloodstones (Iron Will Mode):** In Iron Will mode, if you have at least 10 Zebras, every hunt has a 0.05% chance to drop a bloodstone (this chance increases to 5% if you currently possess zero bloodstones).

*Note: Hunting is completely disabled if you are attempting the Pacifism challenge.*

### **Upgrades and Boosts**
You can improve your Hunters and the hunting mechanic through two distinct upgrade paths in the Workshop:
*   **Catpower Production Boosts:** Upgrades such as the [Composite Bow]( {{< relref "upgrade/Composite_Bow.md" >}}) (+50%), [Crossbow]( {{< relref "upgrade/Crossbow.md" >}}) (+25%), and [Railgun]( {{< relref "upgrade/Railgun.md" >}}) (+25%) directly increase the amount of Catpower your assigned Hunters generate per tick.
*   **Hunting Yield Boosts:** Upgrades such as [Bolas]( {{< relref "upgrade/Bolas.md" >}}), [Hunting Armour]( {{< relref "upgrade/Hunting_Armour.md" >}}), [Steel Armour]( {{< relref "upgrade/Steel_Armour.md" >}}), [Alloy Armour]( {{< relref "upgrade/Alloy_Armour.md" >}}), and [Nanosuits]( {{< relref "upgrade/Nanosuits.md" >}}) increase your civilization's overall "Hunting bonus". This bonus stacks additively and directly multiplies the amount of Furs, Ivory, and other random resources you receive every time you spend 100 Catpower to execute a hunt.
