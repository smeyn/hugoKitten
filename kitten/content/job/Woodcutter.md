[Woodcutters]( {{< relref "job/Woodcutter.md" >}}) are one of the very first specialised jobs you can assign to your kittens, and they serve as the foundation of your early-game economy by continuously harvesting **Wood**. 

### **Base Production and Acquisition**
*   **Base Output:** A kitten actively assigned as a Woodcutter natively generates **+0.0018 wood per tick**.
*   **Unlock Requirement:** To employ Woodcutters, you must first gather Catnip, refine it into Wood, and use that Wood to construct your first [Hut]( {{< relref "buildings/Hut.md" >}}). Once kittens begin arriving in your Small Village tab, you can assign them to the Woodcutter job.

### **Upgrades and Boosts (The Axe Chain)**
You can drastically improve the effectiveness of your Woodcutters through a dedicated series of Workshop technologies known as the "axe chain". These upgrades provide massive percentage-based boosts to their output:
*   [Mineral Axe]( {{< relref "upgrade/Mineral_Axe.md" >}}) +70% effectiveness.
*   [Iron Axe:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Iron_Axe.md" >}}) +50% effectiveness.
*   [Steel Axe:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Steel_Axe.md" >}}) +50% effectiveness.
*   [Titanium Axe:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Titanium_Axe.md" >}}) +50% effectiveness.
*   [Alloy Axe:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Alloy_Axe.md" >}}) +50% effectiveness.
*   [Unobtainium Axe:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Unobtainium_Axe.md" >}}) The final upgrade in the Woodcutter tool chain.

### **Strategic Management**
*   **The Early Game Grind:** Before you unlock advanced buildings, wood will accumulate very slowly. It is highly recommended to heavily focus your workforce on wood production until you can afford [Lumber Mills]( {{< relref "buildings/Lumber_Mill.md" >}}).
*   **Multiplicative Synergy:** The percentage bonuses granted by the workshop axe upgrades stack multiplicatively with the bonuses provided by your Lumber Mills. This means combining both upgrade paths will exponentially skyrocket your Woodcutters' yields.
*   **Workforce Ratios:** When balancing your early-to-mid game economy, a standard community strategy is to **employ half as many Miners as you have Woodcutters**. Because wood is constantly required to build the structures that boost mineral output, this 2:1 ratio generally maintains a stable and parallel growth of both resources. 
*   **Leader Synergy:** If you are desperately bottlenecked by wood, you can assign a Woodcutter as your village **Leader** and promote them with gold. A highly ranked leader provides such a massive percentage boost to their specific job that it can be the equivalent of adding up to 11 extra kittens to your woodcutting workforce.
