[Scholars]( {{< relref "job/Scholar.md" >}}) are a specialized job for your kittens that becomes available after you construct your first [Library]( {{< relref "buildings/Library.md" >}}) (which requires 25 Wood). Their primary function is to generate [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}), which is essential for purchasing new technologies and unlocking Workshop upgrades.

### **Core Production**
Each kitten actively assigned as a Scholar generates a base output of **+0.035 Science per tick**. 

### **Upgrades and Late-Game Utility**
While their main purpose is Science generation, Scholars gain a massive secondary utility in the late game through the [Astrophysicists]( {{< relref "upgrade/Astrophysicists.md" >}}) workshop upgrade. 

*   [Astrophysicists:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Astrophysicists.md" >}}) Costing 250,000 Science and 350 Unobtainium, this upgrade enables each of your Scholar kittens to passively produce **+0.0005 starcharts per second**. 

Because starcharts can become a major bottleneck for space exploration and ship building, reallocating extra kittens to the Scholar job is an effective late-game strategy to boost your starchart generation without relying solely on manual astronomical events.
