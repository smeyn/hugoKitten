[Robotic Assistance]( {{< relref "upgrade/Robotic_Assistance.md" >}}) is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that improves the efficiency of your workforce by reducing their food requirements.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research the [Robotics]( {{< relref "techs/Robotics.md" >}}) science, which costs 140,000 Science and 80 Blueprints.
*   **Upgrade Resource Requirements:** 
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 100,000
    *   [Steel:]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}) 10,000
    *   [Gears:]( {{< relref "craft/Gear.md" >}}) 250.

### **Core Effect**
The primary benefit of Robotic Assistance is that it **reduces the catnip consumption of all working kittens by 25%**. By building robots to assist with labor, your "workers do less effort" and therefore require less food to maintain their productivity.

### **Strategic Importance**
*   **Managing the Food Burden:** Each kitten in your village normally consumes 4.25 catnip per second (0.85 per tick). As your population grows, this consumption becomes a significant constraint on your expansion. Robotic Assistance provides a powerful global reduction to this burden, specifically for those kittens assigned to jobs.
*   **Mitigating Happiness Penalties:** When kitten happiness exceeds 100%, their catnip consumption increases significantly (for example, at 420% happiness, consumption increases by 320%). Researching Robotic Assistance is a vital step in balancing this "happiness liability," allowing you to maintain high production bonuses from a happy population without triggering catastrophic food shortages.
*   **Synergy with Automation:** It is typically researched alongside other automation-focused upgrades like [Factory Robotics]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Robotics.md" >}}) and [Steel Plants]( {{< relref "upgrade/Steel_Plants.md" >}}), which together transition your village from manual labor to a more industrialized and efficient economy.
