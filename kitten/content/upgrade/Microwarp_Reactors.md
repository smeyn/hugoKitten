In **Kittens Game**, the **Microwarp Reactor** is a high-level **Workshop upgrade** that significantly enhances your space-based resource generation, particularly [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}).

### Key Effects
This upgrade represents an advancement in reactor technology that allows your orbital stations to function with much higher efficiency:

* **Lunar Outpost Production:** It provides a **+25% bonus** to the Unobtainium production of your [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}). 
* **Energy Scaling:** It allows your outposts to extract more resources without increasing their energy consumption, making it a critical upgrade for scaling your **Time Crystal** and [Relic]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) economy.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see Microwarp Reactors in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the [Superconductors]( {{< relref "techs/Superconductors.md" >}}) and **Space** tech branches:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Superconductors]( {{< relref "techs/Superconductors.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite |
| [Lunar Outpost]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) | Space Building | You must have at least one [Lunar Outpost]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) built |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **150,000 Science** |
| [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) | Resource | **500 Unobtainium** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **5,000 Alloy** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}), the Microwarp Reactor is one of the most important upgrades you can purchase at this stage.

1.  **The Unobtainium Grind:** As you know, Unobtainium is the bottleneck for almost every late-game mechanic, including [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) and [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}). A 25% boost to its production is a massive accelerator for your entire civilization.
2.  **Energy Management:** Because it increases production without increasing power draw, it helps you maintain a positive energy balance as you build more [Containment Chambers]( {{< relref "space/containment-chamber.md" >}}) for [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}).
3.  **Path to Eludium:** Unobtainium is the key ingredient for [Eludium]( {{< relref "resource/eludium.md" >}}). By increasing your Unobtainium yield, you can craft more Eludium, which is required for the "Long Range Spaceships" and outer-system missions we discussed.

> **Pro Tip:** Before you buy this, ensure your [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) production is stable. While the upgrade itself doesn't use Uranium, the increased Unobtainium output will likely lead you to build more [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}), which *do* consume a large amount of Uranium over time.
