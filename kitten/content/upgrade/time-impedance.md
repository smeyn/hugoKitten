[Time Impedance]( {{< relref "upgrade/time-impedance.md" >}}) is a late-game time manipulation upgrade designed to counteract the negative penalties associated with the Dark Future. 

### **Core Mechanics and Visibility**
*   **Effect:** Each level of Time Impedance **suppresses the Dark Future temporal penalty by 1,000 years**. Without this upgrade, the Dark Future causes the cost of Time Crystals to progressively increase as you advance further into the timeline.
*   **Visibility Requirement:** The upgrade is hidden for the vast majority of a playthrough; it will **only become visible on your menu once your in-game calendar year reaches at least 40,000**.

### **Costs and Scaling**
*   **Base Cost:** Purchasing the upgrade requires **100 Time Crystals and 250 Relics**.
*   **Price Ratio:** The cost of subsequent levels scales relatively slowly, with a price ratio of just **1.05**.

### **Synergies and Boosts**
The base 1,000-year suppression effect of Time Impedance can be enhanced by other time-related upgrades:
*   [Temporal Accelerator:]( {{< relref "time/temporal-accelerator.md" >}}) Each accelerator provides a **5% improvement** to the effectiveness of your Time Impedance.
*   [Blazars:]( {{< relref "transcendence/Blazar.md" >}}) Each level of this advanced Cryptotheology upgrade improves your Time Impedance by an additional **10%**.

### **Strategic Value**
Once you cross the 40k year threshold, you will notice your Time Crystal costs beginning to rise due to the Dark Future. While purchasing a few levels of Time Impedance and Temporal Accelerators is recommended to help ward off these increasing costs, the penalty is relatively minimal, so it doesn't require excessive worrying or over-investment.
