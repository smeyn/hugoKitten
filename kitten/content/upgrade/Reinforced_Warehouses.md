In **Kittens Game**, [Reinforced Warehouses]( {{< relref "upgrade/Reinforced_Warehouses.md" >}}) is a mid-game **Workshop upgrade** that helps solve your storage bottlenecks. As your civilization grows, your basic storage buildings (Barns and Warehouses) often can't hold enough resources to afford expensive new technologies or space missions.

### Key Effects
The primary function of Reinforced Warehouses is to **expand your resource caps**:

* **Storage Capacity:** It increases the storage capacity of all your **Warehouses by +25%**.
* **Broad Impact:** This affects almost all basic resources stored in Warehouses, including **Wood**, **Minerals**, [Iron]( {{< relref "resource/iron.md" >}}), [Coal]( {{< relref "resource/coal.md" >}}), and [Gold]( {{< relref "resource/Gold.md" >}}).
* **Progression:** This is the first of several Warehouse upgrades (followed by Titanium, Alloy, and Concrete versions) that allow you to reach the massive resource caps needed for late-game goals like [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) or [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}).

---

### Requirements & Cost
To see this upgrade in your Workshop, you must have researched [Construction]( {{< relref "techs/Construction.md" >}}).

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Construction]( {{< relref "techs/Construction.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| [Warehouse]( {{< relref "buildings/Warehouse.md" >}}) | Building | You must have at least one [Warehouse]( {{< relref "buildings/Warehouse.md" >}}) built. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **10,000 Science** |
| [Iron]( {{< relref "resource/iron.md" >}}) | Resource | **2,000 Iron** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Distortion management**, Reinforced Warehouses is a "foundational" upgrade you likely acquired long ago. However, its importance scales as you progress:

1.  **Blueprint & Steel Farming:** To craft the thousands of [Blueprints]( {{< relref "resource/Blueprint.md" >}}) and [Steel]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}) needed for high-level industry, you need high storage caps to prevent your "raw" resources (Science and Minerals) from hitting their limits while you're away.
2.  **Unobtainium Scaling:** Later upgrades in this series (like [Titanium Warehouses]( {{< relref "upgrade/Titanium_Warehouses.md" >}})) are essential for increasing your [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) and [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) caps, which directly limits how many [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) or **Space Beacons** you can build.
3.  **Efficiency:** Instead of building 10 more physical Warehouses (which get more expensive with each one), this upgrade boosts your existing infrastructure for a flat science cost.

> **Pro Tip:** If you find yourself frequently hitting your resource caps, check if you've also purchased the **Expansions** or **Cargo Ships** upgrades. Cargo Ships, in particular, provide a massive boost to Warehouse storage based on your total fleet size.
