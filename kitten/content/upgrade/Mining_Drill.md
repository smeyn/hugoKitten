In **Kittens Game**, the [Mining Drill]( {{< relref "upgrade/Mining_Drill.md" >}}) is a vital mid-game **Workshop upgrade** that significantly boosts your mineral production, which is essential as you begin to scale your industrial infrastructure.

### Key Effects
The Mining Drill provides a massive efficiency boost to your [Miners]( {{< relref "job/Miner.md" >}}):

* **Miner Efficiency:** It increases the effectiveness of your **Miners by +20%**.
* **Production Scaling:** This is a multiplicative bonus that affects the base production of every kitten assigned to the Miner job. As you stack more global production bonuses (like [Magnetos]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}}) or [Solar Revolution]( {{< relref "religion/Solar_Revolution.md" >}})), this 20% increase becomes a significant source of raw materials.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see the Mining Drill in your Workshop tab, you must have advanced through the early industrial technologies:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coal Mining** | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite |
| [Workshop]( {{< relref "buildings/Workshop.md" >}}) | Building | Required to craft the upgrade |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **750 Science** |
| [Steel]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}) | Resource | **100 Steel** |

### Strategic Context
Given your progress with [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Machine Learning]( {{< relref "upgrade/Machine_Learning.md" >}}), the Mining Drill is a legacy upgrade that likely fueled your early transition into the Steel Age. Here is how it impacts your current high-level play:

1.  **Steel Production:** Since minerals are a primary ingredient for [Steel]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}) (and later [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}})), the Mining Drill helps ensure your [Smelters]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) and [Calciners]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}) never run out of raw ore.
2.  **Concrete & Skyscrapers:** In the mid-to-late game, your mineral needs skyrocket as you build [Concrete Huts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Concrete_Huts.md" >}}) and **Skyscrapers**. The Mining Drill helps keep your mineral income high enough to support these massive construction projects.
3.  **Geologist Synergy:** While the Mining Drill specifically helps [Miners]( {{< relref "job/Miner.md" >}}), it works alongside Geologist upgrades (like [Geodesy]( {{< relref "upgrade/Geodesy.md" >}})) to ensure your ground-based resource production remains competitive with your space-based income.

> **Pro Tip:** If you find your mineral production is still the bottleneck for crafting [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}), check if you have also purchased the **Unobtanium Drill** upgrade. It provides an even larger boost and is essential for the late-game resource grind.
