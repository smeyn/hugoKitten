[Hunting Armour]( {{< relref "upgrade/Hunting_Armour.md" >}}) is a highly impactful Workshop upgrade designed to significantly increase the rare resources you gather from sending your kittens on hunts.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** To unlock this upgrade in your Workshop, you must first research the [Metal Working]( {{< relref "techs/Metal_Working.md" >}}) science.
*   **Cost:** Purchasing Hunting Armour requires an investment of **2,000 Science** and **750 Iron**.

### **Core Effects and Yields**
*   **The Bonus:** The upgrade makes your hunters three times as effective by granting a massive **+2.0 to your global hunting bonus**. 
*   **Resource Multiplier:** This flat bonus translates to roughly a **165% to 200% increase** in the basic amounts of furs and ivory you receive from each hunt. 
*   **Stacking:** Assuming you have already purchased the previous hunting upgrade, [Bolas]( {{< relref "upgrade/Bolas.md" >}}) (which provides +1.0), acquiring Hunting Armour will bring your total hunting bonus to 3.0. With this combined multiplier, an average hunt is expected to yield approximately **136.50 furs and 42.00 ivory**.

### **Strategic Value**
Acquiring Hunting Armour is often viewed as a major progression milestone, especially during standard mid-game progression or restrictive challenge runs like Iron Will. Because it drastically scales up your fur and ivory income, it directly alleviates the bottleneck for crafting parchment and manuscripts, allowing you to effectively tackle the massive culture requirements needed to unlock trade races and early religion upgrades.
