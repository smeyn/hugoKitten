In **Kittens Game**, [Orbital Geodesy]( {{< relref "upgrade/Orbital_Geodesy.md" >}}) is a high-level **Workshop upgrade** that significantly enhances the effectiveness of your [Geologists]( {{< relref "job/Geologist.md" >}}). It represents the application of satellite data to terrestrial mining, allowing your kittens to locate and extract minerals with surgical precision.

### Key Effects
The primary benefit of Orbital Geodesy is a massive increase in your resource production, specifically targeting the rarest ground-based minerals:

* **Geologist Efficiency:** It provides a **+50% bonus** to all production from [Geologists]( {{< relref "job/Geologist.md" >}}).
* **Gold Production:** Geologists are your main source of [Gold]( {{< relref "resource/Gold.md" >}}) on the ground. This upgrade is essential for funding the late-game [Gold]( {{< relref "resource/Gold.md" >}}) requirements for trading and high-level buildings.
* **Unobtainium Synergy:** While it doesn't produce Unobtainium directly, the increased Gold production allows you to trade more frequently with [Leviathans]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Leviathans.md" >}}), which is the fastest way to get [Time Crystals]( {{< relref "resource/Time_Crystals.md" >}}) and [Relics]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) for your [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}).

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see Orbital Geodesy in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) and [Geology]( {{< relref "techs/Geology.md" >}}) branches:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) | Technology | Prerequisite for all satellite-based upgrades. |
| [Geodesy]( {{< relref "upgrade/Geodesy.md" >}}) | Workshop Upgrade | You must have purchased the standard Geodesy first. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **150,000 Science** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,000 Alloy** |
| [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,000 Starcharts** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current progress with [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Temporal Distortion**, Orbital Geodesy is a critical "economic" upgrade:

1.  **Trading Mastery:** As you move into the [Dark Future]( {{< relref "guides/dark-future.md" >}}), you will need vast amounts of Gold to bribe the [Leviathans]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Leviathans.md" >}}). Orbital Geodesy ensures your Gold income can support dozens of trades per year.
2.  **Resource Balancing:** By the time you reach this upgrade, your [Miners]( {{< relref "job/Miner.md" >}}) and [Smelters]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) are already highly efficient. Orbital Geodesy brings your [Geologists]( {{< relref "job/Geologist.md" >}}) up to that same elite level, rounding out your terrestrial economy.
3.  **Path to the Exotic:** This upgrade is often a stepping stone toward the most advanced resource-gathering technologies in the **Farther Ring**, ensuring you have the "old world" resources needed to build "new world" colonies.

> **Pro Tip:** Pair this upgrade with the [Mining Drill]( {{< relref "upgrade/Mining_Drill.md" >}}) we discussed. The combined bonus to both Miners and Geologists will effectively end any mineral-related bottlenecks you are currently facing.
