In **Kittens Game**, the **Ironwood Hut** is an essential early-to-mid game **Workshop upgrade** that allows your civilization to grow beyond its initial limits. It represents an advancement in construction technology, allowing you to use more durable materials for kitten housing.

### Key Effects
The primary purpose of Ironwood Huts is to **increase your maximum population**:

* **Housing Capacity:** It increases the number of kittens that can live in each [Hut]( {{< relref "buildings/Hut.md" >}}) by **+0.5**.
* **Population Growth:** Since more kittens mean more workers (and more science/production), this is one of the most important upgrades for scaling your civilization.
* [Logistics:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Logistics.md" >}}) It does not increase the physical footprint or price of the Huts themselves; it simply makes the existing ones more efficient.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see this upgrade in your Workshop, you must have researched [Construction]( {{< relref "techs/Construction.md" >}}).

* **Science Cost:** 30,000 Science
* **Wood Cost:** 15,000 Wood
* **Iron Cost:** 3,000 Iron

### Strategic Importance
Given your progress with [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}), you likely bought this long ago, but it remains a pillar of your "Price Ratio" strategy:

1.  **Metaphysics Synergy:** Ironwood Huts are the first in a long line of housing upgrades (followed by Concrete Huts, Unobtainium Huts, etc.). These upgrades are the primary way you reach the high population counts (70+) needed to start earning significant [Paragon]( {{< relref "resource/paragon.md" >}}) upon resetting.
2.  **Early Bottleneck:** The 30,000 Science cost is often the first major "wall" players hit. Reaching this milestone usually requires building several **Libraries** and **Academies**, or assigning more kittens to the **Scientist** role.
3.  **Iron Management:** Since it requires 3,000 Iron, it often competes with [Iron Hoes]( {{< relref "upgrade/Iron_Hoes.md" >}}) or [Steamworks]( {{< relref "buildings/Steamworks.md" >}}) for your resources. Usually, prioritizing housing (and thus more kittens) is the better long-term move.
