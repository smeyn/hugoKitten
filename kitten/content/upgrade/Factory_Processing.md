[Factory Processing]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Processing.md" >}}) is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that significantly enhances your civilization's ability to refine oil into Kerosene by leveraging your industrial infrastructure.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research [Oil Processing]( {{< relref "techs/Oil_Processing.md" >}}), which is unlocked after Rocketry.
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 195,000
    *   [Titanium:]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) 7,500
    *   [Concrete:]( {{< relref "craft/Concrete.md" >}}) 125.

### **Core Effects and Mechanics**
The primary function of Factory Processing is to link your [Factory]( {{< relref "buildings/Factory.md" >}}) buildings to your [Kerosene]( {{< relref "resource/kerosene.md" >}}) production:
*   **Scaling Bonus:** Each Factory built provides a **+5% bonus to oil refinement effectiveness** (the amount of Kerosene produced from Oil).
*   **Multiplicative Stacking:** This effectiveness bonus **stacks multiplicatively** with your global crafting ratio bonuses, allowing for massive Kerosene yields as your industrial base grows.
*   **Workshop Interaction:** While primarily categorized as a Factory upgrade, it is purchased in the Workshop and listed as improving "Kerosene Crafting".

### **Strategic Importance**
*   **Fuel for Space Exploration:** Kerosene is a critical resource for late-game progression, used as fuel for [Spaceports]( {{< relref "buildings/spaceport.md" >}}) and for sending high-tier space missions like [Piscine]( {{< relref "planet/Piscine.md" >}}), [Helios]( {{< relref "planet/Helios.md" >}}), and **T-Minus**.
*   **Managing Oil Caps:** Oil production is often slow but hits its storage cap frequently. By using [Engineers]( {{< relref "job/Engineer.md" >}}) to automate the conversion of Oil to Kerosene, players can prevent Oil production from stalling; Factory Processing ensures this conversion is as efficient as possible.
*   **Infrastructure Synergy:** This upgrade transforms Factories from simple crafting boosters into vital components of your energy and fuel supply chain, making large factory networks essential for sustained space operations.
