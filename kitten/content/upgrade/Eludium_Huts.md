[Eludium Huts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Eludium_Huts.md" >}}) is a high-tier Workshop upgrade that provides the final available reduction to the price ratio of Hut buildings. It is a key component for maximizing your kitten population before a reset to increase prestige rewards.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Science Cost:** 275,000.
*   **Resource Cost:** 125 Eludium.
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research **Exometallurgy** (which was formerly known as Advanced Exogeology).

### **Core Effects and Mechanics**
The primary function of this upgrade is to reduce the **Hut price ratio by 10%**.
*   **Ratio Reduction:** When stacked with earlier upgrades—Ironwood (-50%), Concrete (-30%), and Unobtainium (-25%)—this brings the Hut price ratio down to approximately **1.289**.
*   **Population Scaling:** This reduction allows you to build approximately **23% more Huts** than was possible with Unobtainium Huts alone. For instance, a player who previously capped at 50 Huts could reach 61 Huts after this upgrade, adding **22 extra kittens** to their village.

### **Strategic Importance**
*   **The Eludium Era:** Purchasing this upgrade typically occurs as you transition into the mid-to-late game, often coinciding with the beginning of the **Unicorn Era** and the stabilization of unobtainium production at approximately 1 per second.
*   [Paragon Farming:]( {{< relref "guides/paragon-farming.md" >}}) Because reset rewards (Paragon and Karma) are calculated based on population, Eludium Huts are essential for "deep runs" where you aim to maximize your total kitten count.
*   **Efficiency Considerations:** While valuable for long runs, some optimization guides suggest that Eludium Huts may be **ignored during short paragon-grinding runs**. This is because the additional kittens only improve paragon-per-hour efficiency by about **1%** (for example, increasing efficiency from 86% to 86.6%), which may not justify the 125 eludium cost in a fast-reset scenario.
*   **Science Cap Synergy:** During this era, your science cap must be sufficiently high (around 275k) to purchase the upgrade, which usually requires a significant network of **Observatories, Data Centers, and Bio Labs**.
