In **Kittens Game**, the **Nanosuit** is a high-level **Workshop upgrade** that provides a massive boost to your environmental resilience and global production, particularly during the late-game stage you are in.

### Key Effects
The Nanosuit represents an advanced layer of protection and efficiency for your kittens, allowing them to work more effectively regardless of environmental conditions:

* **Global Production Bonus:** It provides a **+2% bonus** to all production. While 2% sounds small, in the late game, this is a multiplicative bonus that stacks with your [Solar Revolution]( {{< relref "religion/Solar_Revolution.md" >}}), [Magnetos]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}}), and [Relics]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}), leading to a substantial increase in raw resource generation.
* **Environmental Protection:** It drastically reduces the negative effects of [Pollution]( {{< relref "guides/Pollution.md" >}}) on your kitten's happiness and production. This is vital if you are running dozens of [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) and [Calciners]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}) to fuel your space industry.
* **Cold Resistance:** It increases the effectiveness of your kittens during **Winter**, further stabilizing your catnip and wood production.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see the Nanosuit in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the [Nanotechnology]( {{< relref "techs/Nanotechnology.md" >}}) branch of the tech tree:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Nanotechnology]( {{< relref "techs/Nanotechnology.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **175,000 Science** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **5,000 Alloy** |
| [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,000 Unobtainium** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}), the Nanosuit is a "foundation" upgrade for the Dark Future:

1.  **Pollution Mitigation:** As you scale your energy production for [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}), your pollution levels will naturally rise. The Nanosuit is one of the few ways to keep your kittens happy (and thus productive) without having to disable your heavy industry.
2.  **Unobtainium Efficiency:** Since it requires 1,000 Unobtainium, it is often a mid-priority upgrade. You should prioritize it once your [Lunar Outpost]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) production is stable (likely after getting the [Microwarp Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Microwarp_Reactors.md" >}}) we discussed).
3.  **Path to the Exotic:** The Nanosuit is a prerequisite for even more advanced suits and environmental controls that allow for more efficient exploration of the **Farther Ring** planets like [Jupiter]( {{< relref "space/jupiter.md" >}}).

> **Pro Tip:** If you are finding that your production drops significantly during the winter even with [Iron Hoes]( {{< relref "upgrade/Iron_Hoes.md" >}}), the Nanosuit's environmental leveling will make your production curve much flatter and more predictable.
