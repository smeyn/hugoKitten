The [Iron Axe]( {{< relref "upgrade/Iron_Axe.md" >}}) is an early-game Workshop upgrade that significantly boosts your wood production.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Cost:** Purchasing the upgrade requires **100 Science** and **50 Iron**. 

### **Core Effects and Mechanics**
*   **Boost:** It makes your [Woodcutter]( {{< relref "job/Woodcutter.md" >}}) kittens **50% more effective**, directly increasing the amount of wood they generate per tick.
*   **The Axe Chain:** The Iron Axe is part of the "axe" chain of workshop upgrades, which progresses from the Mineral Axe up through Steel, Titanium, Alloy, and Unobtainium axes. 
*   **Synergy:** Importantly, the production bonuses from the axe chain of upgrades stack multiplicatively with the bonuses provided by [Lumber Mills]( {{< relref "buildings/Lumber_Mill.md" >}}), making them incredibly valuable for scaling your wood economy as you progress.
