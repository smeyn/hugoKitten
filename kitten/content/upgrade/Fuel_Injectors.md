The **Fuel Injector** is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade for the [Steamworks]( {{< relref "buildings/Steamworks.md" >}}) building that improves coal management efficiency.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Unlocked By:** Researching the [Combustion]( {{< relref "techs/Combustion.md" >}}) technology.
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 100,000
    *   [Gears:]( {{< relref "craft/Gear.md" >}}) 250
    *   [Oil:]( {{< relref "resource/oil.md" >}}) 20,000.

### **Core Effects**
The primary function of the Fuel Injector is to **reduce the coal production penalty** (or coal consumption) of active Steamworks by **20%**. 

### **Strategic Context**
*   **Coal Efficiency:** Active Steamworks are notorious for consuming large amounts of coal, which can bottleneck the production of [Steel]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}). The Fuel Injector, along with the [High Pressure Engine]( {{< relref "upgrade/High_Pressure_Engine.md" >}}) (which provides an identical 20% reduction), is essential for maintaining active Steamworks without completely depleting your coal reserves.
*   **Synergy:** These upgrades allow you to keep Steamworks running to benefit from their automated features—such as manuscript printing via the [Printing Press]( {{< relref "upgrade/Printing_Press.md" >}}) or the production boost to [Magnetos]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}})—while still gathering enough coal to support industrial crafting.
