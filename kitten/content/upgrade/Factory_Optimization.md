[Factory Optimization]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Optimization.md" >}}) is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that enhances the effectiveness of the [Engineer]( {{< relref "job/Engineer.md" >}}) kitten job, facilitating better automation of the crafting process.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** Researching [Electronics]( {{< relref "techs/Electronics.md" >}}) is required to unlock the upgrade.
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 75,000
    *   [Gears:]( {{< relref "craft/Gear.md" >}}) 250
    *   [Titanium:]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) 1,250.

### **Effects on Engineer Know-How**
The primary function of this upgrade is to increase the "know-how" (efficiency) of Engineers based on their class:
*   **Class 1 Engineer:** Know-how increases to **10**.
*   **Class 2 Engineer:** Know-how increases to **2**.

This improvement allows a smaller number of Engineers to perform a higher volume of automated crafts, which is critical for managing resources like [Oil]( {{< relref "resource/oil.md" >}}) or [Iron]( {{< relref "resource/iron.md" >}}) that would otherwise hit their storage caps while the player is away.

---

### **Related Industrial Optimizations**
While "Factory Optimization" specifically targets Engineer efficiency, several other "Factory" upgrades and mechanics are essential for maximizing industrial output:

*   [Factory Logistics:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Logistics.md" >}}) This upgrade increases the global **craft bonus** provided by each Factory building by **1%** (moving the base bonus from 5% to 6%).
*   [Factory Processing:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Processing.md" >}}) This upgrade links your industrial base to your fuel production, providing a **+5% bonus to oil refinement effectiveness** (Kerosene production) for every Factory built.
*   [Space Manufacturing:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Space_Manufacturing.md" >}}) A game-changing upgrade that allows Factories to provide a bonus to space structures like [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) and [Orbital Arrays]( {{< relref "space/orbital_array.md" >}}). This bonus is calculated as 75% of your total factory craft ratio.
*   **Full Industrialization (Policy):** This policy makes all Factories **5% more powerful** (boosting their crafting and space bonuses) at the cost of increased pollution.

### **Strategic Importance**
In the mid-to-late game, optimizing factories is a top priority because their effects are **multiplicative** and provide **global multipliers**. High-level players typically focus on maxing out their Factory count to drive massive [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) production through Space Elevators, as this is the primary gateway to Phase 4 (Endgame) content like [Time Crystals]( {{< relref "resource/Time_Crystals.md" >}}) and [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}).
