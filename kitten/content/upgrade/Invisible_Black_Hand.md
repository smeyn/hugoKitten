The [Invisible Black Hand]( {{< relref "upgrade/Invisible_Black_Hand.md" >}}) is a late-game upgrade that gives you greater control over the game's cryptocurrency economy. 

Here are the details:
*   **Core Effect:** It adds an option that allows you to force a blackcoin market crash in exchange for time crystals. The in-game description states that it leads the blackcoin market "usually smoothly, but it can punch the market in the face if angry" (and notes that it is not related to the Invisible Pink Unicorn).
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first unlock [Blackchain]( {{< relref "techs/Blackchain.md" >}}).
*   **Cost:** Purchasing the Invisible Black Hand requires **128 Time Crystals**, **64 Blackcoin**, **32 Void**, and **4,096 Temporal Flux**.
