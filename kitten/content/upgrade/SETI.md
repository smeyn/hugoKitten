In **Kittens Game**, [SETI]( {{< relref "upgrade/SETI.md" >}}) (Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence) is one of the most beloved "Quality of Life" upgrades in the [Workshop]( {{< relref "buildings/Workshop.md" >}}). It marks a major turning point where your astronomical observations transition from manual clicking to full automation.

### Key Effects
The primary function of SETI is to automate the gathering of astronomical data:

* **Auto-Observation:** Normally, you have to manually click the "A celestial event has occurred" button to get [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}). SETI **automatically clicks this button for you** with 100% success.
* **Starchart Generation:** Because it never misses an event, your Starchart production becomes a steady, passive stream. 
* **Science Bonus:** It provides a small bonus to your overall science production (usually around **+5%**) by streamlining how your astronomers process data.

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see SETI in your Workshop, you must have advanced into the early space-age technologies:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Electronics]( {{< relref "techs/Electronics.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **125,000 Science** |
| [Titanium]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) | Resource | **250 Titanium** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Space Exploration**, SETI is a non-negotiable upgrade for your stage of the game:

1.  **Automation of the Grind:** Before SETI, your **Starchart** income is limited by your attention span. After SETI, you can leave the game running (idling) and return to thousands of Starcharts, which are essential for those "Long Range Spaceships" and [Jupiter]( {{< relref "space/jupiter.md" >}}) missions we discussed.
2.  **Synergy with Satellites:** SETI works beautifully with your [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) and the [Navigation Relay]( {{< relref "space/Navigation_Relay.md" >}}). While Satellites increase the *amount* of science/starcharts per event, SETI ensures you *never miss* an event.
3.  **End of "Signals Interference":** In older versions or specific challenges, manual clicking is hindered by "interference." SETI effectively bypasses these mechanics, providing a clean data stream.

> **Expert Tip:** If you find your Starchart production is still slow even with SETI, make sure you've built more **Observatories**. SETI only automates the *event*; the *frequency* of those events is determined by your total number of Observatories.
