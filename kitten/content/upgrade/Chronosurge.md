To unlock the [Chronosurge]( {{< relref "upgrade/chronosurge.md" >}}) workshop upgrade and transition from offline-only temporal flux generation to active farming, you need to navigate a specific branch of the late-game Science and Time trees. 

Here is the exact progression path, costs, and requirements to unlock and fund this upgrade:

### **Step 1: The Technology Foundation**
You must progress through the temporal science tree to unlock the necessary mechanics:
1.  [Chronophysics:]( {{< relref "techs/Chronophysics.md" >}}) Costs **250k Science** and **5 Time Crystals**. This unlocks the [Chronosphere]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) building.
2.  [Tachyon Theory:]( {{< relref "techs/Tachyon_Theory.md" >}}) Costs **750k Science**, **25 Time Crystals**, and **1 Relic**. This unlocks the [Chronoforge]( {{< relref "upgrade/Chronoforge.md" >}}) workshop upgrade (which itself costs 500k Science, 5 Relics, and 10 Time Crystals to buy).
3.  [Void Space:]( {{< relref "techs/Void_Space.md" >}}) Costs **800k Science**, **30 Time Crystals**, and **100 Void**.
4.  [Paradox Theory:]( {{< relref "techs/Paradox_Theory.md" >}}) Costs **1M Science**, **40 Time Crystals**, and **250 Void**. Researching this is what officially unlocks the [Chronocontrol]( {{< relref "time/chrono_control.md" >}}) time structure and enables the [Chronosurge]( {{< relref "upgrade/chronosurge.md" >}}) upgrade to be researched.

---

### **Step 2: Build a Chronocontrol Station**
Before you can purchase the Chronosurge upgrade in your Workshop, you must construct at least **one Chronocontrol** structure:
*   **Location:** Under the Time tab (in the Void Space section).
*   **Construction Cost:** **30 Time Crystals**, **500 Void**, and **3,000 Temporal Flux**.
*   **Operating Cost:** Consumes **15 Watts (Wt)** of energy to run. 

---

### **Step 3: Purchase Chronosurge**
Once you have built your first Chronocontrol, the [Chronosurge]( {{< relref "upgrade/chronosurge.md" >}}) upgrade will appear in your Workshop. It requires a significant amount of endgame resources to craft:
*   **Upgrade Cost:** 
    *   **25 Time Crystals**
    *   **100k Unobtainium**
    *   **750 Void**
    *   **6,500 Temporal Flux**

---

### **The Active Flux Loop (How It Works)**
Once Chronosurge is active, your [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) will actively generate **1 unit of Temporal Flux every calendar year**. 

Because shattering (combusting) a Time Crystal immediately advances the calendar by one full year, **every single shatter you perform will instantly reward you with 1 Temporal Flux per active Chronosphere**. This completely removes the bottleneck of waiting in "offline" mode or pausing your game to accumulate flux, allowing you to instantly generate thousands of units of temporal flux for advanced loops (such as repairing Cryochambers).
