The [Mineral Axe]( {{< relref "upgrade/Mineral_Axe.md" >}}) is an early-game Workshop upgrade designed to significantly boost your wood production. 

**Costs**
To purchase the Mineral Axe, you will need an investment of **100 Science** and **500 Minerals**. 

**Core Effects**
Once acquired, the upgrade makes your **Woodcutter kittens 70% more effective** at their jobs, granting a direct +70% bonus to their wood output. 

**Strategic Importance**
The Mineral Axe is the very first upgrade in the "axe chain" of workshop technologies (which is eventually followed by the Iron Axe, Steel Axe, Titanium Axe, and so on). Because workshop upgrades act as multiplicative modifiers that stack alongside the production bonuses of your buildings (like Lumber Mills), acquiring the Mineral Axe and its successors is highly recommended to quickly scale up your wood economy without needing to constantly reassign your kitten population.
