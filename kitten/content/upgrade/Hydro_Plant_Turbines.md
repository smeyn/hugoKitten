In **Kittens Game**, the **Hydro Plant Turbine** is not a standalone building but a powerful **Workshop upgrade** for your [Hydro Plants]( {{< relref "buildings/Hydro_Plant.md" >}}). It represents an engineering improvement that extracts significantly more energy from your existing water infrastructure.

### Key Effects
This upgrade is essential for managing your late-game energy grid, especially when scaling up [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) and **Containment Chambers**:

* **Energy Production:** It increases the energy output of each [Hydro Plant]( {{< relref "buildings/Hydro_Plant.md" >}}) by **+15%**. 
* **Scaling:** Because Hydro Plants are one of the few non-pollution-generating power sources, this upgrade is a "clean" way to boost your total wattage without increasing your [Magneto]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}}) fuel consumption or [Pollution]( {{< relref "guides/Pollution.md" >}}) levels.

---

### How to Unlock and Craft
To see this upgrade in your Workshop, you need to reach a specific technological threshold:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Mechanization]( {{< relref "techs/Mechanization.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary tech requirement |
| [Hydro Plant]( {{< relref "buildings/Hydro_Plant.md" >}}) | Building | You must have at least one [Hydro Plant]( {{< relref "buildings/Hydro_Plant.md" >}}) built |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **25,000 Science** |
| [Energy]( {{< relref "resource/energy.md" >}}) | Resource | **500 Energy** (Total grid capacity) |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Distortion management**, the Hydro Plant Turbine serves a specific role:

1.  **Antimatter Production:** Generating [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) requires a massive energy surplus. Upgrading your turbines is a high-efficiency way to reach the required energy thresholds for **Containment Chambers**.
2.  **Unobtanium Farming:** Since [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) consume significant power, this upgrade helps keep your outposts running while you focus your [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) on enrichment rather than simple power generation.
3.  **Pollution Control:** Unlike coal-fired Steamworks, upgraded Hydro Plants provide consistent power without triggering the climate penalties that can slow down your kitten production in very long runs.

> **Pro Tip:** If you have already researched [Electronics]( {{< relref "techs/Electronics.md" >}}), pair this with [Photovoltaic Cells]( {{< relref "upgrade/Photovoltaic_Cells.md" >}}) for your Satellites to maximize your "green" energy output before you have to rely heavily on Thorium or Fusion.
