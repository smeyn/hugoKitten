[Enriched Thorium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Thorium.md" >}}) is a late-game Workshop upgrade that improves the efficiency of your [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) when they are running on thorium fuel.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research [Thorium Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Thorium_Reactors.md" >}}).
*   **Upgrade Costs:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 500,000
    *   [Thorium:]( {{< relref "resource/thorium.md" >}}) 12,500

### **Core Effects and Mechanics**
The primary function of this upgrade is to reduce the resource burden of the thorium energy cycle:
*   **Consumption Reduction:** It reduces the amount of Thorium consumed by Reactors by **25%**.
*   **Specific Yield:** Depending on the base consumption rate (which varies in sources), this reduction amounts to either **0.0125** or **0.0625 per tick** per reactor.

### **Strategic Context: The Thorium Cycle**
Enriched Thorium is the second of two major upgrades that allow Reactors to use Thorium (a resource crafted from Uranium) to boost energy production:
1.  [Thorium Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Thorium_Reactors.md" >}}) This initial upgrade allows Reactors to consume Thorium in exchange for a **+25% boost to energy production**.
2.  [Enriched Thorium:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Thorium.md" >}}) Once the first upgrade is active, Enriched Thorium makes the process more sustainable by cutting the fuel cost.

### **Optimization Tips**
*   **Crafting Thorium:** Thorium is produced in the Workshop by converting [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}).
*   **Kitten Synergy:** Kittens with the **Chemist trait** provide a **+0.075 bonus to the craft ratio** when producing Thorium, making it significantly cheaper to mass-produce the fuel needed for your reactor network.
*   **Power Management:** Thorium-fueled reactors are particularly useful in the very late game when performing "time skips" to generate [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}), as they provide the high energy output necessary for containment structures.
