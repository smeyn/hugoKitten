The [Temporal Battery]( {{< relref "upgrade/temporal_battery.md" >}}) is a time-manipulation upgrade located in the game's Time tab. 

**Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Base Cost:** Purchasing your first Temporal Battery requires an investment of 5 [Time Crystals]( {{< relref "resource/Time_Crystals.md" >}}). 
*   **Price Ratio:** The cost of each subsequent battery scales with a **1.25 price ratio**.

**Core Effect**
The sole function of the Temporal Battery is to **improve your temporal flux capacity by 25%** per level purchased.

**Strategic Value and Mechanics**
To understand the value of the Temporal Battery, it helps to understand how temporal flux operates within the game. [Temporal flux]( {{< relref "guides/temporal-flux.md" >}}) is a special resource used for time-related experiments. You passively earn 5 units of temporal flux per minute whenever the game is paused or closed (offline). Additionally, if you have the *Chronosurge* upgrade, your Chronospheres will automatically generate a small amount of flux every in-game year. 

This hoarded flux is primarily spent to activate [Tempus Fugit]( {{< relref "guides/tempus_fugit.md" >}}), a powerful toggleable ability that accelerates calendar time and **increases your overall production tick rate by 50%**. Because Tempus Fugit aggressively consumes 5 temporal flux every second it is active, purchasing Temporal Batteries allows you to stockpile a much larger reserve of flux while away from the game. This ultimately lets you sustain the massive 50% production boost from Tempus Fugit for significantly longer periods when you return to active play.
