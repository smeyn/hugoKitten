In Kittens Game, [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) is a specialized resource required for advanced late-game structures and space travel. It represents a refined version of standard Uranium, used primarily for high-energy applications.

### How to Obtain Enriched Uranium
You cannot mine Enriched Uranium directly. It must be crafted in the [Workshop]( {{< relref "buildings/Workshop.md" >}}) once you have unlocked the necessary technology.

* **Prerequisite Tech:** You must research [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}).
* **Crafting Recipe:** $1$ Enriched Uranium costs **500 Uranium**. 
* **Automation:** Once you unlock the **Centrifuge** (an upgrade for Reactors), your Reactors will begin converting standard Uranium into Enriched Uranium automatically over time, though at a very slow rate.

---

### Primary Uses
Enriched Uranium is a "bottleneck" resource for the mid-to-late game, used for:

* **Elevators:** Used to build and upgrade [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}), which significantly reduce the oil cost of launching missions into space.
* [Satellites:]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) Required for launching certain orbital modules that improve map visibility and energy production.
* [Lunar Outposts:]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) Essential for maintaining bases on the Moon, which are your primary source of [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}).
* **Fission Reactors:** Used to upgrade your power grid to produce significantly more energy than standard coal or steam power.

### Strategic Tip
The 500:1 conversion ratio is quite steep. To manage this, focus on increasing your **Uranium production** via [Accelerators]( {{< relref "buildings/Accelerator.md" >}}) and [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}). Once you have a steady flow of Unobtainium, you can trade with the [Griffins]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Griffins.md" >}}) or [Leviathans]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Leviathans.md" >}}) (if available) to bolster your Uranium stocks for mass crafting.
