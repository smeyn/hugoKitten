In **Kittens Game**, [Satellite Navigation]( {{< relref "upgrade/Satellite_Navigation.md" >}}) is a late-mid-game **Workshop upgrade** that serves as a vital bridge between your early space exploration and the industrialization of the solar system. 

It is specifically designed to solve the **Starchart bottleneck** that occurs when you begin reaching for distant bodies like [Jupiter]( {{< relref "space/jupiter.md" >}}), **Saturn**, and **Centaurus**.

---

### Key Effects
The primary function of Satellite Navigation is to increase the throughput of your orbital network:

* **Satellite Efficiency:** It provides a **+25% bonus** to the effectiveness of your [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}).
* **Starchart Production:** Since Satellites are your primary source of passive Starcharts (alongside Observatories), this upgrade drastically increases your income.
* **Science Cap:** If you have researched [Satellite Radio]( {{< relref "upgrade/Satellite_Radio.md" >}}), this efficiency boost also increases the **Maximum Science** provided by your Satellites, helping you reach the caps needed for [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}).

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see this upgrade in your Workshop, you must have moved past basic Rocketry and into deeper orbital mechanics:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| **Satellite** | Space Building | You must have at least one **Satellite** built. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **125,000 Science** |
| [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,000 Starcharts** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **2,500 Alloy** |

---

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Temporal Distortion**, Satellite Navigation is a high-priority "logistics" upgrade:

1.  **Fueling the Farther Ring:** Missions to the outer planets require thousands of Starcharts. This upgrade, combined with the [Navigation Relay]( {{< relref "space/Navigation_Relay.md" >}}) and [Hubble Space Telescope]( {{< relref "upgrade/Hubble_Space_Telescope.md" >}}), is what allows you to launch those missions without waiting hours for the data to accumulate.
2.  **Unobtainium Scaling:** To get more [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}), you need more [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}). To build those, you need [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) and [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}). Satellite Navigation ensures your "map data" (Starcharts) never prevents you from expanding your Unobtainium mines.
3.  **Automation Synergy:** Once you have [SETI]( {{< relref "upgrade/SETI.md" >}}) (which automates the clicking), Satellite Navigation ensures that every single event captured is significantly more valuable.
