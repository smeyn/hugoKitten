In **Kittens Game**, the [Reactor Vessel]( {{< relref "upgrade/Reactor_Vessel.md" >}}) is a high-level **Workshop upgrade** that enhances your industrial efficiency by improving the storage and processing capabilities of your [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}).

### Key Effects
The Reactor Vessel represents an upgrade to the physical containment of your nuclear systems, allowing them to operate at higher capacities:

* **Production Bonus:** It provides a **+5% production bonus** to all resources generated by your [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}).
* **Storage Increase:** It increases the maximum amount of [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) and [Thorium]( {{< relref "resource/thorium.md" >}}) your Reactors can hold. This is vital for long-duration automated play, ensuring your reactors don't stall because they've hit their local storage caps.
* **Efficiency:** This is a multiplicative bonus, meaning it scales with your other late-game multipliers like [Solar Revolution]( {{< relref "religion/Solar_Revolution.md" >}}) and [Magnetos]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}}).

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see this upgrade in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}) branch:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |

| [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| [Reactor]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) | Building | You must have at least one [Reactor]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) built. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **135,000 Science** |
| [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) | Resource | **5,000 Uranium** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **2,500 Alloy** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Temporal Distortion**, the Reactor Vessel is a "throughput" upgrade.

1.  **Fueling the Enriched Uranium Grind:** Since you are currently crafting Enriched Uranium (which costs 500 Uranium per unit), the increased storage provided by the Reactor Vessel helps prevent your standard Uranium from "overflowing" and being wasted before you can craft it.
2.  **Antimatter Stabilization:** Better storage and efficiency in your Reactors mean a more stable power grid. Since **Containment Chambers** for Antimatter require a constant power surplus, this upgrade helps smooth out any energy dips caused by fluctuating resource production.
3.  **Path to Thorium:** This is often a bridge upgrade taken before moving into [Thorium Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Thorium_Reactors.md" >}}). By maximizing your standard Reactor efficiency first, you ensure a smoother transition to the more powerful Thorium-based economy.

> **Pro Tip:** While 5,000 Uranium is a steep cost, the 5% global production boost to the Reactors' output (including their energy generation) makes this one of the most cost-effective ways to squeeze more power out of your existing grid.
