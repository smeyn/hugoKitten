The [Flux Condensator]( {{< relref "upgrade/Flux_Condensator.md" >}}) is a high-tier Workshop upgrade for the [Chronosphere]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) mega-structure that enables the preservation of **crafted resources** during a game reset. 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** It is unlocked by researching [Chronophysics]( {{< relref "techs/Chronophysics.md" >}}), which requires 250,000 Science and 5 Time Crystals.
*   **Upgrade Cost:** Purchasing the upgrade costs **250 Alloy, 5,000 Unobtainium, and 5 Time Crystals**.
*   **Persistence:** The Flux Condensator upgrade **does not carry over** across resets; it must be repurchased in every run where you intend to use its effects.

### **Core Effects and Mechanics**
While the Chronosphere normally only preserves a percentage of non-crafted resources, the Flux Condensator extends this ability to include complex materials.
*   **Preservation Formula:** The amount of each crafted resource preserved is determined by the number of Chronospheres built and the amount held at reset: **$1.5* {number of Chronospheres}* \sqrt{{resource amount}}$**.
*   **Resources Affected:** This includes critical materials such as **Steel, Alloy, Concrete, Eludium, Gear, Scaffold, Ship, Tanker, Kerosene, Parchment, Manuscript, Compendium, Blueprint, Thorium, and Megalith**.
*   **Diminishing Returns:** Because the formula uses a square root function, the efficiency of resource carryover decreases the more of a resource you have.

### **Strategic Importance**
The Flux Condensator is considered a game-changing upgrade for mid-to-late-game progression loops:
*   **Science Storage Carryover:** Its most significant benefit is the preservation of [Compendiums]( {{< relref "resource/Compendium.md" >}}). Since Compendiums increase maximum science storage, bringing thousands into a new run allows players to hit late-game science caps and unlock advanced technologies in the very first years of a new run.
*   **Early Industrial Stability:** Bringing over **Ships** and [Scaffolds]( {{< relref "craft/Scaffold.md" >}}) significantly speeds up early titanium collection and harbour construction, eliminating the early-game trading bottlenecks.
*   **Endgame "Positive Resets":** In the deep endgame, the Flux Condensator is mandatory for "positive resets," where the goal is to start each run with a net profit of materials compared to the previous one.
*   **Resource Management:** Players are advised to stockpile crafted resources before resetting with this upgrade to maximize the square-root-based yield. For example, having a high crafting ratio before reset is more efficient than waiting to craft resources in the next run.
