In **Kittens Game**, [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}) (often referred to as **Temporal Distortion**) is a late-game mechanic tied specifically to [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) and the management of your resource "memory" during resets.

It represents the instability of time as you attempt to carry resources across different timelines.

---

### How Distortion Works
Distortion is primarily a byproduct of **Temporal Paradoxes**. When you have [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}), you can carry resources through a reset. However, if you have **Void Resonators** and certain Paradox upgrades, your game generates "Temporal Paradoxes" (indicated by a purple notification).

* **The Resource Penalty:** High levels of Distortion/Paradoxes can cause your resource production to fluctuate or stall.
* **The Paradox Reset:** Every time a paradox occurs, you gain a small amount of [Void]( {{< relref "resource/void.md" >}}), but you also increase your Distortion level.

### Key Upgrades Related to Distortion
As you progress into the "Dark Future" (Year 40,000+), managing Distortion becomes a full-time job for your kittens:

* **[Temporal Accelerators]:** These increase the rate at which time passes but also increase the likelihood of paradoxes.
* **[Rectilinear Pitcher]:** This upgrade helps mitigate the negative effects of Distortion, allowing your timeline to remain more stable even when carrying massive amounts of resources.
* **[Void]:** Paradoxes are the primary way to generate [Void]( {{< relref "resource/void.md" >}}) in the mid-game. You actually *want* some level of Distortion/Paradoxes to occur so you can harvest Void for Chronotheology upgrades.

### Strategic Impact
1.  **Void Farming:** Early on, you trigger paradoxes (and thus Distortion) intentionally to get enough Void for your first **Obelisks** or **Nexus**.
2.  **Stability:** In very long runs, Distortion can become a nuisance. You eventually build [Mausoleums]( {{< relref "transcendence/Mausoleum.md" >}}) and other structures to stabilize the timeline.
3.  **Flux:** Distortion interacts with [Temporal Flux]( {{< relref "guides/temporal-flux.md" >}}). If your Distortion is too high, your ability to "Shatter Crystals" to skip years becomes less efficient.
