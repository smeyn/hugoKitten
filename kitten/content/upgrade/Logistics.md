In *Kittens Game*, [logistics]( {{< relref "upgrade/Logistics.md" >}}) primarily refers to two distinct Workshop upgrades that improve workforce efficiency and industrial scaling, as well as broader systems for managing resource distribution.

### **1. The "Logistics" Workshop Upgrade**
This is a mid-game upgrade focused on the productivity of your kittens.
*   **Acquisition:** Unlocked after researching the [Industrialization]( {{< relref "techs/Industrialization.md" >}}) technology.
*   **Cost:** 100,000 Science, 100 Gears, and 1,000 Scaffolds.
*   **Effect:** Increases the production bonus derived from **kitten skills by 15%**. 
*   **Synergy:** This bonus multiplies with the base skill bonus and stacks additively with the later [Augmentations]( {{< relref "upgrade/Augmentations.md" >}}) upgrade (+100% skill effectiveness). It ensures that "Master" level kittens provide significantly more resources than "Adequate" ones.

### **2. The "Factory Logistics" Workshop Upgrade**
This is a high-tier industrial upgrade that is critical for transitioning into the endgame "Shatter Engine."
*   **Acquisition:** Unlocked after researching the [Electronics]( {{< relref "techs/Electronics.md" >}}) technology.
*   **Cost:** 100,000 Science, 250 Gears, and 2,000 Titanium.
*   **Effect on Crafting:** Increases the global crafting bonus provided by each [Factory]( {{< relref "buildings/Factory.md" >}}) by **+1%**. (This typically moves the base Factory bonus from 5% to 6% per building).
*   **Effect on Space Production:** It serves as a multiplier for space-based structures. Once the [Space Manufacturing]( {{< relref "upgrade/Space_Manufacturing.md" >}}) upgrade is active, Factory Logistics increases the "Space Bonus" provided by Factories to Space Elevators and Orbital Arrays from 3.75% to **4.5% per Factory**.
*   **Strategic Importance:** This upgrade is essential for maximizing [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) production, as it allows your terrestrial factory network to significantly scale the output of Lunar Outposts.

### **3. Broader Logistic Systems**
The term is also applied to automated management systems within the game:
*   **AI Logistics (AI Bases):** A Workshop upgrade where each [AI Core]( {{< relref "buildings/AI_Core.md" >}}) increases the storage effectiveness of [Moon Bases]( {{< relref "space/moonbase.md" >}}) by 10%. This helps manage the massive Unobtainium caps required for building Chronospheres.
*   **Trade Logistics (Caravanserai):** While not named "logistics," this upgrade allows [Tradeposts]( {{< relref "buildings/Tradepost.md" >}}) to improve your standing with other races, effectively managing the "hostility" of partners like Zebras to ensure successful resource acquisition.
*   **Job Management (Register):** This Workshop upgrade allows your **Leader** to manage kitten deployments more efficiently, automatically prioritizing kittens with higher experience levels for job assignments.
