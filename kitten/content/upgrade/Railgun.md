The [Railgun]( {{< relref "upgrade/Railgun.md" >}}) is a high-tier Workshop upgrade that provides a permanent boost to your civilization's catpower generation.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research the [Particle Physics]( {{< relref "techs/Particle_Physics.md" >}}) science (which requires 185,000 Science and 135 Blueprints).
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 150,000
    *   [Titanium:]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) 5,000
    *   [Blueprints:]( {{< relref "resource/Blueprint.md" >}}) 25.

### **Core Effects**
The Railgun is categorized as a weapon technology for the [Hunter]( {{< relref "job/Hunter.md" >}}) job.
*   **Catpower Production:** It provides a **+25% boost to catpower production**. 
*   **Hunter Efficiency:** This bonus specifically increases the rate at which Hunter kittens (or Zebras in Iron Will mode) generate catpower.

### **Strategic Utility**
*   **Hunting and Trading:** By increasing the rate of catpower accumulation, the Railgun allows you to send out more frequent hunting expeditions (for furs, ivory, and unicorns) and perform more trades with other civilizations.
*   **Iron Will Mode:** It is considered a vital late-game upgrade for [Iron Will]( {{< relref "Challenges/ironWill.md" >}}) runs. Since players in this mode cannot use kittens, they rely entirely on [Zebras]( {{< relref "Civilisations/zebras.md" >}}) to generate catpower; the Railgun helps overcome the scarcity of this resource for high-level tasks like launching orbital missions.
*   **Late-Game Scaling:** It is one of the final upgrades in the weapon chain, which includes the Composite Bow (+50% catpower) and Crossbow (+25% catpower).

### **Challenge Modifier: Pacifism**
In the **Pacifism challenge** (where hunting is disabled), the effectiveness of weapon technologies like the Railgun is significantly penalized. For each completion of the challenge, the effectiveness of the Railgun is reduced by **-10% (additive)**, up to a maximum penalty of **-100%**.
