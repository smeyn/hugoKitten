[Electrolytic Smelting]( {{< relref "upgrade/Electrolytic_Smelting.md" >}}) is a powerful mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that significantly boosts the output of your terrestrial [Smelters]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}).

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research the [Metallurgy]( {{< relref "techs/Metallurgy.md" >}}) science.
*   **Resource Requirements:** 
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 100,000
    *   [Titanium:]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) 2,000.

### **Core Effects**
This upgrade makes Smelters "twice as effective" by providing a massive boost to their primary outputs:
*   **Iron Production:** Increases iron yield from Smelters by **+95%**.
*   [Coal Production:]( {{< relref "resource/coal_production.md" >}}) Increases coal yield from Smelters (assuming you have the [Coal Furnace]( {{< relref "upgrade/Coal_Furnace.md" >}}) upgrade) by **+95%**.

### **Strategic Importance**
*   **Doubling Output:** Electrolytic Smelting effectively doubles your Smelter production rate for two critical industrial resources.
*   **Resource Bottlenecks:** This upgrade is essential for scaling your industrial base. Iron is constantly needed for building components and **Observatories**, while Coal is the primary ingredient for [Steel]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}), which becomes a major bottleneck as you approach the **Space era**.
*   **Synergy:** It works alongside other Smelter upgrades like [Gold Ore]( {{< relref "upgrade/Gold_Ore.md" >}}) (+0.001 gold/tick) and [Nuclear Smelters]( {{< relref "upgrade/Nuclear_Smelters.md" >}}) (+0.0015 titanium/tick), transforming simple early-game buildings into advanced multi-resource refineries.
