In **Kittens Game**, [Satellite Radio]( {{< relref "upgrade/Satellite_Radio.md" >}}) is a game-changing **Workshop upgrade** that transforms your [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) from simple starchart generators into critical scientific infrastructure.

### Key Effects
The primary function of Satellite Radio is to expand your **Science Cap**:

* **Science Max Bonus:** Each Satellite you own now increases your maximum science capacity. 
* **Scaling:** The amount of science added per satellite is influenced by other upgrades (like [Satellite Navigation]( {{< relref "upgrade/Satellite_Navigation.md" >}})) and your total number of **Libraries** and **Academies**. 
* **The "Science Wall":** This is the most effective way to reach the massive science caps (250k+) required for late-game technologies like [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}), [Nanotechnology]( {{< relref "techs/Nanotechnology.md" >}}), and [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}).

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see this upgrade in your Workshop, you must have moved into the orbital era:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| **Satellite** | Space Building | You must have at least one **Satellite** deployed. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **125,000 Science** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **2,500 Alloy** |

### Strategic Importance
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Temporal Distortion**, Satellite Radio is likely the reason you were able to progress this far. Here is how it fits into your current late-game loop:

1.  **Preparation for Antimatter:** To research **Advanced Physics** and build **Containment Chambers**, you need science caps in the hundreds of thousands. While [Compendiums]( {{< relref "resource/Compendium.md" >}}) provide a flat boost, Satellite Radio provides a *scalable* boost that grows as you expand your space presence.
2.  **Synergy with Navigation Upgrades:** Every time you buy an upgrade that improves "Satellite Efficiency" (like [Navigation Relay]( {{< relref "space/Navigation_Relay.md" >}}) or [Satellite Navigation]( {{< relref "upgrade/Satellite_Navigation.md" >}})), the science cap bonus from Satellite Radio also increases. 
3.  **Space Elevator Efficiency:** Since you are using [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) to reduce launch costs, you can spam more Satellites for relatively low oil costs, which in turn balloons your science cap.

> **Pro Tip:** If you are hitting a science cap and can't afford the next big tech, don't just build more Libraries. Launch 5 more [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}). Thanks to Satellite Radio, it’s often the most resource-efficient way to move the needle on your science maximum.
