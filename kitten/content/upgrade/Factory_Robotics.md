[Factory Robotics]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Robotics.md" >}}) is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade that improves the efficiency of your automation by increasing the "know-how" (crafting effectiveness) of your [Engineer]( {{< relref "job/Engineer.md" >}}) kittens.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research the [Robotics]( {{< relref "techs/Robotics.md" >}}) science (which requires 140,000 Science and 80 Blueprints).
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 75,000
    *   [Gears:]( {{< relref "craft/Gear.md" >}}) 250
    *   [Titanium:]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) 1,250.

### **Core Effects: Engineer Know-How**
The upgrade specifically boosts the crafting effectiveness of the first three classes of Engineers. "Know-how" determines how many "Engineer points" each kitten contributes to the Workshop's automated crafting pool.
*   **Class 1 Engineer:** Know-how is set to **10**.
*   **Class 2 Engineer:** Know-how increases to **5**.
*   **Class 3 Engineer:** Know-how is added at **2**.

### **Strategic Importance**
Factory Robotics is a direct successor to the [Factory Optimization]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Optimization.md" >}}) upgrade (which only covers Classes 1 and 2). By expanding and increasing these values, it allows your technical workforce to handle significantly higher resource throughput. 

This is particularly vital as your industrial base grows and storage caps for resources like [Oil]( {{< relref "resource/oil.md" >}}), [Iron]( {{< relref "resource/iron.md" >}}), and [Coal]( {{< relref "resource/coal.md" >}}) become harder to manage manually. Maximizing Engineer efficiency through this and subsequent upgrades (like [Space Engineers]( {{< relref "upgrade/Space_Engineers.md" >}}) or [AI Engineers]( {{< relref "upgrade/AI_Engineers.md" >}})) ensures that your village continues to produce materials like [Kerosene]( {{< relref "resource/kerosene.md" >}}) or [Steel]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}) at high speeds while idling.
