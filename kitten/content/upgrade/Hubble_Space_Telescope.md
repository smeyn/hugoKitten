The [Hubble Space Telescope]( {{< relref "upgrade/Hubble_Space_Telescope.md" >}}) is a powerful mid-to-late game **Workshop upgrade** that significantly boosts your space-based resource generation. It is specifically designed to solve the "starchart bottleneck" that often occurs once you begin exploring the solar system.

### Key Effects
The primary function of the Hubble upgrade is to increase the efficiency of your space infrastructure:

* **Starchart Production:** It provides a **+30% bonus** to starchart production from [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}), [Research Vessels]( {{< relref "space/research-vessel.md" >}}), and [Space Beacons]( {{< relref "space/space-beacon.md" >}}).
* [Astrophysicists:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Astrophysicists.md" >}}) If you have researched [Astrophysicists]( {{< relref "upgrade/Astrophysicists.md" >}}) (which allows Kitten Scientists to produce starcharts), the Hubble upgrade also increases their production by **30%**.
* **Science Storage:** While its main draw is starcharts, the increased effectiveness of your orbital buildings indirectly helps manage your **Science cap**, which is vital for late-game tech like [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}).

---

### Requirements to Unlock
Since you've already been working with [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}), you likely meet most of the prerequisites, but here is the breakdown:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Rocketry]( {{< relref "techs/Rocketry.md" >}}) | Technology | Required to enter the Space Tab |
| [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) | Space Building | You must have at least one **Satellite** deployed |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **125,000 Science** |
| [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,500 Starcharts** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,000 Alloy** |

### Strategic Value
By the time you reach the [Dark Future]( {{< relref "guides/dark-future.md" >}}) (which we discussed regarding [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}})), starcharts usually stop being a primary concern because your production is so high. However, the Hubble upgrade is essential for:

1.  **Mass Satellite Deployment:** If you are using [Solar Satellites]( {{< relref "upgrade/Solar_Satellites.md" >}}) for energy, the Hubble bonus makes the side-product of starcharts much more meaningful.
2.  **Leviathan Trading:** In the very late game, starcharts are used to trade with [Leviathans]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Leviathans.md" >}}) for [Time Crystals]( {{< relref "resource/Time_Crystals.md" >}}) and [Relics]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}). The 30% boost from Hubble helps sustain these trades without stalling your other space missions.
3.  **Path to the Farther Ring:** Reaching distant planets like [Charon]( {{< relref "planet/Charon.md" >}}) or [Centaurus System]( {{< relref "planet/Centaurus_System.md" >}}) requires hundreds of thousands of starcharts. Hubble is the engine that gets you there faster.

> **Note:** If you are currently in a **Black Sky Challenge**, note that Research Vessels do not produce starcharts, making the Hubble-boosted Satellites your most reliable source of income.
