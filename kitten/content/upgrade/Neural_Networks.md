In *Kittens Game*, the **Neural Network** is a high-tier Workshop upgrade that significantly enhances your village's automation capabilities by improving the [Engineer]( {{< relref "job/Engineer.md" >}}) job.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research the [Artificial Intelligence]( {{< relref "techs/Artificial_Intelligence.md" >}}) science (which requires 250,000 Science and 150 Blueprints).
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 200,000
    *   [Titanium:]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) 7,500

### **Core Effects**
The upgrade provides two primary benefits, one mechanical and one thematic:
*   **Doubled Engineer Effectiveness:** The primary mechanical benefit is that it **doubles the efficiency of all Engineer kittens**. Since Engineers are responsible for automating the crafting of resources (such as converting Oil to Kerosene or Iron to Plates), this upgrade effectively doubles your passive crafting rate.
*   **Noosphere Learning:** Thematically, the upgrade allows kittens to "learn from the **noosphere**". This means kittens can learn job skills from other kittens employed in different roles, facilitating faster skill acquisition across your population.

### **Strategic Importance**
The Neural Network is a critical component of the late-game industrial engine. By doubling the effectiveness of your Engineers, it allows your civilization to handle much higher resource throughput without hitting storage caps, which is essential for maintaining the production of advanced materials like [Eludium]( {{< relref "resource/eludium.md" >}}) or [Kerosene]( {{< relref "resource/kerosene.md" >}}) while idling. It is often purchased alongside other AI-related upgrades like [Machine Learning]( {{< relref "upgrade/Machine_Learning.md" >}}) and [AI Engineers]( {{< relref "upgrade/AI_Engineers.md" >}}) to maximize the utility of your technical workforce.
