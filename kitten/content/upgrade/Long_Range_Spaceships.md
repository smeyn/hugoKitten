### The Evolution of Long-Range Missions

* **Research Vessel:** Your first true "explorer." While not long-range by late-game standards, these are vital for generating the [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}) required to calculate the trajectories for further missions.
* **Terraforming Station:** Required for missions to **navigation-heavy** locations. These stations help stabilize your presence on distant planets, allowing for more permanent resource extraction.
* **Space Beacon:** These are the pinnacle of your long-range infrastructure. They provide a massive boost to **Starchart production** and are necessary to "light the way" to the [Centaurus System]( {{< relref "planet/Centaurus_System.md" >}}), the furthest reach of the standard game.

### Requirements for Deep Space Travel

As you push into the Farther Ring, the resource costs shift from basic metals to high-energy components:

| Resource | Primary Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) | Used as high-density fuel for reactors and long-range propulsion. |
| [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) | Required for the most advanced vessels and the **Centaurus** mission. |
| [Eludium]( {{< relref "resource/eludium.md" >}}) | An alloy of Unobtainium and Alloy used for hulls that can withstand interstellar travel. |

### Strategic Impact
By the time you are building missions for the **Farther Ring**, your focus is likely on two things:

1.  **Unobtainium Scaling:** Long-range missions to places like [Cath]( {{< relref "planet/Cath.md" >}}) and [Umbra]( {{< relref "planet/Umbra.md" >}}) are designed to significantly increase your Unobtainium storage and production.
2.  **The Time Paradox:** Deep space travel is often a prerequisite for **Chronotheology**. You need the resources from these distant systems to build the [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) that allow you to reset and keep your progress.

> **Pro Tip:** If you are hitting a "Starchart bottleneck" while trying to launch these ships, ensure you have the [Hubble Space Telescope]( {{< relref "upgrade/Hubble_Space_Telescope.md" >}}) upgrade we discussed earlier; it makes every Research Vessel and Satellite 30% more effective.
