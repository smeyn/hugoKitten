[Factory Logistics]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Logistics.md" >}}) is a late-game Workshop upgrade that enhances the efficiency of your industrial infrastructure, specifically focusing on boosting the bonuses provided by **Factories**.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research [Electronics]( {{< relref "techs/Electronics.md" >}}), which is unlocked via Mechanization.
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 100,000
    *   [Gears:]( {{< relref "craft/Gear.md" >}}) 250
    *   [Titanium:]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}}) 2,000

### **Core Effects and Mechanics**
The primary function of Factory Logistics is to increase the global **crafting effectiveness** bonus provided by each Factory building:
*   **Base Factory Bonus:** Standard Factories provide a **+5% crafting bonus**.
*   **Upgrade Bonus:** Researching Factory Logistics increases this bonus by **+1%**, bringing the total crafting bonus per Factory to **6%**.
*   **Multiplicative Impact:** Because crafting bonuses stack multiplicatively across different resource tiers (e.g., Wood to Beams to Scaffolds), even a small 1% increase per Factory can lead to massive efficiency gains once dozens of Factories are constructed.

### **Strategic Synergies**
Factory Logistics is a critical component of the late-game "Space Manufacturing" engine:
*   **Space Manufacturing Synergy:** When combined with the [Space Manufacturing]( {{< relref "upgrade/Space_Manufacturing.md" >}}) upgrade, Factories provide a bonus to all space structures based on their crafting ratio. 
*   **Space Bonus Scaling:** Without this upgrade, Factories contribute a 3.75% space production bonus; with Factory Logistics active, this contribution increases to **4.5% per Factory**.
*   **Unobtainium Production:** This upgrade is essential for maximizing [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) output, as it significantly scales the effectiveness of [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) and [Orbital Arrays]( {{< relref "space/orbital_array.md" >}}).
*   **Late-Game Farming:** It is listed as a mandatory requirement for high-efficiency **Time Crystal farming** and establishing "positive resets" in the deep endgame.

By improving the efficiency of resource conversion and space production, Factory Logistics helps clear the significant resource bottlenecks that define the transition into the Relic and Chronosphere eras.
