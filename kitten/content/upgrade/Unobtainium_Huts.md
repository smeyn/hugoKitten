[Unobtainium Huts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Unobtainium_Huts.md" >}}) is a major mid-to-late game Workshop upgrade that drastically reduces the cost scaling of your Huts, allowing you to massively expand your kitten population and boost Paragon gains.

---

### **1. Core Effect**
* **Price Ratio Reduction:** Reduces the price ratio of Huts by **0.25** (a flat -0.25 modifier to the base 2.5 price ratio).
* **Impact:** Huts naturally have one of the steepest cost scalings in the game (a base ratio of 2.5). Combined with [Ironwood Huts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Ironwood_Huts.md" >}}) (-0.50) and [Concrete Huts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Concrete_Huts.md" >}}) (-0.30), Unobtainium Huts allows you to build dozens of additional Huts before hitting wood storage limits, yielding significantly more kittens per run.

---

### **2. Cost and Prerequisites**
* **Technology Prerequisite:** [Exogeology]( {{< relref "resource/exogeology.md" >}}) (unlocked via Orbital Engineering; costs 275k Science and 250 Blueprints).
* **Workshop Upgrade Cost:**
  * **200k Science**
  * **350 Unobtainium**
  * **15k Titanium**

---

### **3. Infrastructure Requirements to Reach It**
Reaching Unobtainium Huts requires establishing a foothold in Space and expanding your resource caps:
* **Unobtainium Storage Cap:** Base Unobtainium storage is only 150. You must build at least **1 Moon Base** (which requires 50 Unobtainium, 70k Oil capacity, 9,500 Titanium, and 250 Concrete) to raise your storage capacity past the 350 Unobtainium threshold needed for the upgrade.
* **Unobtainium Generation:** Requires [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "space/lunar-outpost.md" >}}) to convert Uranium into Unobtainium. 
* **Uranium Supply:** Because Lunar Outposts consume Uranium rapidly, relying on [Orbital Geodesy]( {{< relref "upgrade/Orbital_Geodesy.md" >}}) (Quarries produce Uranium) or trading Titanium with Dragons is strongly recommended to keep Outposts running.
* **Starcharts & Oil:** You will need roughly 2,100 Starcharts to fund the prerequisite space missions (Orbital Launch and Moon Mission) and an Oil capacity of at least 70k.

---

### **4. Strategic Role and Reset Checkpoint**
* **Progression Milestone:** Early game resets typically peak at Concrete Huts (around 130–150 kittens). Unobtainium Huts serves as the next primary milestone once your Paragon pool and storage multipliers are high enough to comfortably reach Space.
* **Farming Renaissance:** A common strategy is to repeat runs pushing to Unobtainium Huts to max out kitten housing, stockpiling Paragon until you can purchase all the core price reduction Metaphysics upgrades through to [Renaissance]( {{< relref "metaphysics/renaissance.md" >}}) (750 Paragon) before committing to the heavier Eludium and Time Crystal eras.
