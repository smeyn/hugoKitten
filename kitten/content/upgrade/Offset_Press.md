The [Offset Press]( {{< relref "upgrade/Offset_Press.md" >}}) is a mid-to-late-game Workshop upgrade for the [Steamworks]( {{< relref "buildings/Steamworks.md" >}}) building that significantly enhances automated manuscript production.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Unlocked By:** You must first research the [Combustion]( {{< relref "techs/Combustion.md" >}}) technology.
*   **Resource Requirements:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 100,000
    *   [Gears:]( {{< relref "craft/Gear.md" >}}) 250
    *   [Oil:]( {{< relref "resource/oil.md" >}}) 15,000

### **Core Effects**
The Offset Press serves as a direct multiplier to the [Printing Press]( {{< relref "upgrade/Printing_Press.md" >}}) upgrade:
*   **Production Boost:** It makes the Printing Press **four times as effective**.
*   **Manuscript Rate:** It increases the passive manuscript generation of active Steamworks from 0.0025 per second to **0.01 manuscripts/sec**.
*   **Prerequisite:** Purchasing this upgrade is required to eventually unlock [Photolithography]( {{< relref "upgrade/Photolithography.md" >}}), which further quadruples production to 0.04 manuscripts/sec.

### **Strategic Importance**
*   **Overcoming Bottlenecks:** Throughout the mid-game, [Manuscripts]( {{< relref "resource/manuscript.md" >}}) are a major "speedbump" required for critical research like Theology and Astronomy.
*   **Automation:** By increasing the effectiveness of Steamworks, the Offset Press allows you to generate manuscripts passively while idling or away from the computer, reducing the need to manually "craft all" after hunting for furs.
*   **Synergy:** While Steamworks consume coal, maintaining them is vital for this automated production chain, and the Offset Press ensures that the coal-to-manuscript conversion is as efficient as possible.
