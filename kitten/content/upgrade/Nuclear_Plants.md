In **Kittens Game**, the **Nuclear Plant** is a late-game upgrade for your [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) that significantly increases your energy production to support advanced space and science facilities. It represents the transition from simple steam and coal power to high-density fission energy.

### Key Effects
The Nuclear Plant is a **Workshop upgrade** that transforms your Reactors into more powerful units:

* **Energy Production:** Each Reactor produces **+25% more energy**. This is essential for powering **Containment Chambers** (for Antimatter) and [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) (for Unobtainium).
* **Uranium Consumption:** While it boosts power, it also **increases Uranium consumption** for each Reactor. You must ensure your Uranium production (from [Accelerators]( {{< relref "buildings/Accelerator.md" >}}) and [Lunar Outposts]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}})) is strong enough to handle the extra drain.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see this upgrade in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}) technology branch:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| [Reactor]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) | Building | You must have at least one [Reactor]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) built. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **150,000 Science** |
| [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) | Resource | **2,000 Uranium** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **5,000 Alloy** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Temporal Distortion**, the Nuclear Plant upgrade is a critical component of your energy grid:

1.  **Antimatter Preparation:** Generating [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) requires your energy grid to be in the "green" (positive surplus). Upgrading to Nuclear Plants is often the only way to reach the required surplus without building an excessive number of standard Reactors.
2.  **Thorium Synergy:** Once you unlock [Thorium]( {{< relref "resource/thorium.md" >}}), you can further upgrade these plants to [Thorium Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Thorium_Reactors.md" >}}), which provide even more power and reduce the reliance on standard Uranium for energy.
3.  **Pollution Management:** Note that like standard Reactors, Nuclear Plants contribute to [Pollution]( {{< relref "guides/Pollution.md" >}}). Ensure you have the [Nanosuits]( {{< relref "upgrade/Nanosuits.md" >}}) we discussed earlier to mitigate the happiness penalty on your kittens.

> **Pro Tip:** If your Uranium stocks start crashing after buying this upgrade, consider trading with [Griffins]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Griffins.md" >}}) or increasing your [Lunar Outpost]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) count to stabilize the supply.
