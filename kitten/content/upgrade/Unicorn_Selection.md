Based on the sources, [Unicorn Selection]( {{< relref "upgrade/Unicorn_Selection.md" >}}) is a Workshop upgrade designed to significantly boost your unicorn generation. 

Here are the specific details regarding its costs and effects:

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** To unlock the ability to purchase Unicorn Selection, you must first research the [Genetics]( {{< relref "techs/Genetics.md" >}}) science.
*   **Cost:** Purchasing the upgrade requires an investment of **175,000 Science** and **1,500 Titanium**.

### **Core Effects**
*   **Production Boost:** The upgrade improves the overall effectiveness of your Unicorn Pastures, granting a **+25% multiplier to your unicorn production**.
*   **Scaling:** This 25% boost acts as a true multiplier that scales alongside your other production effects, making it an essential upgrade for establishing a strong unicorn economy and generating tears and alicorns faster.
