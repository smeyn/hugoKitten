In *Kittens Game*, [Fluidized Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Fluidized_Reactors.md" >}}) (often referred to as Fluidised Reactors) is a high-tier Workshop upgrade that significantly enhances the industrial output of your [Calciners]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}).

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite Technology:** You must first research [Nanotechnology]( {{< relref "techs/Nanotechnology.md" >}}) (which costs 200k Science and 150 Blueprints).
*   **Upgrade Cost:**
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 175,000 (175k)
    *   [Alloy:]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) 200

### **Core Effects**
The primary function of this upgrade is to make [Calciners]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}) substantially more productive:
*   **Production Boost:** It increases Calciner **Iron production by +100%** and **Titanium production by +300%**.
*   **Efficiency:** The upgrade is described as making Calciners "twice as effective," as it provides a flat doubling of the iron output and a massive quadrupling boost to the titanium yield.

### **Strategic Importance**
Fluidized Reactors represent the final major tier in the progression of Calciner-specific production upgrades, following [Oxidation]( {{< relref "upgrade/Oxidation.md" >}}) (+95% iron, +285% titanium) and [Rotary Kiln]( {{< relref "upgrade/Rotary_Kiln.md" >}}) (+75% iron, +225% titanium). 
*   **Titanium Bottlenecks:** In the late-to-endgame, titanium is required in massive quantities for space structures and advanced materials like [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) and [Eludium]( {{< relref "resource/eludium.md" >}}). While trading with Zebras remains a primary source, Fluidized Reactors make Calciners a far more viable automated source for maintaining your titanium supply.
*   **Automation:** When combined with upgrades like [Steel Plants]( {{< relref "upgrade/Steel_Plants.md" >}}) (which automatically convert some Calciner iron into Steel), these reactors help create a self-sustaining industrial loop that requires less manual intervention from the player.
