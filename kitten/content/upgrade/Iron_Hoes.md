In **Kittens Game**, [Iron Hoes]( {{< relref "upgrade/Iron_Hoes.md" >}}) is an essential early-game Workshop upgrade that improves your food security and population scaling.

### Key Effects
* **Farmer Efficiency:** It makes your **Farmers 30% more effective** at producing catnip.
* **Production Bonus:** This is a flat multiplicative bonus to the `catnipRatio`, meaning every kitten assigned as a Farmer will produce more catnip per tick.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see this upgrade in your Workshop tab, you must have researched [Agriculture]( {{< relref "techs/Agriculture.md" >}}) and built at least one [Workshop]( {{< relref "buildings/Workshop.md" >}}).

* **Science Cost:** 200 Science
* **Iron Cost:** 25 Iron
* **Prerequisite:** You must first purchase [Mineral Hoes]( {{< relref "upgrade/Mineral_Hoes.md" >}}) (which provides a +50% bonus).

### Strategic Value
Given your current progress with advanced tech like [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}), Iron Hoes is a fundamental "legacy" upgrade you likely purchased hundreds of thousands of years ago in game-time. 

1.  **Early Game:** It is the "make or break" upgrade for your first winter. Without it, you often cannot support enough kittens to generate the science needed for [Mathematics]( {{< relref "techs/Mathematics.md" >}}) or [Construction]( {{< relref "techs/Construction.md" >}}).
2.  **Iron Will Mode:** If you are playing in [Iron Will]( {{< relref "Challenges/ironWill.md" >}}) (no kittens), this upgrade is essentially **useless**, as you have no Farmers to receive the bonus.
3.  **Late Game Synergy:** While 30% seems small when you have [Solar Revolution]( {{< relref "religion/Solar_Revolution.md" >}}) and **Cryotheology** active, these early base-level bonuses are still calculated into your total production multipliers. They help keep your "Catnip per Tick" high enough that you can ignore your food supply entirely and focus your kittens on being [Engineers]( {{< relref "job/Engineer.md" >}}) or **Scientists**.
