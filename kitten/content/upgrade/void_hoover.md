The [Void Hoover]( {{< relref "upgrade/void_hoover.md" >}}) is an advanced upgrade that enhances your resource accumulation during temporal paradoxes. 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first unlock the [Void Aspiration]( {{< relref "upgrade/Void_Aspiration.md" >}}) upgrade.
*   **Base Cost:** Purchasing a Void Hoover requires an investment of **10 Time Crystals, 250 Void, and 1,000 Antimatter**.
*   **Price Ratio:** The cost scales with a 1.25 price ratio for each subsequent purchase.

### **Core Effects**
*   Each Void Hoover directly **increases the maximum amount of Void you can gain on a Temporal Paradox day by 1**.
