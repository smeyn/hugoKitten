The [Relic Station]( {{< relref "upgrade/Relic_Station.md" >}}) is a highly important mid-to-late game workshop upgrade that forms the foundation of your passive Relic economy by upgrading your Space Beacons. 

Here are the details on how to acquire and optimize Relic Stations:

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisites:** To unlock the Relic Station upgrade, you must first research the [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}) science, which costs 5 Relics. 
*   **Cost:** Purchasing the Relic Station upgrade requires a massive investment of **100 Eludium and 5,000 Antimatter**. 
*   **Antimatter Preparation:** Gathering 5,000 Antimatter is a major bottleneck. You will need to build Sunlifters to generate Antimatter, and heavily expand your Antimatter storage capacity by constructing Containment Chambers and Heatsinks.

### **Core Effects and Scaling**
*   **Passive Relic Generation:** Once purchased, every [Space Beacon]( {{< relref "space/space-beacon.md" >}}) you have built will begin passively generating **0.01 Relics per game-day** (which equates to roughly 432 Relics per real-life day per beacon).
*   **Antimatter Storage Penalty:** Your Relic Station's efficiency is directly tied to your Antimatter storage limit. If your maximum Antimatter storage drops below 5,000, your Relic production will decrease by 0.02% for every missing unit of storage.

### **How to Boost Relic Station Production**
While the base production of 432 Relics a day per beacon is somewhat slow, it can be drastically multiplied through late-game synergies:
*   **Black Nexus & Black Pyramids:** This is the most significant way to boost your Relic Stations. Acquiring the [Black Nexus]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Nexus.md" >}}) cryptotheology upgrade increases your Space Beacons' relic production by **10% for every Black Pyramid** you have currently constructed.
*   **Hashes:** Building Entanglement Stations (which consume gigaflops from AI Cores to produce "hashes") will grant you "hashrate levels," which further boost your Relic Station production by 25% per level.
