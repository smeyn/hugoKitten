[Resource Retrieval]( {{< relref "upgrade/resource-retrieval.md" >}}) is a late-game upgrade found in the Time tab that forms the backbone of advanced time-manipulation strategies.

**Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first unlock the [Paradox Theory]( {{< relref "techs/Paradox_Theory.md" >}}) science.
*   **Base Cost:** 1,000 Time Crystals.
*   **Price Ratio:** The cost of subsequent levels scales with a 1.3 price ratio.

**Core Effects**
*   Whenever you shatter a Time Crystal (using the Chronoforge to skip a year), Resource Retrieval instantly gives you **1% of your yearly resource production per level** purchased. 
*   Activating Tempus Fugit does *not* affect the amount of resources you retrieve.

**Synergies**
*   [Blazars:]( {{< relref "transcendence/Blazar.md" >}}) This advanced Cryptotheology upgrade improves the effectiveness of your Resource Retrieval by **+2% per level**.
*   [Chronoengineers:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Chronoengineers.md" >}}) If you have this workshop upgrade, your assigned Engineer kittens will automatically perform crafting cycles based on your Resource Retrieval amounts whenever you combust Time Crystals.

**Strategic Importance: The "Shatter Engine"**
In the deep late-game, Resource Retrieval is essential for creating what the community calls a **"Shatter Engine"** (or a Type 2 Relic Engine). 

The primary goal is to purchase enough levels of Resource Retrieval so that every time you shatter a Time Crystal, you generate a massive dump of resources—specifically unobtainium and alicorns. If your yields from a single shatter are high enough, you can immediately trade that retrieved unobtainium to the Leviathans (or sacrifice the retrieved alicorns) to earn *more* Time Crystals than you just spent. 

Reaching this self-sustaining "infinite loop" typically requires **roughly 7 levels of Resource Retrieval**, which demands an investment of around 12,000 Time Crystals. Once this engine is established, you can rapidly and repeatedly shatter time crystals (or automate your Chrono Furnaces) to farm massive amounts of resources, relics, and Time Crystals without ever running out.
