[Gold Ore]( {{< relref "upgrade/Gold_Ore.md" >}}) is a crucial Workshop upgrade that enables your civilization to generate gold, which is a mandatory step before you can begin trading with other civilizations. 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first unlock the [Currency]( {{< relref "techs/Currency.md" >}}) science technology.
*   **Cost:** Purchasing the upgrade requires **1,000 Science, 800 Minerals, and 100 Iron**.

### **Core Effects**
Once researched, Gold Ore allows your [Smelters]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) to passively create gold alongside their normal iron production. Specifically, each active Smelter will produce **0.001 gold per tick** (which equates to **0.005 gold per second**). Because gold generation is essential for trade and upgrading certain buildings, obtaining this upgrade is a significant milestone for mid-game economic progression.

### **Iron Will Mode**
If you are playing in the specialized [Iron Will]( {{< relref "Challenges/ironWill.md" >}}) mode (playing without kittens), the Gold Ore upgrade grants a different but equally vital effect: it allows your **hunts to produce gold**. Once researched, every hunt has a **25% chance of yielding gold**, with the exact amount determined by random integers that scale with your total hunting bonus.
