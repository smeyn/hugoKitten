In **Kittens Game**, the **Nuclear Smelter** is a high-level **Workshop upgrade** that provides a massive efficiency boost to your metal production by integrating nuclear technology into your existing industrial infrastructure.

### Key Effects
The Nuclear Smelter upgrade transforms your [Smelters]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) into far more productive units:

* **Production Bonus:** It increases the production of [Iron]( {{< relref "resource/iron.md" >}}) and [Gold]( {{< relref "resource/Gold.md" >}}) from each Smelter by **+25%**. 
* **Scaling:** This is a multiplicative bonus that stacks with other Smelter upgrades (like [Electrolytic Smelting]( {{< relref "upgrade/Electrolytic_Smelting.md" >}})). In the late game, this boost is critical for sustaining the massive Iron and Gold requirements of [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) and [Blueprint]( {{< relref "resource/Blueprint.md" >}}) crafting.
* **Coal Efficiency:** While it increases metal output, it does not increase the coal consumption of the Smelters, making it a very "resource-efficient" upgrade for your industrial floor.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see Nuclear Smelters in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}) technology branch:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Nuclear Fission]( {{< relref "techs/Nuclear_Fission.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| [Smelter]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) | Building | You must have at least one [Smelter]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) built. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **150,000 Science** |
| [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) | Resource | **2,000 Uranium** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **5,000 Alloy** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and **Temporal Distortion**, the Nuclear Smelter is a "logistical" upgrade that supports your high-tier resource needs:

1.  **Steel Production:** Since Iron is the primary bottleneck for [Steel]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}), the 25% boost helps ensure your [Steamworks]( {{< relref "buildings/Steamworks.md" >}}) and [Calciners]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}) always have enough raw materials to produce the steel needed for [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) and [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}).
2.  **Gold Management:** Late-game trading (especially with [Leviathans]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Leviathans.md" >}}) for Time Crystals) requires massive amounts of Gold. Nuclear Smelters are essential for keeping your Gold income high enough to fund frequent trading missions.
3.  **Uranium Priority:** Since this upgrade costs 2,000 Uranium, you should generally prioritize it *after* you have stabilized your [Lunar Outpost]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) production. It is a great way to use excess Uranium to fix metal production issues.

> **Pro Tip:** If you have already purchased the [Nuclear Plants]( {{< relref "upgrade/Nuclear_Plants.md" >}}) upgrade, the Nuclear Smelter should be your next priority to balance out your industrial production with your new energy capacity.
