[Eludium Reflectors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Eludium_Reflectors.md" >}}) is an advanced Workshop upgrade that significantly scales your maximum science capacity in the late game. 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** It is unlocked by researching the [Advanced Exogeology]( {{< relref "techs/Advanced_Exogeology.md" >}}) (also known as Exometallurgy) science technology.
*   **Cost:** Purchasing the upgrade requires **250,000 Science** and **15 Eludium**.

### **Core Effects**
*   The upgrade installs improved telescope reflectors, which causes every [Observatory]( {{< relref "buildings/Observatory.md" >}}) you own to grant an additional **+2% maximum science bonus** to the effectiveness of your **Libraries** and [Data Centers]( {{< relref "buildings/Data_Center.md" >}}).
*   This means your Libraries' and Data Centers' max science bonus increases by a percentage equal to **2 multiplied by your total number of Observatories**.

### **Synergy**
This upgrade serves as the third and final tier of reflector upgrades. It stacks directly with the earlier [Titanium Reflectors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Titanium_Reflectors.md" >}}) and [Unobtainium Reflectors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Unobtainium_Reflectors.md" >}}), which each also provide a +2% bonus per Observatory. When combined, these upgrades allow a large array of Observatories to exponentially multiply your base science storage limits.
