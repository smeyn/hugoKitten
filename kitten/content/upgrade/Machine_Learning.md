In **Kittens Game**, [Machine Learning]( {{< relref "upgrade/Machine_Learning.md" >}}) is a high-level **Workshop upgrade** that serves as the primary bridge between your scientific research and your [Artificial Intelligence]( {{< relref "techs/Artificial_Intelligence.md" >}}) infrastructure.

### Key Effects
The Machine Learning upgrade provides a massive scaling bonus to your science capacity by enhancing the synergy between your computer systems and your library archives:

* **Compendium Cap Bonus:** It increases the effectiveness of [Data Centers]( {{< relref "buildings/Data_Center.md" >}}) (the Library upgrade). Specifically, it boosts the amount of maximum science you gain from each [Compendium]( {{< relref "resource/Compendium.md" >}}) you own.
* **AI Synergy:** Once you have [AI Cores]( {{< relref "buildings/AI_Core.md" >}}), Machine Learning allows those cores to further increase the science cap bonus provided by Data Centers. This makes it an essential upgrade for reaching the multi-million science caps required for [Relic]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) and [Void]( {{< relref "resource/void.md" >}}) technologies.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see Machine Learning in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the [Robotics]( {{< relref "techs/Robotics.md" >}}) and **AI** tech branches:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Artificial Intelligence]( {{< relref "techs/Artificial_Intelligence.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary prerequisite |
| [Data Center]( {{< relref "buildings/Data_Center.md" >}}) | Library Upgrade | You must have upgraded your Libraries |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **150,000 Science** |
| [Blueprint]( {{< relref "resource/Blueprint.md" >}}) | Resource | **100 Blueprints** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}), Machine Learning is the upgrade that will allow you to stop worrying about your Science cap.

1.  **The "Science Wall":** In the late game, technologies and space missions start costing hundreds of thousands (or millions) of science. Machine Learning, combined with a large stack of [Compendiums]( {{< relref "resource/Compendium.md" >}}), is how you break through this wall without needing to build thousands of buildings.
2.  **AI Management:** Since you are building [AI Cores]( {{< relref "buildings/AI_Core.md" >}}), remember to monitor your **Gigaflops**. If you let your AI level reach **15** without proper consumption (via **Entanglement Stations**), you will trigger the **AI Apocalypse**, which will devastate your resource stocks (including your Uranium!).
3.  **Efficiency:** This upgrade turns your "dead" resources (Compendiums) into active scaling power. It is one of the most efficient ways to use your blueprints in the mid-to-late game transition.

> **Pro Tip:** Pair Machine Learning with the [Neural Networks]( {{< relref "upgrade/Neural_Networks.md" >}}) upgrade. This further enhances your kittens' ability to generate science, effectively allowing you to move kittens out of the Scientist role and into more productive jobs like [Engineer]( {{< relref "job/Engineer.md" >}}) or [Geologist]( {{< relref "job/Geologist.md" >}}).
