In Kittens Game, [Solar Satellites]( {{< relref "upgrade/Solar_Satellites.md" >}}) is a highly useful Workshop upgrade that fundamentally changes how your Satellites interact with your power grid. 

Here is a breakdown of its prerequisites, costs, and effects:

*   **Prerequisites and Cost:** To unlock the Solar Satellites upgrade, you must first research the [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) technology. Purchasing the upgrade in the Workshop costs **225,000 Science** and **750 Alloy**.
*   **The Effect:** By default, every Satellite you deploy in Cath orbit consumes 1 Watt of energy. The Solar Satellites upgrade completely reverses this, transforming your Satellites into dedicated power generators. After purchasing it, each Satellite will instead **permanently generate 1 Watt of energy**. 
*   **The Trade-off:** The only minor restriction is that once this upgrade is purchased, your Satellites become permanently active and **can no longer be toggled off**. However, since they are now feeding power into your grid rather than draining it, there is virtually no reason you would ever want to turn them off.

Ultimately, this upgrade provides a massive net positive for your late-game energy grid, effectively resulting in a +2 energy swing for every single Satellite you own!
