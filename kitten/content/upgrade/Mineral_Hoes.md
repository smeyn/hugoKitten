In **Kittens Game**, [Mineral Hoes]( {{< relref "upgrade/Mineral_Hoes.md" >}}) is the very first agricultural upgrade you can purchase in the [Workshop]( {{< relref "buildings/Workshop.md" >}}). It is the essential foundation for moving your civilization away from a hunter-gatherer society toward a stable farming community.

### Key Effects
* **Farmer Efficiency:** It increases the effectiveness of your **Farmers by +50%**.
* **Early Game Survival:** This is arguably the most important upgrade for surviving your first few winters. Without it, your kittens will likely starve as soon as the cold weather hits and catnip production drops.

---

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see Mineral Hoes in your Workshop, you must have researched [Agriculture]( {{< relref "techs/Agriculture.md" >}}).

* **Science Cost:** 100 Science
* **Minerals Cost:** 100 Minerals
* **Prerequisite:** None (it is the first tier of the Hoe series).

### Strategic Value
Even though you are currently dealing with advanced late-game mechanics like [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Machine Learning]( {{< relref "upgrade/Machine_Learning.md" >}}), Mineral Hoes remains a permanent part of your production multipliers.

1.  **Multiplicative Stacking:** In Kittens Game, early percentage bonuses like this are "baked in" to your base production. This +50% is eventually multiplied by your [Solar Revolution]( {{< relref "religion/Solar_Revolution.md" >}}), [Magnetos]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}}), and [Relic]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) bonuses, making it much more powerful than it initially seems.
2.  **Resource Priority:** At the start of a reset, Minerals are often more abundant than Iron. This allows you to boost food production immediately without waiting for the [Smelter]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) infrastructure required for [Iron Hoes]( {{< relref "upgrade/Iron_Hoes.md" >}}).
3.  **Iron Will Compatibility:** Unlike most upgrades, this is actually useful in [Iron Will]( {{< relref "Challenges/ironWill.md" >}}) mode because it increases the "Catnip per Tick" from [Catnip Fields]( {{< relref "buildings/Catnip_Field.md" >}}), even though you have no kittens assigned as Farmers.
