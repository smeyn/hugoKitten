[Iron]( {{< relref "resource/iron.md" >}}) is a fundamental common resource in Kittens Game that bridges the gap between your early-game village and your mid-to-late game industrial economy. It is primarily used to craft essential advanced components and construct critical infrastructure.

### **Production**
You cannot gather iron directly; it must be refined or acquired through trade. 
*   [Smelters:]( {{< relref "buildings/Smelter.md" >}}) The earliest way to generate iron is by constructing Smelters, which continuously refine wood and minerals into iron. You can double their efficiency by researching the [Electrolytic Smelting]( {{< relref "upgrade/Electrolytic_Smelting.md" >}}) workshop upgrade, which adds +95% to both iron and coal production.
*   [Calciners:]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}) As you advance into chemistry, you can build Calciners, which refine oil and minerals into both iron and titanium. Their iron output can be drastically enhanced through a series of workshop upgrades: [Oxidation]( {{< relref "upgrade/Oxidation.md" >}}) (+95%), [Rotary Kiln]( {{< relref "upgrade/Rotary_Kiln.md" >}}) (+75%), and [Fluidized Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Fluidized_Reactors.md" >}}) (+100%). 

### **Trade and Strategic Arbitrage**
Because standard production facilities like Smelters often struggle to scale efficiently enough to meet mid-game demands, iron frequently becomes a major bottleneck. To overcome this, players utilize trade arbitrage, converting high-volume resources into iron:
*   [Griffins:]( {{< relref "Civilisations/Griffins.md" >}}) Griffins will sell you iron in exchange for Wood. This is an incredibly powerful strategy early on, effectively allowing you to bypass the slow smelting process. Griffins yield the most iron during **Autumn (+35%)**, but suffer penalties during Spring (-25%) and Winter (-20%). 
*   [Zebras:]( {{< relref "Civilisations/zebras.md" >}}) Zebras will sell you iron, plates, and titanium in exchange for Slabs. Zebras yield the most iron during **Summer (+15%)**, but suffer penalties in Autumn (-10%) and Winter (-20%). 

### **Primary Uses and Crafting**
Iron is the critical raw material required to craft two major mid-game resources in the Workshop:
*   [Metal Plates:]( {{< relref "craft/Metal_Plate.md" >}}) Costs 125 Iron. Plates are heavily used for construction, particularly for Harbours.
*   [Steel:]( {{< relref "craft/Steel.md" >}}) Costs 100 Iron and 100 Coal. Steel is a durable metal mandatory for crafting gears, complex machinery, and advanced buildings. To optimize your steel production later in the game, the [Steel Plants]( {{< relref "upgrade/Steel_Plants.md" >}}) upgrade allows Calciners to automatically reserve 10% of their iron output and craft it into steel.

### **Storage Capacity**
Your initial civilization can only hold **50 iron** by default. Because advanced technologies and buildings require massive amounts of iron, constantly expanding your storage limits is mandatory:
*   **Early/Mid Game:** Iron storage is expanded by building **Barns (+50)**, **Warehouses (+25)**, and **Harbours (+150)**. You can multiply these base values by purchasing workshop storage upgrades such as Expanded, Reinforced, Titanium, Alloy, and Concrete Barns and Warehouses. 
*   **Late Game & Space:** As you progress, massive iron storage vaults become available. [Accelerators]( {{< relref "buildings/Accelerator.md" >}}) (once upgraded with Energy Rifts) add +7,500 to your iron cap. In space, **Moon Bases** provide +9,000 iron storage, and [Cryostations]( {{< relref "space/cryo_station.md" >}}) grant a staggering +50,000 iron storage.
