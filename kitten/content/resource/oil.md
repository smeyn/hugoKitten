[Oil]( {{< relref "resource/oil.md" >}}) is an essential common resource in Kittens Game that acts as the primary fuel for your mid-to-late game industrial revolution and space exploration. 

### **Production and Acquisition**
*   [Oil Wells:]( {{< relref "buildings/Oil_Well.md" >}}) The earliest and primary way to generate oil is by constructing Oil Wells. Their output can be drastically multiplied through a series of workshop upgrades: [Pumpjack]( {{< relref "upgrade/Pumpjack.md" >}}) (+45% output, but costs 1 Watt of energy), [Oil Refinery]( {{< relref "upgrade/Oil_Refinery.md" >}}) (+35%), and [Oil Distillation]( {{< relref "upgrade/Oil_Distillation.md" >}}) (+75%). 
*   [Bio Labs:]( {{< relref "buildings/Bio_Lab.md" >}}) By researching the [Biofuel Processing]( {{< relref "upgrade/Biofuel_Processing.md" >}}) upgrade, your Bio Labs will automatically convert 5 catnip per second into 0.10 oil. You can boost this conversion yield by an additional 60% with the [GM Catnip]( {{< relref "upgrade/GM_Catnip.md" >}}) genetics upgrade. 
*   **Hydraulic Fracturers:** In the space phase, you can build these on the planet Dune to generate a massive 2.5 oil per second. Their production is boosted by Space Elevators and receives a +50% bonus during the Dune cycle. 
*   [Trading:]( {{< relref "guides/trading.md" >}}) You can acquire oil by trading Scaffolds to Spiders. 

### **Primary Uses**
*   **Powering Industry:** Oil is a constant upkeep requirement for key industrial buildings; [Magnetos]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}}) consume 0.05 oil per tick, and [Calciners]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}) consume 0.024 oil per tick.
*   **Space Missions:** Reaching and expanding in space requires huge, lump-sum investments of oil. For example, the Orbital Launch requires 15,000 oil, the Moon Mission requires 45,000, and building a Moon Base costs 70,000. Building [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) will help reduce the oil requirements for space missions by 5% each.
*   [Kerosene:]( {{< relref "resource/kerosene.md" >}}) Oil must be refined into Kerosene, a highly advanced rocket fuel used for deep space exploration. The [Factory Processing]( {{< relref "upgrade/Factory_Processing.md" >}}) upgrade makes this conversion more efficient.

### [Oil Storage]( {{< relref "resource/oil_storage.md" >}})
Your civilization starts with a very restrictive base storage limit of 1,500 oil. Because space technologies require tens of thousands of units at once, aggressively expanding your storage is mandatory. You can increase your oil cap by:
*   Building more [Oil Wells]( {{< relref "buildings/Oil_Well.md" >}}) (+1,500 capacity each).
*   Crafting [Tankers]( {{< relref "craft/Tanker.md" >}}) in the workshop (+500 capacity each).
*   Building [Cryostations]( {{< relref "space/cryo_station.md" >}}) in space (+7,500 capacity each).
