The **Minerology Department** is a special Zebra Upgrade available in the game, primarily relevant for players exploring the Iron Will mechanics.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** To unlock the ability to purchase the Minerology Department, you must first research the [Geology]( {{< relref "techs/Geology.md" >}}) science. 
*   **Cost:** The upgrade requires an investment of **75,000 Science** and **75 Compendiums**.

### **Core Effect**
*   **Meteor Yield Boost:** Once purchased, minerology is taught in your Academies, which directly improves the resources you gather from falling meteors. 
*   **Scaling:** Specifically, this upgrade grants a **+1% production boost to meteor yields for every Academy** you have constructed.

### **Player Insight**
While the upgrade is designed to increase meteor yields based on your Academy count, some players have reported seeing **no noticeable change** in the yield of regular meteors after acquiring it. This suggests its actual impact might be bugged, extremely minor, or potentially only noticeable on special events like ivory meteors.
