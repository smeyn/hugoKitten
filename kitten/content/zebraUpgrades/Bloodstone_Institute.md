The specific term "Bloodstone Institute" does not appear in the provided sources. However, the sources contain extensive information regarding the Bloodstone resource and the specialized Zebra buildings that utilize it.
Bloodstone Resource
Acquisition: In Iron Will (IW) mode, if you have at least 10 Zebras, every hunt has a 0.05% chance of yielding a bloodstone
. This chance increases significantly to 5% if you do not already possess any bloodstone
.
Crafting: Bloodstone is a high-tier craftable resource. It can be produced using 5,000 Time Crystals and 10,000 Relics, provided you have built a Zebra Forge
.
Effect: Possessing bloodstone provides a +10% bonus to kitten happiness
.
Associated Buildings
Bloodstone is the primary resource used to construct and upgrade specialized buildings for Zebras in Iron Will mode:
Zebra Outpost: Costs 1 Bloodstone. It provides a +5% hunting bonus and increases maximum catpower by 5
.
Zebra Workshop: Costs 5 Bloodstone. This building is critical as it unlocks Zebra-specific upgrades in the Workshop tab and increases maximum catpower by 10
.
Zebra Forge: Costs 50 Bloodstone. It unlocks the ability to craft Bloodstone and T-Mythril and provides a +50 bonus to maximum catpower
.
Zebra Upgrades (Unlocked via Zebra Workshop)
Once the Zebra Workshop is active, several upgrades become available that often require Bloodstones:
Dark Revolution: Increases the Zebra base catpower production cap by 1 (or 0.1 in non-Iron Will runs) for each Zebra Outpost built
.
Dark Brew: Requires 1 Bloodstone, 3,000 Parchment, and 100 Science. It causes festivals to boost the chance of Zebra arrivals
.
Whispers: Requires 5 T-Mythril. It unlocks "Alicorn Ascension" rituals at Ivory Temples, which automates mineral production and T-Mythril generation
.
