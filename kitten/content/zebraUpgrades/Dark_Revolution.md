[Dark Revolution]( {{< relref "zebraUpgrades/Dark_Revolution.md" >}}) is a religion-tier upgrade in Kittens Game that acts as a pivot point for your late-game faith and resource strategy. It essentially converts your religious progression into a tool for **efficiency and speed**, particularly regarding the "Dark Future" mechanics.

### Key Benefits & Effects

* **Solar Revolution Cap:** Its most significant effect is providing a way to increase the [Solar Revolution]( {{< relref "religion/Solar_Revolution.md" >}}) production bonus cap. In later stages of the game, your faith bonus can become so massive it hits a ceiling; Dark Revolution helps push through that.
* **Kitten Sacrifice Efficiency:** It increases the yield of [Tears]( {{< relref "resource/tears.md" >}}) when sacrificing kittens (via the *Ziggurat*). This is crucial for building up your [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}) structures (like Black Obelisks and Event Horizons).
* **Blackcoin Interaction:** While it doesn't directly change the market price of Blackcoin, the increased faith and relic generation it supports allow you to engage in much larger **Blackcoin speculation** cycles.
* **Zebra Synergy:** It makes [Zebra Outposts]( {{< relref "buildings/Zebra_Outpost.md" >}}) 10x more effective at increasing your Catpower production limit, which is vital for late-game hunting and trading.

---

### How to Unlock It
To see Dark Revolution in your Religion tab, you typically need to meet several high-level requirements:

1.  **Transcendence Tier:** You generally need to be at least **Transcendence Tier 5-10**.
2.  [Zebra Workshop:]( {{< relref "buildings/Zebra_Workshop.md" >}}) You must have unlocked the Zebra Workshop (which requires hunting with at least 10 Zebras and gathering [Bloodstones]( {{< relref "craft/Bloodstone.md" >}})).
3.  [Cryptotheology:]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}) Having at least one [Black Pyramid]( {{< relref "ziggurat/Black_Pyramid.md" >}}) or [Marker]( {{< relref "ziggurat/Marker.md" >}}) is often a prerequisite to unlock the "darker" side of the religion tree.

### Strategic Use
You should prioritize Dark Revolution when your runs start lasting long enough to hit the [Dark Future]( {{< relref "guides/dark-future.md" >}}) (Year 40,000+). It helps offset the production penalties that occur in the deep future and scales your resource generation to the levels needed for [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) and massive paragon grinding.

> **Pro Tip:** Combine Dark Revolution with [Black Obelisks]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Obelisk.md" >}}). The Obelisks increase the effectiveness of your Solar Revolution bonus, and Dark Revolution ensures you have the raw power to reach those higher caps.
