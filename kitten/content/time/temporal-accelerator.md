The [Temporal Accelerator]( {{< relref "time/temporal-accelerator.md" >}}) is an advanced upgrade found in the Time tab that enhances various time manipulation mechanics and improves the performance of automated time skips. 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Cost:** Purchasing your first Temporal Accelerator requires **10 Time Crystals and 1,000 Relics**. 
*   **Price Ratio:** The cost of each subsequent accelerator scales with a 1.25 price ratio.

### **Core Effects and Mechanics**
*   **Time-Related Bonus:** It provides a 5% bonus to time-related effects. Specifically, this translates to a **1.25% improvement to both your Chronomancy and Unicornmancy** upgrades, and a **5% improvement to your Time Impedance**.
*   **Redshift Boost:** It increases the production of temporal flux you earn from "redshift" (the game's offline progression mechanic) by **5%**.
*   **Lag Reduction:** For late-game players utilizing "Shatter Engines," this upgrade offers a massive quality-of-life improvement. When you have time automation activated (such as with the Chrono Furnace), the Temporal Accelerator directly **reduces the lag caused by rapidly smashing time crystals**.
