[Resource Retrieval]( {{< relref "time/resource-retrieval.md" >}}) is an advanced, late-game Time upgrade that serves as the foundation for the self-sustaining endgame economy.

Here is a complete breakdown of its mechanics, costs, and strategic value:

### **1. How to Unlock It**
To unlock Resource Retrieval, you must advance deep into the temporal science and tech trees:
*   First, research [Void Space]( {{< relref "techs/Void_Space.md" >}}) (requires Tachyon Theory).
*   Then research [Paradox Theory]( {{< relref "techs/Paradox_Theory.md" >}}) (costs 1,000,000 Science, 40 Time Crystals, and 250 Void).
*   This allows you to research **Paradoxal Knowledge** science (requires 250 Void), which officially unlocks [Resource Retrieval]( {{< relref "time/resource-retrieval.md" >}}) under the Time tab.

### **2. Costs and Scaling**
*   **Base Cost:** **1,000 Time Crystals (TCs)**.
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.3**. Because of this steep price ratio, subsequent levels become progressively more expensive very quickly (e.g., level 2 costs 1,300 TCs, level 3 costs 1,690 TCs).

### **3. Core Effects and Calculations**
Each level of Resource Retrieval lets you retrieve **1% of your yearly resource production** every single time you shatter/combust a Time Crystal. 
*   **The "Yearly" Formula:** The game calculates your "yearly" production of resources (such as Unobtainium or Alicorns) based on your active net production per second. Normally, this is your per-second rate **divided by 800**.
*   **Chronosurge Boost:** If you have purchased the [Chronosurge]( {{< relref "upgrade/chronosurge.md" >}}) workshop upgrade, your yearly resource calculations are improved—they are calculated as your per-second rate **divided by 533**, resulting in a much larger payout per skip.
*   **Blazar Synergy:** If you reach Transcendence-tier 15, you can purchase [Blazars]( {{< relref "transcendence/Blazar.md" >}}) (costing 50,000 Relics). Blazars permanently and multiplicatively boost the resource yield from each level of your Resource Retrieval.

### **4. Strategic Value: The "Shatter Engine"**
The ultimate goal of purchasing Resource Retrieval is to build an infinite **Shatter Engine**, transitioning your civilization away from waiting on slow, passive resource generation:
*   **The Infinite Loop:** When you shatter a Time Crystal to skip a year, you instantly receive a massive dump of resources (especially Unobtainium and Alicorns). With enough levels of Resource Retrieval, the Unobtainium you retrieve can be traded to the Leviathans (Elders) and the Alicorns sacrificed to yield **more than 1 Time Crystal** in return. 
*   **The Sweet Spot:** Players generally aim to reach **level 6 or 7 of Resource Retrieval** (requiring roughly 12,000 TCs in total) to cleanly break even and execute this loop indefinitely. If your base Unobtainium production is exceptionally high (over 100/sec), you can sometimes initiate the loop as early as level 3 to 5.
*   **Chronoforge Automation:** Once your Shatter Engine is profitable, you can scale your [Chrono Furnaces]( {{< relref "time/chrono-furnace.md" >}}) to quickly dissipate heat, allowing you to skip thousands of years in rapid succession to generate massive, overcapped stockpiles of resources.
