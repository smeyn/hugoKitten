In *Kittens Game*, a [Void Hoover]( {{< relref "time/void-hoover.md" >}}) is an advanced late-game Time structure located under the Void Space section of your Time tab. It is designed to maximize the amount of Void you gather during temporal anomalies.

### **1. Prerequisites & Unlocking**
To purchase a Void Hoover, you must progress through several layers of the late-game science and workshop trees:
*   **Void Space Technology:** First, you must research the [Void Space]( {{< relref "techs/Void_Space.md" >}}) technology (costs 800k Science, 30 Time Crystals, and 100 Void).
*   **Void Aspiration Upgrade:** Unlocking the Void Space technology allows you to buy the [Void Aspiration]( {{< relref "upgrade/Void_Aspiration.md" >}}) upgrade in your Workshop. This upgrade costs **15 Time Crystals** and **2,000 Antimatter**. 
*   Once Void Aspiration is purchased, the [Void Hoover]( {{< relref "time/void-hoover.md" >}}) (along with Void Rifts) is permanently unlocked for construction.

---

### **2. Costs & Price Ratio**
Constructing a Void Hoover requires premium late-game resources:
*   **10 Time Crystals**
*   **250 Void**
*   **1,000 Antimatter**
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.25** — Like many time-based structures, every subsequent Void Hoover you construct will cost 25% more than the previous level.

---

### **3. The Effect & Strategic Value**
*   **The Effect:** Every level of Void Hoover permanently **increases the maximum amount of Void you might gain on a Temporal Paradox day by +1**.

Active Chronospheres occasionally trigger a [Temporal Paradox]( {{< relref "guides/temporal_paradox.md" >}}) event. Because Void is a strictly capped and slow-to-generate resource in the mid-to-late game, Void Hoovers are essential for amplifying your Void income during these paradox windows. This accelerated Void generation feeds directly into other powerful endgame systems, allowing you to stockpile the massive amounts of Void needed to buy [Void Rifts]( {{< relref "time/void-rift.md" >}}) (which expand global storage capacity) and execute the **Cryo Engine** strategy (which requires 500 Void per repaired Cryochamber).
