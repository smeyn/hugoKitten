The [Time Boiler]( {{< relref "time/time-boiler.md" >}}) is an advanced time-manipulation upgrade that directly improves the functionality of the Chrono Furnace by expanding its maximum heat capacity. This upgrade is essential for late-game "mass shattering" strategies, allowing you to skip thousands of years and farm resources or paragon without hitting your heat limit as quickly.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Base Cost:** Your first Time Boiler requires a massive investment of **25,000 Time Crystals**.
*   **Price Ratio:** The cost scales with a price ratio of 1.25 for each subsequent purchase.

### **Core Effects and Unique Scaling**
Unlike most upgrades, Time Boilers operate with unique, exponential scaling mechanics for both their benefits and their energy requirements:
*   **Base Effect:** The very first Time Boiler you purchase grants a maximum heat expansion of 10 and consumes 1 Watt of energy. 
*   **Scaling Increases:** For every Time Boiler you already own, the heat expansion granted by the *next* one increases by 20, and its energy cost increases by an additional 2 Watts. 
*   **Net Result:** This compounding effect means that your total energy cost will equal the square of your Time Boilers, calculated as **(Time Boilers)²**, while your total heat expansion will equal **10 * (Time Boilers)²**.
