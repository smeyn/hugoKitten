A [Void Rift]( {{< relref "time/void-rift.md" >}}) is an advanced, late-game structure found in the Void Space section of your **Time** tab. Building on our recent look at Void Hoovers, these rifts are vital for scaling your global storage capacities and power output to break through deep late-game bottlenecks.

### **Unlocking & Costs**
To construct a Void Rift, you must first research the [Void Space]( {{< relref "techs/Void_Space.md" >}}) technology and purchase the [Void Aspiration]( {{< relref "upgrade/Void_Aspiration.md" >}}) workshop upgrade (which costs 15 Time Crystals and 2,000 Antimatter). 

Once unlocked, your first Void Rift costs:
*   **75 Void**
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.3** — Like other void space structures, the cost scales aggressively with each subsequent build.

---

### **Core Effects**
Each built Void Rift provides two highly valuable, passive multipliers:

1.  **Global Resource Storage Ratio (+2% per Rift):** This increases your maximum capacity for every single resource in the game. Crucially, this bonus stacks additively with **Event Horizons** (which grant a massive +10% storage ratio per level) and multiplicatively with all other storage bonuses, such as your Paragon multipliers.
2.  **HR Harvester Energy Boost (+10% per Rift):** It permanently boosts the energy production of your Hawking Radiation (HR) Harvesters (located on Umbra) by +10% per rift. Because HR Harvesters automatically generate more energy the further you skip into the "Dark Future," Void Rifts make them highly competitive late-game power generators.

---

### **Strategic Endgame Value**
Though a 2% storage multiplier may seem modest initially, Void Rifts become indispensable in the deep endgame for two main reasons:

*   **Shattering the Unobtainium Capacity Wall:** To execute "positive resets" (where the resources carried over exceed the cost of constructing your next set of Chronospheres), you need to amass billions of units of Unobtainium. Since Moon Bases and Cryostations are the only buildings that grant base Unobtainium storage, Void Rifts and Event Horizons are the primary tools used to compound and scale your storage caps high enough to support 70+ Chronospheres.
*   **Balancing Your Power Grid:** As you scale your Gigaflop-producing AI Cores and Entanglement Stations, your active energy consumption will skyrocket. Investing in Void Rifts allows you to rely on HR Harvesters to generate massive amounts of power, keeping your grid positive even while running heavy quantum computing pipelines.
