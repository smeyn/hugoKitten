[Cryochambers]( {{< relref "time/cryochamber.md" >}}) are late-game Void Space structures that allow you to carry kittens over into your next game reset. Each Cryochamber houses one kitten, and upon resetting, the game will prioritise saving the kittens with the highest experience levels.

### **Acquisition and Costs**
*   **Prerequisite:** You must research the [Void Space]( {{< relref "techs/Void_Space.md" >}}) technology to unlock them.
*   **Standard Cost:** Building one costs **2 Time Crystals, 100 Void, and 1 Karma**.
*   **Cost Reduction:** The Karma cost can be reduced by your burned paragon, with diminishing returns eventually capping at a 100% reduction.

### **Limits and Capacity**
Normally, you **cannot build more Cryochambers than your current number of active Chronospheres**. However, completing the **Post Apocalypse challenge** increases the amount of operational Cryochambers you can support, effectively capping them at your number of Chronospheres plus your total Post Apocalypse challenge completions.

### **Used Cryochambers and Repairs**
After you reset the game and successfully carry your kittens over, your Cryochambers will break and become **Used Cryochambers**. In this state, they do absolutely nothing, but they can be repaired using the [Fix Cryochambers]( {{< relref "time/fix-cryochambers.md" >}}) action.
*   **Repair Cost:** Fixing a Used Cryochamber costs a flat rate of **100 Time Crystals, 500 Void, and 3,000 Temporal Flux**.
*   **Prerequisite for Repair:** You must have the [Chronoforge]( {{< relref "upgrade/Chronoforge.md" >}}) upgrade for the repair button to appear on your menu.
*   *Strategy Note:* You can fix used chambers to theoretically own more Cryochambers than Chronospheres, but the number of kittens you can actually bring into the next run will always remain strictly limited by your active Chronosphere limit.

### **Role in Challenges**
*   **Atheism:** The primary victory condition for the Atheism challenge is to reach space and reset the game with at least one active Cryochamber.
*   **Post Apocalypse:** In this highly polluted challenge timeline, having a high number of broken Cryochambers makes the game significantly harder by causing Catnip Fields to cost unobtainium. If you successfully bring kittens into this challenge via Cryochambers, you will also bring over a proportional store of catnip to help your newly transferred population survive.
