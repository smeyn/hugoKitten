In *Kittens Game*, the [Temporal Battery]( {{< relref "time/temporal-battery.md" >}}) is a crucial Time tab upgrade designed to expand your temporal storage limits so you can utilize advanced temporal mechanics. 

### **Core Mechanics & Costs**
*   **Where to find it:** The **Time** tab, which is unlocked after you research the [Calendar]( {{< relref "techs/Calendar.md" >}}) technology.
*   **The Cost:** Constructing your first Temporal Battery costs **5 Time Crystals**.
*   **The Price Ratio:** It has a price ratio of **1.25**, meaning each subsequent level will cost 25% more Time Crystals than the last (e.g., the second level costs 6.25 TCs).
*   **The Effect:** Every level of Temporal Battery you purchase permanently increases your maximum **Temporal Flux capacity by +25%**.

---

### **Why You Need to Increase Your Temporal Flux Capacity**
A high capacity for Temporal Flux is necessary to interact with two major late-game/end-game systems:

1.  **Repairing Cryochambers (The Paragon/Kitten Carrying Loop):** 
    When you reset your game, each active Chronosphere lets you carry over a kitten in a Cryochamber, but this leaves the chambers in an unsealed "used" state. To repair a Used Cryochamber back to operating condition, you must pay **3,000 Temporal Flux**, **100 Time Crystals**, and **500 Void**. If you plan to carry over dozens of kittens (e.g., up to 71 kittens) to rapidly accumulate Paragon right at the start of a reset, you must build numerous Temporal Batteries to accommodate the massive temporal flux storage requirements.
2.  **Extended Use of Tempus Fugit:** 
    Tempus Fugit allows you to accelerate the game's tick rate and calendar speed by 50%, enabling you to collect resources much faster. However, running this active boost is incredibly expensive, consuming **5 temporal flux per second**. A tiny capacity cap will cause your reserve to evaporate almost instantly.

---

### **How to Fill Your Upgraded Battery**
While the Temporal Battery only increases your *maximum capacity*, you can generate the actual Temporal Flux to fill it through several methods:
*   **Offline Redshift (Passive):** By default, you earn **5 units of temporal flux per minute** while the game is completely closed or paused. 
*   **Temporal Accelerators (Redshift Boost):** This Time tab upgrade (costing 10 Time Crystals and 1,000 Relics) permanently increases the flux generated from offline redshift by **5%**.
*   **Chronosurge (Active):** Once you purchase this upgrade, your [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) will actively generate **1 temporal flux per year** on the calendar, letting you harvest flux actively rather than relying on offline wait times.
