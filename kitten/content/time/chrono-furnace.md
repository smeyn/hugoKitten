The [Chrono Furnace]( {{< relref "time/chrono-furnace.md" >}}) is a powerful late-game upgrade found in the Time tab that heavily enhances your ability to perform time skips (shattering Time Crystals). 

### **Acquisition and Costs**
Before you can build a Chrono Furnace, you must first purchase the Chronoforge upgrade. Each Chrono Furnace has a base cost of **25 Time Crystals and 5 Relics**, and scales with a 1.25 price ratio.

### **Core Mechanics and Effects**
The Chrono Furnace serves several crucial functions for managing the "chronoheat" generated when you shatter Time Crystals:
*   **Heat Capacity and Cooling:** Each furnace increases your maximum chronoheat limit by 100 and passively dissipates heat at a rate of **-0.02 chronoheat per tick**. 
*   **Cooldown Reduction:** It drastically reduces the cooldown time required between time skips. The cooldown is calculated as **50 seconds / (Number of Chrono Furnaces + 1)**. For example, owning 24 Chrono Furnaces allows you to shatter Time Crystals every 2 seconds. 
*   **Fuel and Automation:** Chrono Furnaces automatically convert your accumulated chronoheat into **chrono furnace fuel**. If you toggle automation "on," the furnace will automatically spend 500% of its stored fuel each tick to trigger a free time skip. You also have the option to toggle automation off, allowing you to hoard fuel and delay your time skips for a later burst.

### **Synergies and Related Upgrades**
*   [Time Boilers:]( {{< relref "time/time-boiler.md" >}}) An upgrade that expands the heat capacity of your Chrono Furnaces.
*   **Controlled Delay:** Because massive amounts of stored fuel can cause the furnace to fire too rapidly for you to click anything else, the Controlled Delay upgrade forces the automated Chrono Furnace to wait **0.2 seconds between firing**, allowing you to perform meaningful actions during the skips.
*   **1000 Years Challenge:** Completing this challenge increases the efficiency of converting chronoheat into Chrono Furnace fuel by **10% per completion**, and makes each shatter directly add a bit of heat to the furnace to offset lost heat generation.

### **Strategic Importance**
In the deep endgame, Chrono Furnaces are a mandatory component of **"Shatter Engines"**. By building dozens of Chrono Furnaces alongside Resource Retrieval, you can rapidly and automatically skip thousands of years into the future, harvesting massive amounts of resources (like antimatter and unobtainium) in fractions of the real-world time it would normally take.
