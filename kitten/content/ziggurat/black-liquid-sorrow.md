[Black Liquid Sorrow]( {{< relref "ziggurat/black-liquid-sorrow.md" >}}) (frequently referred to as **BLS** or simply **Sorrow**) is a rare, uncommon resource used heavily in the late game to interact with the Leviathans and permanently boost your civilization's production.

Here is a breakdown of how to acquire and manage this resource:

### **How to Acquire Sorrow**
*   **Refining Tears:** The most consistent way to get BLS is by refining **Unicorn Tears** at a rate of **10,000 Tears to 1 Sorrow**. To unlock this ability, you must first purchase the [Megalomania]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Megalomania.md" >}}) metaphysics upgrade. 
*   [Trading:]( {{< relref "guides/trading.md" >}}) Once the Leviathans arrive, every trade you make with them (costing 5,000 Unobtainium) has a **15% chance** to yield 1 Black Liquid Sorrow.
*   **Events:** You can occasionally acquire it through a random weather event called "Black Rain".

### **Primary Uses**
*   [Black Pyramids:]( {{< relref "ziggurat/Black_Pyramid.md" >}}) The most important use for your first few Sorrows is building [Black Pyramids]( {{< relref "ziggurat/Black_Pyramid.md" >}}), which cost **5 Sorrow** each (alongside Megaliths, Spice, and Unobtainium). You must construct at least one Black Pyramid to make the Leviathans appear for trading. 
*   **The Necrocracy Policy:** After you make your first trade with the Leviathans, you can unlock a powerful late-game government policy called [Necrocracy]( {{< relref "Policy/Necrocracy.md" >}}). This policy grants a **global production bonus of 0.1% for every percent of Black Liquid Sorrow** you possess. 

### **Storage Limits**
*   **Base Cap:** Your civilization starts with a very strict **maximum storage limit of 16 BLS**. 
*   **Expanding Storage:** You can eventually increase this cap by purchasing [Black Cores]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Core.md" >}}), an advanced Cryptotheology upgrade (unlocked at Transcendence Tier 5) that increases your sorrow limit by 1% per level.
*   **Refining Warning:** If you attempt to refine Unicorn Tears into Sorrow while you are already at your maximum storage capacity, the game will prevent it; nothing will happen, but thankfully your Tears will not be consumed.

### **Resetting the Game**
One of the most important aspects of Black Liquid Sorrow is that it is a permanent resource. Your entire stockpile of BLS will **automatically carry over in full whenever you reset your game**. Because it survives resets, it is highly recommended to refine any leftover Unicorn Tears you have into Sorrow right before you hit the reset button to ensure you don't lose that value.
