[Vitruvian Feline]( {{< relref "metaphysics/vitruvian_feline.md" >}}) is a highly valuable Metaphysics upgrade that directly improves your civilization's economy by lowering the escalating costs of buildings.

### **Acquisition and Prerequisites**
*   **Prerequisite:** You must first purchase the [Divine Proportion]( {{< relref "metaphysics/divine_proportion.md" >}}) metaphysics upgrade to unlock Vitruvian Feline.
*   **Cost:** Purchasing the upgrade requires a substantial investment of **250 Paragon**.

### **Core Effects and Strategy**
*   **Price Ratio Reduction:** Vitruvian Feline reduces all building price ratios by **2%**. This means buildings will become cheaper to construct, allowing you to buy significantly more structures before you hit your resource storage caps. 
*   **Progression:** Acquiring Vitruvian Feline unlocks [Renaissance]( {{< relref "metaphysics/renaissance.md" >}}), which is the final price ratio reduction upgrade.
*   **Strategic Importance:** It is highly recommended that you prioritize buying all of the price ratio reduction upgrades (including Vitruvian Feline) early in your playthroughs, as they make everything in your village cheaper and allow for rapid expansion. A common community strategy is to aim for Vitruvian Feline around your fourth reset, which typically requires reaching a population of about **320 kittens** in a single run to generate enough Paragon to afford it.
