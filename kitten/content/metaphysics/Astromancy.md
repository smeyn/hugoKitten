In *Kittens Game*, [Astromancy]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Astromancy.md" >}}) is an early-to-midgame [Metaphysics]( {{< relref "techs/Metaphysics.md" >}}) upgrade that drastically accelerates astronomical events and early-game Starchart generation.

---

### **1. Cost and Prerequisites**
* **Cost:** **50 Paragon**.
* **Prerequisite:** [Chronomancy]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Chronomancy.md" >}}) (25 Paragon).

---

### **2. Core Effects**
Astromancy doubles both the frequency and the automated success rate of astronomical events:
* **Doubles Astronomical Event Chance:** Multiplies your base astronomical event chance and the event chance added by Observatories by **2\times\\)**.
* **Doubles Observatory Effectiveness:** Multiplies the **automatic event observation chance** provided by Observatories by **2\times\\)** (from +1% per Observatory up to +2% per Observatory). 
* *(Note: It affects event chances and auto-observation rates, but does **not** double the science bonus or science cap of Observatories).*

---

### **3. Strategic Impact**
* **Reaching 100% Auto-Observation Faster:** Normally, each Observatory adds +1% chance to automatically observe an event without manual clicking, requiring 100 Observatories to guarantee success. With Astromancy, each Observatory gives +2%, cutting the requirement down to only **50 Observatories** before you achieve 100% automated success (prior to unlocking SETI).
* **Breaking the Starchart Bottleneck:** In early space runs and resets, Starcharts are one of the most punishing bottlenecks needed to craft Trade Ships, unlock Geodesy, and fund early rocket launches. Pairing [Chronomancy]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Chronomancy.md" >}}) (+10% daily chance) with [Astromancy]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Astromancy.md" >}}) 2 times chance) more than doubles your incoming Starcharts early in a run.
* **When to Buy:** Community guides typically recommend purchasing Astromancy around your **3rd or 4th run** (often alongside or shortly after Golden Ratio and Divine Proportion), right as you are preparing to push through early space missions and build up large Trade Ship fleets.
