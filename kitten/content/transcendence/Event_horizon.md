Event Horizon is a Cryptotheology upgrade under the Religion tab that fundamentally expands your late-game resource limits.

Unlike standard bonfire or space buildings, Cryptotheology structures are bought using Relics and persist permanently across resets.

Cost & Requirements

* Tab: Religion to Cryptotheology
* Unlock Requirement: Requires building a Black Pyramid (via Ziggurat sacrifices) to access the Cryptotheology panel.
* Cost: Scales exponentially in Relics and Void:
  * Relic Cost: approx 100 times 1.25^N
  * Void Cost: Scales alongside relics per purchase rank.

What It Does

* Compounding Global Storage Bonus: Each purchase of Event Horizon increases maximum storage capacity across all standard resources (Catnip, Wood, Minerals, Iron, Coal, Titanium, Oil, Unobtainium, Uranium, etc.).
* The Math:
  
  `Storage Multiplier = (1 + 0.10)^N = 1.10^N`
  
  Every level grants a +10% compounding bonus to overall storage caps.
* Stacking with Void Rifts: While Void Rifts (in the Space tab) also give compounding storage bonuses, Void Rifts reset when you reset your game. Event Horizon provides a permanent baseline that makes every subsequent run start with larger storage caps out of the gate.

Why It Is Critical in Late Game

1. Unlocking Late-Game Price Thresholds: Late-game science techs, high-tier Chronospheres, AI Cores, and deep space missions have astronomical resource costs that standard Barns, Warehouses, and Harbors cannot hold. Event Horizon pushes those ceilings higher.
2. Fueling the Shatter Loop: Higher Unobtainium caps mean you can hold more Unobtainium per time-shattered year via Resource Retrieval, allowing for larger bulk trades with Leviathans without overflowing and wasting materials.
3. Reset Cushion: Because Event Horizon never resets, future runs reach high-capacity storage rapidly, letting you build advanced housing and factories much earlier.

Spending Recommendation

With a solid Relic reserve (such as 100k–200k+ Relics), aim for 15–25 levels of Event Horizon. Balancing Event Horizon with Black Nexus (which boosts Relic/TC yields from Leviathan trades) ensures your resource caps grow alongside your trade volume.
