In **Kittens Game**, the [Mausoleum]( {{< relref "transcendence/Mausoleum.md" >}}) is a late-game religious building that serves as a cornerstone for managing **Temporal Distortion** and scaling your high-level faith bonuses.

It represents a permanent monument to your fallen kittens, allowing their "legacy" to stabilize your timeline.

---

### Key Effects
The Mausoleum provides three critical benefits for advanced players:

* **Distortion Reduction:** It is the primary building used to reduce **Temporal Distortion**. Each Mausoleum provides a flat reduction to the distortion generated by **Temporal Paradoxes**, allowing you to farm [Void]( {{< relref "resource/void.md" >}}) more sustainably without crashing your production.
* **Solar Revolution Bonus:** It increases the effectiveness of your [Solar Revolution]( {{< relref "religion/Solar_Revolution.md" >}}) bonus. This allows your total production multiplier from faith to reach even higher levels, which is essential for late-game resource scaling.
* **Epimetheus Synergy:** In the [Metaphysics]( {{< relref "techs/Metaphysics.md" >}}) tree, the Mausoleum interacts with "The Codex" series of upgrades, further boosting your science and faith limits.

### Strategic Use
1.  **Void Farming:** If you are building [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}), you need Void. Use Mausoleums to keep your Distortion under control while your [Void Resonators]( {{< relref "time/void-resonator.md" >}}) trigger paradoxes.
2.  **Faith Caps:** Once you have a high **Transcendence Tier**, Mausoleums are one of the few ways to continue growing your global production bonus when other buildings hit their diminishing returns.
3.  **Stability:** If you notice your resource production "flickering" or dropping unexpectedly, it’s likely due to high Distortion. Building 5–10 Mausoleums usually stabilizes the timeline for mid-range Void farming.

### Summary of Benefits

* **Primary Purpose:** Stabilizing time and boosting late-game production.
* **Scaling:** Its costs scale moderately, making it a reliable place to dump excess [Relics]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) once your [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}) is established.
