The [Dark Future]( {{< relref "guides/dark-future.md" >}}) is a late-game phase that officially begins when your in-game calendar reaches **year 40,000**. Advancing into this era introduces a mix of temporal penalties and significant boosts to your civilization's capabilities.

### **Trigger Condition**
*   **Requirement:** You enter the Dark Future automatically when the game year reaches **40,000**.
*   **Time Skips:** This includes years gained artificially by shattering Time Crystals using the Chronoforge. You do not need to wait 40,000 real-time years; advanced players reach this state by skipping thousands of years instantly.

**The Temporal Penalty**
The primary negative effect of the Dark Future is that the cost to advance time (using [Time Crystals]( {{< relref "resource/Time_Crystals.md" >}})) will progressively increase the further you venture into the timeline. To mitigate this, you can purchase the [Time Impedance]( {{< relref "upgrade/time-impedance.md" >}}) upgrade, which suppresses the Dark Future penalty by **1,000 years per level**. You can further enhance this suppression by building [Temporal Accelerators]( {{< relref "time/temporal-accelerator.md" >}}). Fortunately, the rising cost penalty is considered relatively minimal by the community and shouldn't require excessive worrying or over-investment. 

**The Benefits**
This is the primary strategic reason players aim for the Dark Future.
*   **Storage Boost:** Before the Dark Future, [Burned Paragon]( {{< relref "resource/burnedparagon.md" >}}) provides a relatively small storage bonus (0.05% per point). Once Dark Future activates, this bonus quadruples to **0.2% per point**.
*   **Endgame Scaling:** This massive boost to global storage capacity allows you to hold significantly more Unobtainium, which is required to build the **70–100 Chronospheres** needed for infinite resource loops.

*   **HR Harvesters:** The energy production of your HR Harvesters receives a continuous, infinitely scaling boost for **every year that passes after 40,000**. Importantly, this includes the thousands of years you actively skip by shattering Time Crystals.


### **Strategic Summary**
Reaching the Dark Future is a goal for "deep endgame" runs. Players purposefully shatter Time Crystals to reach year 40,000 to activate the **Burned Paragon bonus**, which provides the storage necessary to buy more [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) and **Event Horizons**. They then manage the rising costs by purchasing [Time Impedance]( {{< relref "upgrade/time-impedance.md" >}}).
