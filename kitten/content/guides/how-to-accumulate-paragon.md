Accumulating **2,000 Paragon** in *Kittens Game* is a massive milestone that requires a deliberate, multi-reset farming strategy (often called "paragon farming" or "speed runs"). Because Paragon is earned primarily by resetting your game with high kitten populations, you cannot achieve this in a single long run.

Here is the step-by-step roadmap to efficiently farm your way to 2,000 Paragon:

---

### 1. Understand How Paragon is Calculated

* **The Formula:** You earn **1 Paragon for every kitten over 70** that you have in your village when you reset.
* If you reset with 130 kittens, you get $130 - 70 = 60$ Paragon.
* If you reset with 200 kittens, you get $200 - 70 = 130$ Paragon.


* **Diminishing Returns:** The higher your kitten count goes, the harder it is to house, feed, and keep them happy. Pushing past 180–200 kittens takes exponentially more time than hitting 150. Therefore, **fast, repeated medium-length resets** are much faster than one massive multi-week run.

---

### 2. The Fast-Reset Strategy (The Speedrun Loop)

To grind 2,000 Paragon efficiently, you want to set up a cycle:

1. **Aim for the "Sweet Spot" Population:** Don't try to crawl up to 250+ kittens every time. Push your village to around **150 to 180 kittens**. This gives you a solid **80 to 110 Paragon per reset** without hitting the brutal late-game wall where housing and catnip management slow you down to a crawl.
2. **Hit the Tech Milestones:** Run the game until you unlock [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) and enough Space infrastructure to comfortably bootstrap your next run.
3. **Execute the Reset:** Bank your Paragon, then start a fresh reset.
4. **Repeat:** At ~90–100 Paragon per run, you only need about **20 total resets** to hit your 2,000-paragon goal. With a streamlined setup, a single quick run can be pushed to the target population in just a few days or even hours of active/idle play.

---

### 3. Key Upgrades & Unlocks to Speed Up the Grind

To make each farming run lightning-fast, make sure you invest in these key areas:

* [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) (Space Tab): The most critical tool for fast resetting. When you reset, Chronospheres allow a percentage of your raw resources (like titanium, uranium, and unobtainium) and Time Crystals to carry over into the next run. The more Chronospheres you build, the less time you will spend grinding early-game resource walls on your subsequent runs.
* [Burned Paragon:]( {{< relref "resource/burnedparagon.md" >}}) Once you pass a comfortable safety buffer of unburned paragon (usually around 150–200 for the production/storage multipliers), **burn the rest** of your paragon. Burned Paragon gives a massive, compounding production bonus (up to a cap of around 200% bonus), which makes every subsequent farming run significantly faster.
* [Temporal Flux]( {{< relref "guides/temporal-flux.md" >}}) & [Chronosurge:]( {{< relref "upgrade/chronosurge.md" >}}) Use your active temporal acceleration during your farming runs to skip past the tedious mid-game resource-gathering phases.

### Summary Plan:

Don't wait for one giant run. Run a few targeted civilization cycles, push your village to ~160 kittens, bank ~90 Paragon each time while burning your excess for production speed, and let your Chronosphere carryovers turn your next run into an even faster sprint!
