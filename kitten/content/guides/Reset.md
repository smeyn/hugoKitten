Resetting is the primary mechanic used to exchange current progress for permanent prestige currencies—[Karma]( {{< relref "guides/karma.md" >}}) and [Paragon]( {{< relref "resource/paragon.md" >}})—which provide global multipliers to production and resource storage capacity.

### **Timing Your Reset**
Deciding when to reset depends on your current game phase and prestige goals:
*   **The First Reset**: It is strategically recommended to reset only after researching [Concrete Huts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Concrete_Huts.md" >}}) and reaching at least **130 kittens**. This ensures you gain roughly 60 Paragon, enough to purchase essential early [Metaphysics]( {{< relref "techs/Metaphysics.md" >}}) upgrades like [Diplomacy]( {{< relref "Policy/Diplomacy.md" >}}) or [Golden Ratio]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Golden_Ratio.md" >}}).
*   **Subsequent Early Runs**: Aim for **170+ kittens** on your second run to secure 100 Paragon for the [Divine Proportion]( {{< relref "metaphysics/divine_proportion.md" >}}) upgrade. 
*   **Prestige Minimums**: You must have more than **35 kittens** to gain Karma and more than **70 kittens** to receive Paragon (one point per kitten over 70). 
*   **The "Slow-Down" Rule**: Generally, you should reset when progress becomes "too slow" or when building costs exceed your current production and storage limits.

### **The Pre-Reset Checklist (BMF)**
Experienced players use the **BMF** mnemonic (Backup, Metas, Faith) to ensure no progress is wasted before a reset:
*   **Backup**: Manually save and export your game data to prevent accidental loss.
*   **Metas (Metaphysics)**: Use your accumulated Paragon to buy permanent upgrades in the Metaphysics tab, particularly **price ratio reductions** like Enlightenment or Renaissance, which make future buildings cheaper.
*   **Faith (The TAP Sequence)**: To maximize spiritual carry-over, perform the **TAP sequence**: **[Transcendence]( {{< relref "religion/Transcendence.md" >}})** (to increase your tier), then **Adore the Galaxy** (to convert worship into [Epiphany]( {{< relref "religion/epiphany.md" >}})), and finally [Praise the Sun]( {{< relref "religion/praise_the_sun.md" >}}).
*   **Population Push**: Max out all housing, including [Huts]( {{< relref "buildings/Hut.md" >}}), [Log Houses]( {{< relref "buildings/Log_House.md" >}}), and [Mansions]( {{< relref "buildings/Mansion.md" >}}), to squeeze out every possible Paragon point.
*   **Resource Conversion**: Sacrifice all unicorns for tears and alicorns for [Time Crystals]( {{< relref "resource/Time_Crystals.md" >}}) before they are lost.

### **Carry-over Mechanics**
Understanding what survives the reset is vital for planning your next run:
*   **Permanent Carry-over**: Karma, Paragon, Metaphysics upgrades, [Epiphany]( {{< relref "religion/epiphany.md" >}}), **[Transcendence]( {{< relref "religion/Transcendence.md" >}}) tiers**, Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS), and **Cryptotheology buildings** are preserved.
*   **Resources Lost**: You lose all standard buildings, technology, and luxury resources like furs, ivory, and spice.
*   **Conditional Carry-over**:
    *   [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}): Each sphere preserves 1.5% of your non-crafted resources.
    *   [Flux Condensator]( {{< relref "upgrade/Flux_Condensator.md" >}}): This workshop upgrade is required to carry over **crafted resources** like steel, plates, and blueprints.
    *   [Cryochambers]( {{< relref "time/cryochamber.md" >}}): These allow you to carry over one kitten per chamber, retaining their job skills and experience.

### **Advanced Reset Strategies**
*   **Iron Will (IW) Mode**: In this kittenless mode, the goal is to reset immediately after researching [Archery]( {{< relref "techs/Archery.md" >}}) to accumulate [Zebras]( {{< relref "Civilisations/zebras.md" >}}), which provide passive Catpower for future runs.
*   **Positive Resets**: In the endgame, the goal is to build at least **73 Chronospheres**. At this level, the resources carried over exceed the cost of the spheres themselves, allowing for infinite, self-sustaining growth loops.


## Checklist
To maximize the benefits of a reset and ensure your next run starts with a massive economic foundation, you should follow a thorough pre-reset preparation sequence. The community often uses the acronym BMF (Backup, Metas, and Faith) to remember the core steps
.
Here is a comprehensive pre-reset checklist:
1. Maximize Your Population (Paragon Preparation) Before anything else, cap out all of your housing structures (Huts, Log Houses, Mansions, Space Stations)
. Because Paragon rewards are based directly on your kitten population at the time of reset (granting 1 Paragon for every kitten over 70), you should push your population as high as your storage caps allow
.
2. Backup and Metaphysics (The "B" and "M" in BMF)
Backup: Always export and save a backup of your game just in case you forget a step or realize you didn't have enough resources stockpiled
.
Metaphysics: If you have enough Paragon, purchase any planned Metaphysics upgrades (such as price ratio reductions)
. If you plan to burn any Paragon for its unique production and storage bonuses, do so now while you have a small spare amount
.
3. Optimize Your Religion (The "F" in BMF) To ensure your faith bonuses carry over effectively, execute the TAP sequence
:
Transcend: Spend your accumulated epiphany to buy Transcendence tiers
.
Adore the Galaxy: Convert all of your worship into epiphany. The game will do this automatically if you forget, but manually clicking the button grants an extra 1% bonus
.
Praise the Sun: Convert your remaining faith pool into worship
.
4. Refine Luxury Resources Do not leave valuable resources behind, as luxuries do not carry over on reset
.
Sacrifice all of your accumulated Alicorns to turn them into Time Crystals, ensuring you have the Anachronomancy metaphysics upgrade so the crystals survive the reset
.
Refine any spare unicorn tears into Black Liquid Sorrow (BLS), which is permanently retained
.
5. Chronosphere and Resource Stockpiling Chronospheres allow you to carry over 1.5% of your standard resources per sphere into the next run
. To optimize this:
Turn off automation: Disable any scripts or autoclickers (like auto-praise or auto-crafting) so they don't consume your carefully balanced stockpiles right before you reset
.
Buy Flux Condensator: Purchase this workshop upgrade (costing 5,000 unobtainium and 5 Time Crystals) so your crafted resources, such as blueprints, alloy, and compendiums, will also carry over
.
Stockpile everything: Cap out your non-crafted resources (like gold, faith, and starcharts)
. Heavily stockpile crafted resources; carrying over a massive amount of Compendiums is especially critical, as they will vastly increase your science cap at the very start of your next run, allowing you to instantly rebuy key technologies
.
Liquidate Buildings: Right before buying your Chronospheres, you can sell off expensive buildings like Orbital Arrays and Space Elevators at a 50% refund to squeeze out extra resources for your carryover pool
.
Buy Chronospheres: Finally, purchase as many Chronospheres as your unobtainium allows
.
Once all these steps are complete, you are ready to hit the reset button.
