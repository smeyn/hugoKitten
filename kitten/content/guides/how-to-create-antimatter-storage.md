To successfully push your antimatter storage cap to **5,000** (the crucial threshold required to maximize your Relic Station output), you cannot rely on antimatter production buildings alone. You have to combine specific space structures, workshop multipliers, and your global player stats.

The optimal combination to hit and surpass a 5,000 antimatter cap relies on these core pillars:

### 1. The Core Building: [Containment Chambers]( {{< relref "space/containment-chamber.md" >}})

* **What they do:** [ Containment Chambers ]( {{< relref "space/containment-chamber.md">}}) are your primary space building for storing antimatter. Each base chamber provides **+100 antimatter storage**.
* **The Math:** Without any modifiers, you would need 50 Containment Chambers to hit 5,000. However, because building costs scale exponentially, you want modifiers to lower how many raw chambers you actually have to build and power.

### 2. The Stat Multiplier: Paragon

* **The Secret Weapon:** Every single point of [Paragon]( {{< relref "resource/paragon.md" >}}) you own gives a **+0.1% storage bonus** to your empire (meaning every 1,000 Paragon adds a +100% capacity multiplier to your infrastructure).
* **Why it matters:** If you have 2,000 Paragon, your storage capacity across the board is doubled. This drastically reduces the number of expensive Containment Chambers you need to purchase. If your paragon count is low, grinding out a few resets for more paragon is often faster than trying to raw-resource your way through late-stage building cost scaling.

### 3. The Workshop Upgrade: Heatsinks

* **The Upgrade:** Once you have access to advanced workshop technologies, make sure to pick up **[ Heatsinks ]( {{< relref "space/heatsink.md">}})**.
* **What it does:** It increases the storage capacity of each Containment Chamber by **+2%** (at the cost of a slight 1% increase in energy consumption per chamber). Stacking this across all your chambers cuts down the total building count significantly.

### 4. Managing the Power Crunch

* **The Bottleneck:** Containment Chambers consume a massive amount of energy. To support running dozens of them alongside your Sunlifters, Space Beacons, and Entangling Stations, you will need to heavily scale your power grid.
* **The Solution:** Max out your **[ Sunlifters ]( {{< relref "space/sunlifter.md">}})** and build **[ Tectonics ]( {{< relref "space/tectonic.md">}})** (from the Centaurus system) to shore up your energy grid, and selectively deactivate non-essential deep-space buildings (like extra Space Stations) if your power drops into the negative during winter.

### Summary Checklist for 5,000 Antimatter Storage:

1. **Accumulate Paragon:** Aim for a healthy Paragon pool (ideally 1,000 to 2,000+) to passively multiplier-boost your storage ceiling.
2. **Unlock & Buy Heatsinks:** Research the workshop upgrade to squeeze an extra 2% storage per chamber.
3. **Scale Containment Chambers:** Build out as many Containment Chambers as your energy grid and unobtainium supplies allow, letting your Paragon and Heatsinks bridge the gap to 5,000.
