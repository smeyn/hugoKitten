n Kittens Game, the mid-game spans the transition from initial industrialization to your first Moon landing and early Paragon farming—roughly between Navigation / Chemistry and Rocketry / Unobtainium.
During this phase, progress shifts from manually gathering base resources to establishing production loops, balancing the power grid, and mitigating bottlenecks like Titanium, Coal, and Oil.

# The Three Critical Mid-Game Engines
1. The Magneto + Steamworks Synergy
This is your civilization's primary production engine:
* Base Mechanic: Each Magneto grants a +2% global production bonus.
* The Synergy: Each active Steamworks boosts the bonus of all Magnetos by +15%.
* Formula: `Bonus = Magnetos * 0.02 * (1 + Steamworks * 0.15)`.
* A setup of 10 Magnetos and 10 Steamworks multiplies every resource in your settlement by +50%, scaling Catnip, Minerals, Wood, Coal, and Iron simultaneously.
2. The Trade Fleet (Zebra Titanium Automation)
* Manual Titanium mining is far too slow to sustain mid-game construction.
* Build hundreds of Trade Ships using Starcharts and Scaffolds.
* Trade with the Zebras using Catpower and Gold. A high Trade Ship count guarantees large shipments of Titanium and Iron per trade caravan, easily funding Calciners, Magnetos, and Warehouses.
3. Power Grid & Clean Energy
* Turning on [Magnetos]( {{< relref "buildings/Magneto.md" >}}) (-5 W each) and [Calciners]( {{< relref "buildings/Calciner.md" >}}) (-1 W each) without enough energy triggers brownouts, slashing efficiency across the village.
* Prioritize [Hydro Plants]( {{< relref "buildings/Hydro_Plant.md" >}}) (upgrades [Aqueducts]( {{< relref "buildings/Aqueduct.md" >}})) and [Solar Farms]( {{< relref "buildings/Solar_Farm.md" >}}) to establish at least +20 to +30 W of clean baseline power before adding heavy consumers.

# Essential Mid-Game Workshop Upgrades
* __Pneumatic Bellows / Blast Furnace__: Automatically converts raw Coal and Iron into Steel via Calciners and Smelters.
* [Offset Press]( {{< relref "upgrade/Offset_Press.md" >}}): Upgrades Steamworks to automatically generate Manuscripts each spring, cutting the Culture bottleneck on Compendiums.
* [Pumpjack]( {{< relref "upgrade/Pumpjack.md" >}}) & [Oil Processing]( {{< relref "techs/Oil_Processing.md" >}}): Boosts Oil Well production and storage caps to prepare rocket fuel reserves.
[Concrete Huts]( {{< relref "upgrade/Concrete_Huts.md" >}}) / [Concrete Barns]( {{< relref "upgrade/Concrete_Barns.md" >}}): Uses [Concrete]( {{< relref "craft/Concrete.md" >}}) (Slabs + Steel) to break ground-level housing and storage caps.

# The Mid-Game Reset Target
If you are on your first 1–3 runs, the mid-game is your reset staging ground:
* __Target__: Push housing until you hit 130–170 kittens (via Concrete Huts and early Space Stations).
* **Paragon Harvest**: Earning 60–100 Paragon per reset unlocks the early Metaphysics price-ratio discounts (Golden Ratio, Divine Proportion), making subsequent runs significantly faster.
