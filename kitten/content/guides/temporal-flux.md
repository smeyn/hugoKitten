[Temporal Flux]( {{< relref "guides/temporal-flux.md" >}}) is an uncommon endgame resource used for various time-related experiments and technologies. 

**How to Acquire Temporal Flux:**
*   **Offline Generation:** It is passively earned at a rate of 5 units per minute while the game is not active (i.e., when it is closed or paused).
*   **Chronosurge Upgrade:** Once you purchase the Chronosurge upgrade, each of your Chronospheres will automatically generate 1 temporal flux every game year.

**Key Uses:**
*   [Tempus Fugit:]( {{< relref "guides/tempus_fugit.md" >}}) You can spend temporal flux to activate Tempus Fugit, a state which accelerates the game's tick rate and calendar time speed by 50%, significantly boosting your resource gain. This consumes 5 temporal flux per second while activated.
*   **Fixing Cryochambers:** Repairing a Used Cryochamber to bring kittens into your next run costs 3,000 temporal flux, alongside 100 Time Crystals and 500 Void.
*   **Time Upgrades:** It is a required currency to purchase advanced time-manipulation upgrades, including [Chronocontrol]( {{< relref "time/chrono_control.md" >}}) (costs 3,000 flux) and [Chronosurge]( {{< relref "upgrade/Chronosurge.md" >}}) (costs 6,500 flux).

**Game Resets:**
Conveniently, any accumulated temporal flux you have is automatically saved and carries over when you reset the game.


## Storage
In Kittens Game, the maximum capacity for [Temporal Flux]( {{< relref "guides/temporal-flux.md" >}}) is determined by a specific time-related upgrade. 

### **The Primary Method: Temporal Battery**
To increase your maximum temporal flux capacity, you must purchase the [Temporal Battery]( {{< relref "time/temporal-battery.md" >}}) upgrade:
*   **Where to find it:** It is located under the **Time** tab, which is unlocked after researching Calendar.
*   **The Cost:** Constructing your first Temporal Battery costs **5 Time Crystals**. 
*   **The Price Ratio:** It has a price ratio of **1.25**, meaning each subsequent level will cost 25% more Time Crystals than the last.
*   **The Effect:** Every level of Temporal Battery you purchase permanently increases your maximum temporal flux capacity by **+25%**.

---

### **Optimising Your Temporal Flux Generation**
While you are building up your capacity, you can also improve how quickly you accumulate and gather temporal flux:

1.  **Offline Accumulation (Base Rate):** Temporal flux is earned passively at a rate of **5 units per minute** while the game is either paused or completely closed.
2.  [Temporal Accelerators:]( {{< relref "time/temporal-accelerator.md" >}}) This Time tab upgrade (costing 10 Time Crystals and 1,000 Relics) increases the production of temporal flux generated via redshift (offline progression) by **5%**.
3.  **Chronosurge (Active Gathering):** Once you can afford this Workshop upgrade (which requires 1 Chronocontrol, 25 Time Crystals, 100k Unobtainium, 750 Void, and 6,500 Temporal Flux), your [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}) will begin actively generating **1 temporal flux per year** on the calendar, acting similarly to Sunlifters. 

---

### **Why Is High Capacity Important?**
Maximising your temporal flux capacity is crucial for two major late-game systems:
*   [Tempus Fugit:]( {{< relref "guides/tempus_fugit.md" >}}) Activating this accelerates the game's tick rate and calendar speed by 50%. However, it consumes a heavy **5 temporal flux per second** while active, meaning a low cap will quickly drain your reserve.
*   **Fixing Cryochambers:** If you are using Cryochambers to carry over kittens across resets, repairing a used chamber back to operating condition requires a massive flat cost of **3,000 temporal flux** (along with 100 Time Crystals and 500 Void). Having a high maximum capacity is mandatory to store enough flux to repair multiple chambers at once.
