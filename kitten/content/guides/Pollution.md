In *Kittens Game*, [pollution]( {{< relref "guides/Pollution.md" >}}) (represented as atmospheric CO2) is a late-game mechanic that negatively impacts your civilization's productivity and growth as you industrialize. While its effects are generally negligible up to approximately **1,000 ppm**, higher levels progressively harm your village in several ways.

### **Sources of Pollution**
Pollution is primarily generated by industrial buildings and certain environmental policies:
*   **Industrial Buildings:** Mines, Smelters, Steamworks, Calciners, Quarries, Magnetos, Oil Wells, Bio Labs, and Factories all contribute to pollution.
*   **Policies:** Choosing production-heavy policies like [Strip Mining]( {{< relref "Policy/Strip_Mining.md" >}}), [Clear Cutting]( {{< relref "Policy/Clear_Cutting.md" >}}), [Full Industrialization]( {{< relref "Policy/Full_Industrialization.md" >}}), or [Open Woodlands]( {{< relref "Policy/Open_Woodlands.md" >}}) each adds a **+5% increase** to global pollution.
*   **Challenges:** In the **Post Apocalypse** challenge, you start in a broken timeline where pollution begins at a staggering **10 million ppm**.

### **Negative Effects**
As pollution levels rise, they trigger a series of cascading penalties:
*   **Production Hits:** High levels of pollution directly **reduce catnip production**. Eventually, it also reduces the **Solar Revolution bonus** for certain resources.
*   **Societal Impact:** Pollution **decreases kitten happiness** and slows down the **kitten arrival speed**.
*   **Economic Costs:** In extreme cases (like the Post Apocalypse challenge), trade costs for gold and catpower increase, and Catnip Fields may even begin to cost Unobtainium.

### **Mitigation and Management**
While some pollution is considered inevitable, it can be managed through the following methods:
*   **Environmental Policies:** 
    *   [Environmentalism]( {{< relref "Policy/Environmentalism.md" >}}), [Sustainability]( {{< relref "Policy/Sustainability.md" >}}), and [Conservation]( {{< relref "Policy/Conservation.md" >}}) each provide a **-5% reduction** to global pollution.
    *   **Lizard Relations: Ecologists** can be enacted to further reduce pollution by 5%.
*   [Carbon Sequestration:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Carbon_Sequestration.md" >}}) This high-tier Workshop upgrade (unlocked via Ecology) allows your **Factories** to capture pollution instead of producing it. When enabled, each Factory consumes 4 Watts of energy but reduces pollution by **2 points per tick**.
*   **Clean Energy:** Utilizing environmentally friendly energy sources helps mitigate the need for polluting structures.
*   **Operational Control:** You can simply **turn off polluting buildings** to halt their contribution to CO2 levels.

For players who want to see the specific impact, typing `game.detailedPollutionInfo=true` into the browser console will display more detailed pollution numbers on the Science tab.

### Building Impact on Pollution
Pollution per Tick for

* Calciner: 1
* Mine: 0.08
* Magneto: 5
* Factory: -2 if automation is enabled
* OilWell : 1 if automation is enabled, else 0
* Quarry : 0.25
* Smelter: 0.15
* Steamworks: 1
