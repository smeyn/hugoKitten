The late game begins once standard resets are effortless and you transition into deep temporal manipulation, relic economies, and cryptotheology. Instead of measuring progress in kittens or ground resources, your civilization runs on Time Crystals (TC), Void, Antimatter, and Relics, powered by thousands of years skipped per session.

### The Core Late-Game Engines
1. The Resource Retrieval (RR) Shatter Loop

 Combusting Time Crystals advances the calendar instantly. At base, time skips yield zero production, but Resource Retrieval extracts a percentage of full annual production per shattered TC ($1\% \text{ per level}$ ):
* __Break-Even Point__: Around RR Level 20–30, shattering a single TC produces enough Unobtainium to trade with Leviathans for more than 1 replacement TC.
* __Hyper-Scaling__: At RR 100+, every click dumps multiple years of gross civilization output straight into your stockpiles, instantly maxing out Unobtainium, Alloy, Faith, and Oil.
2. Heat Management & Blazars

Shattering TCs builds up Heat in the Chronoforge:
* **Chrono Heat Sinks**: Increase maximum heat capacity and cooling rates so you can combust hundreds of crystals in rapid bursts.
* [Blazars]( {{< relref "transcendence/Blazar.md" >}}): Accelerate resource yields per year skipped and improve heat dissipation, keeping the combustion engine sustainable.
3. Cryptotheology (Reset-Proof Compounding)

Relics purchased through Leviathan trades or generated passively via Black Pyramids are invested into [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}) under the Religion tab. Unlike standard bonfire structures, these upgrades persist forever across resets:
* [Black Nexus]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Nexus.md" >}}): Multiplies Relic and TC yields from Leviathan trade fleets.
* [Event Horizon]( {{< relref "transcendence/Event_horizon.md" >}}): Exponentially scales global storage caps.
* [Black Obelisk]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Obelisk.md" >}}): Elevates the maximum ceiling of your Solar Revolution multiplier.
* [Black Core]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Core.md" >}}) & [Black Library]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Library.md" >}}): Massively expand Science capacity, eliminating compendium grinding.
4. The Void & Antimatter Economy

* [Void Rifts]( {{< relref "time/void-rift.md" >}}): Consumes Void and Antimatter in Space to grant compounding percentage bonuses to global storage caps, enabling multi-million Unobtainium requirements.
* [Sunlifters]( {{< relref "space/sunlifter.md" >}}) & **Containment**: Harvest Antimatter during year-skips to fund [Void Rifts]( {{< relref "time/void-rift.md" >}}), [Dark Novas]( {{< relref "transcendence/Dark_Nova.md" >}}), and [AI Core]( {{< relref "buildings/AI_Core.md" >}}) management.
### The Late-Game Reset Routine: Chronospheres & Cryochambers
Late-game runs often last for tens of thousands of calendar years, but when you do reset:
* [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}}): Carry forward fixed percentages of your current resource stockpiles into the new run.
* [Cryochambers]( {{< relref "time/cryochamber.md" >}}) (Stasis): Preserve kitten ranks and leaders, letting you start Run $N+1$ with hundreds of seasoned kittens instantly rather than breeding them from zero.
* [Apocrypha]( {{< relref "religion/Apocripha.md" >}}) Bank: Massive cumulative faith earned during thousands of skipped years is sacrificed into Apocrypha, compounding your Solar Revolution multiplier into six- and seven-figure percentages.
