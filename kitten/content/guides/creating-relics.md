### **Phase 1: Gathering Your First Relics (The "Type 0" Relic Engine)**
Before you can automate Relics, you have to obtain your first few manually through Leviathan trading. 

1. **Climb the Unicorn Tree:** Sacrifice Unicorns in the Religion tab to get Unicorn Tears. Accumulate 10,000 tears and click "Refine Tears" to create 1 **Sorrow (Black Liquid Sorrow / BLS)**.
2. **Build Black Pyramids:** Purchase the [Megalomania]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Megalomania.md" >}}) upgrade in the Metaphysics tab (costs 10 Paragon) to unlock Ziggurat space-religion structures. Once you have refined 5 BLS, construct your first [Black Pyramid]( {{< relref "ziggurat/Black_Pyramid.md" >}}).
3. **Trade with the Elders:** Once you own a Black Pyramid, the **Leviathans (Elders)** will begin randomly appearing in your Trade tab. Trade 5,000 Unobtainium to them; each trade has a 98% chance to give you Time Crystals and a **5% chance to award a Relic**.
4. **Acquire 6 Relics:** Trade until you have secured 6 Relics. You will need 5 Relics to research [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}) and 1 Relic to research [Tachyon Theory]( {{< relref "techs/Tachyon_Theory.md" >}}) on your Science tree.

---

### **Phase 2: Transitioning to Space and Antimatter**
Now you need to establish the infrastructure required to store and generate Antimatter, which acts as the power source for automated Relics.

1. **Research Technologies:** Research [Cryptotheology]( {{< relref "techs/Cryptotheology.md" >}}) (costs 650k Science and 5 Relics) to unlock the Cryptotheology tab and enable the Relic Station upgrade. Research [Tachyon Theory]( {{< relref "techs/Tachyon_Theory.md" >}}) (costs 750k Science, 25 Time Crystals, and 1 Relic) to unlock the Chronoforge.
2. **Generate Antimatter:** Build [Sunlifters]( {{< relref "space/sunlifter.md" >}}) in Helios. If your overall energy grid has a positive surplus, Sunlifters will automatically generate 1 Antimatter every time the game transitions to a new calendar year. You can speed this up by shattering (combusting) Time Crystals to skip years instantly.
3. **Expand Antimatter Storage:** Construct **Containment Chambers** (and Heatsinks) in Helios to expand your maximum Antimatter storage capacity to **at least 5,000**. 
   * *Note: Upgrading Space Beacons into Relic Stations requires a 5,000 Antimatter cap. If your maximum Antimatter cap falls below 5,000, your automated relic production is penalized by 0.02% for every missing unit.*

---

### **Phase 3: Automated Production (The "Type 1" Relic Engine)**
At this stage, you transition away from trading for Relics and set up an automated production engine.

1. **Unlock Relic Stations:** Once your Antimatter cap is 5,000 and you have 100 Eludium, purchase the [Relic Station]( {{< relref "upgrade/Relic_Station.md" >}}) upgrade in your Workshop. This upgrades your **Space Beacons** to automatically generate Relics.
2. **Deploy Space Beacons:** Build and max out your **Space Beacons** in Kairo. Each Beacon will now automatically generate **0.01 relics per day**.
3. **Amplify Your Output:** Boost your daily Relic production with Cryptotheology upgrades:
   * [Black Nexus:]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Nexus.md" >}}) Costs 5,000 Relics. Each Black Nexus you buy increases your Relic Station output by **+10% per Black Pyramid** you own. It also increases the yield when you manually refine Time Crystals into Relics.
   * [Black Cores:]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Core.md" >}}) Costs 10,000 Relics. These increase your maximum BLS capacity, allowing you to hoard more Sorrow and build more Black Pyramids to multiply your Black Nexus bonus.

---

### **Phase 4: Quantum Hash Scaling and Blackchain**
The final stretch uses quantum computing to boost your Relic Stations even further and reach the 4,096 threshold.

1. **Research Quantum Cryptography:** Once you accumulate 1,024 Relics, research [Quantum Cryptography]( {{< relref "techs/Quantum_Cryptography.md" >}}) (costs 1.25M Science and 1,024 Relics). This unlocks **Entanglement Stations** and [AI Cores]( {{< relref "buildings/AI_Core.md" >}}).
2. **Farm Hashrate Levels:** AI Cores generate Gigaflops, and Entanglement Stations consume Gigaflops to produce **Hashes**. Each Hashrate Level provides a multiplicative **+25% bonus to your automated Space Beacon relic production**.
3. **Unlock Blackchain:** Accumulate Relics from your boosted Space Beacons (and time-skipping) until you have **4,096 Relics** and 5,000,000 Science. Research [Blackchain]( {{< relref "techs/Blackchain.md" >}}) to permanently unlock the [Blackcoin]( {{< relref "resource/black_coin.md" >}}) market.
