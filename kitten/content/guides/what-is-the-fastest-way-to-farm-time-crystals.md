The absolute fastest way to farm Time Crystals in the late game is through **mass trading Unobtainium with the Leviathans**, which is roughly 30 times faster than waiting for Alicorns to sacrifice. 

To achieve this, you need to maximize your Unobtainium production and your trade efficiency, eventually creating a self-sustaining **"Shatter Engine"**. Here is the step-by-step strategy:

**1. Maximize Unobtainium Production**
Since each trade with the Leviathans costs 5,000 Unobtainium, your base production must be astronomically high:
*   **Space Structures:** Max out Lunar Outposts, Space Elevators, Orbital Arrays, and Factories.
*   **Upgrades and Policies:** Research the *Factory Logistics* workshop upgrade, and enact the *Full Industrialization*, *Cosmological Libertarianism*, and *Necrocracy* policies.
*   [Festivals:]( {{< relref "guides/festivals.md" >}}) Maximize Breweries and ensure festivals are running non-stop.
*   **The Redmoon Cycle:** With the *Numerology* and *Numeromancy* metaphysics upgrades, the Redmoon cycle grants a massive multiplier to Unobtainium production. Use Time Crystals to skip right to the Redmoon cycle and spend the majority of your time there.

**2. Maximize Time Crystals per Trade**
You want to ensure that every 5,000 Unobtainium traded yields as many Time Crystals as possible:
*   **Leviathan Energy:** Feed Necrocorns to the Leviathans (Elders) whenever possible. Each point of energy increases the resources they give you by 2%.
*   [Tradeposts:]( {{< relref "buildings/Tradepost.md" >}}) Max out your Tradeposts, as each one adds a +1.5% bonus to your trades.
*   **Leader:** Assign a **Merchant** leader, which significantly boosts trade efficiency, especially if you have a lot of burned paragon.

**3. Build a "Shatter Engine"**
Once your base Unobtainium production reaches hundreds per second, you can transition to a Shatter Engine:
*   Purchase levels of [Resource Retrieval]( {{< relref "upgrade/resource-retrieval.md" >}}) (1,000 TCs each). Each level instantly grants you 1% of your yearly resource production every time you shatter a Time Crystal to skip a year.
*   Combine this with [Chronofurnaces]( {{< relref "time/chrono-furnace.md" >}}) (to manage the heat generated from shattering time) and [Blazars]( {{< relref "transcendence/Blazar.md" >}}) (to improve Resource Retrieval effectiveness).
*   **The Infinite Loop:** With roughly 7 levels of Resource Retrieval and high base Unobtainium production, shattering a single Time Crystal will instantly yield enough Unobtainium to trade back to the Leviathans for *multiple* Time Crystals. 

Once this break-even point is reached, you can rapidly shatter crystals, trade the retrieved Unobtainium, and farm millions of Time Crystals without ever running out.
