Several buildings and structures increase your maximum science capacity, ranging from early-game terrestrial buildings to late-game space structures. 

### **Standard Terrestrial Buildings**
*   **Libraries:** The first science building, providing **+250 Max Science**.
*   **Academies:** Provides **+500 Max Science**.
*   **Observatories:** Provides **+1,000 Max Science**.
*   [Bio Labs:]( {{< relref "buildings/Bio_Lab.md" >}}) Provides **+1,500 Max Science**.
*   [Data Centers:]( {{< relref "buildings/Data_Center.md" >}}) An upgraded version of the Library that provides **+750 Max Science**. 

### **Buildings Requiring Specific Upgrades**
*   [Temples:]( {{< relref "buildings/Temple.md" >}}) Initially provide no science, but after purchasing the [Scholasticism]( {{< relref "religion/Scholasticism.md" >}}) religion upgrade, each Temple provides **+500 Max Science**. Each additional level of Scholasticism purchased via Transcendence increases this by an extra **+100**.
*   [Accelerators:]( {{< relref "buildings/Accelerator.md" >}}) Normally used for uranium, but once the [LHC]( {{< relref "upgrade/LHC.md" >}}) (Large Hadron Collider) workshop upgrade is researched, each Accelerator provides **+2,500 Max Science**.

### **Space Structures**
*   [Research Vessels:]( {{< relref "space/research-vessel.md" >}}) Found on the Piscine mission, each provides **+10,000 Max Science**.
*   [Space Beacons:]( {{< relref "space/space-beacon.md" >}}) Found on the Kairo mission, each provides a massive **+25,000 Max Science**.

### **Craftable Resources**
*   [Compendiums:]( {{< relref "resource/Compendium.md" >}}) While not a building, crafting this resource is vital for science storage. Each Compendium adds **+10 to your Max Science**. This bonus is capped at 100% of your maximum science from buildings (or 1000% if playing in Iron Will mode).

### **Important Scaling Upgrades**
Your base maximum science can be multiplied and scaled through several Workshop and Space upgrades:
*   [Astrolabe:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Astrolabe.md" >}}) Increases the capacity of Observatories by 50% (to +1,500).
*   [Satellites:]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) Each active satellite in space increases the science cap of your Observatories by 5%.
*   **Reflectors (Titanium, Unobtainium, Eludium):** These upgrades allow your Observatories to boost the effectiveness of your Libraries and Data Centers.
*   **Uplink & Starlink:** These upgrades create a synergy where your Bio Labs and Data Centers provide percentage-based max science boosts to each other.
*   **Antimatter & Void Reactors:** These endgame upgrades massively multiply the science capacity of your Space Beacons and Research Vessels by up to **+400%**.
