The ten time cycles in Kittens Game, which are named after space missions, each have a unique astronomical symbol or glyph that appears to the left of your current year. 

Here are the glyphs for each of the cycles:

1.  [Charon]( {{< relref "planet/Charon.md" >}}): **⍙**
2.  [Umbra]( {{< relref "planet/Umbra.md" >}}): **⍦**
3.  [Yarn]( {{< relref "planet/Yarn.md" >}}): **⍧**
4.  [Helios]( {{< relref "planet/Helios.md" >}}): **⌒**
5.  [Cath]( {{< relref "planet/Cath.md" >}}): **⌾**
6.  [Redmoon]( {{< relref "planet/Redmoon.md" >}}): **⍜**
7.  [Dune]( {{< relref "planet/Dune.md" >}}): **⍫**
8.  [Piscine]( {{< relref "planet/Piscine.md" >}}): **⎈**
9.  **Terminus** (T-minus): **⍝**
10. [Kairo]( {{< relref "planet/Kairo.md" >}}): **℣**

### **Understanding the Glyph Colors**
Each cycle lasts for **five years**. The active glyph will dynamically change color to let you know exactly which year of the current cycle you are in:
*   🔴 **Red**: Year 0
*   🟡 **Orange-Yellow**: Year 1
*   🟢 **Green**: Year 2
*   🔵 **Blue**: Year 3
*   🟣 **Violet**: Year 4

Once you research the **Numerology** and [Numeromancy]( {{< relref "metaphysics/numeromancy.md" >}}) metaphysics upgrades, these cycles will actively alter your space structures' production rates and grant powerful, unique bonuses whenever you hold festivals.
