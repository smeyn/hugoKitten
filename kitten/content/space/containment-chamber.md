In **Kittens Game**, the [Containment Chamber]( {{< relref "space/containment-chamber.md" >}}) is an essential mid-to-late game Space structure located under the [Helios]( {{< relref "planet/Helios.md" >}}) mission tab. It is the primary means of scaling your maximum capacity for [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}).

### **Costs and Scaling**
*   **Base Cost:** **500,000 Science** and **2,500 Kerosene**.
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.125**.
*   **The Space Penalty:** Because it is a Space structure, Containment Chambers **do not** benefit from Bonfire metaphysics price-reduction upgrades (such as Golden Ratio or Renaissance). 

### **Core Effects & The Power Problem**
*   **Antimatter Storage:** Each built chamber increases your maximum antimatter capacity by **+100**.
*   **Energy Consumption:** Each active chamber consumes a massive **50 Watts (Wt)** of power.

Because of this steep energy requirement, Containment Chambers quickly become a massive strain on your active power grid. If your power grid falls into a deficit (negative energy), your [Sunlifters]( {{< relref "space/sunlifter.md" >}}) will completely stop generating antimatter. Managing your energy surplus with clean power sources like Sunlifters (generating 30 Wt each) or Centaurus Tectonics (generating 25 Wt each) is crucial to keeping these chambers running.

### **Synergy with Heatsinks**
Building [Heatsinks]( {{< relref "space/heatsink.md" >}}) (also under Helios) directly enhances your existing chambers:
*   Each Heatsink increases the antimatter storage capacity of your Containment Chambers by **+2%**.
*   However, each Heatsink also increases their energy consumption by **+1%**.

### **Strategic Value: Reaching the 5,000 Antimatter Cap**
The ultimate goal of building Containment Chambers is to raise your maximum antimatter capacity to **5,000**. This threshold is critical for the automated endgame Relic Engine:
1.  **Unlocking Relic Stations:** You must have a maximum capacity of at least 5,000 antimatter to buy the [Relic Station]( {{< relref "upgrade/Relic_Station.md" >}}) upgrade in your Workshop.
2.  **Avoiding the Relic Penalty:** If your maximum storage capacity is below 5,000, your relic station production is penalized by **-0.02% for every single unit** of capacity you are short of that threshold.

To efficiently hit this 5,000 cap, you must balance the Science cost of your chambers against the Thorium and Relic costs of Heatsinks. The most common and recommended configuration is to build **29 Containment Chambers** and **34 Heatsinks** (which requires 13.5M Science and 526k Thorium). While your science cap may seem like a barrier to building this many chambers, it can be easily expanded by compiling massive stacks of [Compendiums]( {{< relref "resource/Compendium.md" >}}).
