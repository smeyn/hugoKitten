Similar to the Moon Base, the [Space Station]( {{< relref "space/space-station.md" >}}) (or Deploy Space Station) is a critical orbital structure designed to scale your housing and science limits beyond Cath-bound buildings. It is located under the [Cath]( {{< relref "planet/Cath.md" >}}) section of your Space tab.

### **1. Unlocking & Costs**
To deploy a Space Station, you must first complete the [Orbital Launch]( {{< relref "space/Orbital_Launch.md" >}}) space mission (unlocked by Rocketry) and research the [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) technology. 

Constructing each station requires:
*   **425 Starcharts**
*   **750 Alloy**
*   **150,000 Science**
*   **35,000 Oil**
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.12** (and **1.05** for Oil). Because it is a Space structure, it does not benefit from Bonfire metaphysics price reductions.

---

### **2. Core Effects & Energy Needs**
*   **Maximum Kittens: +2**
*   **Science Bonus: +50%**
*   **Energy Consumption: 10 Watts (Wt)**

---

### **3. Critical Mechanics: Deficit vs. Manual Disabling**
Because Space Stations house active citizens, managing their power profile requires careful attention to avoid catastrophic happiness drops:

*   **The Energy Deficit Safety Net:** If your global energy grid slips into a deficit (negative net energy), your Space Stations will experience a penalty to their Science production bonus. However, the game protects your population—the stations' non-productive aspects remain fully functional, meaning **they will continue to safely house and support your 2 kittens** even if you have 0 power.
*   **The Danger of Manual Disabling:** If you **manually toggle off** or disable a Space Station to free up 10 Wt of electricity, **you will instantly lose its housing capacity**. This forces your population over the limit, triggering a massive unhappiness penalty of **-2% happiness per homeless kitten**. This severe dip can cripple your global production multipliers.

---

### **4. Planetary Cycle Modifiers (With Numerology)**
If you have researched **Numerology**, the active planetary cycle will directly scale the efficiency of your Space Stations' scientific capabilities:
*   **Cath Cycle (⌾):** Temporarily boosts the Space Station science bonus by **+50%**.
*   **Kairo Cycle (℣):** Temporarily penalises the Space Station science bonus by **-25%**.
