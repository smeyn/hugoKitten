In *Kittens Game*, a [Heatsink]( {{< relref "space/heatsink.md" >}}) is an advanced Space structure located in the [Helios]( {{< relref "planet/Helios.md" >}}) section of your Space tab. It is officially unlocked once you complete the [Helios Mission]( {{< relref "space/Helios_Mission.md" >}}), which costs 3,000 Starcharts, 15,000 Titanium, 250,000 Science, and 1,250 Kerosene.

---

### **1. Construction Costs & Scaling**
*   **Base Cost:** **125,000 Science**, **12,500 Thorium**, **1 Relic**, and **5,000 Kerosene**.
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.12**. Like other Space structures, it does not benefit from Bonfire metaphysics price reductions, meaning every subsequent Heatsink will strictly cost 12% more than the last.

---

### **2. Effects & Mechanics**
Heatsinks do not store antimatter on their own; instead, they enhance your existing [Containment Chambers]( {{< relref "space/containment-chamber.md" >}}):
*   **Antimatter Storage Capacity (+2% per Heatsink):** This percentage boost is applied to your Containment Chambers' base storage (each base chamber holds 100 antimatter).
*   **Containment Chamber Energy Consumption (+1% per Heatsink):** Each level increases the power consumption of your active Containment Chambers, which normally require a hefty 50 Watts (Wt) of power each.

Additionally, Heatsinks are highly useful in the late-game for increasing your **Chronoforge heat cooldown**, allowing you to dissipate heat faster when performing mass time-skips.

---

### **3. Strategic Importance: Smashing the 5,000 Antimatter Wall**
The primary purpose of mass-producing Heatsinks in the late game is to help expand your **maximum Antimatter capacity to 5,000**. This threshold is crucial for two reasons:

1.  **Unlocking Relic Stations:** You must have a maximum capacity of at least 5,000 antimatter to purchase the [Relic Station]( {{< relref "upgrade/Relic_Station.md" >}}) upgrade in your Workshop (which officially kicks off your automated endgame Relic engine).
2.  **Avoiding the Relic Penalty:** If your maximum antimatter storage cap is **below 5,000**, your relic station production is penalized by **-0.02% for every single unit** of capacity you are short of that threshold.

#### **Optimal Setup Configurations**
To reach the 5,000 antimatter storage limit, players must balance the high Science cost of Containment Chambers (CCs) against the high Thorium and Relic cost of Heatsinks. For example, in an Iron Will endless run, two common efficient configurations to reach the 5k cap are:
*   **34 Heatsinks** (costing roughly 526,000 Thorium in total) and **29 Containment Chambers** (costing 13.5M Science).
*   **37 Heatsinks and 28 Containment Chambers**, though the Thorium costs for this alternative path are often considered too prohibitive.

---

### **4. The Energy Conundrum**
Because Heatsinks increase the power draw of your Containment Chambers, expanding your storage cap this way will put an immense strain on your active energy grid. You must ensure you have highly built-up energy sources—such as [Sunlifters]( {{< relref "space/sunlifter.md" >}}) on Helios (which generate 30 Wt) or [Tectonic]( {{< relref "space/tectonic.md" >}}) structures on Centaurus—to keep your grid positive. If your active power grid slips into a deficit, your Sunlifters **will not produce any antimatter**.
