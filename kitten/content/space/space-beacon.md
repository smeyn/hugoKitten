A [Space Beacon]( {{< relref "space/space-beacon.md" >}}) is an advanced, orbital structure constructed on the dwarf planet [Kairo]( {{< relref "planet/Kairo.md" >}}). It plays a major role in the late game, serving as a massive booster for your maximum Science cap, a passive source of Starcharts, and most importantly, the hardware foundation for your automated endgame **Relic Engine**.

---

### **1. Cost and Scaling**
*   **Location:** Found in the Space tab under the [Kairo]( {{< relref "planet/Kairo.md" >}}) section.
*   **Base Cost:** 
    *   **25,000 Starcharts**
    *   **50 Antimatter**
    *   **25,000 Alloy**
    *   **7,500 Kerosene**
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.15**. Like all Space structures, Space Beacons **do not** benefit from Bonfire-tab price ratio reductions (such as Golden Ratio or Renaissance). Each subsequent beacon will strictly cost 15% more than the previous one.

---

### **2. Core Passive Effects**
Each active Space Beacon provides:
*   **Max Science +25,000:** This huge boost helps you push through massive science caps to reach late-game exophysics and exometallurgy milestones.
*   **Starchart Production (+0.125/sec):** Provides a steady, passive income of Starcharts.

---

### **3. The Relic Station Upgrade (Type 1 Relic Engine)**
The primary goal of building Space Beacons in the late game is to host [Relic Stations]( {{< relref "upgrade/Relic_Station.md" >}}) (a Workshop upgrade costing 100 Eludium and 5,000 Antimatter). Once researched, your Space Beacons will automatically reverse engineer and **generate 0.01 Relics per day**.

This passive relic generation can be scaled exponentially through several synergistic mechanics:
*   **Black Nexus Synergy:** Each level of Black Nexus boosts your Space Beacon relic production by **+10% per active Black Pyramid**.
*   **Hashes/Hashrate Synergy:** By running Entanglement Stations, you generate Hashes to gain hashrate levels. Each level grants a multiplicative **+25% bonus** to Space Beacon relic production.
*   **The Antimatter Storage Requirement:** Relic production scales dynamically with your maximum antimatter capacity. If your maximum antimatter storage cap is **below 5,000**, your relic production is penalized by **-0.02% for every single unit** you are short of that threshold. 

---

### **4. Science Cap Multipliers**
You can dramatically boost the +25k Science storage bonus of your Space Beacons by purchasing specialized Workshop upgrades:
*   [Antimatter Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Antimatter_Reactors.md" >}}) Boosts maximum Science from Research Vessels and Space Beacons by **+95%**.
*   [Advanced AM Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Advanced_AM_Reactors.md" >}}) Upgrades this bonus to **+150%**.
*   [Void Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Void_Reactors.md" >}}) Upgrades this bonus to a massive **+400%**.

---

### **5. Black Sky Challenge**
Space Beacons are also the focal point of the **Black Sky** challenge (where astronomical events are entirely disabled). To successfully complete this challenge, your ultimate goal is to build at least **one Space Beacon** (with the requirement increasing by +1 beacon for each subsequent completion of the challenge).
