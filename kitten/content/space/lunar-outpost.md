A [Lunar Outpost]( {{< relref "planet/lunar_outpost.md" >}}) is an orbital structure located on [Redmoon]( {{< relref "planet/Redmoon.md" >}}) (accessible after completing the Moon Mission). It serves as your primary gateway to the late-game economy, acting as the baseline source for gathering [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}).

### **1. Construction Costs & Price Ratio**
*   [Starcharts:]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}) 650
*   [Uranium:]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}}) 500
*   [Alloy:]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) 750
*   [Concrete:]( {{< relref "craft/Concrete.md" >}}) 150
*   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 100k
*   [Oil:]( {{< relref "resource/oil.md" >}}) 55k
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.12** (and **1.05** for Oil). Like all Space structures, Lunar Outposts **do not** benefit from Bonfire tab price reductions (such as Golden Ratio or Renaissance). 

---

### **2. Core Effects & Consumption**
Each active Lunar Outpost operates with the following rates:
*   **Unobtainium Production:** **+0.035 / second**
*   **Uranium Consumption:** **-1.75 / second**
*   **Energy Consumption:** **5 Watts (Wt)** of power

If you do not have enough active Uranium to support the outpost, it will still run slowly (at a rate bottlenecked by your incoming Uranium) rather than shutting down entirely.

---

### **3. Scaling and Production Boosts**
You can dramatically increase your Unobtainium yield per outpost through several synergistic mechanisms:
*   **Microwarp Reactors (Workshop Upgrade):** Costs 50 Eludium. Once researched, it boosts the Unobtainium production of all Lunar Outposts by **+75%**.
*   **Space Manufacturing (Workshop Upgrade):** Allows your Factories to amplify the effects of your [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) (+1% space structure bonus each) and [Orbital Arrays]( {{< relref "space/orbital_array.md" >}}) (+2% bonus each). This compounding space production multiplier is the main way to skyrocket your overall Unobtainium yield.
*   **Cycle Modifiers (with Numerology):**
    *   **Charon Cycle (⍙):** Temporarily reduces Unobtainium production by **-10%**.
    *   **Redmoon Cycle (⍜):** Temporarily boosts Unobtainium production by **+20%**. 
    *   *Strategic Tip:* If you have both **Numerology** and [Numeromancy]( {{< relref "metaphysics/numeromancy.md" >}}) researched and run a Festival during the Redmoon Cycle, your Unobtainium production receives a massive **~2.5x global multiplier**. Many players use the Chronoforge to skip seasons and stay inside the Redmoon cycle indefinitely to farm Unobtainium.

---

### **4. Overcoming the Uranium Deficit**
The primary bottleneck when starting up Lunar Outposts is their heavy Uranium consumption. Because one outpost consumes Uranium 140 times faster than a single standard Accelerator can produce it (+0.0125/sec), you will quickly deplete your reserves. To counteract this:
1.  **Trade with Dragons:** Continually trade your Titanium with Dragons to purchase massive amounts of Uranium to feed your outposts.
2.  [Planet Crackers:]( {{< relref "space/planet_cracker.md" >}}) Build Planet Crackers on Dune (which produce +1.5/sec Uranium) to actively balance out the drain.
3.  **Redshift (Offline progression):** If offline progression is enabled in your settings, the game prevents your resources from dropping to zero during offline calculation. Consequently, your Lunar Outposts will run as if they have infinite Uranium, allowing you to generate Unobtainium at maximum speed while offline.
