The [Sunforge]( {{< relref "space/Sun_Forge.md" >}}) is a late-game space structure located on the star [Helios]( {{< relref "planet/Helios.md" >}}) that provides a global multiplier to your civilization's metal storage capacity.

### **Acquisition and Building Requirements**
*   **Mission Unlock:** You must first complete the [Helios Mission]( {{< relref "space/Helios_Mission.md" >}}), which costs **3,000 starcharts, 15,000 titanium, 250,000 science, and 1,250 kerosene**.
*   **Construction Costs:** Building a Sunforge requires the following resources:
    *   [Science:]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) 100,000
    *   [Relic:]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) 1
    *   [Kerosene:]( {{< relref "resource/kerosene.md" >}}) 1,250
    *   [Antimatter:]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) 250
*   **Price Ratio:** 1.12.

### **Core Effects**
The Sunforge provides a **+1% bonus to the storage capacity of all base metals**. This storage boost specifically applies to:
*   [Iron]( {{< relref "resource/iron.md" >}})
*   [Titanium]( {{< relref "resource/titanium.md" >}})
*   [Gold]( {{< relref "resource/Gold.md" >}})
*   [Uranium]( {{< relref "resource/uranium.md" >}})
*   [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}})

### **Strategic Importance**
*   **Late-Game Scaling:** While a 1% bonus per building may seem small, Sunforges are critical in the late game for breaking through resource caps that terrestrial buildings (like Barns or Warehouses) can no longer effectively expand.
*   **Unobtainium Benchmarks:** Sunforges are a key component in reaching the massive **8.14 billion unobtainium capacity** required to build **100 Chronospheres**. This capacity is necessary to establish a "positive reset" loop, where you start each new run with more resources than you spent on Chronospheres in the previous one.
*   **Endgame Estimates:** Advanced strategy guides suggest that during a deep endgame run, players should aim for approximately **100 Sunforges** to provide a massive cumulative boost to their storage limits.
