### **What is Molten Core?**
[Molten Core]( {{< relref "space/molten-core.md" >}}) is an advanced Space structure located in the [Centaurus System]( {{< relref "planet/Centaurus_System.md" >}}) (unlocked via the Centaurus Mission). It serves as a power-amplification mechanism for your interstellar energy grid.

*   **Prerequisite Technology:** [Exophysics]( {{< relref "techs/Exophysics.md" >}}) (which costs 25M Science and 500 Relics to research).
*   **The Effect:** Every level of Molten Core purchased permanently improves the energy production of your Centaurus [Tectonic]( {{< relref "space/tectonic.md" >}}) structures by **+5%**. Since a base Tectonic structure generates 25 Wt of energy, Molten Cores help you squeeze massive amounts of additional power out of your existing setup.

---

### **Cost and Scaling**
*   **Base Cost (Level 1):** **25M Science** and **5M Uranium**.
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.25**.
*   **The Space Structure Penalty:** Unlike your Bonfire tab buildings, **Space structures do not benefit from Metaphysics price ratio reductions** (such as Golden Ratio or Renaissance). The price ratio remains a flat, unforgiving **1.25 multiplier** for every single level you buy. 
*   **The Scaling Problem:** Because of this aggressive scaling, the Science and Uranium costs quickly balloon. For example, by the time you want to purchase Level 11, the cost will have soared to over **230M Science**, along with a massive Uranium requirement.

---

### **How to Overcome the Science and Uranium Bottlenecks**
Many players hit a massive wall when trying to purchase multiple levels of Molten Core. To reach the extreme science and uranium caps needed, you must stack several multiplicative systems:

#### **1. Smashing the Uranium Cap (The True Bottleneck)**
Uranium is generally considered the hardest limit to expand for Molten Core. You can expand your maximum Uranium storage using these strategies:
*   **Expand Base Storage:** Max out [Reactors]( {{< relref "buildings/Reactor.md" >}}) (+250 max Uranium each) and [Cryostations]( {{< relref "space/cryo_station.md" >}}) on T-Minus (+5,000 max Uranium each).
*   **Compile Paragon:** Paragon provides a passive **+0.1% storage bonus per unit** (and Burned Paragon is twice as effective after you reach the Dark Future).
*   **Stack Storage Modifiers:** 
    *   Build [Sunforges]( {{< relref "space/Sun_Forge.md" >}}) in Helios (+1% metal and Uranium storage per level).
    *   Buy **Event Horizons** (+10% global storage ratio per level) and [Void Rifts]( {{< relref "time/void-rift.md" >}}) (+2% global storage ratio per level).
*   **Time skip to the T-Minus Cycle:** The T-Minus planetary cycle provides a temporary **+20% storage boost specifically to Cryostations**.

#### **2. Scaling your Science Cap**
Unlocking a 250M+ Science cap is highly achievable if you focus on your library and computer networks:
*   **Craft Compendiums:** Every Compendium you hold in stock adds a flat **+10 to your maximum Science cap** (up to 100% of your building-based storage). Buy **Codex Levi** and **Black Libraries** t your Libraries:** Convert Libraries into **Data Centers** and purchase the **Uplink** upgrade (+1% max science per Bio Lab) and **Machine Learning** upgrade (+10% effectiveness per AI Core).
*   **Max out Observatories:** Upgrading them with [Astrolabe]( {{< relref "upgrade/Astrolabe.md" >}}) (+50% storage), plus **Titanium, Unobtainium, and Eludium Reflectors**, will add +2% Library/Data Center effectiveness per Observatory.
*   **Boost Space Science Structures:** Construct [Research Vessels]( {{< relref "space/research-vessel.md" >}}) (+10k base Science) and [Space Beacons]( {{< relref "space/space-beacon.md" >}}) (+25k base Science). Maximize them using [Antimatter Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Antimatter_Reactors.md" >}}) (+95% science), [Advanced AM Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Advanced_AM_Reactors.md" >}}) (+150%), and [Void Reactors]( {{< relref "upgrade/Void_Reactors.md" >}}) (+400% science).

### **Is Molten Core Worth It?**
While Molten Core is highly beneficial for boosting Tectonic energy, it is generally considered a late-endgame luxury. By the time you can comfortably afford high levels of Molten Core, you will likely have established a high-level **Shatter Engine**. In the very late game, skipping thousands of years will make your **Hawking Radiation (HR) Harvesters** on Umbra (which scale based on the Dark Future years) produce far more energy than Centaurus Tectonics, reducing your dependency on Molten Cores.
