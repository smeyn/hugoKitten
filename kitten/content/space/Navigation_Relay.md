Part of the locked Space Exploration Tee.
 

In **Kittens Game**, the [Navigation Relay]( {{< relref "space/Navigation_Relay.md" >}}) is a mid-to-late game **Workshop upgrade** that significantly enhances your space-based logistics. It is designed to work in tandem with your [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) to make space exploration and resource management much more efficient.

### Key Effects
The Navigation Relay serves as a force multiplier for your orbital network:

* **Satellite Efficiency:** It provides a **+15% bonus** to the effectiveness of your [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}). 
* **Starchart Generation:** Because it boosts Satellite efficiency, it indirectly increases your **Starchart production**. This is crucial for launching the "Long Range Spaceships" and outer-system missions like [Jupiter]( {{< relref "space/jupiter.md" >}}) and **Saturn** that we discussed earlier.
* **Energy Management:** By making your existing Satellites more effective, you can often generate the starcharts you need without having to build as many individual units, saving you on the scaling [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) and [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) costs.

### Unlock Requirements & Cost
To see the Navigation Relay in your Workshop, you must have advanced into the **Space** era:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Satellite** | Space Building | You must have at least one **Satellite** deployed. |
| [Orbital Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Orbital_Engineering.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary scientific prerequisite. |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **150,000 Science** |
| [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,500 Starcharts** |
| [Alloy]( {{< relref "craft/Alloy.md" >}}) | Resource | **1,000 Alloy** |

### Strategic Context
Given your current focus on [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and managing **Temporal Distortion**, the Navigation Relay is a "maintenance" upgrade that ensures your foundation remains solid as you push into the Dark Future.

1.  **Scaling for the Farther Ring:** As missions to [Jupiter]( {{< relref "space/jupiter.md" >}}) and beyond require thousands of Starcharts, the 15% boost from the Navigation Relay (especially when stacked with the [Hubble Space Telescope]( {{< relref "upgrade/Hubble_Space_Telescope.md" >}})) is essential to prevent a "Starchart drought."
2.  **Antimatter Preparation:** To build **Containment Chambers** for Antimatter, you need massive Science caps. Since Satellites contribute to your Science cap via the [Satellite Radio]( {{< relref "upgrade/Satellite_Radio.md" >}}) upgrade, the Navigation Relay helps push those caps even higher.
3.  **Efficiency:** It is a relatively "cheap" upgrade compared to the cost of building 10 additional Satellites in the late game, making it a high-priority purchase after a reset.

> **Expert Tip:** If you have already started farming [Void]( {{< relref "resource/void.md" >}}), the Navigation Relay helps stabilize your Starchart income so you can keep your **Research Vessels** and **Space Beacons** active even when the timeline is experiencing Distortion.
