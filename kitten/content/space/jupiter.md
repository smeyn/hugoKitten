In **Kittens Game**, [Jupiter]( {{< relref "space/jupiter.md" >}}) is a pivotal location in the **Space tab** that marks your transition from basic lunar exploration to heavy industrialization. It is the primary gateway to the "Farther Ring" of the solar system.

### Key Buildings & Missions

* **Jupiter Mission:** Launching this mission unlocks the planet. It is relatively expensive in the mid-game, requiring a significant amount of [Starcharts]( {{< relref "resource/star_charts.md" >}}) and **Rocket Fuel**.
* **Saturn Mission:** Reaching Jupiter is a strict prerequisite for advancing to Saturn and the outer reaches of the system.
* **HE-Fuel (Hydro-Electric Fuel):** Jupiter allows you to start utilizing hydrogen-based resources more effectively, which is essential for scaling your [Rocketry]( {{< relref "techs/Rocketry.md" >}}) efforts.

---

### Strategic Importance

For a player at your stage—managing [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}})—Jupiter serves a few specialized roles:

1.  **Science Cap Scaling:** Jupiter-related missions and buildings are often the key to pushing your **Science Max** into the range required for [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) and [Relic]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}}) technologies.
2.  **Unobtainium Boost:** While the Moon is your first source, Jupiter (and its moons in certain versions/mods) provides the infrastructure needed to automate and accelerate your [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) grind.
3.  [Space Elevators:]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}) Improving your presence at Jupiter often complements your [Space Elevators]( {{< relref "space/space_elevator.md" >}}), reducing the friction of moving resources between the ground and your orbital stations.

### Managing the Starchart Bottleneck
If you are struggling to afford the Jupiter mission or its upgrades, remember the [Hubble Space Telescope]( {{< relref "upgrade/Hubble_Space_Telescope.md" >}}) upgrade we discussed. It will provide the 30% boost to your [Satellites]( {{< relref "techs/Satellites.md" >}}) and **Research Vessels** needed to generate the thousands of starcharts required for Jupiter operations.
