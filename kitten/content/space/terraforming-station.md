[Terraforming Stations]( {{< relref "space/terraforming-station.md" >}}) are highly advanced, late-game space structures located on the planet [Yarn]( {{< relref "planet/Yarn.md" >}}). They act as a specialized way to expand your maximum population limit from orbit.

Here is a complete breakdown of how to unlock, fund, and optimize these structures:

### **1. Unlocking & Costs**
Before you can build Terraforming Stations, you must travel to their home planet and research the necessary technology:
*   **The Mission:** Complete the [Yarn Mission]( {{< relref "space/Yarn_Mission.md" >}}) in your Space tab (costs 7,500 Starcharts, 35,000 Titanium, 350,000 Science, and 12,000 Kerosene).
*   **Prerequisite Tech:** Research the [Terraformation]( {{< relref "techs/Terraformation.md" >}}) technology, which costs 750,000 Science and 5 Relics.
*   **Base Cost:** 
    *   **25 Antimatter**
    *   **5,000 Uranium**
    *   **5,000 Kerosene**
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.25**. Because it is a Space structure, **Terraforming Stations do not benefit from Metaphysics price ratio reductions** (such as Golden Ratio or Renaissance). Each subsequent station will strictly cost 25% more than the last.

---

### **2. Core Effect & Lore**
*   **Max Kittens +1:** Every station you build permanently increases your maximum kitten capacity by 1. 
*   **Lore Description:** *"Explode a charge of antimatter to melt yarn ice and throw oxygen into the atmosphere."*

---

### **3. Endgame Synergies**
While a flat +1 kitten cap per station is modest given the high resource costs, you can amplify their housing capacity using late-game upgrades:
*   **Hydroponics Boost (+1.0% per structure):** Building [Hydroponics]( {{< relref "space/hydroponics.md" >}}) on Yarn (which costs 1 Unobtainium and 500 Kerosene) permanently increases the kitten housing bonus of your Terraforming Stations by **+1.0%** per Hydroponics structure. Compounding these two structures is one of the premier ways to squeeze extra population out of your space empire.
*   **Post Apocalypse Policy Reward (+0.1% bonus):** Completing the **Post Apocalypse challenge** unlocks a special late-game policy that awards a permanent **+0.1% housing bonus specifically to your Terraforming Stations**.
