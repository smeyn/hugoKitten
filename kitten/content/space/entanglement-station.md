Building on our recent discussion about the AI Core balancing act, the [Entanglement Station]( {{< relref "space/entanglement-station.md" >}}) is a highly specialized late-game space structure located in the [Charon]( {{< relref "planet/Charon.md" >}}) planetary system. 

Its primary function is to generate entangled particles (qbits) to drive your quantum computing systems, enabling you to "perform a cryptographical attack on the elders' encryption algorithms".

### **1. Unlocking and Costs**
To construct Entanglement Stations, you must first research the [Quantum Cryptography]( {{< relref "techs/Quantum_Cryptography.md" >}}) technology (which costs 1.25M Science and 1,024 Relics). 

Once unlocked, each station is highly resource-intensive to build:
*   **Base Cost:** **1,250 Relics**, **5,250 Antimatter**, and **5,000 Eludium**.
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.15**. (As a space structure, it does not benefit from Metaphysics price reductions).

### **2. Core Operations & Stats**
*   **Energy Consumption:** Consumes **25 Watts (Wt)** of active power.
*   **Gigaflop Consumption:** Consumes **0.1 Gigaflops** per tick.
*   **Hash Production:** Generates **0.1 Hashes** per tick.

### **3. Strategic Value: The Hashrate Relic Multiplier**
Hashes produced by the Entanglement Station are used to reverse engineer relics. This process establishes **Hashrate Levels**. Each Hashrate Level provides a multiplicative **+25% bonus to your automated relic production** from Space Beacons upgraded with Relic Stations. 

This makes Entanglement Stations a vital tool for exponentially scaling your Type 1 Relic Engine in the late game.

### **4. Cycle Multipliers (With Numerology)**
If you have researched [Numerology]( {{< relref "metaphysics/numerology.md" >}}), the active planetary cycle will directly modify how much processing power your stations consume:
*   **Charon Cycle (⍙):** Gigaflop consumption is increased by **+100%** (consuming **0.2 Gflops/tick**).
*   **Redmoon Cycle (⍜):** Gigaflop consumption is reduced by **-50%** (consuming only **0.05 Gflops/tick**).

### **5. The Gigaflop Balancing Act**
Because a single [AI Core]( {{< relref "buildings/AI_Core.md" >}}) produces **+0.02 Gigaflops per tick** and a standard Entanglement Station consumes **0.1 Gigaflops per tick**, maintaining a ratio of **5 AI Cores to 1 Entanglement Station** keeps your Gigaflop accumulation flat. If you lack the Transkittenism policy, failing to balance this ratio will cause Gigaflops to pile up, increasing your AI level and eventually triggering the AI Apocalypse at AI Level 15.
