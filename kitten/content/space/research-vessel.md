A [Research Vessel]( {{< relref "space/research-vessel.md" >}}) is an advanced, orbital structure constructed on the aquatic planet [Piscine]( {{< relref "planet/Piscine.md" >}}). It serves as a major scientific booster and is the premier mid-to-late game structure for passive starchart generation.

Here is a full breakdown of its costs, effects, upgrades, and cycle modifiers:

### **1. Unlocking & Costs**
To construct a Research Vessel, you must first reach its home planet by launching the [Piscine Mission]( {{< relref "space/Piscine_Mission.md" >}}) under your Space tab. Once unlocked, each Research Vessel has the following base costs:
*   **100 Starcharts**
*   **2,500 Alloy**
*   **12,500 Titanium**
*   **250 Kerosene**
*   **Price Ratio:** **1.15**. Like all other space structures, Research Vessels **do not benefit** from Bonfire-tab price ratio reductions (such as Golden Ratio or Renaissance), meaning each subsequent build strictly costs 15% more.

---

### **2. Core Effects**
Each active Research Vessel provides:
*   **Max Science +10,000:** This significant boost helps you dramatically expand your overall science capacity to unlock late-game exometallurgy and exophysics milestones.
*   **Starchart Production (+0.05/sec):** It serves as an incredibly efficient, automated source of starcharts, which is key for funding further space exploration.

---

### **3. Upgrades & Synergies**
You can drastically amplify the Max Science capacity of your vessels by purchasing specialized antimatter and void-themed Workshop upgrades:
*   [Antimatter Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Antimatter_Reactors.md" >}}) Boosts maximum Science from Research Vessels and Space Beacons by **+95%**.
*   [Advanced AM Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Advanced_AM_Reactors.md" >}}) Upgrades this bonus to **+150%**.
*   [Void Reactors:]( {{< relref "upgrade/Void_Reactors.md" >}}) Upgrades the bonus to a massive **+400%**.

---

### **4. Cycle Multipliers (With Numerology)**
If you have researched the [Numerology]( {{< relref "metaphysics/numerology.md" >}}) metaphysics upgrade, the active planetary cycle will directly scale the starchart generation of your vessels:
*   **Piscine Cycle ():** Provides a **+50%** boost to Research Vessel starchart production.
*   **Yarn Cycle (⍧):** Imposes a **-50%** penalty to Research Vessel starchart production.

---

### **5. Black Sky Challenge Interaction**
During the **Black Sky** challenge, **Research Vessels do not produce any starcharts**. This design prevents you from easily bypassing the challenge's primary bottleneck, forcing you to find alternative methods for accumulating starcharts.
