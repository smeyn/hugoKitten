In *Kittens Game*, [Knowledge Sharing]( {{< relref "Policy/Knowledge_Sharing.md" >}}) and [Cultural Exchange]( {{< relref "Policy/Cultural_Exchange.md" >}}) are mutually exclusive foreign policies unlocked after researching [Astronomy]( {{< relref "techs/Astronomy.md" >}}) and adopting the [Diplomacy]( {{< relref "Policy/Diplomacy.md" >}}) policy. Because they are in the same policy group, choosing one will permanently lock the other for the remainder of your current run.

### **Direct Comparison**

| Policy | Cost | Primary Effect | Strategic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Knowledge Sharing]( {{< relref "Policy/Knowledge_Sharing.md" >}}) | 4,000 Culture | **+5% science income** | Generally considered **suboptimal** because science is relatively easy to generate through Scholars and astronomical events. |
| [Cultural Exchange]( {{< relref "Policy/Cultural_Exchange.md" >}}) | 4,000 Culture | **+5% culture income** | Generally **preferred** because culture is a notorious mid-game bottleneck required for manuscripts and advanced research. |

### **Strategic Considerations**
*   **The Culture Bottleneck:** Throughout the mid-game (the "Theology" era), players often struggle to produce enough culture to craft the manuscripts needed for scientific advancement. Sources explicitly recommend [Cultural Exchange]( {{< relref "Policy/Cultural_Exchange.md" >}}) over Knowledge Sharing because "culture is slow" while "science is easy".
*   **Science Management:** Science income is rarely the primary constraint on progress, as science caps (expanded by Libraries and Academies) are usually more significant hurdles than science production rates. Consequently, a 5% boost to science income often provides negligible benefits.
*   **Iron Will Progression:** In the specialised [Iron Will]( {{< relref "Challenges/ironWill.md" >}}) mode (playing without kittens), where resources are even tighter, some guides suggest the **Diplomacy → Cultural Exchange** path as a way to bolster early culture production, which is vital for unlocking trade with the Nagas.

In summary, unless you are in a very specific scenario where your science production is lagging significantly behind your ability to store it, [Cultural Exchange]( {{< relref "Policy/Cultural_Exchange.md" >}}) is the standard choice to help mitigate the game's cultural bottlenecks.
