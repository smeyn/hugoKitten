In **Kittens Game**, [Immortalism]( {{< relref "Policy/Immortalism.md" >}}) is a high-level **Metaphysics upgrade** that fundamentally changes how your kittens interact with the cycle of life and death—specifically concerning their **rank and experience**.

### Key Effects
The primary function of Immortalism is to preserve the progress of your kittens across certain game events:

* **Experience Retention:** Normally, when a kitten dies or a reset occurs without certain upgrades, their accumulated experience and rank are lost. Immortalism allows kittens to **retain their rank and XP** even after they "die" from age or other factors (though they still need to be replaced by new kittens).
* **Leader Longevity:** It is particularly useful for maintaining high-level **Leaders**. Since leaders provide bonuses based on their rank (like production boosts or price reductions), keeping those ranks intact is vital for efficiency.
* **Reduced Micromanagement:** You won't have to spend as much time re-assigning and re-leveling leaders in very long runs.

---

### How to Unlock
To access Immortalism, you must have unlocked the [Metaphysics]( {{< relref "techs/Metaphysics.md" >}}) tab (which requires the [Astronomy]( {{< relref "techs/Astronomy.md" >}}) and [Philosophy]( {{< relref "techs/Philosophy.md" >}}) technologies and at least one [Ziggurat]( {{< relref "buildings/Ziggurat.md" >}})).

* **Cost:** **150 Paragon**.
* **Prerequisite:** You must first purchase the **malkuth** (or similar lower-tier) Metaphysics upgrades.

### Strategic Context: Is it worth it?
Since you are currently managing complex systems like [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}), here is how Immortalism fits into your late-game strategy:

1.  **Paragon Efficiency:** Early on, 150 Paragon is better spent on "Price Ratio" upgrades (like [Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Engineering.md" >}}) or [Golden Ratio]( {{< relref "metaphysics/Golden_Ratio.md" >}})). However, once you have your core ratio upgrades, Immortalism is a great "quality of life" purchase.
2.  **Long Runs (Dark Future):** In runs where you are pushing toward Year 40,000+, your kittens will inevitably go through many cycles. Immortalism ensures that your workforce remains "elite" without constant intervention.
3.  **Synergy with Cryptotheology:** As you build more [Black Obelisks]( {{< relref "transcendence/Black_Obelisk.md" >}}), your production bonuses skyrocket. Having high-ranked kitten engineers and scientists further multiplies these bonuses, making Immortalism a solid late-game investment.

> **Expert Tip:** If you are planning a [Reset]( {{< relref "guides/Reset.md" >}}) soon to farm Paragon, make sure you've spent your current Paragon on these permanent upgrades first, as they persist across all future timelines!
