In Kittens Game, [Outer Space Treaty]( {{< relref "Policy/Outer_Space_Treaty.md" >}}) and [Militarize Space]( {{< relref "Policy/Militarize_Space.md" >}}) are mutually exclusive Foreign policies that become available after you launch your first Satellite. Both cost 10,000 Culture to unlock, and picking one will permanently lock you out of the other for the rest of your run.

Here is a comparison of their effects and the community's strategic advice on when to choose them:

### [Outer Space Treaty]( {{< relref "Policy/Outer_Space_Treaty.md" >}})
*   **The Effect:** This policy establishes that space is the domain of all races, which improves your standing with all trading partners. The boost is equivalent to having **10 extra Tradeposts**. 
*   **When to choose it:** Community guides generally recommend picking Outer Space Treaty for most standard situations. Trade is a fundamental pillar of the mid-to-late game economy, making the diplomatic relations boost consistently useful.

### [Militarize Space]( {{< relref "Policy/Militarize_Space.md" >}})
*   **The Effect:** This policy turns your satellites into military research hubs. It makes the **maximum science capacity bonus** that your Satellites provide to your Observatories **10% more powerful**.
*   **When to choose it:** You should pick Militarize Space specifically if you find that your progression is constantly being blocked by your maximum science limit. The extra capacity boost can help you afford expensive space structures and technologies when your science storage is bottlenecked.
