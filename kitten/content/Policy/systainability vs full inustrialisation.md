In *Kittens Game*, [Sustainability]( {{< relref "Policy/Sustainability.md" >}}) and [Full Industrialization]( {{< relref "Policy/Full_Industrialization.md" >}}) are mutually exclusive environmental policies. They are unlocked after researching [Industrialization]( {{< relref "techs/Industrialization.md" >}}) and require you to have previously chosen either the [Strip Mining]( {{< relref "Policy/Strip_Mining.md" >}}) or [Clear Cutting]( {{< relref "Policy/Clear_Cutting.md" >}}) policy.

### [Sustainability]( {{< relref "Policy/Sustainability.md" >}})
*   **Cost:** 10,000 Culture.
*   **Primary Effects:** 
    *   **Removes the happiness penalty** (-2%) incurred from choosing Strip Mining or Clear Cutting.
    *   **Reduces global pollution** by 5%.
*   **Strategic Use:** This policy is generally recommended for the **first run**. During your initial playthrough, you will likely only build one or two Factories to unlock the policy system, meaning you cannot take significant advantage of industrial boosts. Sustainability allows you to keep the production bonuses of your earlier policy (wood or minerals) while eliminating the morale hit.

### [Full Industrialization]( {{< relref "Policy/Full_Industrialization.md" >}})
*   **Cost:** 10,000 Culture.
*   **Primary Effects:**
    *   **Boosts Factory Power:** Makes Factories **5% more powerful**. This increases their base crafting bonus from 5% to 5.25% (or from 6% to 6.3% if Factory Logistics is researched).
    *   **Increases Pollution:** Adds a **+5% penalty** to global pollution.
    *   **Space Production Synergy:** Provides a **5% bonus** to the "Space Production Bonus" generated by Factories once the [Space Manufacturing]( {{< relref "upgrade/Space_Manufacturing.md" >}}) upgrade is active.
*   **Strategic Use:** This is considered the **superior choice for the mid-to-late game** (typically starting from the second run onwards). Once you begin building dozens of Factories, the multiplicative nature of the crafting bonus becomes far more valuable than the small happiness gain from Sustainability.

### **Comparison Summary**

| Feature | Sustainability | Full Industrialization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Happiness & Environment | Crafting & Industrial Scaling |
| [Happiness]( {{< relref "guides/Happiness.md" >}}) | +2% (restores lost happiness) | No bonus |
| [Pollution]( {{< relref "guides/Pollution.md" >}}) | -5% reduction | +5% increase |
| **Factories** | No effect | +5% effectiveness boost |
| **Space Bonus**| No effect | +5% space production bonus |
| **Best For** | First Run / Low Factory Count | Mid-to-Late Game / High Factory Count |

**Strategic Recommendation:** Most players choose [Clear Cutting]( {{< relref "Policy/Clear_Cutting.md" >}}) early in the run to resolve wood bottlenecks, then pivot to [Full Industrialization]( {{< relref "Policy/Full_Industrialization.md" >}}) once they have a robust factory network. In the late game, Full Industrialization is essential for the "Shatter Engine," as it significantly scales [Unobtainium]( {{< relref "resource/unobtainium.md" >}}) production through Space Elevators and Orbital Arrays.
