In *Kittens Game*, [Strip Mining]( {{< relref "Policy/Strip_Mining.md" >}}), [Clear Cutting]( {{< relref "Policy/Clear_Cutting.md" >}}), and [Environmentalism]( {{< relref "Policy/Environmentalism.md" >}}) are mutually exclusive environmental policies unlocked after researching the [Engineering]( {{< relref "techs/Engineering.md" >}}) technology. You may only choose one of these policies per run.

### **Direct Comparison of Effects**

| Policy | Cost | Primary Effects | Pollution Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Clear Cutting]( {{< relref "Policy/Clear_Cutting.md" >}}) | 2,000 Science | **+30% wood production**, -2% happiness | +5% |
| [Strip Mining]( {{< relref "Policy/Strip_Mining.md" >}}) | 2,000 Science | **+30% mineral production**, -2% happiness | +5% |
| [Environmentalism]( {{< relref "Policy/Environmentalism.md" >}}) | 2,000 Culture | **+3% happiness** | -5% |

#### **Iron Will Bonuses**
In [Iron Will]( {{< relref "Challenges/ironWill.md" >}}) mode (where you have no kittens), these policies provide unique bonuses to your zebras and celestial events:
*   [Clear Cutting:]( {{< relref "Policy/Clear_Cutting.md" >}}) +10% bonus to catnip refinement (equivalent to one un-upgraded Lumber Mill).
*   [Strip Mining:]( {{< relref "Policy/Strip_Mining.md" >}}) +10% bonus minerals from meteors.
*   [Environmentalism:]( {{< relref "Policy/Environmentalism.md" >}}) No specific Iron Will bonus is listed for the base policy, but it leads to [Open Woodlands]( {{< relref "Policy/Open_Woodlands.md" >}}), which provides a 4.16% bonus to both refinement and meteor minerals.

---

### **Strategic Advice from the Sources**

*   **The Case for Clear Cutting:** This is widely considered the **strongest choice for early-to-mid-game runs**. Wood is a major bottleneck when transitioning into the "Titanium Era," as you need massive amounts of it for **Observatories**, [Harbours]( {{< relref "buildings/Harbour.md" >}}), and **Ships**. 
*   **The Case for Strip Mining:** Generally viewed as the least useful of the production-based choices. Minerals are typically easier to accumulate than wood through standard progression, making the 30% boost less impactful than the wood boost from Clear Cutting.
*   **The Case for Environmentalism:** Sources describe this policy as "pretty weak" because the 3% happiness boost is often outweighed by the raw production benefits of the other two. However, it is the only option that directly **reduces pollution**, which otherwise reduces catnip production and Solar Revolution bonuses.

---

### **Downstream Policy Unlocks**
The choice you make here determines which advanced policies you can research later in the run after reaching [Industrialization]( {{< relref "techs/Industrialization.md" >}}) or [Ecology]( {{< relref "techs/Ecology.md" >}}):

#### **Following Clear Cutting or Strip Mining:**
*   **Sustainability (10k Culture):** Removes the -2% happiness penalty from your chosen production policy and provides a further -5% pollution reduction.
*   **Full Industrialization (10k Culture):** Makes **Factories 5% more powerful** (boosting their crafting bonus), but increases pollution by another +5%.

#### **Following Environmentalism:**
*   **Conservation (10k Culture):** Increases the happiness bonus from Environmentalism to **+5%** and provides another -5% pollution reduction.
*   **Open Woodlands (10k Culture):** A compromise policy that provides **+12.5% wood and mineral production** at the cost of +5% pollution.
