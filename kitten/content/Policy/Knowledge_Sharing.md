In **Kittens Game**, [Knowledge Sharing]( {{< relref "Policy/Knowledge_Sharing.md" >}}) is a mid-game **Workshop upgrade** that significantly streamlines your science progression by allowing your kittens to "learn from each other" more effectively.

### Key Effects
The primary function of Knowledge Sharing is to **increase your Science production** based on your population and infrastructure:

* **Science Generation:** It provides a **+10% bonus** to all Science production. 
* **Scholar Efficiency:** It specifically boosts the effectiveness of [Scholars]( {{< relref "job/Scholar.md" >}}). In the mid-game, Scholars are your primary way of reaching high Science caps for technologies like [Electricity]( {{< relref "techs/Electricity.md" >}}) and [Industrialization]( {{< relref "techs/Industrialization.md" >}}).
* **Cultural Synergy:** It improves the rate at which [Manuscripts]( {{< relref "resource/manuscript.md" >}}) are consumed or utilized for certain scientific breakthroughs, making it easier to maintain your "Culture" levels.

---

### Requirements to Unlock
To see Knowledge Sharing in your Workshop, you must have advanced through the basic educational technologies:

| Requirement | Type | Cost / Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Philosophy]( {{< relref "techs/Philosophy.md" >}}) | Technology | The primary academic prerequisite |
| [Academy]( {{< relref "buildings/Academy.md" >}}) | Building | You must have at least one [Academy]( {{< relref "buildings/Academy.md" >}}) built |
| [Science]( {{< relref "resource/science.md" >}}) | Resource | **15,000 Science** |
| [Manuscripts]( {{< relref "resource/manuscript.md" >}}) | Resource | **100 Manuscripts** |

### Strategic Value
Given that you are managing [Enriched Uranium]( {{< relref "upgrade/Enriched_Uranium.md" >}}) and [Distortion]( {{< relref "upgrade/Distortion.md" >}}), Knowledge Sharing is a fundamental building block you likely acquired to scale your Science cap. Here is why it remains relevant in the late game:

1.  **Reaching the Antimatter Era:** To unlock [Antimatter]( {{< relref "resource/Antimatter.md" >}}) and [Relic]( {{< relref "resource/relic.md" >}})-based technologies, you need Science caps in the millions. Knowledge Sharing acts as a multiplier that makes every [Library]( {{< relref "buildings/Library.md" >}}), [Academy]( {{< relref "buildings/Academy.md" >}}), and [BioLab]( {{< relref "buildings/Bio_Lab.md" >}}) more potent.
2.  **Reset Efficiency:** When you reset (especially with [Chronospheres]( {{< relref "buildings/Chronosphere.md" >}})), Knowledge Sharing is one of the early upgrades you want to grab to quickly regain your tech tree progress.
3.  **Leader Synergy:** If your **Leader** is a **Scientist**, the Knowledge Sharing bonus stacks with the Leader's rank bonus, allowing for massive bursts of Science generation.

> **Pro Tip:** If you find yourself "Science-capped" (unable to afford the next tech even when your storage is full), focus on building more [BioLabs]( {{< relref "buildings/Bio_Lab.md" >}}) and [Compendiums]( {{< relref "resource/Compendium.md" >}}). Knowledge Sharing will then apply its percentage bonus to that higher base.
